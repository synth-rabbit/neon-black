# The Cutloose — Membership

The Tokyo pattern (Tokyo p. 158, 200): what a PC of the Cutloose looks like on the sheet, and what the cell expects.

## Who gets in

**You cut, and then you are found.** There is no application and no recruitment. A Doppel gets out — by an owner's mistake, a unit's collapse, a job that went wrong, a decision made in four seconds — and then spends days or weeks alone with a tracker still inside them, and is found by somebody who was looking, or is not found and is recovered. That is the whole entry process, and most of it happens before the cell knows the person exists.

What follows is the queue ([[changeling-cells|the Cutloose]] twist, BC-123): the tracker out, then the switch out, at the Sump, up the Old Sump Stair, in the order [[odile-ferraz|Six]] decides. Nobody is cut for free. Everybody works — a face, a courier run, an infiltration — because there is no other money, which means every member of the cell is doing the job they escaped, for less, with no protection (Bible §4, theme 3).

**Non-Doppels.** The cell is not exclusive by ideology, only by fear. A Baseline, a Caster, a Howler, or anyone else can be *known to* Gallery Nine, vouched for, brought down the route, and even trusted — the Bible says escapees "form groups with other escapees" (§2), and under the Wall, escapee is a broader word than Doppel. What no non-Doppel gets is the list.

**Leaving.** People go back. It is the thing nobody at the hatch says out loud: a Doppel who returns to an owner is sometimes taken back, sometimes made an example of, and always debriefed, and everything they know about the route goes with them. That is why the route changes ([[gallery-nine-watch]]).

## What the cell expects

- **Arrive dark.** Nothing transmitting, ever, on any route, for any reason.
- **Take the work.** The list is paid for by jobs, and a member who will not work is a member somebody else is being cut behind.
- **Vouch carefully.** A voucher whose visitor is wrong comes off the list, and everyone at the hatch knows what that costs.
- **Wear your own face inside.** The one rule that is not about survival, and the one nobody breaks.
- **Do not go looking for your makers.** Everyone wants to. The Wall of Faces is what happens to people who tried.

## On the sheet

**Self kit:** [[cutloose-cell]] (Affiliation) — the escaped-cell kit. Its Identity should be about the list, not about freedom: something like *nobody gets left outside the hatch*, written so that acting against it is obvious at the table ([[style-guide]] §3).

**Noise side.** A Doppel PC's required primary is Noise — Augmentation, body-level mods — with Mythos as the optional secondary, the Tao half (Brief §2.1, §2.5). Essence minimum: at least one Noise theme (BC-1). WP2 writes the custom kits (`02-splats/changeling/theme-kits/`), including the **escaped** weakness set: the owned version carries *kill switch* and *tracker*; the escaped version replaces them with *hunted by my makers* (Brief §2.5).

**Book kits to offer alongside** (Brief §0.1 — always both):

| Impossibly Good Looks · Hidden Gadgets | Augmentation (Noise) | Core p. 239 | *alternate face*, *self-suggestion techniques*, *holographic face mask*, *hide my secret life* — the Face-Dancer package the Brief names (§2.5). |

| Corporate Citizenship | Affiliation (Self) | Core p. 198 | For a PC still working an owner's clearance from the inside, wearing the citizenship they escaped. |

**Specials.** The persistent condition (Brief §2.4; BC-3) is where the escaped Doppel lives on the sheet — the PC Specials are [[the-face-doesnt-come-off]], [[switch-and-handler]], [[retrieval-contract]] and [[the-cells-will-answer]] (WP2). The Plan's pairing is *kill switch (owned) / hunted (escaped)*; a Special in the escaped register should carry the two facts this package's Challenges lean on: **the switch and tracker are gone and nothing can be done remotely to this character** (Bible §2), and **their face is on somebody's recovery list forever**.

**What affiliation costs.** A place on the list, which means somebody else is behind you. Jobs that are the job you escaped. And no protection of any kind: no corporation, no syndicate, no police, no Chancery category ([[government]]), and no second refuge.

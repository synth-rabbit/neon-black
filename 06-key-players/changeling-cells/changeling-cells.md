# The Cutloose

![[assets/key-players/changeling-cells-scene.png]]

![[assets/key-players/changeling-cells-emblem.png]]

**Role:** changeling-cells · **Territory:** [[foundation-galleries]] (Gallery Nine) · [[gullet-market]] · **On the street:** *a cutloose*, *the Cutloose* ([[names]] §1)

## Base concept

Doppels are made and owned. Body mods at the Tao-and-tech level, in some cases allowing Face-Dancer impersonation; built as corporate special units, syndicate assassins, and spies; controlled by whoever built them, with kill switches and trackers (Bible §2). Some have got out and removed both. They live in constant danger for having done it, they group with other escapees, and their society is **purely underground with no political pull** (Bible §2, §3).

In [[palisade|Palisade]] that means the Wall's foundation. The [[foundation-galleries]] are honeycombed with construction galleries, drainage tunnels, pump chambers and sealed voids, nobody governs them, and the corporations' security does not go below the Foot. Somewhere in there is **Gallery Nine**: a dry concrete hall two hundred metres long behind a submarine hatch, lit by scavenged panels, holding perhaps thirty Cutloose at a time and never the same thirty (BC-22). It is reached by a route that changes, through the water, past people who will kill a tracker on sight. Inside it, faces are not worn — it is the only place in the city where a Doppel is nobody but themselves.

The Cutloose are a Key Player because of what they know and what they can do, not because of anything they hold. They have no money, no turf worth the name, no allies, and nothing to trade except the skills they were built with.

## Twist

**Source:** invented — **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-123). The Bible fixes that escapees removed their switches and trackers, are in constant danger, group with other escapees, and are purely underground with no pull. Everything below is this package's invention.

**Gallery Nine is a queue.**

Coming out is not one act. A tracker has to be found and cut out, a kill switch has to be found and cut out, and both are surgery — deep, specific surgery on a body built by somebody who did not want it reversed. There is one surgeon in Palisade who will do it: the Sump, the lowest surgery in [[suture-row]], reached from the Galleries by the Old Sump Stair (BC-22). The surgeon is not a saint. The surgeon charges.

So the cell's real, daily function is **triage**. Who gets cut next. Who eats. Who is turned away at the hatch because they are still transmitting and does not understand that the refusal is not cruelty. Who is worth the risk of the route. [[odile-ferraz|Six]] decides, and every decision is somebody left outside. The Galleries' only law is the pump schedule, and the cell's only law is the list.

**And the money comes from the work.** The one asset a Doppel has is a Doppel. A face, an infiltration, a courier run, a signature, a voice on a call — the things they were built to do, which is why they were valuable enough to own. So the cell sells them: to fixers, to [[fence-network|the Weighhouse]], to whoever is paying under the Wall this month, one job at a time, and the money buys surgery and food and a clean identity. Escapees who came out of a corporate special unit do corporate-special-unit work for a fifth of the price and no protection at all. **Freedom, so far, is the same job with a different buyer** — the ownership theme (Bible §4) played straight, with nobody heroic in it. Everyone in Gallery Nine knows this. It is the argument they have with each other, forever, and it is why some of them go back.

**The Wall of Faces.** Every escapee arrives with a partial picture of who made them: a room, a smell, a logo half-remembered, a technician's voice, a batch designation. No two pictures agree. The cell keeps them anyway — a wall of drawings, notes, and burnt-out ID fragments at the far end of the gallery — because knowing who makes Doppels is the only thing that would change anything, and after eleven years the wall still does not add up to an answer. **[OPEN]** (OQ-30) Who makes Doppels is not decided by this package. `00-meta/additions/WP4-trio3.md` records a proposal for the GM; nothing in any file here asserts it, and the Wall of Faces is written so that it can be true either way.

- [[corin-alvarez]] — "Tuesday." Eleven days out, scars still closing, and being hunted by the people who made her.

## Resources

- **Gallery Nine** — a sealed pump gallery behind a submarine hatch that opens from the inside; about thirty people at a time. The refuge (BC-22).
- **The Old Sump Stair and the Sump** — the maintenance stair inside the Wall to a sealed door behind Suture Row's lowest surgery, and the one surgeon who will cut a tracker out. Known to perhaps a dozen people; a crew that learns the route has learned something worth killing for ([[foundation-galleries]]).
- **Routes that change** — through the water, at pump-schedule hours, past watchers. Profiled as [[gallery-nine-watch]].
- **Faces** — the escapees themselves, and everything a Doppel was built to do: impersonation, infiltration, courier work, a voice, a signature, a body double. This is the cell's entire economy.
- **The Wall of Faces** — every fragment anybody remembers about their maker, none of it agreeing.
- **Clean identities** — bought at the Weighhouse one at a time, at [[marisol-okonkwo|Tally's]] prices, which are fair and are still more than the cell has.
- **The pump schedule** — the Galleries' only law: when the water comes, everyone moves up, and whoever does not, drowns.
- **Nothing else.** No money, no pull, no allies, no second refuge, and nowhere to go if this one is found (Bible §3: purely underground, no political pull).

## Agenda

**Cut the next one out. Feed the ones already out. Keep the Galleries unfindable.**

That is the whole of it, and it does not scale. This Series the cell is running three problems at once. **The list is too long** — more escapees have come out in the last eighteen months than in the previous five years, because the corporations are rearming and a rearming corporation makes new special units and loses old ones. **The Sump's price has gone up**, because the surgeon's risk has gone up, because there is more corporate recovery traffic under the Wall than there has been in a decade. And **somebody has been asking about the Galleries** — not a corporation, not a police detail, somebody with money who is asking the right questions in the Gullet.

Six's answer to all three is the same: take more work. Which means more Cutloose doing the work they escaped, in more places, wearing more faces, for more buyers — and every job is a chance to be recognized.

## What the upstart's destabilization means here

The Bible: every faction smells opportunity, including the secret ones (§5). The Cutloose's opportunity is chaos and buyers, and both come with a bill.

**Chaos to hide in.** Four corporations busy with each other means recovery teams pulled onto other work, concurrences bought for districts that are not the Galleries, and an entire city's attention on a corporate war ([[secret-war-goes-public]]). It is the safest year Gallery Nine has had.

**Buyers for what they know.** It is also the first year anyone has wanted to *pay* for a Doppel's knowledge rather than their face: who was in that building, what a special unit's rotation looks like, how a corporate clearance actually works from the inside. That money is real, and it is the fastest way the cell has ever found to get somebody off the list and onto the Sump's table.

And it is exactly how a purely underground society stops being underground. A cell that becomes useful becomes findable. Six knows it. She takes the work anyway, because the list is too long, and every name on it is somebody currently sleeping in a drainage tunnel with a tracker still inside them.

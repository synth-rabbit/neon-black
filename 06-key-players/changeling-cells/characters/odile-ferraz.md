# Odile Ferraz

![[assets/npcs/odile-ferraz.png]]

**Handle:** "Six" · **Affiliation:** [[changeling-cells|the Cutloose]] · **Role in the pilot:** the hatch, and the price of it.

## Who they are

She runs [[foundation-galleries|Gallery Nine]] — as much as anything down there is run. Forty-ish by appearance, though appearance is not a fact about a Doppel; short, careful, quiet-voiced, with the flat unhurried competence of someone who has spent eleven years deciding which of two people gets the surgery.

Her handle is a refusal. Her makers' paperwork called her the sixth of a batch of eight, and when she got out she kept the number instead of taking a day of the week like most escapees do (BC-124). She says it is because a name should be true. The other seven of her batch are dead, owned, or unaccounted for, and the last is on the Wall of Faces in her own handwriting.

Six's kill switch and tracker are out — she is Cutloose, which is the only thing that word means (Bible §2). The scars are old. What is not old is the list: the order in which the people sleeping in Gallery Nine will be taken up the Old Sump Stair and cut free, which she writes and rewrites and does not show to anyone. She has turned people away at the hatch for still transmitting. She has left somebody outside for a week because the route was wrong that night. She has sold a friend's face to a Continuity contract to pay for a stranger's surgery, and she would do it again, and she is not at peace about any of it.

She is not a leader in any sense a corporation would recognize. She has no authority except that people do what she says, no force except thirty frightened escapees, and no plan beyond the next name. What she has instead is that she has never once, in eleven years, given the Galleries away.

## Vector

- **Want:** Everyone on her list cut free and fed, and Gallery Nine never findable. The list gets longer every month, and those two wants have started to be the same want pointing in opposite directions.
- **Push:** The cell's only currency — Doppel work. A face, an infiltration, a courier run, a signature, a voice. She will hire the crew, sell the crew a route, sell the crew *to* somebody, or trade a piece of what the cell knows about a corporation's interior, and she will price all of it in surgeries.

## What the crew knows / does not know

**Knows** (only if a PC is Cutloose, or has been taken there): there is a place under the Wall where escapees go, and a woman who decides who gets in. **Does not know:** the list; that the Sump charges; that the cell's income is the same work it escaped; that somebody with money has been asking about the Galleries in the Gullet and Six has known for two weeks and told nobody.

# The Cutloose — Challenge Reuse

Existing book Challenges [[changeling-cells|the Cutloose]] field and face, per Brief §0.1. The custom profiles are [[gallery-nine-watch]], [[fresh-cut]], and [[six-at-the-hatch]]. **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-119) `type: meta` — reuse index, not an entity.

## The face is Noise, not Mythos — `[RULES CONFLICT]` (CR-19)

The book's **Shape-Changing** Power Set (Tokyo p. 137) is filed under **Mythos**, and its framing assumes a being whose changing shape comes from a legend or a Source. Canon here: a Doppel is "a mix of Tao and tech at the body level" whose Face-Dancer impersonation comes from **body mods** (Bible §2), and the Brief maps the changeling's required primary theme to **Noise** (Augmentation) with Mythos only as the optional secondary — the Tao half (Brief §2.1). So the Power Set the book gives us for shapeshifters is categorized against this setting's canon.

Two options; the GM picks, and this package does not:

1. **Use Shape-Changing as written, re-labelled Noise.** Keep every mechanic — *Beast Mode* becomes *another face*, the beast tags become *somebody else's voice*, *somebody else's gait*, *a clearance that is not mine*. Simplest, and correct for the fiction; costs the book's Mythos/Noise bookkeeping nothing at the table.
2. **Wait for WP2.** The changeling package writes a custom Doppel Power Set (`02-splats/changeling/power-sets/`) in the Noise category. Use Shape-Changing until it lands, then replace.

Either way: **no Doppel Challenge in this folder uses a Mythos-framed effect for the face.** The Tao half of a Doppel (Bible §2) is not what the face is, and no profile here gives it a mechanic — **[OPEN]** (OQ-30, OQ-5).

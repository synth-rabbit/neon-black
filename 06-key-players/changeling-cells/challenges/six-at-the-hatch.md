# Odile Ferraz, "Six"

**Role:** barrier · **Scale:** 0 (one person, with thirty behind her) · **Alias:** *the woman who decides* · *The cell leader in the scenes where the hatch does not open.*

The NPC file is [[odile-ferraz]]; this is her profile for the scenes she stands in the way of. She is a barrier and not an attacker on purpose: fighting her is possible, easy, and the worst outcome available to a crew that wants anything from [[changeling-cells|the Cutloose]] ever again.

She has no force, no money, no pull, and no allies (Bible §3: purely underground, no political pull). What she has is the list, the routes, thirty people who will move when she says so, and eleven years of never having given the Galleries away. Her kill switch and tracker are long out — she is Cutloose, which is the only thing that word means (Bible §2). The scars are old, and she is very tired, and she has already decided what she is going to do before the crew starts talking.

## Specials

**The List:** Everything the cell has is priced in surgeries. She names the price plainly and does not haggle, because the price is somebody's turn at the Sump.

**Cannot Be Bought Off:** *Buy off* is immune. Money offered instead of the work she asked for adds a tier to *decided* and ends the conversation.

**She Moves First:** If she believes the crew was followed, the cell moves, the route retires, and the hatch is elsewhere by morning. Progress on [[gallery-nine-watch]] resets and re-earning it needs a voucher, which after this is nobody.

**Not The Same Woman:** Until *earn the hatch* is maxed, the crew cannot confirm they have spoken to her before, and any plan that depends on recognizing her fails.

**Thirty People:** A status a member of the cell can take instead, they take — not out of loyalty, but because the list dies with her. Escapees: Gang Member (Core p. 306), Scale 1.

# Ondine Ferreira

![[assets/npcs/ondine-ferreira.png]]

**Handle:** "the Coat" · **Affiliation:** [[corp-a|Meliora Bioworks]] · **Role in the pilot:** the vector face — Meliora at street level, in a van, being kind.

## Who they are

Meliora Community Health Outreach, Foot districts. Thirty-four, Fitted, unhurried, good with children and better with the elderly; she has worked the same circuit for six years and knows more names in [[marlow-blocks]] and [[suture-row]] than the Chancery's census does. The van is white, the awning says the leaf-and-lattice mark, and the service is real: vaccinations, dressings, antibiotics, blood pressure, a bag of chlorine tablets when the storms come. She has kept people alive who would otherwise be dead, and she will remind a crew of that, gently, if they get in her way.

The street calls her **the Coat**, which is not affectionate. Everyone under the Wall understands that the free clinic is a purchase; what they do not know is what is being bought. What she is buying is variance. Every draw she takes from someone with visible Patched sculpt-work goes up the Wall in the warm-handled case with a barcode and a district, and the file it lands in is Settling Sciences' ([[solenne-marchetti]]). She does not know that file's real name and has never asked. She has been told the program is called *field variance*, that the subject population is "the black-market sculpt cohort," and that the work will one day make chop-shop bodies survivable. She believes this. It is even, in a narrow sense, true.

She is not stupid. She has noticed that her collection quotas rise the week after a bad night in [[cinder-yards]]; she has noticed that her supervisors want photographs of a specific kind of scarring and never say why; she has noticed that the one thing she may never do is refer a subject to a Meliora warranty clinic. She has decided, so far, that noticing is enough. A crew that gives her one more thing to notice has a vector face who might turn — and a corporation that will notice her turning.

## Vector

- **Want:** Clean samples and clean paperwork out of bodies nobody is counting, in the volume Settling Sciences asks for — and, under that, a floor number in the Spire and out of the van.
- **Push:** Money and medicine, never force. She pays cash for access, in small honest amounts, and the asks sound like nothing: a name for the list, a blood draw, a photograph of a healed cut, five minutes with somebody who used to run with a pack and stopped. When she is refused she does not argue; she goes back the following week, and the following, and the district's need does the arguing.

## What the crew knows / does not know

A crew from under the Wall has almost certainly met the van. They know the Coat's face, that the medicine is real, and that Meliora does not do anything for free. They do not know that the samples are indexed against a fifteen-year study of the Run, or that the study has a name. She does not know either — she knows the phrase *field variance* and the barcode format, which is exactly enough to be the thread a crew pulls.

## Challenge

None. If a scene needs her escorted, the escort is a [[sterile-field-unit]] pair off-duty or a hired security element from [[corp-a-reuse]]; the Coat herself does not fight, and a scene in which she is threatened is a scene about what the district will do about it.

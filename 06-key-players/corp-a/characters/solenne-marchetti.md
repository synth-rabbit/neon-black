# Solenne Marchetti

![[assets/npcs/solenne-marchetti.png]]

**Handle:** — · **Affiliation:** [[corp-a|Meliora Bioworks]] · **Role in the pilot:** the executive whose signature is on the Long Settle, and the one person who could close the leak with a phone call.

## Who they are

Vice President, Settling Sciences. Sixty-something and Sculpted so competently that the only tell is how well she moves; she came up through the theaters, not through finance, and she still says *the table* when she means a decision. She keeps an office on the Spire's twenty-eighth floor with a working bench in it, wears the same soft grey coat everywhere including the Crest, and carries a warm-handled sample case that she sets down on the floor beside her chair the way other people set down a dog.

Marchetti is the reason [[corp-a|Meliora]]'s sculpting business has a defect rate anyone can print. She solved settling, and Accord is hers. She is genuinely proud of it and will talk about it to anyone at length, in the register of a surgeon describing a good outcome. She is also the person who read the first cleanup report out of [[cinder-yards]] fifteen years ago, understood in one page what had happened and why, and wrote the memo recommending that the supply arrangement be left in place and monitored. She has never described that memo as a decision about people. She describes it as *not intervening in a natural experiment*, and she means it; she thinks the Run's existence is a tragedy she did not cause, that ending the supply would kill them faster than continuing it, and that a program which turns their suffering into a licensed product is the only outcome in which any of it was worth anything. Every part of that is a real argument. She has had fifteen years to make it airtight.

What she does not have is time. The Long Settle is one submission away from being a product and one leaked invoice away from being a scandal, and she knows which of those is likelier this year.

She does not threaten and does not need to. Her instrument is the offer: a contract, a clinic, a warranty, a name removed from a list, a body fixed at no cost. She has never in her career been the person who was refused.

## Vector

- **Want:** Eleven more years of variance data, and a regulatory submission that gets waved through while [[corp-b|Orison]] and [[corp-c|AP&I]] are looking at [[upstart|Continuity]].
- **Push:** Money, access, and a warranty. She funds problems rather than fixing them: a crew asking hard questions about the dose gets a grant, a clinic contract, a Meliora badge, and a very reasonable explanation — and the badge is a tracker and the explanation is true.

## What the crew knows / does not know

Everyone on the Wall knows Meliora's name and that it sells bodies. Almost nobody outside the Terraces has heard of Settling Sciences, and nobody under the Wall connects a pharmaceutical vice president to a dose bought at a rail siding in [[cinder-yards]]. What a crew could learn, in this order: that Meliora sells a surgical adjunct called Accord; that a licensed relief clinic under the Wall buys it in bulk on a standing contract; that the clinic is the [[syndicate|Almoners']] Kitchen; that the account has never been audited; and that the person who signs off on not auditing it is Marchetti.

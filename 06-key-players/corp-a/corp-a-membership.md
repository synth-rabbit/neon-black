# Meliora Bioworks — Membership

What a character affiliated with [[corp-a|Meliora Bioworks]] looks like, in the Tokyo membership pattern (Tokyo p. 191, 209, 228).

## Getting in

Meliora hires at two doors and they do not connect.

**The front door** takes qualifications: a degree, a theater residency, a nursing license, a bench record. It leads to corporate citizenship — a warranted body, monitored implants, terrace or dormitory housing in [[meliora-terraces]], a house feed, and a contract that says what your tissue is worth if you leave. Fitted at intake, Sculpted if you last.

**The side door** takes bodies. Meliora runs outreach, clinics, and trials under the Wall, and the people who come through them are not employees; they are subjects, contractors, and stringers — the driver who moves a case, the Row cutter with an aftercare account, the person who lets the van take a draw every month for cash. A character from under the Wall who is "with Meliora" almost always came in this way, and knows it.

Nobody who came in at the side door is ever told what the program is called.

## What Meliora expects

- **Paperwork before people.** An incident is a report and a report is an audit. Meliora's first instruction in every crisis is to document it and its second is to contain it; helping someone is third and is genuinely permitted, third.
- **The company's tissue stays the company's.** Grafts, cultures, samples, and the bodies they came out of are inventory. Taking one home is theft, and Internal Assurance treats it as theft.
- **Never say what the compound is for.** Employees on the Accord line are told they work on a surgical adjunct, which is true. Asking the second question is a career, not a crime — the file notes it, and the notes accumulate.
- **Kindness is the product.** Meliora's people are expected to be gentle, patient, and unhurried, on the Crest and in the Sinks alike. It is not hypocrisy in the corporation's own mind; it is quality control.

## What Meliora gives

Access to bodies that money cannot otherwise reach: a graft, a repair, a sculpt at cost, aftercare that works, a warranty that a Row clinic will honor on sight. A badge that opens medical doors on three tiers. A name on a lift permit. Cash, on time, for work nobody documents.

## On the character sheet

- **Self kit (Affiliation):** [[meliora-citizenship]] — corporate citizenship in Meliora, in the pattern of Corporate Citizenship (Core p. 177) with Meliora's own tags. A side-door character takes the same kit with the weakness tags foregrounded and no housing.
- **Existing kits worth offering alongside** (Brief §0.1): **Corporate Citizenship** (Affiliation, Core p. 177) as written; **Expertise** kits for a clinician or bench scientist (Core p. 196–219); **Assets** for a stringer with a van and a case.
- **Specials:** any Megacity-style PC Special tied to warrantied, monitored implants belongs here (WP2 writes the splat-condition Specials; a Meliora warranty is not one of the five and must not be written as one).
- **What it is not:** affiliation with Meliora is not a route to becoming one of the five. Nothing in this kit sculpts a character into a Howler; that requires a chop-shop, a frame, and a dose the corporation does not sell to you ([[packs|the Run]]).

## Leaving

Front door: a non-compete, a de-warranting appointment at which monitored implants are re-flashed to consumer firmware, and a file. Side door: you simply stop being visited, unless you were interesting, in which case the van keeps coming. A character who leaves carrying documentation — an invoice, a dosing record, a page of the variance file — is not an ex-employee; they are a retrieval.

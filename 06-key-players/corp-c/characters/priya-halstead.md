# Priya Halstead

![[assets/npcs/priya-halstead.png]]

**Handle:** — · **Affiliation:** [[corp-c|Amalgamated Prosthetic & Interface]] · **Role in the pilot:** none on screen; she names the event, and the name governs everything after.

## Who they are

Senior Vice President, Reconciliation & Recovery. Her office is on the Top Deck — the one deck of [[amalgam-stack]] with windows — and *Reconciliation* in her title means what it means everywhere else in the company: the arithmetic of accounts that have gone wrong. Forty-five, immaculate, entirely pleasant, and the only executive on the deck who has actually walked the causeway at Coldwater, once, eleven years ago, and did not need to again.

She owns the Standing Account's collections apparatus, the Recovery Desk, the remediation lease, and — as an operational line item that appears in no organizational chart — Reconciliation Facility 4. [[vera-solano]] reports to her. So does [[rasheeda-novak]], four grades down.

Her problem is not the strike. Her problem is the *word*. A site incident is reportable to the board's audit committee. A remediation-contract disruption is not. A labor action is not. Between those three words is her career, the lease renewal, and whether AP&I is a corporation that was robbed or a corporation that has a camp — and she has been drafting the sentence for a week.

She is polite to people she is destroying, and means it. It is not a technique; she genuinely does not think of it as personal, because it is not. It is the account.

## Vector

- **Want:** a name for Coldwater that keeps it out of the audit committee, and a lease renewed on schedule.
- **Push:** the contracts office, the security command, and money — a discretionary budget for problems that are cheaper bought than fought, which is most of them, and which is why AP&I's security office has twice hired through [[fence-network|Tally's network]] for work it did not want on its own payroll ([[amalgam-stack]]).

## What the crew knows / does not know

**Knows:** nothing. They have never heard her name and there is no reason they should.

**Does not know:** which five of the hundred-odd escaped accounts were on one work crew, or that they matter to anyone. She has not read the list. Reading the list is four grades below her.

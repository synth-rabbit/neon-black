# Rasheeda Novak

![[assets/npcs/rasheeda-novak.png]]

**Handle:** — · **Affiliation:** [[corp-c|Amalgamated Prosthetic & Interface]] · **Role in the pilot:** none; she is why nobody comes for the crew, and she is why that could change.

## Who they are

Recovery Analyst, grade three, Contracts Office, thirtieth deck of [[amalgam-stack]]. Thirty-one, tired, good at her job in a way nobody above her has noticed yet. She has a desk, two screens, a chair that does not adjust, and one hundred and four open files from the Coldwater event, of which she has priced ninety-six.

She does not hunt anybody. That is the thing worth understanding about the Recovery Desk: **AP&I's recovery function is a pricing function** ([[corp-c|AP&I]]'s twist). Novak's job is to determine, for each escaped account, the cost of collection against the expected return, and to mark the file *pursue*, *monitor*, or *write off*. Ninety-one of her ninety-six are written off — the crew among them, because five Patched bodies with second-hand chrome and no assets are not worth a collector's day rate (Bible §6: on the list, not worth a bounty).

*Write off* is not *close*. The file stays. And Novak, who is thorough and bored, re-prices written-off files whenever something in them changes: a warranty tag that starts reporting again, a name that turns up on a Chancery permit, an insurance claim, a clinic invoice in [[suture-row]], a rumor bought for the price of a month's rent from somebody who knew them. She is very good at buying rumors. It is cheaper than sending anyone, and it is the whole of her method.

She has no feeling about the people in the files. She has a feeling about the *arithmetic*, and it is the closest thing she has to a temper: a file mispriced by somebody else is the only thing that makes her raise her voice.

## Vector

- **Want:** to price the list correctly.
- **Push:** a small discretionary budget for information, a list of subcontracted collectors she never meets in person, and telemetry from chrome that most of the list has forgotten is warrantied.

## What the crew knows / does not know

**Knows:** nothing, unless they go looking. What they may learn, on the street, is the shape of it: that AP&I did not come after them, and that this is not the same as being free of it.

**Does not know:** why one hundred and four accounts defaulted in a single night. She has flagged the correlation in a comment field. Nobody has read it.

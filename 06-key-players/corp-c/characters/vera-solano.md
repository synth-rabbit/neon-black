# Věra Solano

![[assets/npcs/vera-solano.png]]

**Handle:** "Nought" · **Affiliation:** [[corp-c|Amalgamated Prosthetic & Interface]] · **Role in the pilot:** the camp's authority during the strike and the breakout.

## Who they are

Facility Reconciliation Officer, Reconciliation Facility 4. Not a warden — AP&I does not have wardens, it has officers, and her title is an accounting title because the camp is an accounting instrument ([[corp-c|AP&I]]'s twist). Sixty, narrow, grey, in a company coat that has been pressed every day for nineteen years. She has run the Ledger since the second block went up.

Twice a shift she reads the count over the yard speakers herself. She does not delegate it and she does not hurry it: the block, the number, the number she expected, and then, if there is a difference, the difference, and then a silence long enough for everyone in the yard to think about what the difference means. The inmates call her **Nought**, for the number she is trying to reach — nothing outstanding, nobody unaccounted for, a column that balances. She knows the name and does not mind it.

She is not cruel in the way a camp officer in a story is cruel. She is worse: she is *fair*, in the specific sense that the numbers are applied to everybody identically and the numbers are the whole of her morality. She will not have a man beaten for spite and will add three months to his account for a broken tool without changing expression. She knows the crew's faces and their block, and could recite what each of them owed to the day.

Her guards are AP&I corporate security — exactly as legal as the police (Bible §3), and out on the sludge-flats the only law there is.

**The night of the strike** she stayed in the administration block, because the debt records are in the administration block, and she was still trying to raise the Stack on a dead Nearspace when a hundred people went over the wire.

## Vector

- **Want:** the count level. Every body accounted for; every account serviced or properly written off; the facility's numbers matching what the Top Deck expects them to be.
- **Push:** the wire, the floodlights, the guards, the causeway gate, and the arrears column. She can add to what a person owes faster than any person can work it down, and everybody at the Ledger knows it.

## What the crew knows / does not know

**Knows:** her voice, twice a shift, for as long as they were inside. What she sounds like when the difference is not zero. That she signed something with each of their names on it. That she was never once in the yard when violence happened and always knew about it by the next count.

**Does not know:** what she says to the Top Deck after the strike, or that her own position depends on how she words it.

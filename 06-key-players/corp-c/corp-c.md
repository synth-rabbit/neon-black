# Amalgamated Prosthetic & Interface

![[assets/key-players/corp-c-scene.png]]

![[assets/key-players/corp-c-emblem.png]]

**Role:** corp-c · **Specialty:** cybernetics (BC-8, GM-approved) · **Street:** *AP&I*, *Amalgam* · **Territory:** [[amalgam-stack]] (HQ) · [[coldwater-outfall]] (the Ledger) · [[suture-row]] · [[corbel-gallery]]

## Base concept

The oldest-sounding of the Big Three and the one whose product is in the most bodies. AP&I makes mass-market chrome: replacement limbs, eyes, hands, organs, interface ports, warranty-grade implants — the metal half of the ladder everybody in Palisade is climbing (Bible §3). It makes it in [[amalgam-stack]], a vertical factory-city of thirty decks bolted into the Face of the Wall, and it fits it in the same building, in mass-fitting theaters that run three shifts, two hundred bays under one vaulted ceiling and a queue of new hires waiting to be made **Fitted**.

It has fingers in every pie the other two have fingers in. It is known for this one. A Stock body that signs on with AP&I comes out Fitted, and that sentence is the company's entire advertising and the company's entire trap.

It also owns **Reconciliation Facility 4** — the Ledger — a secret debt-labor and penal camp in [[coldwater-outfall]], behind a Chancery uninhabitability declaration and a public remediation contract (Bible §3, §5; BC-24). It is neither the corporation that manufactures the Howlers' trigger nor the corporation the Wuji hold (Bible §3). The camp produces something generic and **the camp is not the mystery** (Bible §5).

## Twist

**Source:** invented — **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-101), per Brief §6.4's authorization to invent the twists the Bible leaves open. It contradicts nothing in the Bible, touches no legend-Mythos, and requires no Tao.

**AP&I is not a manufacturer with a lending arm. It is a lending book with a factory attached.**

The chrome is sold at a margin that would embarrass a foundry, because the chrome is not the product — it is the **collateral**. Every fitting opens a **Standing Account**: a financed instrument secured on the hardware, and therefore on the body it is installed in. The warranty is the servicing agreement. The monitoring clause is the covenant. *In good standing* is not a courtesy phrase in the Stack, it is a status on a ledger line, and it is checked at the lift.

Three things follow, and they are the corporation.

1. **AP&I's real asset is what Palisade owes it.** Not the foundry, not the patents — the aggregate account. Its board manages a city the way a lender manages a portfolio: grade the population, extend credit one tier down when growth is wanted, tighten the covenants when it is not. The caste system's slow formalization (Bible §3) is, from the Top Deck, a risk model getting better.
2. **A default is not a loss, it is a change of collateral.** When the account cannot be serviced and the hardware cannot be economically repossessed, the obligation is transferred to the only asset remaining, which is the debtor's time. That is what Reconciliation Facility 4 is *for*, and why a corporation that makes prosthetics runs a labor camp in a drainage waste. The camp's generic product pays the camp's costs; the camp's actual output is amortization.
3. **Nothing ever closes.** An account can be current, in arrears, reconciled, or written off. Written off is not settled. It is a line that stays on the book at zero value, and a line at zero value costs nothing to keep — which is precisely why the crew are on an escapee list **and not worth a bounty** (Bible §6). The corporation is not chasing them. It is waiting to see whether they ever become worth collecting. See [[escapee-recovery-desk]].

The inmates named the camp the Ledger as an insult ([[names]] §2). They were being literal and did not know it.

## Agenda

**This Series, AP&I is a corporation trying to have a quiet quarter after being robbed in a way it cannot report.**

- **Keep the Ledger unsaid.** A Big Three corporation's secret facility was breached, its population scattered across the sludge-flats, and one specific body removed (Bible §5). Reporting it means admitting the camp. The lease renewal is due; the Chancery's inspector signs twice a year; the fifth block is still going up. Everything AP&I does this year is downstream of not saying a word.
- **Price what was lost.** Not revenge — reconciliation. The Recovery Desk's job is to work out which of a hundred-odd escaped accounts are worth collecting and which stay written off, and to do it without anyone asking why so many defaulted at once.
- **Hold the agreements.** AP&I is the Big Three member with the most to lose from the stalemate cracking, because a lending book needs a stable city and a war is a mass default. It will absorb an insult to keep the civil agreements standing — up to a point nobody at the table has located yet.
- **Keep grading the city.** The Fitting Theaters run three shifts regardless. Credit goes one tier further down this year in [[suture-row]] and [[corbel-gallery]] because growth targets say so.

## Resources

- **The Stack.** Foundry, assembly, [[amalgam-stack|Fitting Theater Twelve]] and eleven like it, warehouses, worker housing, the Top Deck's contracts office and security command, and the Bottom Bays where everything leaves.
- **Warranty telemetry.** Every Fitted body reports: hours, load, location coarse enough to be deniable, and servicing status. Disclosed in the fitting paperwork. This is the largest continuous dataset in Palisade about what people's bodies are actually doing, and AP&I is the only Key Player that owns it.
- **The Standing Account.** The instrument, the covenants, and the collections apparatus behind it.
- **Reconciliation Facility 4**, the remediation lease, the barge line, and the causeway.
- **Corporate security**, exactly as legal as the police (Bible §3), and considerably more present than the police anywhere AP&I owns the ground — which on the Face is a district.
- **The Recovery Desk** and the escapee list.
- **A seat at the civil-agreements table**, and the institutional memory of fifty years of keeping it.

## What the destabilization means here

AP&I is the Key Player the crack hurt first, which is exactly why it has an opportunity and knows it.

The stalemate held AP&I to its lane as the one with the mass market and the pit at the bottom of it ([[spine-and-clock]]). The crack offers **cover**. A city in which corporate security is jumpy, cordons go up without explanation, and everyone assumes the worst about everyone is a city in which a corporation can move populations, close a site, open a bigger one, and reprice a whole class of accounts, and nobody looks — because everybody is looking at [[upstart|Continuity]]. The Ledger's fifth block was signed for before the strike; the labor to fill it can now be described as an emergency measure.

And it offers a **grudge with a receipt**. AP&I cannot name Continuity publicly. It can make certain that Meliora and Orison privately understand exactly what happened to it, which converts a humiliation into the strongest argument at the civil-agreements table in fifty years: *the fourth one does not respect the arrangement, and next quarter it will be your site.* Whether the board is willing to spend the Ledger's secrecy to make that argument is the question this Series puts to it — **[OPEN]** (OQ-41).

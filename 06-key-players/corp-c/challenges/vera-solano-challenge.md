# Věra Solano, "Nought"

**Role:** watcher · **Scale:** 0 (a shift of guards is Scale 2 beside her — [[ledger-line-security]]) · **Alias:** *the voice on the yard speakers* · *She is not cruel. She is fair, and the numbers are the whole of her morality.*

Facility Reconciliation Officer, Reconciliation Facility 4, nineteen years. See [[vera-solano]]. Her role is **watcher**, not attacker: she counts, she notices, she prices, and she is almost never in the room where the violence is. Her guards are AP&I corporate security, exactly as legal as the police (Bible §3), and in the [[coldwater-outfall|Outfall]] they are the only law.

## Limits

| Limit | Tier |
|---|---|
| hurt or subdue | 3 |
| convince | 4 |
| implicate | 5 |
| discredit the count | 4 |

**discredit the count** at 4 is the real fight with her: prove to the camp — not to her — that the column can be wrong. Maxing it removes *reads the count herself, twice a shift* for the rest of the job, and *The Ledger Is Never Wrong* stops working.

**implicate** at 5: she has kept nineteen years of records and has never once falsified one, which is both her defense and the reason her records are worth more than she is.

## Tags & statuses

reads the count herself, twice a shift, keys to the administration block, nineteen years of debt records, knows every face by its number · *unhurried-3*

## Specials

**Her Guards Take It:** physical statuses go to Security Guard (Core p. 305), Scale 1, in AP&I colors.

**The Column, Not The Fist:** her preferred Consequence is *arrears-3* — unhealing, not physically mitigable, and it follows the PC out of the camp ([[escapee-recovery-desk]]). She orders violence only when arrears cannot reach.

**The Ledger Is Never Wrong:** arguing a number adds *arrears-1* before the roll.

**She Signs For The Fifth Block:** once per downtime, names move onto a work crew — Present a New Challenge (fifth-block construction detail, or the shed floor as Hazard Zone, Core p. 310) or Escalate the Situation.

## Threats / Consequences

› The yard speakers click on between counts, which they do not do.
» A name, an account and a figure read to the whole camp (*shamed-2*, *arrears-2*)
» She reads the difference and waits (*braced-for-it-2* to the yard)

› She comes to the shed floor with a clerk and looks at the quota board, not at anyone.
» The shortfall apportioned across the shift (*arrears-2*)
» She indicates one person; the clerk writes (Escalate the Situation)

› She asks a question she already has the answer to, and does not blink.
» The true version, inside a minute (burn a cover-story or alibi tag)
» The answer changes a number, and they pick which (*arrears-3*, theirs or somebody else's)

› She locks the administration block from the inside while the yard is still shouting.
» The debt records are sealed (Deny Them Something They Want; *the records are sealed*)
» She raises the Stack on a hard line — Present a New Challenge: [[escapee-recovery-desk]]

## Power Sets

None. **Connected & Protected** (Core p. 328) is wrong for her: she is a facility officer at the far end of a lease, and if the Top Deck decides Coldwater was a labor action, she is the one it happened to.

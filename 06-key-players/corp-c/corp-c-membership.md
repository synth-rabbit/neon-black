# AP&I — Membership

What a PC who is [[corp-c|Amalgamated Prosthetic & Interface]]'s looks like — because in the Stack, *citizenship* is the right word and *employee* is not. The Tokyo pattern (Tokyo p. 209).

## Getting in

You queue. That is the whole process, and it is the most ordinary door in Palisade: a hiring hall on the Bottom Bays of [[amalgam-stack]], a medical, a grade, a deck assignment, and a fitting appointment in one of the twelve theaters. Two days from the queue to the table. AP&I hires **Stock** bodies by the thousand and does not care where they came from.

What you sign at the fitting desk is three documents that are one document: an employment agreement, a fitting consent, and a **Standing Account** — a financed instrument secured on the hardware about to be installed in you. The chrome is a benefit. It is also collateral. The paperwork discloses all of it, including the monitoring, and nobody reads past the first page, and AP&I is not lying to anyone.

**A Stock body that signs on with AP&I comes out Fitted** ([[names]] §4). That is not a metaphor for social mobility; it is the mechanism of it, and the reason a hiring hall on the Bottom Bays has a queue around the block on a wet morning.

## What the company expects

- **The augs are the job.** A company interface port at the base of the skull, a warranty tag in the forearm, and whatever the deck needs — foundry hands, assembly eyes, a fitting technician's steadied nerves ([[amalgam-stack]]).
- **Good standing.** Serviced on schedule, account current, quota met. *In good standing* is a status on a ledger line and it is checked at the lift.
- **The company's property leaves with you.** The warranty voids the day you quit; the hardware does not stop being collateral; the account does not close.
- **Nothing goes out the Bottom Bays that is not on a manifest.** The giveaway ring is the standing insult on this point, and the security office has never caught it.
- **Reconciliation.** If the account cannot be serviced and the hardware cannot be economically repossessed, the obligation transfers to the only asset left, which is your time. Nobody in the hiring hall is told what that means. Everybody in the Stack has heard of Coldwater and thinks it is a rumor.

## What it gives you

Warranty-grade chrome most of Palisade cannot buy; a deck, a bunk, a canteen number; corporate security's protection anywhere AP&I owns the ground, which on the Face is a district; a warranty that will actually service your arm; and a band. **Fitted** is a real rung and this is the ladder most people climb it on.

## Which kit

**Self kit:** [[api-citizenship]] *(Affiliation)* — the Corporate Citizenship pattern (Core p. 177, 199) written for a company that finances you. Expected shape: power tags around company access, warranty-grade hardware, deck and shift networks, the security office's protection, and knowing how the theaters and manifests actually work; weakness tags around the monitored port, the warranty tag, *in good standing*, and an account that never closes.

**Existing kits that fit beside it** (Brief §0.1 — offer both):
- **Corporate Citizenship** (Affiliation, Core p. 199 range) — *monitored by the company*, *corporate logo tattoo* as a warranty tag. The book's kit is almost this setting already.
- **Chipped Weapon Mastery** / **Animalistic Modifications** and the rest of **Augmentation** (Core p. 238–239) for a Fitted body whose work is physical — with the canon line below.
- **Exoskeleton Suit** (Cutting Edge, Core p. 242 range) for a foundry or bay rig.
- **Escaped Servitude** (Troubled Past, Core p. 218) for a PC who came out of the Ledger — which is every PC in this Series. WP3's camp kit is the custom counterpart.

**Specials.** Megacity-style, Tokyo p. 72–74 pattern, condition-gated, prerequisite required, surviving theme replacement (Brief §2.4). Candidates for WP2/WP3:
- **In Good Standing** — prerequisite: an open Standing Account with no arrears. Doors, lifts, and clinics that check the account open for you. The moment you take *arrears*, this stops until it is cleared.
- **The Tag Still Reports** — prerequisite: AP&I chrome, worn or removed-but-not-extracted. You can be found by it, and you can also feed it: once per downtime, you may deliberately make the telemetry say something untrue about where you are.
- **Theater Hands** — prerequisite: work in a fitting theater. You can fit, service, or crudely remove AP&I hardware without a licensed bay.

## The canon line, and a conflict

**[RULES CONFLICT]** (CR-16). Two canon statements collide here:

- [[amalgam-stack]] (WP1, BC-17): AP&I **requires invasive body augmentation of all its workers** as a condition of employment.
- Bible §2 (hunter): Baselines use **cybernetics only as replacements for lost body parts** — arm, leg, eyes — and **body sculpting and bio-manipulation are not on the table**. This is the line between "still human" and one of the five.

A Baseline PC cannot take an elective interface port and remain what the Bible says a Baseline is. Options, presented rather than resolved:

1. **Exempt them by grade.** AP&I sells a wearable **band-grade** interface — a collar unit and a wrist tag rather than a port and an implanted tag — for employees who cannot or will not take the implant. It exists, it works, and it pays a grade less, which makes *band-grade* a slur on the decks. This is the reading adopted in this file because it obeys both statements and makes the caste theme sharper rather than softer. **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-106).
2. **Narrow the requirement.** The mandate applies to production decks only; contracts, security, and logistics staff are exempt. Simple, and costs the Stack some of its menace.
3. **Let the Baseline take it and treat the port as a replacement.** Contradicts Bible §2 as written. Not adopted.

The GM chooses. Nothing else in this package depends on which.

## Essence

A Fitted AP&I PC is **Self + Noise** — Affiliation plus Augmentation or Cutting Edge — and lands on **Cyborg** or **Nexus** (Brief §2.2). A **Baseline** AP&I citizen built toward **Real** takes option 1 or 2 above and carries no Augmentation theme at all (Brief §2.3). Members of the other four work AP&I's decks like anyone else; the company grades bodies and does not ask what is in them beyond what it installed.

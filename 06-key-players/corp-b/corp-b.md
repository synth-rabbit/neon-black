# Orison Defense Systems

![[assets/key-players/corp-b-scene.png]]

![[assets/key-players/corp-b-emblem.png]]

**Role:** corp-b · **Specialty:** weapons (BC-8, GM-approved) · **Territory:** [[orison-reach]] · [[chancery-hill]] · [[halloran-circus]] · **Street:** *Orison*

## Base concept

One of the Big Three, and the one everybody buys from. Small arms, security systems, the drones over the Face, the sidearm on a Chancery patrol's hip, the platforms the three corporations keep in reserve for the war they are not fighting — Orison makes them, sells them to all four powers at once, and is the only corporation in [[palisade|Palisade]] that openly keeps an army, because the civil agreements say it may (Bible §3; [[spine-and-clock]]). Its headquarters, ranges, and armories occupy the whole seaward headland of [[orison-reach]], walled off from the rest of the Crest by a second palisade of Orison's own.

Orison is the *orderly* corporation. Clean, drilled, quiet, unfashionable. Its executives sculpt for endurance and reaction time rather than beauty; its catalogue is conservative; its advertising is a serial number. Where [[corp-a|Meliora Bioworks]] sells you a better body and [[corp-c|Amalgamated Prosthetic & Interface]] sells you the ladder, Orison sells the one product whose value is that it works exactly as specified, every time, and can be audited. That reputation is the whole company. It is also, at the bottom, a lie.

The name is a prayer, and reads on the Wall as empty corporate Latin ([[names]] §2).

## Twist

**Source:** invented — **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-116). The Bible fixes only that Corp B is controlled by the Tao secret society and that the corporations prefer the society stay secret (Bible §2, §3). Everything below is this package's invention and the GM may overrule it.

**Two percent of Orison's output is not manufactured.** The catalogue is ordinary and merely very good: competent metallurgy, competent software, competent quality control. What holds Orison's seat among the Big Three is **Pattern Four** — a small line of weapon cores, ammunition batches, and security modules whose brass plates carry a fourth digit stamped deeper than the rest, sold at ruinous prices to buyers who are told only that the line is "hand-finished at the Reach." They are. In a sealed shop cut into the rock beneath the Reach Armory, initiates of [[tao-society|the Wuji]] finish them by hand — Tao worked into an object, in the way this setting has always described it (Bible §1; [[pillars]], Tao Weaponized) — and the result is an item that does what its specification says under conditions where the specification should fail, and that no rival laboratory has ever taken apart and understood. Fifty years of Meliora and AP&I teardowns have produced fifty years of tolerance charts and no explanation. **[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** (CR-1) The engine would call such an item a Source-touched relic; here it is a Tao-worked item, made by a practice, with no legend behind it and nothing that wants anything.

**Orison does not know this about itself.** Below the executive floor — below a list of names that fits on one page — Orison believes Pattern Four is a proprietary finishing process protected by the most aggressive trade-secret regime in Palisade. That regime is **Product Integrity**: a department with recovery crews, seal auditors, a contract arm that buys silence, and one written purpose, *no Orison item is opened by anyone*, which its own staff read as ordinary corporate paranoia and execute with religious thoroughness. Product Integrity is the Masquerade in a single department (Brief §4.1), and the people enforcing it are not in on the secret.

**The twist has a clock in it.** A war doubles the orders for Pattern Four, and Pattern Four is finished by hand by a small number of people who cannot be replaced by hiring. Orison can meet a war's demand for its ordinary catalogue easily. It cannot meet the demand for the line that is the reason anyone calls first. Every month of the Series, Orison's order book runs further ahead of a shop that does not scale — and the corporation's answer to that gap, whatever it turns out to be, is the story this Key Player is in.

## Resources

- **The Reach Armory** — headquarters, executive floors underground, armories below those, the hardest Secured Site on the Wall ([[orison-reach]]). Profiled as [[reach-armory-seal]].
- **The seaward proving grounds** — kilometres of cratered headland, target hulks, observation bunkers, and a fence that faces the city rather than the water.
- **The private port** — Orison's own ships come and go at the headland's foot without Chancery customs. Everything that leaves the sealed shop leaves this way.
- **Product Integrity** — recovery crews who retrieve Orison items that have travelled where they should not, seal auditors who check that cases came back unopened, and a contract department whose settlements are quieter and larger than a lawsuit. Profiled as [[product-integrity-team]].
- **Pattern Four** — the hand-finished line. Two percent of output, a third of the margin, and all of the reputation. Nobody outside the executive floor and the shop knows why it is different.
- **The sealed shop** — under the Armory, behind an airlock with a maintenance log that has never recorded a maintenance. Its technicians are Wuji initiates on Orison payroll and Orison never rotates them.
- **Police contracts** — the Chancery's police carry Orison sidearms under a service contract that includes armourers, a repair depot, and a standing relationship with the police command on [[chancery-hill]].
- **Arena contracts** — the licensed ring at [[halloran-circus]] buys its permitted weapons from Orison, which is how a weapons corporation keeps a public face that is entertainment rather than war.
- **A seat in the civil agreements** — the compact that assigned the heavy weapons to Orison is the oldest of the three, and the one the other two have most reason to keep (Bible §3).

## Agenda

**Fill the order book without letting anyone open the box.** Continuity's ruthlessness has done Orison the favour every arms manufacturer waits for: the three corporations that agreed to keep things civil are all buying as though they had not. Orison's public agenda is the ordinary one — take the orders, hold the price, stay out of the politics, sell to all four, and be the last corporation anyone can afford to attack.

Underneath, three things run at once. **Ration the line:** Pattern Four is allocated by the executive floor and always has been; the allocations have become political, and every allocation is a favour that will be called. **Protect the seal:** Product Integrity's budget has doubled in two years and its recovery crews now operate under the Wall as freely as on the Crest, which is how a trade-secret department turns into an armed one. **Buy time:** if the shop cannot go faster, the answer is to make the ordinary catalogue look more like the extraordinary one — a marketing problem that becomes a fraud problem the first time a buyer tests a batch that is not Pattern Four and finds out.

Orison will not start the public war. It is the one corporation that profits more from the war staying private, because a private war is a bidding war and a public one is a supply crisis ([[secret-war-goes-public]]).

## What the upstart's destabilization means here

The Bible's spine: Continuity is breaking the civil agreements, and every faction smells opportunity (§5). For Orison the opportunity is money and the price is exposure, in the same transaction.

An arms manufacturer's best year is the year before a war, and this is that year: three corporations rearming without admitting they are rearming, a fourth buying openly, and a Chancery that wants "assistance." Orison can name its price for the ordinary catalogue. But the buyers who matter all want Pattern Four, and every crate of it that goes out of the private port is a Tao-worked object leaving the only place it can be protected, into a war where things get captured, torn open, and photographed. **The corporation's edge and the Wuji's secret are the same object.** Push the line hard enough and the secret walks out of the Reach in a case, one delivery at a time; hold the line back and the corporation loses the reason anyone calls it first.

That is Orison's whole Series: an order book it cannot fill, a department that will do anything to keep a box shut, and a controlling interest that would rather lose the quarter than lose the quiet.

## Hooks

- A Pattern Four case is lost between the private port and a buyer under the Wall. Product Integrity wants it back unopened, and is hiring outside the corporation for the first time anyone can remember — because inside crews ask what is in it.
- [[reidar-solano]] wants a competent, deniable crew to do what his own quality department is forbidden to do: acquire one Pattern Four core off Orison's own supply chain and have somebody, anybody, open it.
- A Meliora team offers the crew a package to leave on the seaward ranges during a test window and does not say what it detects ([[orison-reach]]). It is an Adjunct Series field trial, and Product Integrity is already watching the fence.
- The Chancery's police armoury reports a batch of service sidearms that will not pass Orison's own acceptance test. Somebody has been shipping ordinary cores with a fourth digit on the plate.

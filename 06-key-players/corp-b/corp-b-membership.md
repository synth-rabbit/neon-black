# Orison Defense Systems — Membership

The Tokyo pattern (Tokyo p. 158, 200, 209): what a PC affiliated with this Key Player looks like on the sheet, and what the Key Player expects in return.

## Who gets in

Orison hires two ways and they barely overlap.

**Cleared employment.** Range crews, armourers, drivers, escorts, logistics, quality, contracts. Orison does not hire Stock into the Reach at all — its stated reason is clearance, and nobody on the Crest has ever asked for a second one ([[orison-reach]]). A Patched applicant gets as far as the depot on [[chancery-hill]] or the port's casual labour list; a Fitted one with a clean warranty and a service record gets inside the wall. The clearance process is thorough, slow, and interested in debts: Orison would rather employ someone it can afford to keep than someone it cannot afford to lose.

**Contract work.** Everything Orison needs done that cannot be done by a cleared employee: a recovery under the Wall, a delivery that must not be manifested, a range that must be walked before a test. Contractors are hired through cut-outs, paid through [[product-integrity-team|Product Integrity]]'s contract arm, and never told what they are carrying. This is the crew's most likely relationship with Orison, and it is a relationship [[adaeze-ferreira]] regards as a form of ownership.

There is a third way in, and nobody applies for it. Some Orison employees are noticed at [[chancery-hill|Aldine House]] — a scholarship, a morning practice, no interview — and end up in the forty-one, or on the executive floor. That road belongs to [[tao-society|the Wuji]] and is written in `06-key-players/tao-society/tao-society-membership.md`, not here. An Orison PC does not know it exists.

## What Orison expects

- **Nothing is opened.** The rule is absolute, unexplained, and enforced. An employee who opens a sealed case is not disciplined; they are settled with and removed from the record.
- **The manifest is the truth.** What the paper says is what was in the crate, whatever was in the crate.
- **No politics.** Orison sells to all four powers. An employee who has an opinion about which of them should win is a liability in a year when all four are buying.
- **The company pays for problems.** Debts, clinics, landlords, relatives with a habit — Orison will buy them, and then Orison owns them. This is presented as a benefit and is one.

**Specials.** Orison affiliation pairs with the Baseline PC Specials ([[cheap-prosthetic]], [[just-human-and-thats-enough]], [[another-baseline-with-a-gun]]) — a replacement prosthetic is company property until the last instalment is paid.

**What affiliation costs.** An Orison PC carries a settlement on somebody: a debt Orison bought, a relative's clinic account, a warranty that lapses the day they leave. Contract PCs carry the same thing with no salary attached.

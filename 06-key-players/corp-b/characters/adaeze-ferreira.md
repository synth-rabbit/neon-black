# Adaeze Ferreira

![[assets/npcs/adaeze-ferreira.png]]

**Handle:** — · **Affiliation:** [[corp-b|Orison Defense Systems]], [[tao-society|the Wuji]] · **Role in the pilot:** the officer who buys the problem back.

## Who they are

Vice-President, Product Integrity. Sixty-ish, small, grey-suited, gloved indoors, with the flat courtesy of someone who has read your file and does not intend to mention it. She keeps an office at the [[orison-reach|Reach Armory]] with nothing personal in it and a second desk at Orison's floor on [[chancery-hill]], where she does most of the work that matters: buying, settling, and arranging which force answers a call.

Ferreira came in through contracts, not weapons. She was noticed at twenty-two in the garden at [[chancery-hill|Aldine House]] — a scholarship, a morning practice, no interview — and she has kept the discipline every morning since, standing before dawn in the Armory's empty parade square because the practice needs a condition and hers is stillness and a level floor. Of Orison's officers she is the only one who knows what the sealed shop is for. She is the reason the maintenance log under the Armory has never recorded a maintenance.

She is not cruel and does not enjoy the work. Her whole career is a containment job that she believes in: a Tao-worked item on the open market is a thing that gets copied, mass-produced, weaponized at scale, and traced back to the people who can make it — and the practice cannot survive that. She would rather pay a crew than kill them. She has a budget for paying and she has a budget for the other thing, and both are approved annually.

**[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** (CR-1) Ferreira is a Caster in the vault's terms: a practitioner of a discipline, with tech that augments it (Bible §2 mage). She is not an avatar or an incarnation of anything, her practice has no story in it, and nothing she keeps has a will of its own.

## Vector

- **Want:** Every Pattern Four item that has left Orison's hands back in a sealed case, unopened, with nobody able to say what was interesting about it.
- **Push:** Orison money first — a contract, a settlement, a debt bought out from under someone, a job offer that is really a leash — and [[product-integrity-team|Product Integrity]] second. She has an account with [[emeric-vann]] at the Chancery and a standing arrangement with [[halima-boyce]]'s office, so the question of who was legally in the room is usually settled before she arrives.

## What the crew knows / does not know

**Knows** (if they have any street standing): Orison's trade-secret people are the worst possible attention, they pay first, and they are not police. **Does not know:** that Product Integrity is a Masquerade department; that Ferreira is of the Wuji; that the "process" she protects is a practice and not a machine. Nothing in her manner offers it — she is a corporate lawyer who gets up early.

## Challenge

None. Ferreira does not fight; she is the reason a fight is already funded, and her instrument is [[product-integrity-team]]. If a scene forces her into one, run her with the Corporate Executive profile (Core p. 304) plus **Connected & Protected** (Core p. 328), and note that she is a practitioner — the **Arcane** Power Set (Core p. 330), reinterpreted as a Tao discipline **[TAO-REINTERPRETED]**, or WP2's mage Power Set once it exists (`02-splats/mage/power-sets/`).

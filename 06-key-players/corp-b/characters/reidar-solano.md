# Reidar Solano

![[assets/npcs/reidar-solano.png]]

**Handle:** — · **Affiliation:** [[corp-b|Orison Defense Systems]] · **Role in the pilot:** the employer whose job is a betrayal of his own company.

## Who they are

Senior Vice-President, Volume Munitions. Forty years old, ex-range crew, promoted through logistics, and by any ordinary measure the most successful executive at the [[orison-reach|Reach]]: he runs ninety-eight percent of what Orison makes, on time, at margin, in a year when everyone is buying. He is broad, blunt, brand-loyal in the way [[corbel-gallery]] would recognize, and drinks with his shift managers, which the executive floor finds vulgar and useful.

He is also measured, every quarter, against a line he is not allowed to see. Pattern Four sits in a separate ledger, priced by people who do not attend his meetings, allocated by the executive floor as a favour, and credited with the reputation his division's product actually earns. Solano has spent two years assembling a document about it: yield figures that do not reconcile, a shop with no maintenance history, a headcount of forty-one technicians who have never been rotated, a finishing process no engineer he trusts can describe. He has asked for a teardown twice, in writing, through channels. Both requests came back approved and then unactioned, which is worse than a refusal and he knows it.

He is not a whistleblower and has no politics. He believes his company is being robbed by an internal fiefdom, and he wants the fiefdom broken so that the numbers come to him. He will hire people he can disown, pay them out of a discretionary line, and hand them to [[adaeze-ferreira]] himself the moment the exposure gets closer to him than to her.

## Vector

- **Want:** One Pattern Four core, taken off Orison's own supply chain, opened by somebody who does not work for Orison, with a written answer he can put in front of the board.
- **Push:** A volume division's discretionary budget; a shipping schedule that says which crate is on which barge and when the private port is quiet; and, if it comes to it, a subordinate's name on the requisition instead of his own.

## What the crew knows / does not know

**Knows:** a senior Orison executive is paying well for a job against Orison's own logistics, which is either the best contract of the year or a trap. **Does not know:** that the thing he wants opened is a Tao-worked item; that Product Integrity is not a trade-secret department; that his two written requests are the reason [[adaeze-ferreira]] has a file on him. Solano does not know any of that either.

## Challenge

None. Solano does not fight — he signs. If a scene needs him under pressure, use the Corporate Executive profile (Core p. 304) with **Corporate Sponsored** (Core p. 328) and no bodyguards worth the name; his security is [[product-integrity-team|Product Integrity]]'s, which is the joke.

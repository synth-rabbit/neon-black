# The Wuji — Challenge Reuse

Existing book Challenges [[tao-society|the Wuji]] fields, per Brief §0.1. The custom profiles are [[wuji-operative-challenge]] and [[aldine-house]]. **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-119) `type: meta` — this is a reuse index, not an entity.

**Standing rule for this Key Player.** Every reused profile below that assumes a Mythos, a Source, a legend, a cult, a conjured being, or worship is used only after that assumption is struck out (Brief §3.2; CR-1). Under Tao there is a practice, a condition, an item, and — rarely — a made thing. There is nothing to banish, petition, or bargain with. Where a book Challenge's Consequences depend on bargaining with a spirit or exorcising a legend, those Consequences are dropped, not translated.

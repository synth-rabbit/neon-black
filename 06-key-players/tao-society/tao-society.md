# The Wuji

![[assets/key-players/tao-society-scene.png]]

![[assets/key-players/tao-society-emblem.png]]

**Role:** tao-society · **Territory:** [[chancery-hill]] (Aldine House) · [[orison-reach]] (hidden) · **Internally:** *the Wuji*; a member is *of the Wuji*. **Rumour calls it:** *the Quiet Hand* ([[names]] §2).

## Base concept

Tao is real, older than the mods, and rarer than any implant; it is impersonal, it has no will and no agenda, and it can be interwoven with technology and can produce beings (Bible §1; Brief §3.1; [[pillars]]). The Wuji is the one organization in [[palisade|Palisade]] that understands it. It is a society of practitioners that identifies talented people and brings them into the fold (Bible §2), it controls one of the Big Three (Bible §2, §3), and it is otherwise hidden — which the corporations prefer (Bible §3).

Its Crest front is **Aldine House**: three storeys of pale brick behind a wall and a gate on [[chancery-hill]], a library, a recital hall, a garden of raked gravel and one old tree, a movement-and-breath practice taught in the mornings to the Sculpted and their children, and a Chancery minister on the board (BC-19). Impeccably respectable, and the most-used meeting room on the Hill for people who do not want to be overheard. On the Hill they whisper that some of the House's long-standing members can *do things*. The whisper is called the Quiet Hand. The House finds the name charming and has never corrected it.

**[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** (CR-1) Nothing here is a cult and nothing is worshipped, because there is nothing to worship. The Wuji's word for what it does is *the practice*: a discipline kept under a condition, which either holds or does not. Members do not pray, do not petition, and do not speak of Tao as wanting anything ([[style-guide]] §6).

## Twist

**Source:** invented — **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-120). The Bible fixes that the society is secret, recruits the talented, controls Corp B, and that the corporations prefer its secrecy. Everything below is this package's invention.

**The Wuji is a containment operation, and it has been losing since the day it started.**

Its founding proposition is simple and unheroic: Tao cannot be manufactured, but a *practice* can be copied, and a copied practice in a corporation's hands becomes a research programme, and a research programme becomes a product line, and a product line becomes the reason a city goes to war over gateways. So the Wuji does three things and has done them for longer than anyone in it can document. It **recruits** — every talented person left standing outside is a gateway somebody else could buy, so the House finds them first, at twenty-two, with a scholarship and a morning practice and no interview. It **keeps nothing in writing** — no curriculum, no archive, no file; the House's library is a real library about everything except the one subject, and what a member knows is held in the member. And it **owns a corporation** — Orison was taken a century ago not to gain power but to be unpressurable: a body big enough that no other body can compel it, with walls it built itself and a legal army the civil agreements permit ([[corp-b|Orison Defense Systems]]).

**The trap is the corporation.** Containment is expensive — people bought out of contracts, records tidied, a shop staffed by forty-one technicians who are never rotated and never leave — and the money comes from **Pattern Four**, the two-percent line of hand-finished Orison cores that the initiates work in the sealed shop under the Armory. The Wuji funds the hiding of the practice by *selling the practice's output*, at ruinous prices, to the exact organizations it exists to keep the practice away from. Every crate is a Tao-worked item leaving the one place it can be protected. Fifty years of teardown labs have produced nothing but tolerance charts, and fifty years is not forever, and Continuity has just made everyone rich enough to try harder.

**And recruitment has a second face.** "Identifies talented people and brings them into the fold" is the Bible's phrase, and inside the House it means two things at once: an offer, and a removal. Those who accept are taught. Those who decline are not harmed — they are made unfindable and unemployable in anything that matters: records tidied, teachers bought, doors quietly closed, a good job in another district that comes with a relocation. The House considers this merciful and it is the House's oldest argument with itself.

**[OPEN]** (OQ-14) Whether there are places in Palisade where Tao runs dense is not answered here. The House's *Long Room* — the reading room where the practice is easier to hold and nobody says why — is written as a thing members believe, never as a fact on the map.

## Resources

- **Aldine House** — club, foundation, library, recital hall, garden, and the most respectable neutral ground on the Crest. Profiled as [[aldine-house]].
- **Control of Orison** — exercised from the executive floor, invisible in the district ([[orison-reach]], BC-18), and unknown below the top page of the org chart. Practically: allocation of Pattern Four, the disposition of [[product-integrity-team|Product Integrity]], and the private port.
- **The sealed shop and the forty-one** — the initiate technicians who finish Pattern Four. They are the Wuji's largest single concentration of practitioners and its greatest exposure.
- **Practitioners** — Casters: pure Tao users who augment the practice with technology (Bible §2). Few, unrecorded, and placed where a corporation would not think to look — a Chancery clerk, a Suture Row surgeon, an Orison contracts officer ([[adaeze-ferreira]]). Profiled as [[wuji-operative-challenge]].
- **Tao-worked items** — Pattern Four, and the older pieces the House keeps and does not sell: a lamp, a lock, a coat, none of them impressive, all of them impossible to explain. **[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** (CR-1) items into which a practice has been worked, not relics of any legend.
- **Tao-made beings** — Tao can produce beings (Brief §3.1), and the Wuji can make one. It does so rarely and briefly, because a made thing is a witness that cannot be settled with and a thing that persists is a thing somebody can find. Where one is used, it is used once and unmade.
- **An unwritten curriculum** — the whole of the society's knowledge, distributed across perhaps two hundred people. This is a resource and it is the reason the Wuji can be killed.
- **The Quiet Hand rumour** — free cover. A rumour that is laughed at is better protection than a denial, and the House knows it.
- **A century of favours on the Hill** — a minister on the board, and the kind of standing where nobody asks the House for its accounts.

**Faced, not held.** Canon: the other corporations keep "a few rebel Tao users and anti-Tao solutions of their own" (Bible §2). Both are aimed here. **Meliora's Adjunct Series** — doses and reagents that make attention impossible to hold and make a Tao-worked item behave ordinarily. **AP&I's Field Assurance** — tell-readers that log where a discipline is being kept, and Noise flooders that drown a room until nobody in it can hold a condition (Core p. 22 on the Noise). And the **rebel practitioners**: a handful of people, in each of the other two corporations, who learned some part of a practice outside the House and were not brought in, or were brought in and left. The Wuji does not hunt them. It watches them, employs their debts, and waits for them to need something. **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-117) The two programmes and their names are invented; the countermeasure Challenge itself is `08-challenges/custom/`.

## Agenda

**Keep the practice out of the world's hands while selling its output to stay solvent, and decide — this year — which of those two the Wuji is actually for.**

Concretely, this Series: **ration Pattern Four** and take the political cost of refusing corporations that are arming; **hold the forty-one**, whose shop is now working double shifts and whose faces are the only faces the shop door knows; **find and quietly close** whatever route is letting Orison items reach the Weighhouse with the fourth digit still on the plate; **finish the recruitment** of anyone in Palisade doing something they should not be able to do, before Meliora's Adjunct people find them first; and **do nothing at all** about Continuity, the Chancery, or the war, because the Wuji's entire theory of survival is that a war fought over corporations is a war that is not looking for the practice.

The House will not seize anything. It has never wanted the city. It wants the city to keep not noticing.

## What the upstart's destabilization means here

The Bible: the crack creates opportunity for every faction, including the secret ones (§5). For the Wuji the crack is mostly *danger* — and that is the opportunity.

A war is attention. Attention means teardown budgets, field trials, tell-readers on barges, and buyers who test what they bought. It means Pattern Four ordered faster than forty-one people can finish it, and it means the first buyer in a century with a real reason to find out why Orison's line is different: [[upstart|Continuity]], which sells crisis management and has just discovered that Orison's product does not fail in a crisis.

But a war is also cover. Every corporation in Palisade now has something more urgent to investigate than a members' club, and every strange thing that happens on the street this year will be blamed on the corporate war, the way it used to be blamed on Baselines ([[secret-war-goes-public]]). If the Wuji is ever going to move an asset it left in the open, close a route, or bring somebody in against their will, this is the year when nobody will look twice.

The House's problem is that both of those are true at once, and it has never had a plan for a year in which they were.

## Hooks

- The crew is offered a scholarship at Aldine House — a real one, with money, after doing something they should not have been able to do. There is no interview. There is a morning practice, and after a month, a favour.
- Somebody in the Gullet is selling a lamp that does not need power. The House wants it bought back before [[marisol-okonkwo]] finds a corporate buyer, and will not say why a lamp matters.
- A Meliora field team is dosing a Suture Row surgeon with something that has no name on the vial, to see what stops working. The surgeon has treated a PC.
- One of the forty-one has not come back from a shift and her flat has been cleared out by people who folded the laundry first.

# Nasrin Vogel

![[assets/npcs/nasrin-vogel.png]]

**Handle:** — · **Affiliation:** [[tao-society|the Wuji]] · **Role in the pilot:** the recruiter. The first Wuji contact a crew is likely to have, and the friendliest.

## Who they are

One of the instructors who takes the morning practice in [[chancery-hill|Aldine House]]'s garden — one of several, which is why the district says the instructor "never seems to be the same person twice" ([[chancery-hill]]). Thirties or forties, hard to place, dressed like a physiotherapist, warm in a way that is not performed. She is genuinely good company. That is the tool.

Vogel's work is downhill of the House and mostly happens under the Wall. She spends her weeks in [[suture-row]] clinics, [[gullet-market]] stalls, the [[relay-fields]] coffin-blocks and the [[halloran-circus]] undercards, looking for the thing that does not add up: the fighter whose opponent's weapon jammed three times, the surgeon whose infection rate is impossible, the kid on the Stretch whose landlord's door will not open for the bailiff. She is patient, unhurried, and does not test people; she watches, twice, and then buys them a meal.

The offer is real. A scholarship at the House, or a job, or a debt cleared — money the crew's whole district does not have, with no interview, no ceremony, and no explanation beyond "come in the mornings." What she does not say at the table is that the same budget exists for a refusal, and that a refusal is answered by making the person unfindable: records tidied, a very good opportunity in another district, teachers and contacts quietly bought. She has done it eleven times. She has never described it as a threat, because to her it is not one — it is the other half of the same care.

**[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** (CR-1) Vogel is a Caster (Bible §2 mage). Her practice is a discipline of attention, augmented by an unremarkable lens; she notices what is out of place because she has trained to and because she has worked with people who can do things. Tao does not point her at anyone.

## Vector

- **Want:** Every person in Palisade who can do something they should not be able to do, either inside Aldine House or out of anybody else's reach — Meliora's Adjunct people most of all.
- **Push:** Attention, money, and patience. A meal, a scholarship, a job, a cleared debt, a door held open for a year. If declined: the same resources spent closing every other door, gently, until the person leaves the district on their own.

## What the crew knows / does not know

**Knows:** a friendly woman from a Crest charity has been around twice, knows their names, and made an offer that is too good and not conditional on anything. **Does not know:** what the House is; that the offer was decided by [[constance-marchetti]]; that declining is not free. A PC who is a Caster may notice she keeps a discipline; a PC who is not will notice only that she is easy to talk to.

## Challenge

None by default. Vogel's Challenge is the House itself — [[aldine-house]], role temptation. If a scene turns on her, run [[wuji-operative-challenge]] with the fighting Limits lowered and *convince* at 5, or the Tokyo Skywatcher profile (Tokyo p. 208) re-flavoured as a House member, **[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** with every reference to a goddess, a cult, or worship removed — the House believes in nothing and recruits anyway.

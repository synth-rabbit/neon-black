# Constance Marchetti

![[assets/npcs/constance-marchetti.png]]

**Handle:** — · **Affiliation:** [[tao-society|the Wuji]], [[corp-b|Orison Defense Systems]] · **Role in the pilot:** the decision the offer came from.

## Who they are

Steward of [[chancery-hill|Aldine House]] — a club title, printed on the House's letterhead beneath the names of the board, which is exactly what she wants it to look like. Seventies, small, plainly dressed, gloved indoors, with a voice that does not rise. She runs the House's calendar, its scholarships, its recital calendar, and its accounts, and she has done so for thirty-one years, during which the House has never once been late paying a supplier or early answering a question.

Marchetti is the senior practitioner in Palisade and treats that as an administrative fact rather than a distinction. Her practice is the House's oldest and least impressive: a discipline of stillness kept every morning in the garden, whatever the weather, in front of forty other people. She teaches nobody personally any more. She decides what is taught.

What she actually does is arithmetic. Every year there are more people in Palisade who might be talented, more corporate money looking for whatever Orison has, and the same two hundred practitioners. She spends the House's century of favours on closing routes, buying silences, and moving the four or five people a year who cannot be left where they are. She authorized the Pattern Four expansion forty years ago because containment needed funding, and she has watched that decision turn into the largest leak the Wuji has ever run. She does not defend it. She has not found anything better.

She refuses on principle to be interesting. No aura, no presence, no chill in the air — the opposite: a woman on a committee. That is the whole design.

**[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** (CR-1) Marchetti is a Caster (Bible §2 mage): pure Tao user, tech-augmented, with a Ritual that is a discipline and a condition and never a legend's demand ([[style-guide]] §3). Tao does not choose her, favour her, or ask anything of her.

## Vector

- **Want:** Nothing new in the world — no page written, no practice copied, no Tao-worked item sold to a buyer who will take it apart, no talented person left where a corporation can find them.
- **Push:** In order: money and favours on [[chancery-hill]]; Orison's allocation of Pattern Four, which is a lever on every corporation that wants it; [[product-integrity-team|Product Integrity]], which does not know whose instrument it is; and last, when the first three have failed, [[wuji-operative-challenge|a practitioner]] — which she regards as an admission of failure and uses anyway.

## What the crew knows / does not know

**Knows:** Aldine House is respectable, expensive, and easy to get into if somebody sponsors you; the old woman who runs it is on every board on the Hill. **Does not know:** that the House is the Wuji's front; that she is the reason a friend of theirs got a very good job in another district and stopped answering; that a refusal to her is not answered with violence but with a closing door, one door at a time.

## Challenge

None by default. She does not fight and will not be in a scene where fighting is likely — the House's answer arrives as [[wuji-operative-challenge]], or as [[product-integrity-team]], or as a letter. If a job corners her, run her as [[wuji-operative-challenge]] with *hurt or subdue* at 3, *convince* at 6, and the **Connected & Protected** Power Set (Core p. 328).

# The Wuji — Membership

The Tokyo pattern (Tokyo p. 158, 209): what a PC of the Wuji looks like on the sheet, and what the House expects.

## Who gets in

One way, and you do not apply for it: **you are noticed**.

Canon: "a secret society identifies talented people and brings them into the fold" (Bible §2). In Palisade that means [[nasrin-vogel]], or one of the four or five people doing her job, watching somebody twice in [[suture-row]], [[gullet-market]], [[relay-fields]], or the undercards at [[halloran-circus]], and then buying them a meal. What gets you noticed is doing something that should not have worked — and a Caster PC who has been operating in public for a few months *has already been noticed*, whether or not the House has moved yet.

What follows is a scholarship letter to [[chancery-hill|Aldine House]] with no interview attached, a place at the morning practice in the garden, and, for months, nothing asked in return. Membership is not announced, sworn, or marked. There is no initiation, no ceremony, no tattoo, and nothing to find on a body. The whole of what a member knows is held in the member, because the Wuji writes nothing down — a curriculum that exists as two hundred people and no files (BC-120).

Full membership arrives in stages nobody names out loud: attending, being taught, being asked for a favour, being told something. Some members practise their whole lives and are never told what Orison is.

**Declining.** The offer is genuine and so is the alternative. A refusal is answered by making the person unfindable — records tidied, contacts bought, a very good opportunity in another district ([[aldine-house]], *A Refusal Is Not a No*). The House considers this care. Anyone who has been closed out knows only that their life quietly stopped having doors in it.

## What the House expects

- **Nothing is written down.** No notes, no recordings, no diagrams, no explanations to a friend. A member who writes has done the one unforgivable thing.
- **The practice is not sold.** Not to a corporation, not to a fixer, not for a life. The House sells items; a member does not sell the discipline that makes them.
- **Nobody is impressive in public.** Doing the impossible where anyone can see it is how a practice ends up in a Meliora lab report.
- **Talented strangers are reported, not recruited.** A member who finds someone brings the name to the House.
- **Favours are answered.** Months of nothing, and then one thing, and it is not negotiable.

## On the sheet

**Self kit:** [[wuji-initiate]] (Affiliation) — the society-membership kit. Its Identity is a conviction about *keeping*, not about power: what you know does not leave you.

**Mythos side.** A PC of the Wuji is a Caster: primary Mythos, with Noise as the optional secondary — Tao augmented by technology (Brief §2.1, §2.5). Essence minimum: at least one Mythos theme (BC-1). The Esoterica themebook (Core p. 228–231) is the home of the practice; WP2 writes the custom Tao-discipline kits (`02-splats/mage/theme-kits/`), and the **Ritual** is a discipline, a price, or a condition — never a legend re-enacted (Brief §3.2; [[style-guide]] §3). **[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** (CR-1) Every Esoterica question that assumes a Mythos, a patron, or a legend is answered about a practice and a condition instead.

**Specials.** Wuji affiliation pairs with WP2's mage PC Specials (`02-splats/mage/pc-specials/`) — the society-oath Special the Plan calls for is this membership, written as a condition the PC keeps.

**What affiliation costs.** A member has an unpaid favour outstanding, always. And a member is *findable by the House* forever: the one organization in Palisade that will never lose track of them.

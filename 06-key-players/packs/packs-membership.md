# The Run — Membership

What a character in [[packs|the Run]] looks like, in the Tokyo membership pattern (Tokyo p. 191, 228).

## Getting in

Two things have to happen and they happen in this order.

**Somebody has to pay for your body.** The sculpt is the expensive part: months of work at a chop-shop on [[suture-row]] that will do it without records, load-bearing anchors from a Row parts bin, and four months of healing during which you cannot work. Almost nobody under the Wall can pay for that, so almost nobody does — the [[syndicate|Almoners]] extend the credit, a pack vouches, and the line goes in the Kitchen's ledger before the first incision ([[uncle]]). It is irreversible. Body sculpting and bio-manipulation cannot be undone (Bible §1); the chrome could come out, but a body with the sculpt and without the frame does not survive the settle.

**Then a pack has to take you.** Paying for the body makes you a debtor; running with people makes you a pack member. That takes months of being useful and a night where you do not run home, and it ends without ceremony — you are simply in the hall, and somebody counts you when the crates come off the van.

There is no initiation, no colors, no mark. The Run's members do not use a word for what they are. Outsiders who need one say *gang*.

## What the Run expects

- **You take the ground seriously.** Turf is not sentiment: the Yards are why nobody looks at them, and a member who brings attention to the flats has cost everyone the only thing that keeps them alive ([[kiln-row]]).
- **You settle where somebody can see you.** Nobody doses alone. A body that comes apart with no one there is a body found in the morning by a corporate cleanup crew ([[sterile-field-unit]]).
- **You take what the alpha says.** Not out of love — out of arithmetic. One counter sells the dose, one voice talks to that counter, and a pack that goes around the alpha finds out what a bad month costs ([[chimney]]).
- **You do not work for the Wall.** Chimney's rule: a pack that takes corporate money stops being a pack. It is currently being broken by the youngest pack, quietly ([[jarek-kovac]]).
- **Nobody says what we are.** Not a policy; a habit of not saying things. It is most of why the Masquerade holds down here (Bible §3).

## What the Run gives

The body. That is the whole offer and it is not a small one: a body that works, that carries, that nobody in a stairwell will try anything with, in a city that sorts people by what has been done to their flesh (Bible §3; [[palisade|Palisade]]). Beyond that: a hall, people who feed you while you heal, a share of the reclamation concern's protection money, and a place on the flats at night where for a few hours you are not at the bottom of anything.

And the dose, every week, at whatever the price is — which is the same sentence as *and the leash, every week*.

## On the character sheet

- **Self kit (Affiliation):** [[pack-affiliation]] — the pack as an affiliation, built like Street Gang (Affiliation, Core p. 199): the hall, the ground, the people who count you. Its weakness side carries the tab and the alpha's word, **not** the dose.
- **The dose and the sculpt are not this kit.** The trigger dependency and the sculpted body are Noise themes and belong to WP2's werewolf package (`02-splats/werewolf/`), with the condition persisting as narrative fact plus a Megacity-style PC Special (Brief §2.4, BC-3). A character can be in the Run's orbit without them; a character cannot be a Howler with only this Affiliation kit.
- **Essence:** the werewolf minimum is ≥1 Noise theme (Brief §2.2, BC-1). This Affiliation kit is Self and does not satisfy it.
- **Existing kits worth offering alongside** (Brief §0.1): **Street Gang** (Affiliation, Core p. 199); **Animalistic Modifications** and **Reflex Booster Implants** (Augmentation, Core p. 238–239) for the sculpt and the dependency, per Brief §2.5; **Troubled Past** for a character who was in the Blocks before the offer.
- **Crew note:** a pack tie is a standing obligation to two Key Players at once — the alpha and the counter — and the second one is the [[syndicate|Almoners']] ledger, which is the same hook as [[syndicate-membership]] approached from underneath.

## Leaving

You do not. The sculpt is permanent, the frame is permanent, and the dose comes from one counter in Palisade. A member can leave a *pack* — go quiet, go to another hall, go to the Blocks and keep their head down — and people do. What nobody has done is leave the Run, because leaving the Run means a week without settling, and then two, and then a body that is trying to finish something it cannot finish.

The exception nobody has tested: a member who found a second source. That is what the alpha is looking for ([[teodora-sowande]]), and **[OPEN]** (OQ-45) it is not decided here whether one exists.

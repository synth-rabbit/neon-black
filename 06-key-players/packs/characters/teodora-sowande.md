# Teodora Sowande

![[assets/npcs/teodora-sowande.png]]

**Handle:** "Chimney" · **Affiliation:** [[packs|the Run]] · **Role in the pilot:** the alpha. WP1 left this person unnamed ([[cinder-yards]] development 2); she is named here.

## Who they are

Forty-one. Alpha of the **Smokes** for eleven years, which is a long time in the Yards, and the Run's arbiter across the Foot: when two packs cannot agree, they come to the kiln-hall with the smoking chimney and she tells them how it will be, and it is. She takes the delivery at the siding, sets what each pack pays, and hands the [[syndicate|Almoners']] envelope back through the cab window without looking at the driver.

She was a dock hand in [[gullet-market]] before the sculpt, with a spinal injury and a settlement that ran out, and she made the calculation everyone in the Yards has made: an irreversible body that works, on credit, versus a body that does not, forever. She does not regret it in any simple way and does not permit the subject to be discussed as regret.

She is heavy, grey at the temples, slow-moving in daylight, and quiet in a way that people mistake for calm. The chrome is old and visible — anchors along the shoulders and hips, a jaw line that does not match — and she has never had it prettied up. In the shape, she is the largest thing in the Yards and the least eager to use it. Her authority does not come from that; it comes from eleven years of being right about the price.

**What she has noticed.** She has taken every delivery for eleven years and she has counted crates for all of them, and the dose has never once varied. Not in a flood, not in the two supply scares, not in the winter three packs went short and nobody died of a bad batch because there has never *been* a bad batch. She has watched every other drug under the Wall behave like a drug — strong weeks, cut weeks, funerals — and Leash behave like a thing made in a room with a machine in it. She does not have the word *Meliora* and does not have the word *Accord*; what she has is the observation, and she has started, very carefully and through people who do not live in the Yards, to ask who could make something that consistent.

She has not told the other alphas. She has not told her own pack. She has told two people, and one of them is somebody's cousin who works nights on [[suture-row]].

Her end is not liberation. It is a supplier she can negotiate with instead of a counter she can only pay. She has thought about what happens if there is no such supplier — if the answer is a corporation that would rather close the study than take her call — and she has decided that knowing is still better, and that is the most dangerous decision anyone in the Run has made in fifteen years.

## Vector

- **Want:** The maker's name, and a line to them that does not go through the Kitchen.
- **Push:** She pays in the Yards' currencies — passage across the flats, a night's violence somewhere the crew needs one, a body nobody will find, a roof, and a debt honored. Her method with outsiders is disarming honesty: she says exactly what she wants and exactly why, and lets the crew work out that she has told them something that could get her killed.

## What the crew knows / does not know

A crew that works under the Wall will hear *Chimney* long before they meet her: the name is what people say instead of *the Run*. They will know the Yards are hers, the flats are dangerous after dark, and that the Almoners deal with her and not with the packs.

She knows what she is (Bible §2) and assumes a crew does not, and she will not explain it — the Masquerade is not a policy in the Yards, it is a habit of not saying things. She does not know Meliora exists in her life; she does not know the word Accord; she does not know that the Almoners' supply is legal, or that her packs are a study, or that a corporate program has fifteen years of photographs of her people. **[OPEN]** (OQ-45) What she does when she learns any of it.

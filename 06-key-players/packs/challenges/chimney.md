# Chimney

**Role:** attacker · **Scale:** 1 · **Alias:** *the one they all listen to* · *A negotiation before she is a fight.*

The person is [[teodora-sowande]]. This profile is for the kiln-hall with the smoking chimney on the Kiln Row ([[kiln-row]]), and for the two or three scenes in a Series where the crew has to deal with the Run rather than run from it.

She is filed as an **attacker** because she will be one if the scene goes that way, and because a Challenge whose role is *the conversation* is [[first-alms]] and this is not that. But the useful thing about her is *convince* at tier 4 and *The Question* below: Chimney is the one Key Player figure under the Wall who is actively looking for something a crew can get her.

## Specials

**She Talks First:** Her first Consequence in any scene must be social, including scenes that open with weapons drawn. She says out loud when the talking is over.

**The Row Answers:** In [[cinder-yards]], her pack takes statuses for her (Present a New Challenge: [[pack-on-the-run]], Scale 1). Anywhere else she is alone, and knows it.

**Arbiter:** *Turn the Run against her* takes tiers only from demonstrated facts — a delivery she kept, a deal with the Wall, a night she spent another pack. Lies do nothing. The Nineteens' Continuity money is one such fact, and it currently cuts against them, not her ([[uncle]]).

**The Question:** Show her anything real about where the dose is made — a case number, a corporate label, an invoice, a clinic name — and the scene stops. She trades for it without haggling: passage, violence, a body nobody finds, a debt honored, the Run's protection in the Yards for as long as she holds them.

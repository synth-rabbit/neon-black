# Running Shape

**Applies to:** any Challenge representing a Howler within a few hours of a dose · **Category:** Noise · **Splat:** werewolf

The overlay for a body that has settled without a template. Meliora's compound opens a remodeling window; in a body built out of cheap sculpt-stock and second-hand anchors there is no drawing for the window to converge on, so the tissue goes to the nearest shape the stock and the frame can carry and holds it until the compound clears ([[corp-a]]). What that looks like from outside is the classic thing: bigger, wrong-jointed, fast, and covered in a hide the sculpt-stock came with. What it is from inside is a person whose body has finally, for a few hours, finished.

Add it to [[pack-on-the-run]], [[chimney]], [[uncle]], or to any Challenge from [[packs-reuse]] that a scene needs to be a Howler.

**Two Howler overlays (WP6, BC-133).** The vault keeps two: [[running-on-leash]] (WP2) is the splat package's general overlay for any Howler on a dose — a Gang Member, a Leg-Breaker, a named body anywhere in Palisade — and is the one [[leash-frenzy-pack]] layers. This file is the **Run's own** overlay, built on Meliora's settling mechanism (BC-108) so the *settled* clock is something the pack reads and the crew cannot; the packs' Challenges use it. Never put both on one Challenge. Under either, a dosed Howler stays a person who can come down and be talked to; neither is a frenzy (OQ-49 records the wording difference for the GM).

## Specials

**The Window:** *Settled* is polar against *unsettling*. A dose gives *settled-3*, dropping one tier per scene, two in sustained violence. At *settled-0* the shape comes apart: *coming-apart-3*, the overlay's tags are lost, and the Challenge becomes a person on the floor. It knows the count better than the crew and will spend the last tier rather than carry it home.

**Built To Carry, Not To Take:** Ignores the first tier of impact, fall, and blunt force. Takes one extra tier from anything that cuts, burns, or goes through — and from electricity, which finds the frame.

**Not A Beast:** The sculpt did not take their mind. It speaks, aims, obeys an alpha, breaks off, and negotiates. Any Challenge with this overlay keeps a *convince* or *intimidate* Limit beside *hurt or subdue*. This overlay creates no frenzy and no inability to stop.

**Cleanup Comes At First Light:** Witnesses, a body, or a feed brings a corporate cleanup element before dawn (Present a New Challenge: [[sterile-field-unit]]) and the public account is a Baseline with a gun. Making that account impossible is what advances [[secret-war-goes-public]] — the violence alone does not.

## Canon and flags

- Splat canon (Plan A.6; Bible §2): a Howler is bio-sculpt plus cybernetics plus a chemical trigger, dependent on the [[syndicate|Almoners']] supply, and irreversible. Rarity is preserved — this overlay is never applied to a generic Gang Member without a named reason. The Masquerade holds: everything here is deniable by morning (Bible §3).
- **[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** Built beside Shapechanger (Core p. 331) with the Mythos removed, and Chromed Up (Core p. 332); the *settled* clock is modeled on Oni Rider / Greater Oni's escalating overlays (Tokyo p. 189–190). **No Tao anywhere**, and no legend of any kind.
- **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-108) the settle mechanism; (BC-114) the *settled* polar status, the impact-versus-cutting asymmetry, and *Not A Beast*.
- **[OPEN]** (OQ-5) whether Howlers can use Tao — not touched; **[OPEN]** (OQ-46) resolved by WP6 — both overlays kept, scoped (BC-133); (OQ-49) how much of a person a dosed Howler remains, for the GM.

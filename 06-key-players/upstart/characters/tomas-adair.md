# Tomas Adair

![[assets/npcs/tomas-adair.png]]

**Handle:** "Rook" · **Affiliation:** [[upstart|Continuity Risk & Response]] — as the person its strike was for; nothing further is stated · **Role in the pilot:** the crew's leader inside the Ledger, and the object of the strike.

**How to read this file.** Everything in *Canon* below is stated by the Bible (§2, §5, §6). Everything in *Texture* is surface only — how he looks, how he sounds, what a work crew saw — and asserts nothing about why he was in the camp, what he was doing before it, what he intends, or who he answers to. Everything else about him is in *Open*, listed and not answered. No later file may fill an Open item without the GM (Brief §12; [[open-questions]] OQ-10).

## Canon — what the Bible states

- He is the **crew leader**: the PCs' leader on the same work crew inside [[coldwater-outfall|Reconciliation Facility 4]], the Ledger (Bible §5, §6).
- He is a **Bloodware spy** (Bible §5: "a vampire spy").
- He was **caught leaving one of the corporations' research facilities** and **played it off as a random street thug paid by a gang to grab tech** (Bible §5). Which corporation's facility, and what he was after, is **[OPEN]** (OQ-10).
- Inside the camp he **helped acclimate the PCs and protected them** (Bible §5).
- **The party knows this person — but does not know why they matter or the full picture** (Bible §5).
- [[upstart|Continuity]]'s strike on the camp was to **extract him specifically**; the general breakout was **cover for that person's importance**; the PCs escaped in the breakout (Bible §5).
- **After the escape, he directs the PCs toward a fence** who can help them get set up on the outside — [[marisol-okonkwo|Marisol Okonkwo, "Tally"]] at [[fence-network|the Weighhouse]] in [[gullet-market]] (Bible §5, §6). That is the crew's one day-one contact (Bible §6).

Bloodware canon applies to him as to any Bloodware (Bible §2, Plan A.6): iron or blood, because the nanites replicate with iron; the classic package — strength, longevity, regeneration; a progressively nanite-rebuilt body; and the two weaknesses, **electromagnetic** effects and the **master node**, the Kernel — and no others; the folklore weaknesses do not exist in Palisade.

## Texture — surface only

Forties by the look of him and has looked it for a while. Heavy through the shoulders, hands like a man who has unloaded things for a living, a broken nose set by somebody who was not a surgeon. Camp coveralls with a number on the back and the sleeves pushed up. He speaks like the Foot — flat vowels, short sentences — and the story he tells about himself, when he tells it at all, is the one he told the arresting officers: a thug, a gang, a job that went wrong, bad luck. He tells it the same way every time.

On the line he is the one who knows which guard counts twice, which shed door sticks, how to make a quota look met, and how long a new arrival can go without eating before it starts to show. He gave the crew that. He did not explain why.

*Bloodware do not read as anything in a camp:* pale is normal at the Ledger, thin is normal, and a man who does not eat his ration is a man trading it.

**Does not know:** why he matters. What he is. Who came for him or why. Whether the strike was for him at all — from inside a general breakout, it looked like a breakout (Bible §5, §6).

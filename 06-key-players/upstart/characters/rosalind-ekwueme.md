# Rosalind Ekwueme

![[assets/npcs/rosalind-ekwueme.png]]

**Handle:** — · **Affiliation:** [[upstart|Continuity Risk & Response]] · **Role in the pilot:** none; she is the vector face for the contracts that follow.

## Who they are

Vice President, Client Continuity. She works out of the eleventh floor of the plaza tower in [[the-lattice]], on a floor that does not appear on the building's public Domain, and she comes down to the lobby herself when the client is worth it. Fifties in the face, in the way of somebody who chose that. Grey wool, no jewellery, reading glasses she does not need and uses as punctuation. She keeps a paper diary. People find it charming.

Her job is the sentence after the emergency. A cell secures a site; within the hour Ekwueme is at the client's elbow with the continuity-of-service papers and a tone of enormous sympathy, and she is not selling — she is *helping*, and the help happens to have a schedule of monitors attached to it. She has never raised her voice at a negotiation and has never once been the one who blinked. The firm's growth over the last decade is on her signature more than anyone's ([[timeline]]).

She is unfailingly kind to people who cannot help her, which is the tell, if anyone were counting.

## Vector

- **Want:** a signature and the access it carries. Not the job — the retainer.
- **Push:** cash up front, indemnity in writing, and the unspoken observation that Continuity is already inside whatever the client is worried about, so the only question is which side of the contract they would rather be on.

## What the crew knows / does not know

**Knows:** nothing, at first. Later: a very polite executive who returns calls, pays on time, and knows things about the crew that were never in the brief.

**Does not know:** what she is, or that the firm she runs the client relationships for is run by things like her.

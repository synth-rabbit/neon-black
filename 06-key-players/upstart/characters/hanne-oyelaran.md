# Hanne Oyelaran

![[assets/npcs/hanne-oyelaran.png]]

**Handle:** "Amber" · **Affiliation:** [[upstart|Continuity Risk & Response]] · **Role in the pilot:** she runs the Ledger strike, and the crew's escape is a line item in her plan.

## Who they are

Crisis-response lead, Cell Eleven. Late thirties, tall, close-cropped grey-blonde hair, a face built for reassuring people at cordons. Matte soft-shell armor with a serial number on the chest plate and no name anywhere on her; a helmet she takes off the moment a scene is secured, because people talk to a face. Nine years with the firm — two of them on the Lattice ward — and a promotion history that reads as competence rather than favor.

She is a **Baseline** and unremarkable about it (Bible §2): trauma-grade replacement work only — a left eye lost to a flashback in her second year, replaced, and a knee — no sculpt, no bio-work, and a flat professional contempt for people who take chrome to be frightening rather than to work. Her advantages are a breaching frame, a cell that has trained together for four years, and the fact that in Palisade her authority at a cordon is exactly as legal as a police officer's (Bible §3).

Her voice is the thing. She talks to civilians the way a paramedic does — *stay where you are, we've got this, look at me* — and it works on frightened people at a rate that unsettles her own team. Continuity's client managers show recordings of her at cordons to prospects. She has never asked why.

She calls the work *contracts*, never jobs, and she reads them the way a surgeon reads a chart: what is to be removed, from where, in what condition. The Ledger contract named one person, gave a window, and specified *undamaged*. It did not say why, and she did not ask, because asking is how contractors become witnesses.

## Vector

- **Want:** complete the contract as written — the named person off site, undamaged, cell intact, no incident to bill.
- **Push:** Cell Eleven, a breaching frame, and the emergency. She will drop the camp's power, cut its Nearspace, open the block doors, and let the breakout happen around her, because a hundred people running for the causeway is the cheapest cover Palisade sells and it costs the client nothing.

## What the crew knows / does not know

**Knows:** a woman in grey armor with a serial where a name should be, who came through the wire with people who moved like one animal, who took Rook out at walking pace with a hand under his elbow, and who looked directly at the crew and did not raise her weapon.

**Does not know:** her name, her employer, or that the entire strike was for the man she had by the elbow. Nothing about the crew was in her contract. She let them run because letting them run was the plan.

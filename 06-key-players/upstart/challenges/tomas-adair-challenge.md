# Tomas Adair, "Rook"

**Role:** asset · **Scale:** 0 · **Alias:** *the work-crew leader* · *Built only from what the Bible states.*

The profile exists because he is a named character who can fight (Brief §8) and because in the breakout he is on the floor with the crew while a strike goes off around them. Everything about him that is not mechanical is in [[tomas-adair]]; that file's rule applies here too — **nothing about his motives beyond canon**.

His role is **asset**, not attacker: in the pilot he is the thing being taken, and for the crew he is the reason they are still standing. He is Bloodware (Bible §5: a vampire spy), and Bloodware canon governs him without exception — iron or blood; the classic package; a progressively nanite-rebuilt body; and two weaknesses only, **electromagnetic** effects and the **master node**, the Kernel (Bible §2).

## Limits

| Limit | Tier |
|---|---|
| hurt or subdue | 5 |
| convince | 4 |
| expose | – |
| destroy the Kernel | 6 |

**hurt or subdue** at 5 is the correct number for something that heals; it does not end him, it stops him. **destroy the Kernel** at 6 is the hardest tier the book allows (Core p. 298) and is the only way he dies.

**expose** is immune. See *Nothing Beyond This*.

## Tags & statuses

passes for a street thug, dockworker hands, knows which guard counts twice, never once explained himself · *underestimated-3*

## Specials

**Rebuilt From The Iron:** physical statuses come off at the end of the scene unless inflicted with an EM tag or status, or aimed at the master node.

**The Kernel:** *destroy the Kernel* is available only after the node is located — a Tracked action needing a node-scanner ([[kernel-scanner-rig]]) or an EM effect that makes him flinch toward it.

**He Protected Them:** no Consequence to a PC from his work crew; he takes one meant for them if he can reach it (Bible §5).

**Nothing Beyond This:** no element of this profile reveals why he matters. **[OPEN]** (OQ-10, OQ-17, OQ-39, OQ-40).

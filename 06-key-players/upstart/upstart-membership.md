# Continuity — Membership

What a PC on [[upstart|Continuity Risk & Response]]'s books looks like. The Tokyo pattern (Tokyo p. 209, 166–167): how you get in, what it gives you, what it costs, and which kit carries it.

## Getting in

Two doors, and the firm prefers the second.

**The front door.** Continuity recruits crisis-response staff the way any security firm does: prior corporate security, camp or facility guard work, ex-Chancery police, merc contracts with references. Two rounds of assessment, a medical, a background pass, and a probationary year on cordon posts where nothing happens. It pays well and on time, which under the Wall is remarkable enough on its own.

**The back door.** A recruiter in the ground floor of a coffin-block in [[relay-fields]] makes an offer to whichever kid just cracked something they should not have. The offer is a Harnessing suite better than anything in the district, a salary, and no questions about the cracking. Refusing it is also an option, and the recruiter's tone changes very slightly, and a file exists either way.

Neither door asks who your people are. Continuity does not care about your band, your papers, or your record — it cares whether you can hold a line or a Domain, and whether you can be billed for. A **Patched** body with a useful history gets hired here when it would not get past the lobby at [[corp-c|AP&I]].

## What the firm expects

- **You are on the clock or you are not there.** Everything a contractor does is either billable or an incident. Incidents come out of the cell's number.
- **You do not ask what the contract is for.** You are given a name, an address, a window, and a condition. [[hanne-oyelaran]] has run nine years on that basis and been promoted for it.
- **The scene is the firm's.** Once a cell declares a scene, everything in it belongs to Continuity until the emergency is over, and the emergency is over when the contract says.
- **You do not talk to the feeds, ever.** Cleanup is the product.
- **Your gear is issued and your gear is monitored.** So is your Nearspace. This is disclosed in the contract you signed.

## What it gives you

Standing that a Patched PC has no other route to: the legal authority of a police officer at a scene (Bible §3), a client list that reaches every corporation on the Crest, an issued soft-shell and a breaching frame, access to Harnessing hours at [[the-cold-suite]]'s rates, and the single most useful thing Continuity sells — being the one who is already inside when the thing goes wrong.

## Which kit

**Self kit:** [[continuity-contractor]] *(Affiliation)* — the corporate-citizenship pattern (Core p. 177, 196–219) written for a firm that issues serial numbers instead of names. Expected shape: power tags around cordon authority, cell coordination, issued kit, client-list access, and knowing which emergency is scheduled; weakness tags around monitoring, billability, the serial number that identifies you when your face does not, and the fact that the firm's contracts outlive the people who signed them.

**Existing kits that fit beside it** (Brief §0.1 — offer both):
- **Corporate Citizenship** (Affiliation, Core p. 199 range) — *monitored by the company* reads exactly right; the corporate logo tattoo becomes a serial.
- **Law Enforcement** (Affiliation) — for a contractor who came out of the Chancery's police and found the pay better on the other side of the same authority.
- **Trained Killer** or **Covert Agent** (Expertise, Core p. 207 range) — the cordon and the door.
- **Exoskeleton Suit** or **Cloaking Jumpsuit** (Cutting Edge, Core p. 242 range) — the issued kit as a Noise theme.
- **Cyberspace** kits for a Cold Suite operator; Harnessing runs as written (Brief §6.5).

**Specials.** Megacity-style PC Specials in the Tokyo p. 72–74 pattern, condition-gated, prerequisite required, surviving theme replacement (Brief §2.4). Candidates, for WP2/WP3 to finalize:
- **Declared Scene** — prerequisite: a Continuity contractor kit. Once per session, when you arrive first at an incident, you may declare the scene: for the rest of it your authority there is the police's, and anyone appealing to law confirms it.
- **The Serial, Not The Name** — prerequisite: issued soft-shell. Nobody at a cordon can identify you afterward without first getting the firm to release the roster.
- **Billable** — prerequisite: on the books. Once per downtime, a thing you needed was already paid for by a client. The client eventually finds out.

## Essence and the line

A Continuity contractor built toward **Real** or **Cyborg** is the intended shape for a **Baseline** PC (Brief §2.3): Self themes for the affiliation and the trade, Noise only for issued gear, and — per canon — **cybernetics only as replacements for lost body parts, never sculpting or bio-manipulation** (Bible §2). The firm issues nothing that crosses that line, because a cell lead who needs a sculpt is a cell lead who costs more than they earn.

Contractors from the other four are hired on the same terms and asked the same nothing.

## What a contractor does not learn

Who the client was. Why the contract named what it named. What is on the eleventh floor. Whether *the duration of the emergency* has an end date.

**MC only.** A PC who is Bloodware and works a cell is in no different a position from any other contractor: street-level and newly made Bloodware do not know what Continuity is (Bible §2, §5), and nothing in the working life of a cordon post would tell them. **[OPEN]** (OQ-17).

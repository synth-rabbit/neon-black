# The Chancery — Membership

The Tokyo pattern (Tokyo p. 158): what a PC affiliated with the city government looks like on the sheet, and what the Hill expects.

**No custom Self kit.** WP3 writes custom Affiliation kits for the corporations, the syndicate, the packs, the Wuji, and the Cutloose (Brief §4.4) — not for the Chancery, and this package does not add one. The government is the one institution the book already covers exactly: **Law Enforcement** (Affiliation, Self, Core p. 199) and **Corporate Citizenship** (Affiliation, Self, Core p. 198) are the Chancery on the sheet, and per Brief §0.1 the right move is to offer them as written rather than to substitute something.

## Who gets in

**The police.** Chancery officers are recruited from the Foot, trained cheaply, and paid badly. The badge is real, the authority is real, and it is *exactly* the authority a corporate security contractor has (Bible §3) — which every recruit works out in their first month, and which is the beginning of every arrangement they will ever make. Serving officers keep an envelope schedule the way other people keep a shift rota.

**The clerks.** The registry, the licensing bureaus, the courts, the Office of Concurrent Jurisdiction. Entry is by examination and by knowing somebody; promotion is by knowing somebody. A clerk's salary is nominal and a clerk's income is not.

**Everybody else.** A PC does not have to work for the Chancery to be *of* it: a permit-assistance forger behind a law firm, a fixer who sells registry looks, a councillor's driver. The Hill's economy is larger than its payroll.

**Leaving.** Nobody is stopped. But an ex-officer's badge number stays in the record, an ex-clerk knows things that are worth money to four corporations, and the Chancery's institutional memory is a basement full of paper with your handwriting in it.

## What the Chancery expects

- **Take the envelope.** An officer who does not is a problem for the officers who do, which is a shorter career than corruption.
- **File it the way it was bought.** The report is the product ([[chancery-process]]). Writing what happened instead of what was purchased is the one real offence.
- **Check the concurrence first.** Never be the lawful party by accident (see [[halima-boyce]]).
- **Do not audit anything.** Especially not the registry.
- **Be seen to be grateful** by anyone below you, and grateful to anyone above.

**Essence.** A Chancery PC is a Baseline in the vault's terms — gear, weapon systems, and replacement cybernetics only, no sculpt or bio-manipulation (Bible §2; Brief §2.3) — and should be built toward Real or Cyborg. Nothing about the badge implies otherwise.

**What affiliation costs.** An envelope schedule. A badge number in a basement. And the specific, ordinary knowledge that whatever you write down about tonight will be the truth forever, or will be the second copy.

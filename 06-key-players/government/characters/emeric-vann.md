# Emeric Vann

![[assets/npcs/emeric-vann.png]]

**Handle:** — · **Affiliation:** [[government|the Chancery]] · **Role in the pilot:** the crew's cheapest door into the record, and their most expensive one.

## Who they are

Deputy Registrar. Fifties, tidy, tired, a Fitted body a decade out of warranty, a good coat kept fifteen years. He works in the Chancery's basement among the paper shelves and takes his envelopes in the café at the lower gate like everybody else, because he has never in his life done anything that was not standard practice on the Hill ([[chancery-hill]]).

Vann sells looks. A look at a lease, a look at a licence, a look at an escapee list — five minutes at a shelf with a page held flat, no copies, no lens, nothing that leaves the room. He also sells the more expensive service, which is a name struck through with a single line and initialled. What he does not tell buyers is what the whole Hill has stopped saying out loud: there are two copies of most pages in the registry, they were separated years ago as a clerical convenience, and he can only reach one. A struck name is struck on *a* list. The other copies left the Hill years ago under a records-retention lease — a fact anyone can buy, and he sells it ([[investigation-09-what-the-book-cannot-say]]) — but he has never been inside the building, and the few second copies he has sold came to him up a supply line at a mark-up ([[ileana-boakye]], two removes below him). He has never followed the line to its end, because a man who goes looking for the second copy is a man who has decided to be a witness (WP8 reconciliation, BC-171).

He is not brave, not cruel, and not stupid. He is three years from a pension he does not believe will exist, sitting on top of the fact that Palisade's official record has not been true for a decade, and his entire strategy is to be somewhere else when it comes out. He likes the crew, if they are polite. He will still sell them.

## Vector

- **Want:** Out — retired, off the Hill, with enough put by, before anyone audits the registry and before he becomes the person who has to explain it.
- **Push:** Access. Every list, lease, licence, contract, and court date in Palisade, sold a page at a time and priced by how badly the buyer wants it. And the quiet alternative: a page he can sell to somebody else who is also asking about the crew.

## What the crew knows / does not know

**Knows:** the Chancery registry holds AP&I's escapee list with their names on it, no bounty attached (Bible §6), and there is a clerk who will show it to them ([[chancery-hill]]). **Does not know:** that a name struck off is struck off one of two copies; that Vann knows this and does not say; that [[adaeze-ferreira]] has an account with him and so does [[marisol-okonkwo]]. In the pilot he is a vector in both [[investigation]] and [[acquisition]].

## Challenge

None. Vann does not fight; his Limits are *bribe* and *frighten* and he is not worth a profile. If a scene needs him under pressure, use Shady Tech Merchant (Core p. 303) with *negotiate 2*, *threaten 3*, and the tags *not a snitch* replaced by *has seen what happens to witnesses*, or run him inside [[chancery-process]], which is where he actually lives.

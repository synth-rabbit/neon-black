# Marisol Okonkwo, "Tally"

**Role:** temptation · **Scale:** 0 (the Weighhouse behind her is Scale 2 — [[the-weighhouse]]) · **Alias:** *the woman behind the counter* · *Forty years of other people's property.*

The crew's one day-one contact (Bible §5, §6). See [[marisol-okonkwo]]. She is a **temptation** and not an attacker: she has never hurt anybody and does not need to, because the counter is the only door open and because in the Gullet everyone bleeds for her instead ([[weighhouse-muscle]]).

The correct way to lose to Tally is to get exactly what you asked for.

## Limits

| Limit | Tier |
|---|---|
| hurt or subdue | 2 |
| negotiate | 5 |
| learn what she knows | 4 |
| learn what she wants | 6 |

**hurt or subdue** at 2 is honest — she is sixty and unarmed — and is almost never reachable, because *Weights Takes It* and *Nobody Bleeds Inside* stand between it and anybody.

**learn what she wants** at 6 is the hardest tier the book allows (Core p. 298) and deliberately dead-ends: see *Not Yours To Read*.

## Tags & statuses

prices by the kilo when she's in a mood, forty years of other people's property, every runner on the Foot, feeds you while she weighs it · *unhurried-3*

## Specials

**Weights Takes It:** [[bohdan-adeyemi]] or any Weighhouse contact takes physical statuses for her; everyone who committed takes *barred-4*.

**A Price For Everything:** as a Consequence, the crew get exactly what they asked for and take *owed-2*.

**She Is Owed By People Who Matter:** once per job, somebody the crew are dealing with turns out to owe the house — Escalate in the crew's favor, *owed-1*.

**Not Yours To Read:** *learn what she wants* yields the existence of the Book and nothing about its buyers. **[OPEN]** (OQ-11, OQ-37).

## Threats / Consequences

› She wipes her hands on her apron and asks after somebody's family by name.
» She knows more than she should, comfortably (*at-ease-2*; she gains a page)
» One mild question, answered, entered (Escalate the Situation, later)

› She names a figure, writes it on the Slate, puts the chalk down.
» The price stands and the room knows it (*out-of-cash-3*, or the deal and *owed-2*)
» The back room, the surgeon and the papers as one package (remove up to three tiers of privation; *owed-3*)

› A runner comes in with a chit and she reads it without changing expression.
» There is work, it is theirs, it starts now (*there's work*; Present a New Challenge from the job's Key Player)
» She will not say who is paying, and the fee is too good (Escalate the Situation)

› She looks at the Slate a moment longer than she needs to.
» She calls in what is owed, and it is not money (*Owed* — see [[the-weighhouse]])
» She wipes a line off unpaid, which has never happened, and does not explain (Escalate the Situation)

## Power Sets

None. **Connected & Protected** (Core p. 328) is close and wrong: her friends are not in high places, they are on the quay, and *Do You Know Who I Am?* is not how the Gullet works.

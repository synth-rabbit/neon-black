# Marisol Okonkwo

![[assets/npcs/marisol-okonkwo.png]]

**Handle:** "Tally" · **Affiliation:** [[fence-network|Tally's Weighhouse]] · **Role in the pilot:** the crew's one day-one contact (Bible §6).

## Who they are

Sixty-odd, broad, slow-moving, in a canvas apron with brass-dust on it, behind the counter of the old customs weighhouse on the Gullet quay from the second hour of the morning until whenever the last coaster is unloaded. Grey hair cut short by herself. Hands that have handled forty years of other people's property and can tell chrome grade by weight and thumbnail. One cheap prosthetic eye that she has never bothered to upgrade, on the theory that a good one would make people nervous.

She is called Tally because she keeps count, and she keeps it in public: the **Slate** by the counter door, chalk, names, figures, and nobody rubs one out without paying. Under the Wall this is regarded as almost aggressively honest. In the Gullet a Patched body with a network outranks a Fitted one with a warranty ([[gullet-market]]), and Tally is the top of that order without ever having hurt anybody herself.

Her manner is kitchen-table. She feeds people. She asks after their mother. She weighs the thing they brought while she is doing it, names a price, and does not move off it, and the whole transaction is so domestic that people forget they have just told her where the thing came from — which is the part she wanted ([[fence-network|the Book]]).

She has two rules she says out loud. **Nobody bleeds inside the Weighhouse**, which the entire Foot enforces on her behalf because everyone needs the counter to exist. And **she does not take guns across her own balance**, which costs her money every month and which she has never explained.

She has one rule she does not say out loud, and it is the Book.

**On the crew:** Rook's referral reached her before they did. She has a figure next to their names on the Slate on the day they arrive, and she will not say what it is for, and it is not zero.

## Vector

- **Want:** the crew working, owing, and clearing everything they touch through her counter. Not loyalty — throughput.
- **Push:** everything they need on the day they need it. The back room, a meal, papers that will pass at a lift, a surgeon on [[suture-row]] who will not ask, and a first job — at a percentage that does not end, chalked on the Slate where the Gullet can see it.

## What the crew knows / does not know

**Knows:** a name and a place, from [[tomas-adair|Rook]], and that he expected them to use it (Bible §5). Within an hour of meeting her: that she is the only door open to them, that she is kind, and that kind is not the same as free.

**Does not know:** why Rook had her name. What the Book is. Why their names were already on the Slate.

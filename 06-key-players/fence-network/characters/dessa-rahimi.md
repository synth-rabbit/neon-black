# Dessa Rahimi

![[assets/npcs/dessa-rahimi.png]]

**Handle:** "Chit" · **Affiliation:** [[fence-network|Tally's Weighhouse]] · **Role in the pilot:** the crew's guide to the Gullet and their first ally who is not also their creditor.

## Who they are

Twenty-four, small, fast, permanently damp like everyone in [[gullet-market]], in a cut-down docker's jacket with the pockets resewn into a dozen smaller ones. She has been running for the Weighhouse since she was eleven. She is called **Chit** because she carries them: paper slips with a thumbprint pressed into wax, which is how Tally's network moves instructions, because paper cannot be Harnessed and a runner who is stopped can eat one.

She knows the Labyrinth the way people know their own hands — every cut-through under the stalls, which corrugated roof takes weight, which lock-keeper is on which shift, which pawnbroker will hold something for an hour without asking. She can cross the Gullet faster than anything on wheels and has never once been robbed, because being robbed would mean the entire market had decided to stop needing the Weighhouse.

She is also the reason the Book has a copy. Two years ago she talked Tally into letting a rig-builder in [[relay-fields]] mirror the last two years of the ledgers onto a rig, on the grounds that paper burns. The mirror is bad — incomplete, out of order, and about a third indexed — and it is the only version of the Book that exists anywhere but the back room shelves. Dessa has never read it. She thinks about it constantly.

She is warm, fast-talking, funny, and about eighteen months from doing something reckless, which is the shape she is built in.

**With the crew:** Tally sends her to walk them to the surgeon, the papers, the coaster, whatever the job needs. She likes them immediately, tells them what the job is actually worth as opposed to what was offered, and does not tell Tally that she told them.

## Vector

- **Want:** a counter of her own — or, when she is honest at three in the morning, to read the Book.
- **Push:** the network's legs and the network's gossip. She will spend routes, shifts, favors and the whole map of who-owes-whom on people she has decided are hers, and she decides fast.

## What the crew knows / does not know

**Knows:** within a week, that Dessa is the fastest way to get anything done in the Gullet and the only person in the network who will tell them the truth about a price.

**Does not know:** that Tally already knows about the mirrored copy and has decided to let it exist.

## Challenge

None custom. She does not fight. Where she needs a profile, use **Hacker** (Core p. 309) at Scale 0 for the mirror rig — a Relay Fields build, not a professional's kit — or the Weighhouse's own protection ([[weighhouse-muscle]]) if somebody puts hands on a runner in the Gullet, which is a thing the market does not permit.

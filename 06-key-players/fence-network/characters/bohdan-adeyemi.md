# Bohdan Adeyemi

![[assets/npcs/bohdan-adeyemi.png]]

**Handle:** "Weights" · **Affiliation:** [[fence-network|Tally's Weighhouse]] · **Role in the pilot:** he is in the water door when the crew come up the quay, and he decides whether they get to the counter.

## Who they are

Forty, enormous through the chest and shoulders in the specific way of a man who has unloaded coasters for twenty-five years rather than bought a body. A docker, not a soldier. He works the Weighhouse's **water door** — the loading door on the quay side — which means he handles every heavy thing that comes off a boat and everyone who comes with it.

Called **Weights** for the obvious reason and for a less obvious one: he is the one who sets the counterweights on the brass balance, which is a job Tally trusts to exactly one other person, which is nobody.

He is a **Baseline** and it matters to him (Bible §2): a replacement left hand from a crushed pallet nine years ago, a replacement hip, and nothing else — no sculpt, no bio-work, and a docker's flat contempt for people who buy strength they did not earn. He is also entirely aware that a Fitted body could take him, and has decided that the quay is worth more than any individual body on it. He has never lost a fight in the Gullet, because a fight in the Gullet is thirty dockers stopping work at once, and everyone knows it.

He warns people once. It is a genuine warning and it is not delivered aggressively: he tells you what will happen, in a normal voice, and then he waits, and the waiting is the thing that empties the doorway.

His son is fitted, on the Stack, and they do not talk about it.

# The Almoners — Membership

What a character associated with [[syndicate|the Almoners]] looks like, in the Tokyo membership pattern (Tokyo p. 191, 228).

## Getting in

You do not join the Almoners. You owe them, and then you are useful, and then one day somebody hands you an apron and nobody says anything about it.

The path is always the same and it always starts at a counter: a meal, a coat, a clinic visit, a loan for a rent shortfall or a funeral or a cheap sculpt. The line goes in the ledger. You work it off — carrying crates, driving a stop on [[the-round]], sitting with somebody's grandmother, standing where you are told to stand. If you are steady, the work gets more particular. If you are steady for years, you get told the next stop instead of guessing it.

There is no ceremony, no colors, no tattoo, and no name for what you are. Members call each other **almoners**, small a, and the word is doing a lot of quiet work: it means *one who distributes*, and it is what the organization genuinely believes about itself.

Nobody is ever told the whole shape. [[bettina-alarcon]] has run the Kitchen for nineteen years and has met the boss eleven times.

## What the Almoners expect

- **Manners.** The apron is the organization's entire public argument, and a volunteer who is seen being ugly in front of the district costs more than a lost crate. Escalation happens away from the counter or not at all.
- **Nothing written down that is not the ledger.** No lists, no schedules, no messages, no recordings. The Round is memorized. This is drilled, and the drilling is why the Almoners have never lost a case in front of the Chancery.
- **The tab is sacred in both directions.** Debts are collected and favors are honored, and an almoner who forgives a debt on their own authority has stolen.
- **Never ask about the supply.** The one question that ends a career: *where does it come from?* Volunteers are told the clinic buys aftercare, which is true. Two people in the organization know the rest.
- **Never sell to a corporation.** A twenty-year rule, currently under pressure from the largest offer the Almoners have ever received ([[upstart]]).

## What the Almoners give

The thing nobody else under the Wall gives: a floor. Food, a clinic, a loan, a funeral, a lawyer when the Chancery takes an interest, somewhere for your family to eat while you are inside. Standing in [[marlow-blocks]] that a badge cannot buy. And, for a character who needs it, the dose — reliably, on the Round, at whatever this month's price is.

## On the character sheet

- **Self kit (Affiliation):** [[almoners-associate]] — the Almoners as an affiliation, built like Street Gang (Affiliation, Core p. 199) but with the gang's turf replaced by a district's gratitude and a ledger line. The weakness side carries the tab.
- **Existing kits worth offering alongside** (Brief §0.1): **Street Gang** (Affiliation, Core p. 199); **Forbidden Cult** (Affiliation, Core p. 198) only for a character who experiences the Kitchen as a faith, which some do; **Assets** for a driver with a van and a route; **Troubled Past** for a character who grew up on the tables.
- **A pack character's relationship** is different and should not use this kit: a Howler is not an associate of the Almoners, they are collateral ([[pack-affiliation]] and [[packs-membership]]).
- **Crew note:** an Almoners tab is one of the cleanest hooks a Crew theme can carry, and a character can hold one without ever having worked for them.

## Leaving

Quietly, and only if the ledger is square. An almoner who leaves owing is not hunted; they are simply *still owing*, in a district where everyone eats at the same tables, forever. An almoner who leaves carrying paperwork — the ledger's structure, a supply invoice, a clinic renewal — is the only person the Almoners have ever killed.

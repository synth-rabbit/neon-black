# Halina Ansah

![[assets/npcs/halina-ansah.png]]

**Handle:** "First Alms" — the title is **First Almoner** · **Affiliation:** [[syndicate|the Almoners]] · **Role in the pilot:** the boss, and the only person in Palisade who understands the whole shape of the Leash trade.

## Who they are

Sixties, Fitted, small, still, dressed like a solicitor's clerk. She works out of two rooms above a shipping agent's office at the shore end of [[gullet-market]], with a window onto the locks, a filing cabinet, and a kettle. There is no security in the building. There has never needed to be.

Before the Almoners she spent eleven years in contracts at [[corp-a|Meliora Bioworks]] — vendor compliance, a career of reading other people's paper — and she left when it became clear she was never going to be promoted past the floor she was on. What she took with her was not a document. It was a fact: that a licensed relief clinic is entitled to buy surgical settling agents in whatever quantity it can justify, that the justification is a form, and that nobody in a corporation that size ever reads a form twice from a customer who pays on time. She wrote the clinic's first supply application herself, in a hand she still recognizes, and she has renewed it every year since ([[syndicate|the Almoners' twist]]).

Her genius, and it is genius, was the second step: seeing that a drug people die without is not a product but a lien. She built a lending business under the Wall on the only collateral that cannot run away, and she built it out of a soup kitchen because a soup kitchen is the one institution that can hold a district's debts and be thanked for it.

She is not cruel and does not enjoy any of it. She thinks of the Run the way an underwriter thinks of a portfolio: unfortunate, necessary, and replaceable. She has authorized four deaths in twenty years, all of them collectors who talked, and she remembers each one as a failure of hiring.

The thing that is happening to her this year is that [[upstart|Continuity]] has offered to buy the book, and she has discovered that she wants to say yes.

## Vector

- **Want:** The book scaled to the whole Megacity, on her own paper, without becoming anybody's subsidiary.
- **Push:** She buys. Debts, contracts, licenses, clinic numbers, a Chancery seat, a crew's future. Where buying will not do it she spends the Run, because the packs are replaceable and the supply contract is not — and she will do that without raising her voice or attending in person.

## What the crew knows / does not know

Nobody under the Wall knows her name. The people who know there is a boss say *the office*, or, if they have heard the phrase, *First Alms*. A crew that works its way up the Round ([[the-round]]) reaches [[bettina-alarcon]] and stops; getting past Bettina requires either the ledger or an invitation, and invitations go to people who have made themselves expensive to ignore.

What almost nobody knows, including most almoners: that the supply is legal, that the paperwork is Meliora's, and that the woman who wrote it is still alive and still renewing it. **[OPEN]** (OQ-44) Whether Meliora has ever identified her as a former employee is not answered here.

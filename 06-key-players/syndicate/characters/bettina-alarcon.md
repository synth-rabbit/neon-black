# Bettina Alarcón

![[assets/npcs/bettina-alarcon.png]]

**Handle:** "the Ladle" · **Affiliation:** [[syndicate|the Almoners]] · **Role in the pilot:** the Marlow Relief Kitchen's director, and the vector face of the Alms.

## Who they are

Fifties, Patched, broad-shouldered, sleeves permanently rolled, a voice built for a room with two hundred people in it. She has run the Kitchen for nineteen years. She was born four blocks away, ate at the tables she now runs when she was nine, and came back with a bookkeeping certificate and a grudge against the Chancery that has never faded. Everyone in [[marlow-blocks]] calls her **the Ladle**, and the nickname is not affectionate the way it sounds; it is what you call the thing that decides how much you get.

She is not pretending. The meals are real, the clinic is real, the coats in winter are real, and she has personally kept several hundred people alive who had nowhere else. She is also the person who writes in the ledger, who decides which loan is forgiven and which is called, and who tells a mother in the courtyard that her son's tab can be worked off if he goes and sees somebody in Block Nineteen ([[packs]]). She does not experience these as two jobs. In her account of herself, the Kitchen is the only institution under the Wall that has never failed the Blocks, and everything it does is what keeping that promise costs.

Ask her about the back door and she will tell you, calmly, that the Kitchen runs a licensed clinic, that the clinic dispenses aftercare, that people who have had cheap work done need aftercare or they die badly, and that if she stopped tomorrow the first funerals would be within the week. All of that is true. It is also the most successful piece of misdirection in the district, because it makes the argument about the dose, and the dose is not the business ([[syndicate|the Almoners' twist]]).

She reports to a person she has met eleven times in nineteen years and refers to only as *the office*.

## Vector

- **Want:** The counters full, the tab collected quietly, and the Round unwatched. Above all: no incident in the Blocks that brings the Chancery back to the clinic license.
- **Push:** She gives first. A crew that comes to her hungry, hurt, hunted, or short leaves fed, patched, hidden, or funded, and with a line in pencil. She never names a price at the time; she names it later, in front of people, in a way that makes refusing a thing the district watches you do.

## What the crew knows / does not know

Any crew that lands under the Wall will be pointed at the Kitchen within a week — it is where you eat, where you get stitched, and where you borrow. They will know within a month that the Alms run it and that Leash goes out the back. What they will not know: that the ledger, not the dose, is the business; that the clinic license is the corporation-facing document that makes the whole thing possible; or that Bettina Alarcón is not the boss and has never made a strategic decision in her life. She is the district's face of an organization whose actual shape she has only ever seen from underneath.

## Challenge

None of her own. In a scene where the Kitchen resists, the resistance is [[almoners-volunteer]]; in a scene where the tab is the obstacle, it is [[first-alms]]'s reach, voiced by her.

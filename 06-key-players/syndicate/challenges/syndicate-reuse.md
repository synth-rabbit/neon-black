# The Almoners — Challenge Reuse

Book Challenges [[syndicate|the Almoners]] field, with pages and the re-flavor line. Custom content is in addition to book content (Brief §0.1); the three custom profiles are [[first-alms]], [[almoners-volunteer]], and [[the-round]]. Shared generics belong to WP5's `08-challenges/generic-reuse-map.md`.

## Characters

| Need | Challenge | Page | Re-flavor |
|---|---|---|---|
| A collector who has run out of patience | **Syndicate Leg-Breaker** | Core p. 307 | The Almoners' version does not enjoy it and apologizes; the hands and knees, never the face, because the debtor has to keep working. |
| Street-level muscle borrowed for a night | **Gang Member** | Core p. 306 | Marlow kids working off a tab. *Home field advantage* applies anywhere in [[marlow-blocks]]; *Let's Go Nitro!* is a Row stimulant, never Leash. |
| A boss who is not the First Almoner — a counter head at the Circus or the Gullet | **Criminal Overlord** | Core p. 306 | Keep the entry's bodyguards and jamming: the new counters behave like ordinary syndicate bosses, which is exactly how the Almoners can tell they are being run badly. |
| A hired shooter for something the Kitchen must not be seen doing | **Mercenary Gunslinger** | Core p. 307 | Contracted through the Gullet so no volunteer is present. |
| The Tuesday clinic's surgeon | **Shady Tech Merchant** | Core p. 303 | A [[suture-row]] cutter on retainer: sculpt aftercare upstairs, the dose downstairs, and a Meliora account number he has never questioned. |
| An investigator the Almoners have hired to find out who is asking | **Investigator** | Core p. 305 | Paid in forgiveness rather than money. |
| A Chancery officer on the Kitchen's board | **Corporate Executive** | Core p. 304 | Reskinned to a municipal auditor: *implicate* is what he is protecting, not profit ([[chancery-hill]]). |

**Not used:** the Yakuza clan Power Sets (Tokyo p. 227) and Horimono (Tokyo p. 227) — the Almoners have no insignia, no tattoos, no colors, and nothing mystical; their whole design is that a member is indistinguishable from a volunteer. **Connected & Protected** (Core p. 328) is likewise excluded at every level: they have debtors, not friends in high places.

## Canon and flags

# The Almoners

![[assets/key-players/syndicate-scene.png]]

![[assets/key-players/syndicate-emblem.png]]

**Role:** syndicate · **Street:** *the Alms* · **Territory:** [[marlow-blocks]] (the Kitchen), [[cinder-yards]], [[gullet-market]], [[halloran-circus]]

## Base concept

An almoner hands out what you cannot live without, on their terms ([[names]] §2). The Almoners run the **Marlow Relief Kitchen** on the ground floor of the central block in [[marlow-blocks]]: hot meals twice a day, a clinic on Tuesdays, coats in winter, school fees, a loan when the rent is short. Nobody in Marlow has not eaten there. Nobody in Marlow does not owe it.

They are a separate entity from the corporations (Bible §3), they resell the trigger to the packs (Bible §2), and every dose of **Leash** that reaches [[cinder-yards]] goes out of the Kitchen's back door on the vans. That much any careful person under the Wall could work out in a month. It is also the part the Almoners are least worried about being known.

Structurally they are small and old-fashioned: no colors, no turf war, no name on anything. Members are **almoners**; the head of the organization is the **First Almoner**, a title, not a person, and the few who have reason to say it out loud say *First Alms*. Everyone else in Marlow uses the only word that matters, which is what they owe: **the tab**.

## Twist

**Source:** invented — **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-109). This partly answers **[OPEN]** (OQ-18), the syndicate's scope and whether it has other significant business; the invention is authorized as the syndicate's twist (Brief §6.4) and the GM may overrule it.

**They are a lender.** Read the Kitchen the obvious way and the charity is a front for the drug trade. Read the ledger and it is the other way round. The Almoners' business is credit under the Wall, in a district where no bank will lend and every debt is unenforceable — and the whole problem with lending to the Foot is that a debtor can simply walk into the Stretch and disappear. Leash solves it. A borrower who has been sculpted on the Almoners' credit cannot leave, because the thing that keeps their body from coming apart is sold by one counter in Palisade, once a week, at whatever price is set that month. The dose is not the product. The dose is the security instrument. **The packs are not the Almoners' customers; they are the Almoners' collateral, and their collectors.**

That is why the price of Leash moves the way it does — not with supply, which is constant, but with the book. A pack that has been useful gets a good month. A pack that has been difficult finds the price has changed, and the pack's own alpha does the disciplining, because the alternative is the whole kennel unsettling at once.

**The paper.** The second floor is that nothing in the supply chain is stolen. The Almoners hold a licensed relief clinic — the Tuesday clinic in the Kitchen's back room is a registered facility with a real Chancery number — and licensed clinics that do sculpt aftercare buy surgical settling agents. The Almoners buy **Accord** from [[corp-a|Meliora Bioworks]] on a standing aftercare supply contract, in bulk, invoiced monthly, paid on time, for years ([[corp-a|Meliora's twist]]). The quantities are absurd for a clinic that size. Nobody at Meliora has ever asked, and the Almoners have spent a long time convincing themselves that this is because they are careful.

It is not. Meliora wants the leak. The Almoners' most closely held secret — that they have quietly gone legitimate at the one point where it counts — is the same fact their supplier is relying on, and neither party has told the other. **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-109)

**What they do not do.** They do not manufacture; they resell (Bible §2). They do not run turf; the Kitchen has never fought a gang for a corner because it does not need corners. They do not touch the corporations' business on the Face and the Crest, and they have never sold to a corporate buyer — which is precisely the line the crack is now inviting them to cross.

## Agenda

Scale the book. The Almoners have proved the instrument works on a district and want to prove it works on a city, and the crack is the moment: displaced people, wrecked wages, corporate layoffs on the Face, a Chancery that cannot pay for anything and is looking for someone to feed the Foot for it. The Kitchen is opening second and third counters — [[halloran-circus]] and the shore end of [[gullet-market]] are already lending — and the First Almoner is being courted by people who want to buy debt paper in volume.

Under that runs the defensive want, which is older and sharper: keep the aftercare contract unread. Every expansion adds a line to an invoice, and the invoice is the only document in Palisade that connects a relief kitchen to a corporation on the Crest.

## What Continuity's destabilization means for us

The stalemate held the Almoners inside one trade and four districts. The crack offers them the two things they have never had: volume and a corporate counterparty.

Volume comes free — a corporate war under the Wall means unemployment, displacement, and a Chancery with no budget, which is a lending boom. The counterparty is the dangerous part. [[upstart|Continuity]] wants to buy the book, and buying the book means owning the packs, and the First Almoner understands exactly what she is being offered: a fortune, and the end of the Almoners as an independent thing. It is the same offer the Almoners have been making to Marlow for twenty years, arriving from the other direction, and she is finding it much harder to refuse than she expected.

The nearer risk is attention. A war means auditors — not the Chancery's, which are handled, but a rival corporation's, looking for something to hold over Meliora. The one loose thread on the Wall that leads from a boardroom on the Crest to a kennel in the Yards runs through the Kitchen's clinic license, and every new counter the Almoners open makes it a little thicker.

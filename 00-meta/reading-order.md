# Reading Order

The order in which an MC reads the vault before session one. Content pointers only; each line says what the file is, not what to do with it. The whole list is about a day's reading; the first two sections are enough to run session one.

## 1. What is fixed

1. `Shared Lore Bible.md` — the canon. Everything in the vault answers to it.
2. `Otherscape Campaign Skeleton Brief.md` §0–§8 — the design decisions and the standing rules; §10–§12 are the seeds of the three registers.
3. [[names]] — every name in the vault and whether the GM has adopted it; §3 the corporate specialties, §4 the caste slang.

## 2. Enough for session one

4. [[series-concept]] · [[pillars]] · [[spine-and-clock]] · [[tone]] — the Series, its themes, its one story and its one clock.
5. [[palisade]] and [[palisade-player-primer]] — the city as the vault knows it and as a resident knows it; then [[coldwater-outfall]] and [[gullet-market]], the two districts session one crosses.
6. [[corp-c]] and [[upstart]] — the camp's owner and the firm that strikes it; [[tomas-adair]] (what the Bible says about Rook and nothing more); [[marisol-okonkwo]] and [[the-weighhouse]].
7. [[breakout]] → [[breakout-vectors]] → the seven scenes in order → [[breakout-roster]] → [[breakout-aftermath]]; the Challenges it runs on: [[the-strike-hour]], [[ledger-security-system]], [[ledger-line-security]], [[continuity-crisis-response-cell]], [[escapee-list]], [[the-sludge-flats]].
8. [[session-zero-packet]] — what the players are holding; [[crew-index]] and [[crew-motivations]] for the end of the session.

## 3. The rest of the city

9. [[district-directory]] and [[timeline]]; the remaining sixteen districts by tier ([[megacity-index]]).
10. The eight remaining Key Players, each overview then its membership file, characters and Challenges ([[key-players-index]]): [[corp-a]] · [[corp-b]] · [[government]] · [[syndicate]] · [[packs]] · [[tao-society]] · [[changeling-cells]] · [[fence-network]].
11. The five MC-notes files — the facts a starting character of each kind does not have: [[bloodware-mc-notes]] · [[howlers-mc-notes]] · [[casters-mc-notes]] · [[doppels-mc-notes]] · [[baselines-mc-notes]]; then the Power Sets ([[splats-index]]).
12. [[challenges-index]] — the cross-cutting custom Challenges and [[generic-reuse-map]]; [[loadout-index]] — the catalog additions and which are player-safe.

## 4. The two paid jobs

13. [[investigation]] → [[investigation-vectors]] → the nine scenes → the four custom Challenges and [[investigation-roster]] → [[investigation-aftermath]]; its three job-only people in `01-investigation/characters/`.
14. [[acquisition]] → [[acquisition-vectors]] → the nine scenes → the three custom Challenges and [[acquisition-roster]] → [[acquisition-aftermath]] (the pivot statement); its three job-only people in `02-acquisition/characters/`.
15. [[jobs-index]] — the handoff between the two, and [[spine-and-clock]] "Clock arithmetic through Job 2".

## 5. What is still the GM's

16. [[open-questions]] — the status board at the top, then the rows marked *GM decision needed*.
17. [[build-choices]] — the proposals the jobs depend on: BC-164 (whose the standing account is), BC-140 (the Ledger's product), BC-149 and BC-172 (the clock), BC-171 (the reconciled handoff).
18. [[conflict-register]] — CR-16 and CR-19 are unresolved and touch play; the rest are the Brief's own resolutions.
19. [[integration-2]] — the short list of decisions to take before session one.

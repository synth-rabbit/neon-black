# Conflict Register

Rules-vs-canon conflicts. Brief §0.3: *flag, don't resolve* — where the book's rules and the Bible's canon conflict, mark `[RULES CONFLICT]`, present the options, and never silently pick one. The eight below are quoted **verbatim from Brief §10**, with the Brief's section numbers. "Resolution adopted" restates the Brief's own adopted resolution; it is the Brief's, not the vault's, and the GM may overrule it. Any file that touches a row cites it as `(CR-n)` ([[style-guide]] §7).

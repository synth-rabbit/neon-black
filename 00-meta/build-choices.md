# Build Choices

Judgment calls the GM did not make and may overrule. Two kinds live here:

- **Interviewer's choices** — the five decisions the GM deferred during the interview, recorded in Brief §11 and labeled `[INTERVIEWER'S CHOICE]` there. They stand unless the GM overrules (Plan A.1 rule 4). Quoted verbatim.
- **Build choices** — new judgment calls made by build packages, marked `[BUILD CHOICE]` in the file where they occur and registered here with an id (`BC-n`). See [[style-guide]] §7.

## How this register is grouped (WP8)

Rows are grouped by the package that made them, in build order, each with its own Status column (BC-180): Brief §11 (BC-1–5) · WP0 (BC-6–10) · WP1 (BC-11–26) · WP5 (BC-27–30) · WP2-vampire (BC-31–43) · WP2-werewolf (BC-44–52) · WP2-mage (BC-53–71) · WP2-changeling (BC-72–88) · WP2-hunter (BC-89–96) · WP3 (BC-97–100) · WP4-trio1 (BC-101–107) · WP4-trio2 (BC-108–115) · WP4-trio3 (BC-116–124) · WP6 (BC-125–138) · WP7a (BC-139–150) · WP7b (BC-151–160) · WP7c (BC-161–170) · WP8 (BC-171–180). **Superseded rows** are annotated in place rather than deleted: BC-153/BC-155 by BC-171; the `type` halves of BC-31, BC-44, BC-57, BC-104, BC-115, BC-119 by BC-127; BC-56 by BC-176. The rows the jobs depend on and that are still the GM's to overrule are listed in [[integration-2]] §5.

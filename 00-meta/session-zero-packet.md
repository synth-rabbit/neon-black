# Session-Zero Packet

The files a player may be handed whole before session one — every one carries `player_safe: true`, and `tools/validate.py` confirms that nothing linked from this page, or from any page it links, leads to MC-only material (Plan C.8; BC-175). What is *not* here is as deliberate as what is: no district file, no Key Player, no job, no Power Set, no register. The city as a resident knows it is one page, [[palisade-player-primer]]; everything else a player needs is a character option.

The order below is the order of character creation (Core p. 174–195): read the city, pick one of the five, take its kits and a trope or build from the questions, add Self kits, then — at the end of session one — make the crew theme together (Brief §5.1).

## 1. The city

- [[palisade-player-primer]] — the Wall and its four tiers, the caste slang, the eighteen districts by their public face, the companies everyone knows, and the five things the crew know that the city does not.

The Series files, the districts, the Key Players, the jobs, the Challenges, the Power Sets, the MC notes split from each overview, and the registers. A player who wants to know more about a place or a company than the primer says asks the MC, which is the point of a primer.

# Names

**Status: reviewed by the GM (2026-09-03).** The GM adopted every primary below after replacing the city name and Corp B name and striking Tao-society alternates that borrowed from another campaign. Every later package uses the **primary** verbatim (Plan A.7); alternates remain on file only for reference. The register these names follow is explained in [[style-guide]] §1. Nothing here adds canon: a name may *resonate* with a fact in the Bible, but the reasoning column never asserts a founding date, a location, a business line, or a history the Bible does not state.

Two things on this page go beyond naming and are flagged separately: the **Corp A/B/C specialty assignment** (§3) and the **caste slang** (§4). Both are `[BUILD CHOICE]` proposals under Brief §6.4 and §6.6.

**On "Tao."** The Bible writes magic as *Tao* and asks the builder to use the engine's own term. The engine has none that survives the Bible's removal of legend-Mythoi (Brief §3.1), so **Tao remains the word** for the force and its disciplines — `[BUILD CHOICE]` (BC-6), see [[style-guide]] §6.

Reading the table: *Reads as* is the newcomer test — what someone who has never seen the campaign guesses the thing is. For the five splats it names the classic World of Darkness counterpart a newcomer would guess, which the Bible requires (Bible §2).

## 4. Caste slang — `[BUILD CHOICE]` (BC-9)

The Bible: the caste system began as economic, is slowly formalizing through unequal access to cybernetics, gene mods, and sculpting, and whether castes are *named, legal, or visibly marked* is `[OPEN]` (Bible §3; Brief §6.6). This proposes **street slang only** — no legal tiers, no markers, no registry. WP1 may place the bands on the map; nobody may give them legal status without the GM.

| Band (top → bottom) | Primary set (proposal) | Alternate set A | Alternate set B | Reads as |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Top | **Sculpted** | Chrome | Skyline | Bodies rebuilt to spec; the work is invisible because it is perfect. |
| Upper-middle | **Fitted** | Plate | Midstack | Corporate-issue implants, warrantied and monitored. |
| Lower | **Patched** | Tin | Streetlevel | Second-hand, black-market, DIY; it shows. |
| Bottom | **Stock** (*"born stock, die stock"*) | Rust | Sublevel | Factory-standard flesh, unmodified because unaffordable. |

The primary set follows the participle rule ([[style-guide]] §1.2) and encodes the first theme directly: your band is what was done to your body. Set A names the finish. Set B names the address. **Tension to note, not resolve:** *Stock* describes unaffordable flesh, while a Baseline (hunter) may be unmodified by choice at any income; the slang is class talk, not a classification of the five, and a wealthy Baseline is not "stock." `[OPEN]` (OQ-8) remains: whether any of this is ever formal.

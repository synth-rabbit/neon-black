# Crew Motivations

Prebuilt Identity, Ritual, and Itch statements for every crew kit in this vault — three existing ([[existing-crew-kits]]) and three custom (`crew-kits/`). At least four options per kit, each with a one-line **acting against it looks like** so Decay is adjudicable at the table without anyone having to invent the line in the moment (Core p. 75, 154). Pick one, or write your own in the same voice — the book's own instruction is that the whole crew, players and characters both, has to actually agree with whichever one is chosen (Core p. 154).

## Found Family

| Type | Statement | Acting against it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | We're in this together. (book default) | Cutting a deal, or running, that leaves another crew member to face the Consequences alone. |
| Identity | Blood's got nothing on the people who show up. | Choosing someone outside the crew over the crew when it actually costs you something. |
| Ritual | We eat together before every job, no matter what. | Skipping the meal, or the job, without telling the rest why. |
| Itch | Say the thing nobody wants to say out loud. | Letting a lie or a silence sit between crew members when honesty would have cost less than the silence does. |

## Rebellious Street Gang

| Type | Statement | Acting against it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | Dominate the streets or be dominated by others. (book default) | Backing down from a challenge to the crew's turf or reputation without a fight worth having. |
| Identity | This ground is ours because we said so and made it stick. | Letting someone else's claim on the crew's turf go unanswered. |
| Ritual | We mark what's ours, and we defend the mark. | Abandoning a claimed piece of turf, a tag, or a promise without a fight. |
| Itch | Push back, every time, on anyone who tries to push first. | Letting a slight, a shove, or a threat against the crew go unanswered. |

## Wanted

| Type | Statement | Acting against it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | Never. Get. Caught. (book default) | Taking a job, a risk, or a shortcut that puts the whole crew's exposure ahead of any one score. |
| Identity | We clear our name, or we become someone new trying. | Giving up on clearing the crew's name or building a new one, and settling for staying hidden forever. |
| Ritual | We always have two ways out of any room before we start. | Walking into a job without a second exit planned. |
| Itch | Trust the crew over anyone outside it, every time. | Taking an outsider's word over a crew member's when the two conflict. |

## Scrape By Together

| Type | Statement | Acting against it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | Nobody looks out for us but us. (kit default) | Leaving one of the crew to face a Consequence alone when you could have shared it. |
| Identity | We split everything, fair, every time. | Holding back a cut of a job's take, or a piece of information, from the rest of the crew. |
| Itch | Take the sure thing over the big score. | Gambling the crew's safety on a bigger, riskier payout when a smaller, safer one was on the table. |
| Ritual | We check in with each other before every job, no exceptions. | Walking into a job, or a room, without telling the rest of the crew what you're about to do. |

## The City Is Ours For The Taking

| Type | Statement | Acting against it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | This city changes hands, and it changes into ours. (kit default) | Passing up ground, leverage, or a claim the crew could have taken and held. |
| Identity | Every score makes us bigger, not just richer. | Taking a payout that doesn't advance the crew's standing when a riskier play would have. |
| Itch | Take the leverage, every time it's offered. | Walking away from a chance to put someone in your debt, even a small one. |
| Ritual | We never let anyone outside the crew see the whole plan. | Telling an outsider — even one who's helped before — more of the crew's actual plan than they need to know. |

## We Owe Rook

| Type | Statement | Acting against it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | We pay what we owe him, whatever it costs. (kit default) | Turning down a real chance to find out what happened to Rook, or to help him, because it's inconvenient. |
| Identity | He got us out. We don't leave that unanswered. | Treating Rook as old business the crew has moved past. |
| Ritual | Every job, someone asks if there's been word. | Letting a job's aftermath pass without anyone checking whether it turned up anything about him. |
| Itch | Chase any lead on where they took him, no matter how thin. | Passing up a thin, risky lead on Rook in favor of the safer job. |

## Canon and flags

- Player-facing, no GM advice: this page tells players what the statements mean and what breaks them, never how the MC should present or pace that break (Brief §0.2).
- Voice follows `style-guide` §3: Identity is a conviction stated flatly; Ritual is a discipline, price, or condition the crew keeps; Itch is an impulse the crew indulges together.
- Any crew may write its own statement instead of choosing from this list — the table's agreement is what matters (Core p. 154), not the specific wording here.

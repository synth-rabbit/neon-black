# Scrape By Together

**Themebook:** Crew · **Category:** Crew-OS

You have nothing but each other and whatever the Ledger let you carry out — no money, no standing, no institutional anything, just a work crew that already knows how to move together and a fence's back room to sleep in if she'll have you (Bible §6). This is the escapee crew's starting shape: the recommended kit for a table that wants the nascent crew theme to grow directly out of the breakout, per the book's own guidance that a crew "already knows each other" going in (Core p. 148, 154).

## Nascent form (end of session one)

Title tag **scrape by together** + one weakness (recommended: *nothing but each other*) + a motivation from the list above — three sentences, no more, and the crew theme reads complete: five people, nothing, each other. This is the form Brief §5.1 asks for at the close of the breakout job.

## Canon and flags

- Canon relied on: the crew's standing after the breakout — at the bottom, on an escapee list with no bounty, the fence as the one day-one contact, the work crew bond (Bible §6). Nascent crew theme timing (Brief §5.1; `conflict-register` CR-5).
- **[BUILD CHOICE]** Tally's Weighhouse ([[palisade-player-primer#Gullet Market|Gullet Market]], `build-choices` BC-20) is used as tag B on the assumption the crew accepts her hospitality; a table that doesn't should swap it for another meeting place without changing the kit's Identity.

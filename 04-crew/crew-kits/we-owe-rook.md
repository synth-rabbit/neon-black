# We Owe Rook

**Themebook:** Crew · **Category:** Crew-OS

The crew leader who acclimated you, protected you, and pointed you at Tally after the strike took him is gone, and the debt hasn't gone anywhere (Bible §5, §6). This kit builds the crew theme around that unanswered question rather than around survival or ambition — an alternative to [[scrape-by-together]] for a table that wants Rook's absence to be the crew's center of gravity from the start.

## Nascent form (end of session one)

Title tag **we owe Rook** + one weakness (recommended: *nobody knows where they took him*) + a motivation reads as a complete nascent theme: a crew defined by an open debt, not yet by anything they've built for themselves.

## Canon and flags

- Canon relied on: the crew leader's role, the extraction, and the fence referral (Bible §5, §6). Nothing here states or implies why Rook mattered, what he is, or who took him — those facts belong to [[palisade-player-primer#Continuity Risk & Response|Continuity]] and stay unrevealed by this file.
- **[BUILD CHOICE]** Written to be usable **before** the table knows the crew leader's nature: every tag reads as grief and unfinished business, nothing else. Tag C ("we do it the way Rook taught us") and tag G ("what Rook told us before they took him") are the only places a table might later connect fragments to something larger — the MC decides what those fragments mean, if anything; this kit does not.

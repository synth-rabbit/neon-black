# The City Is Ours For The Taking

**Themebook:** Crew · **Category:** Crew-OS

You didn't stop at surviving the breakout. Somewhere in the jobs since, this crew decided it means to end up holding a real piece of Palisade — territory, leverage, a Key Player's fear — not scraps of it. This is the *take it* half of the Series' fourth theme (Bible §4) at the crew level, the natural growth path from [[scrape-by-together]] once a table decides the crew wants power, not just a way to eat.

## Nascent form (end of session one)

This kit is written for a crew that has already had at least one job go well enough to think bigger — it is not the recommended session-one kit (see Scrape By Together for that). If a table wants to start here anyway, title tag **the city is ours for the taking** + one weakness (recommended: *we want more than we can hold*) + a motivation still reads as a complete nascent theme.

## Canon and flags

- Canon relied on: "scrape it out … to fix the city, or take it over" (Bible §1); theme 4 (Bible §4); the upstart's destabilization creating opportunity "for every faction" (Bible §5) — a crew with this theme is one more faction smelling it.
- The sandbox stays open: this kit does not decide the Series' outcome, only that this table's crew has chosen a side of the fork (`series-concept`).

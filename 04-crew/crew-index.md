# Crew Package Index

At the end of session one — after the breakout (Job 0, `breakout`) — the table creates the crew theme. This campaign creates it as a **nascent theme**: one power tag (the title), one weakness tag, and a motivation, rather than the book's usual three-power-tag start, because the crew has just met each other in the fiction one job ago and grows the theme through Upgrades as play gives it more to be about (Brief §5.1; `conflict-register` CR-5). The pre-existing tie the book assumes a crew starts with — "already know each other, at least tangentially" (Core p. 147) — is the shared work crew from the Ledger (Bible §6).

## Order of choices

1. **Pick a kit, or build from the crew themebook questions.** Take one of the existing kits ([[existing-crew-kits]]), one of this vault's custom kits (`crew-kits/`), or answer the crew themebook's own questions from scratch (Core p. 155). Any of the three is a legal way to reach a nascent crew theme.
2. **Take the title tag and one weakness tag.** From the chosen kit, take tag A (the title tag) and one weakness tag. That is the whole crew theme for now.
3. **Choose a motivation.** Pick an Identity, Ritual, or Itch from [[crew-motivations|motivations]], or write one in the same voice. Every player — and every character — has to actually agree to it (Core p. 154): it is what the crew is agreeing to be about, not one PC's private goal.
4. **Confirm the crew relationship tags**, if they weren't already set during character creation (Core p. 177) — one tag per other PC, chosen together, from the book's relationship list (below).

## Growing past nascent

The crew theme starts with one power tag and one weakness tag. As Decay marks and Upgrades (Core p. 133–134) add to it in play, it grows toward the three power tags each custom kit marks with a ★ — the "recommended starting three," the combination that reads as a complete, book-standard crew theme once the table gets there. No table is required to grow toward the marked three; it is a starting point for the Upgrade choice, not a requirement, and a crew that changes what it's about can choose differently.

## Crew relationship tags (Core p. 177)

Each PC picks one tag per other crew member, after that player's approval, from: *admiring, amused, angry, appreciative, bad blood, bossing them around, casual lover, communication failure, crushing, friendly, good vibes, hatred, have their back, in love, indebted, indifferent, jealous, leverage on them, look down on them, mentoring, mocking, need them for..., offended, painful to watch, party together, pining, protective, remind me of..., resentful, rival, soulmates, submissive, supportive, tandem, work well together.* These are not part of any theme; they're burned when invoked like any tag, but never for Power, and can be restored or changed during Credit Roll.

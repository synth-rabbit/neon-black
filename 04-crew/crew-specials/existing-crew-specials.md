# Existing Crew Theme Specials

The five Crew Theme Specials printed on Core p. 155, available to any crew theme in this vault as an upgrade choice (Core p. 133) — offered alongside the custom Crew Theme Specials in this folder, never instead of them (Plan A.1 rule 1).

| Special | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Huddle** | Once per session, one crew member can take an action that affects one target and apply it to the whole crew, ignoring the crew's Scale of 1. |
| **Last Second Leap** | Once per session, when a PC misses a roll, another PC can jump in to help at the last second if they're capable of doing so. The roll becomes a mixed hit (7–9); the helper suffers the same Consequences as the PC in the spotlight, and both may Mitigate as usual. |
| **Sacrifice** | When two or more of you face the same Consequences, any player whose PC can reasonably defend another PC may spend any or all of their Mitigation Power on that PC's Consequences instead of their own. |
| **Strong Bonds** | During downtime, when a crew member spends Power creating relationship story tags representing bonds with other crew members, they get 1 extra Power to spend. |
| **United By The Cause** | During Credit Roll, if the entire crew cooperated in a single scene while aligned with the crew theme's motivation, remove one Decay from the crew theme. |

# We Got Out Together

**Prerequisite:** The crew has encountered, in play, at least one other survivor of the Ledger's work crews since the breakout — an NPC or PC who was inside when the strike happened.

Not everyone who escaped [[palisade-player-primer#Coldwater Outfall|Reconciliation Facility 4]] the night of the strike stayed together. Once per session, when the whole crew acts together to protect, extract, or make good on a promise to a fellow Ledger survivor, treat a mixed hit (7–9) as a strong hit (10+) — the solidarity of people who know exactly what that fence line and that floodlight meant.

## Ties

- [[palisade-player-primer#Coldwater Outfall|Coldwater Outfall]] — the Ledger; the shared experience this Special is built on.

## Canon and flags

- Canon relied on: the general breakout, the work crew, the escapee list (Bible §5, §6).
- Condition-gated in the Tokyo p. 75 pattern: another survivor has to actually appear in play before a table can choose this Special, keeping it earned rather than assumed.
- **[OPEN]** (OQ-13) This Special does not explain why the Ledger held all five on one crew, and does not need to — it only requires that *someone else* from the camp turns up.

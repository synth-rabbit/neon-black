# We Know the Under

**Prerequisite:** The crew has invoked a district story tag from the Under (Foundation Galleries, Coldwater Outfall, Lowmere Sinks, or another Under district) at least twice in play.

Whatever brought the crew down under the Wall — the breakout, a job, hiding out — they've learned the flood tunnels, the pump schedules, and the places nobody official has ever come back from with a report ([[palisade-player-primer#Foundation Galleries|Foundation Galleries]], Core p. 42). Once per session, while operating in an Under district, the crew may gain a shared tier-2 status (e.g. *gone-to-ground-2*) reflecting how hard the crew is to find or follow down there. It lasts until the crew leaves the Under or the scene ends, whichever is later.

## Ties

- [[palisade-player-primer#Foundation Galleries|Foundation Galleries]] — the Wall's hollow bones.
- [[palisade-player-primer#Coldwater Outfall|Coldwater Outfall]] — where the Series opened.
- [[palisade-player-primer#Lowmere Sinks|Lowmere Sinks]] — the drowned marsh, another Under district a crew might come to know.

## Canon and flags

- Canon relied on: the Under as the lowest tier, sprawl, and outfall wastes at the map's edge ([[palisade-player-primer#The Wall|Palisade]], `build-choices` BC-15).
- Condition-gated in the Tokyo p. 75 pattern: the crew has to have actually spent story-tag-worthy time in the Under before this Special is available.

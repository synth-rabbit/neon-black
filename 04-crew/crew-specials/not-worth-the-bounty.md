# Not Worth The Bounty

**Prerequisite:** No Key Player has ever posted a bounty on the crew, and the crew has not taken the Wanted crew kit (Core p. 157) or otherwise chosen to become genuinely hunted.

The crew is on [[palisade-player-primer#Amalgamated Prosthetic & Interface|AP&I]]'s escapee list, and every list like it says the same thing about them: not worth a bounty, not worth a recapture team (Bible §6). Once per job, the first time a Challenge representing official law or corporate security identifies a crew member from the escapee list or a similar record, the crew may treat that Challenge's opening read of them as "not worth the trouble" — downgrade its default alert status by one tier, or, if it has none, treat its first action against the crew as a Quick Outcome rather than a Tracked one.

This Special stops applying, in fiction and at the table, the moment that stops being true — a table that later takes the [[existing-crew-kits|Wanted]] crew kit or otherwise earns a real bounty has moved past what this Special represents, and should retire it as an upgrade choice going forward.

## Ties

- [[palisade-player-primer#Amalgamated Prosthetic & Interface|AP&I]] — the escapee list's owner.

## Canon and flags

- Canon relied on: the escapee list with no bounty (Bible §6).
- Condition-gated in the Tokyo p. 75 pattern, and self-limiting: it names its own expiration condition rather than requiring the table to remember to retire it manually.

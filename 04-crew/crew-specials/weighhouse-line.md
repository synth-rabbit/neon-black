# Weighhouse Line

**Prerequisite:** The crew has completed at least one job through Tally or her network and split the proceeds with her honestly (no shorted cut, no cutting her out).

Once per job, any crew member may declare that the Weighhouse's network can get the crew one non-combat item, service, or piece of information on credit — a fence's resources called in before they're needed, the way [[palisade-player-primer#The Weighhouse|Tally]]'s own runners work the [[palisade-player-primer#Gullet Market|Gullet]]. Create a story tag for whatever's supplied. Every use of this Special counts against what the crew owes her; if the debt runs ahead of what the crew has paid down in play, the MC may invoke the crew's debt-to-Tally weakness tag as part of any PC's action without the crew needing to burn it into play first — the line only stays open as long as the crew pays it down.

## Ties

- [[palisade-player-primer#Gullet Market|Gullet Market]] — the Weighhouse, the physical place this Special draws on.
- [[palisade-player-primer#The Weighhouse|Tally]] — Tally herself; the network is hers.

## Canon and flags

- Canon relied on: the fence as the crew's day-one contact and ongoing hiring channel (Bible §5, §6); the Weighhouse (`build-choices` BC-20).
- Condition-gated in the Tokyo p. 75 pattern: a crew must earn the fence's trust in play before this Special is available as an upgrade choice.

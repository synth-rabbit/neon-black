# Howlers

![[assets/splats/howlers.png]]

The city's word for them is **Howlers**, and the city almost never uses it, because the city almost never knows. A pack that runs the [[palisade-player-primer#Cinder Yards|Cinder Yards]] at night is, to anyone who reads about it the next morning, a Baseline with a gun and a grudge and some very expensive chrome. That is the arrangement, and everyone above the Foot is happy with it.

## What a Howler is

A Howler is a body that was rebuilt to become something else, and a chemical that tells it when.

The rebuild is two jobs done to one person. The first is **sculpt** — bone lengthened and re-anchored, muscle re-laid, a second set of teeth seated in a re-cut jaw, glands grown where none were, skin thickened until it takes a knife badly. The second is **chrome** — sheaths for the claws, a shunt at the spine, ports, a cage of hardware that holds the new shape together when it moves in a way the old one never had to. Neither half works alone. Both halves are permanent: chrome can be pulled, the sculpt cannot (`names` §1; Bible §1). Suture Row will repair a sculpt and will not undo one, and the people who go to [[palisade-player-primer#Suture Row|the Row]] and ask are sent back out onto the street.

The chemical is **Leash**. Without it a Howler is a strange, hungry, hard-to-place body with a bad jaw. With it, the shape arrives — minutes, not seconds — and holds for a night, and what walks off the cinder flats at dawn is a person again, wrung out, starving, and owed for. Leash is not sold in the open. It is sold by [[palisade-player-primer#The Almoners and the Kitchen|the Almoners]], from the back of a relief kitchen in [[palisade-player-primer#Marlow Blocks|Marlow Blocks]] and out of a van at a rail siding in the Yards, at whatever the price is this month.

Howlers organize in packs, and the packs call the whole business **the Run**. A pack is a gang: turf, a kennel, an alpha, a count of who owes what. It is also the only institution that will feed a Howler, house a Howler, repair a Howler, and put a dose in a Howler's hand on a week when they cannot pay for one. Both of those facts are the same fact.

They are **rare**. Every Howler is one expensive, irreversible procedure and a standing bill. Nobody catches this; nobody is bitten into it. They are made, one at a time, on credit (Bible §2; Brief §2.6).

## What a pack member knows, and what they do not

**[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-45) The Bible fixes the supply chain but not where a street Howler's knowledge stops. Proposed line, so that the same fact is not accidentally common knowledge in one file and a revelation in another:

**A Howler on the street knows:**

- The sculpt is irreversible and the chrome is not; who on [[palisade-player-primer#Suture Row|the Row]] will work on either without a record; what a bad graft costs.
- Leash comes from the Almoners and from nobody else that lasts. The Alms set the price, the price moves, and the reason it moves is never explained.
- Doses are counted. The kitchen has a ledger, the alpha has a count, and both are older than anyone's memory of joining.
- Leash is made somewhere with a real production line, because it arrives clean, sealed, identical, and in crates — not out of a bathtub.
- Knockoff exists, comes off [[palisade-player-primer#Suture Row|the Row]] and out of the Sinks, and does something wrong to a sculpted body often enough to be a story every pack tells.
- Being seen is death for everyone, not just for the one who was seen, and that the corporations clean up after them and call it a Baseline.

**A Howler on the street does not know:**

- That the line belongs to [[palisade-player-primer#Meliora Bioworks|Meliora Bioworks]].
- That Leash is not a product. It is something else Meliora makes, for a reason of Meliora's own, that happens to do this to a sculpted body. **[OPEN]** (OQ-3) That reason is Meliora's twist and belongs to WP4; nothing in this package decides it.
- That the Almoners are therefore a reseller of somebody else's inventory and not a manufacturer — which means the Alms' price is not the Alms' cost, and the supply could end for reasons that have nothing to do with the Run at all.
- Whether Meliora knows what its compound is being used for.

## Mapping

**Hybrid** (Brief §2.1). Identity lives in theme kits, tropes, PC Specials, and Essence — not in a category of its own.

| | Category | Themebook | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Required primary** | **Noise** | **Augmentation** | The sculpted beast: the rebuilt body and the chrome that holds it. [[sculpted-beast]] |
| Optional secondary | Noise | Cutting Edge *or* Augmentation | The trigger and the supply. [[a-line-on-leash]], or **Reflex Booster Implants** (Core p. 239) |
| Optional secondary | Self | Affiliation | The pack. [[pack-affiliation]], or **Street Gang** (Core p. 199) |

A Howler PC must carry the sculpt somewhere. A Howler PC need not carry the trigger or the pack as themes — plenty of them are a rebuilt body, a standing debt, and three ordinary Self themes.

## Essence minimum

**At least one Noise theme** (`build-choices` BC-1; Brief §2.2 option (ii)). Essence is otherwise free.

- One Augmentation theme plus three Self themes lands on **Cyborg** (Core p. 135) — the common shape, and a good one: the Cyborg Special resists intangible mystical effects, which is exactly the corner a Howler is least equipped for.
- Sculpt plus trigger plus two Self is still **Cyborg**.
- Four Noise themes is **Singularity**, and reads as a Howler who is nothing but the work now.
- **[OPEN]** (OQ-5) Whether Howlers can touch Tao at all is unresolved. The Hybrid mapping offers no Mythos theme for a Howler and this package writes none. A table that wants **Nexus** or **Transhuman** for a Howler is answering OQ-5 by playing it; flag it to the GM rather than assuming it.

## Persistence

**[RULES CONFLICT]** (CR-7) The sculpt is irreversible (Bible §1) and themes Decay and get replaced (Core p. 136). Resolution adopted by the Brief (§2.4) and used here:

- The **power** lives in [[sculpted-beast]] and behaves like any Noise theme: mark Decay for stifling the Itch, three marks and it is replaced.
- The **condition** does not go anywhere. A Howler whose Augmentation theme Decays away is still a Howler: still sculpted, still unable to pass, still needing the dose to be anything but a strange body, still on somebody's count. What the replacement means in fiction is a Howler who stopped using it — refusing the dose, staying human-shaped, letting the work go to waste. It is not a cure, and [[palisade-player-primer#Suture Row|the Row]] does not sell one.
- The inescapable hooks are carried by PC Specials in the Tokyo p. 72–74 pattern, which sit on the character card and survive theme replacement: [[kept-on-leash]], [[the-sculpt-does-not-come-off]], [[kennel-bond]].

## Motivation

Kit-fixed **Itch** on every Howler Noise theme (`build-choices` BC-2; Brief §2.5). Both custom kits are written so that indulging the Itch is the pull deeper into being owned, and resisting it marks Decay — the inverse of a humanity meter, per Brief §4.2, and there is no humanity track anywhere in this folder (Brief §4.3).

- [[sculpted-beast]] — *Take the dose and hunt as the beast.*
- [[a-line-on-leash]] — *Take more than I need.*

Dependency on the Almoners' supply is carried **twice**, as the Brief requires: as the direction the Itch pulls, and as a weakness tag on both kits (*needs the dose to work*; *the supply is not mine*).

## How a Howler reads on the sheet

Four themes, a loadout, an Essence, and a card with one or two Specials on it that no theme owns.

- **Themebooks you will see:** Augmentation (always), Cutting Edge (the supply), Affiliation (the pack), Troubled Past (the Blocks, the Row, the Ledger), Personality, Assets.
- **Essence:** Cyborg, usually. Singularity at the far end.
- **Tags that give it away in play:** a title tag naming the sculpt; a weakness tag naming the dose; a loadout line with a dose case on it.
- **The thing every Howler has:** a count. Somebody is owed a number, and it is written down.
- **Tropes:** [[run-enforcer]], [[off-the-run]].
- **NPC overlays:** `running-on-leash`, `pack-tactics`.

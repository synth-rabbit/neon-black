# The Sculpt Does Not Come Off

**Splat:** Howler · **Prerequisite:** You carry a theme representing the sculpt, and you have been looked at once by someone with the authority to look twice.

Chrome can be pulled. The sculpt cannot. The jaw is the wrong shape, the shoulders sit wrong under a coat, the gait is wrong on stairs, and the second set of teeth does not come out. Whenever you need to be taken for unmodified — a checkpoint, a clinic, a scanner, a Face lobby, a landlord with a form — you either cover the work first with an action that can fail, or you take *made-2*: it hinders social actions with anyone who has reason to look, and it helps anyone who is trying to find you again.

While *made* is on you, the trade runs the other way as well. You may invoke it as helping any action to frighten, warn off, or claim ground, because everyone in the room has now seen what you are and none of them wants to be the one who tests it.

*made* clears when you are somewhere that does not care — the Kiln Row, the flats, the Blocks after dark, a room full of people who are also something — or at the end of the job, whichever comes first.

## Persistence

Survives theme replacement (Brief §2.4; `conflict-register` CR-7). Replacing [[sculpted-beast]] is a Howler who stopped using the work; it is not a Howler who got their old body back, which Bible §1 does not allow and [[palisade-player-primer#Suture Row|the Row]] does not sell. Removed only for narrative reasons, and mark an Evolution point (Tokyo p. 72) — the removal is a character who has stopped trying to pass, or has found somewhere they no longer need to.

## Canon and flags

- The persistent condition carried: **the sculpt is irreversible; only implants can be removed** (Bible §1), and **Howlers are secret** while extreme modding is public and legible as bodyguards, sculpted entertainers, and arena fighters (Bible §3; Brief §4.1). What *made* actually costs is caste, not exposure: a body like this reads as Patched at best in a district that expects Fitted, which is Bible §4 themes 1 and 2 — the ladder is your body, and the mods decide who counts.
- The Masquerade is not broken by *made*. Nobody at a checkpoint concludes *werewolf*; they conclude *heavy illegal work, probably a Baseline, definitely somebody else's problem*. That is the cover the corporations maintain (Bible §3).
- **[RULES CONFLICT]** (CR-7) is the row this answers.
- **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-46) display name; (BC-51) the *made-2* status, its tier, and the two-way trade — the same status hinders passing and helps threatening, so the cost of the body and the use of the body are one thing on the card rather than two.

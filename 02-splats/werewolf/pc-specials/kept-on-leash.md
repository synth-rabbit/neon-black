# Kept on Leash

**Splat:** Howler · **Prerequisite:** You have taken Leash on screen and shifted, and you have gone at least one scene wanting a dose you did not have.

Your body was built around a compound it does not make, and somebody else decides what a month of it costs. If a downtime passes in which you did not secure a supply, you begin the next job with *strung-out-2*: it hinders every action that uses the sculpt, it hinders mitigating Consequences that represent pain, hunger, or exhaustion, and it does not expire on its own. When you take a dose in a scene, remove *strung-out* entirely and gain *running-2* until the end of that scene — for as long as it lasts, you are what the work was paid for. When *running* expires you take *wrung-out-2*.

Securing a supply is a downtime action with a price attached: money, a name, a favour to [[palisade-player-primer#The Almoners and the Kitchen|the Almoners]], a night's work for a pack, or somebody else's count. What the price is this time is a matter for the table; that there is one is not.

## Persistence

Survives theme replacement (Brief §2.4; `conflict-register` CR-7). A Howler whose [[sculpted-beast]] theme Decays away and gets replaced still wakes up needing the dose — the theme was the power, this is the condition. Removed only for narrative reasons, and mark an Evolution point (Tokyo p. 72): the removal describes a character who has stopped arranging their life around the supply, not a body that stopped needing it. There is no cure on [[palisade-player-primer#Suture Row|Suture Row]] and none anywhere else (Bible §1).

## Canon and flags

- The persistent condition carried: **dependency on a chemical trigger manufactured by one of the Big Three and resold to the packs by a syndicate** (Bible §2; Bible §3). Bible §4 theme 3 — ownership versus freedom — is the reason this sits on the character card rather than in a theme.
- This is a status loop, not a track. **[RULES CONFLICT]** (CR-7) is the row this answers; it does **not** introduce a humanity meter, which Brief §4.3 forbids, and nothing here counts up or down between sessions.
- **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-46) display name; (BC-51) the specific tiers and the *strung-out* / *running* / *wrung-out* status names, and the choice to make relief a real benefit rather than only a penalty — the Itch has to be worth scratching for Brief §4.2 to work.
- **[OPEN]** (OQ-3) nothing here names what Leash is for. **[OPEN]** (OQ-18) what securing a supply costs is left to the table and to WP4's syndicate.

# Kennel Bond

**Splat:** Howler · **Prerequisite:** You run with a pack of the Run, or you did and they have not written you off. Someone in that pack keeps your count.

Once per job you may call on your pack for one thing they can actually give, and gain two appropriate story tags for the scene: a body to stand beside you (*someone at my back*, *two more coming up the stairs*), a place to sleep (*a kennel with a door*, *nobody followed me here*), a name (*who the Almoner is*, *whose ground this is*), or a dose out of somebody else's count (*a night I did not pay for*).

A pack is what it is (Bible §2): turf, a kennel, an alpha, and a ledger. The pack keeps count. Before the next downtime ends they come and ask for something back — a night's work, a body moved, a shed held, a name given up, the dose returned with interest. If you refuse, you cannot use this Special again until you make it right.

What a pack can give is bounded by what a pack has, which under the Wall is people, ground, and knowledge of the street — never money, never leverage on the Face, and never a supply that did not come from the Almoners in the first place.

## Persistence

Survives theme replacement (Brief §2.4). A Howler who drops [[pack-affiliation]] or **Street Gang** (Core p. 199) as a theme has stopped making the pack the centre of their life; the pack has not stopped knowing them, housing them, or counting them. That is the difference between a theme and a card.

Removed only for narrative reasons, and mark an Evolution point (Tokyo p. 72): the pack is gone, the pack is finished with you, or you have paid out and walked — which is the point where [[off-the-run]] starts.

## Canon and flags

- The persistent condition carried: **packs function like gangs and are the Howler's only institution** (Bible §2), and the pack's hold is the same hold the Almoners have one step further down the chain (Bible §4 theme 3). This is the third of the three inescapable hooks the Brief names for Howlers (§2.5: trigger dependency, pack ties, irreversibility of the sculpt), alongside [[kept-on-leash]] and [[the-sculpt-does-not-come-off]].
- Built on the Tokyo *Friends in the Bureau* pattern (Tokyo p. 73): a standing relationship that gives story tags once per job and bills you for them.
- **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-46) display name; (BC-51) two story tags per use, once per job, and the refusal clause.
- **[OPEN]** (OQ-20) the pack's age and depth of lineage; nothing here depends on it. Pack internals — how alphas are made, how counts are kept, what a pack owes another pack — belong to WP4's `packs` and WP3's `pack-affiliation`, and are not decided here.

# Howler Existing Kits

Book kits a Howler can take as printed, with the page and the one line of re-flavor that makes it read as Palisade rather than as generic chrome. Custom in addition to existing, never instead of it (Brief §0.1; Plan A.1 rule 1) — the custom kits are [[sculpted-beast]] and [[a-line-on-leash]], and the pack Affiliation kit is [[pack-affiliation]].

Re-flavor here means **words, not tags**. Nothing below changes a printed tag, a printed weakness, or a printed Itch; a table that wants the kit exactly as written loses nothing.

## The two the Brief names

| Kit | Themebook | Page | What it is for a Howler | Re-flavor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Animalistic Modifications** | Augmentation (Noise) | Core p. 238 | The nearest printed kit to the sculpt, and the fastest way to make a Howler without opening a custom kit at all. *superior anatomy*, *natural weapons*, *animal reflexes*, *bioengineering*; weaknesses *weak immune system*, *I get exhausted fast*; Itch *Experience the world as an animal.* | The tags describe work that was **paid for and cannot be returned** — a sculpt out of a back room on [[palisade-player-primer#Suture Row|the Row]] or on the Almoners' credit, not a lifestyle aesthetic. *bioengineering* is what was done to the character, not a hobby. The Itch reads correctly as printed, but a Howler can only scratch it **on a dose**, which is what turns the printed kit into a Howler kit and hands the table the dependency for free. |
| **Reflex Booster Implants** | Augmentation (Noise) | Core p. 239 | The printed trigger-dependency kit, and the alternative to [[a-line-on-leash]] for a table that wants the dependency **implanted** rather than carried in a case. *adrenaline rush*, *increased reaction speed*, *burst of strength*, *inject someone else*, *biochemistry set*; weaknesses *withdrawal symptoms*, *stigmatized as a junkie*, *bloodstream already flooded*, *body can't self regulate*; Itch *Push my body to the limit.* | Every printed weakness is already the Howler's. The booster is a **spinal shunt and a sternum port fed by the dose**, not an off-the-shelf reflex chip: *inject someone else* means dosing another Howler, which under the Run is a favour, a claim, or a threat depending on who is counting. |

## Not for Howlers

- **Cloud of Nanites** (Cutting Edge, Core p. 242) and the nanite half of **Self-Healing** — the Ferrante strain is the Bloodware's, and the *strong magnetic fields* weakness is their canon vulnerability, not a Howler's (Bible §2; `names` §2).
- Any **Exposure** or **Esoterica** kit — **[OPEN]** (OQ-5) whether Howlers can touch Tao at all is unresolved, and this package writes no Mythos theme for a Howler. See `overview`.
- **Impossibly Good Looks** (Augmentation, Core p. 239) — *alternate face* is the Doppels' ground, and a Howler who can change faces stops being the one of the five who cannot pass.

## Canon and flags

- Canon relied on: Bible §2 werewolf (bio-sculpt and cybernetics, the chemical trigger, the syndicate, gang-like packs), Bible §1 (becoming one of the five can be a choice or something done to you; the sculpt is irreversible), Bible §2 rarity; Brief §0.1, §2.5 werewolf.
- Rarity: none of these kits is offered as a common aug. Every entry above assumes the sculpt already happened and was expensive.
- **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-44) vault-only frontmatter field `type: splat` on this index file.
- **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-47) the re-flavor lines and the "not for Howlers" list; all are words over printed tags, and any of them can be dropped without touching a kit.

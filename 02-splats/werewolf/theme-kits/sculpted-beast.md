# Sculpted Beast

**Themebook:** Augmentation · **Category:** Noise · **Source:** custom

Somebody rebuilt you to be something else, and somebody else sells you the reason it works. The bone was lengthened and re-anchored, the jaw re-cut and given a second set of teeth, the claws grown and then seated in hardware because grown claws tear their own beds out. It took months and it cost more than you had. It does not come off. On a dose you are the fastest, heaviest, best-smelling thing on the flats for a night; off one you are a strange body with a bad jaw and an appetite, waiting for the van.

## Itch

> Take the dose and hunt as the beast.

Scratching it keeps the theme and takes you further into the count. Stifling it — staying human-shaped, refusing the dose, sitting the night out — marks Decay (Core p. 136; Brief §4.2). This is the kit-fixed Howler Itch (`build-choices` BC-2).

## Specials

Inherits the Augmentation themebook's five (Core p. 237): **Backstory**, **Formidable Tech**, **More than Human**, **Quick Reboot**, **This is Who I Am**.

Two of them do particular work here and are worth naming for the player: **More than Human** ignores harm of tier 3 or lower while scratching the Itch, which is what a night on the flats actually looks like; and **Quick Reboot** removes two tiers of a malfunction status, which is the answer to *a graft that never healed* giving out at the worst moment.

## Nascent form

**sculpted beast** + *needs the dose to work* is a whole theme on one line: a body that was rebuilt, and a supply it cannot live without. Legal as a replacement theme or as an end-of-session-one nascent theme (Plan A.5).

## Canon and flags

- Canon relied on: Bible §2 werewolf — a mix of bio-sculpt and cybernetics (tags A, D, C), a chemical trigger (B, weakness A), dependence on a supply the character does not control (weakness A); Bible §1 — irreversible (weakness D); Bible §4 themes 1 and 3 — the ladder is your body, owned or free.
- Tag voice checked against `style-guide` §2: two to five words, lower case, no numbers, no game vocabulary, specific rather than broad (Core p. 192). *scent blood through walls* and *outrun a vehicle* are specific; no tag here is a downgrade candidate.
- **Rarity** (Bible §2; Brief §2.6): every tag assumes one expensive irreversible procedure that a person had to be talked into or forced into, plus a standing bill. Nothing in the kit implies a Howler can be caught, spread, or made in bulk.
- **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-46) the kit's display name and every coined tag.
- **[OPEN]** (OQ-3) what Leash actually is for is Meliora's, not this kit's; no tag here names its purpose. **[OPEN]** (OQ-5) no Mythos content, per `overview`.

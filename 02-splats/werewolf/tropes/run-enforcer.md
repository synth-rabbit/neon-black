# Run Enforcer

**Splat:** Howler · **Built toward:** Cyborg

Your pack holds two square kilometres of dead rendering yards, and holding is a job somebody has to do. You settle who owes what, you stand behind the alpha when the vans come, and when a shed gets taken you are the one who goes and takes it back. The work paid for the sculpt and the sculpt pays for the work. You have been told your whole life that you are somebody's dog, and you have never once been told it twice.

## On the sheet

Three fixed themes give one Noise and two Self, so the Essence minimum — at least one Noise theme (`build-choices` BC-1) — is satisfied before the fourth is picked, whichever it is.

- With **A Line on Leash**: two Noise, two Self → **Cyborg** (Core p. 135). The heaviest version, and the one where the count and the shape sit on the same sheet.
- With **Guns & More Guns** or **Survived the Streets**: one Noise, three Self → still **Cyborg**. A Howler who is mostly a person with a job, and a beast one night a week.

No Mythos theme is offered, per `overview` and **[OPEN]** (OQ-5).

Motivation load: one Itch (*Take the dose and hunt as the beast*), one Identity from Street Gang (*Snitches get stitches*, or a pack-specific one), one Identity from Tough as Nails. Acting against the Street Gang Identity and stifling the sculpt's Itch pull in opposite directions, which is the trope's whole engine.

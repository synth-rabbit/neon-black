# Off the Run

**Splat:** Howler · **Built toward:** Cyborg

You left. Maybe the alpha decided you owed more than you did, maybe you decided you were finished being counted, maybe you walked out of the Ledger to find your kennel gone and nobody left in it who remembered you. Either way you are a sculpted body with no pack behind it and a number of doses you can say out loud, and every one of them is one closer to a night you cannot take. You have got very good at arithmetic. You are getting worse at sleeping.

## On the sheet

Two Noise fixed and one Self fixed, so the Essence minimum (`build-choices` BC-1) is satisfied twice over. All three choice kits are Self, so the trope lands on **Cyborg** (Core p. 135) whichever is taken: two Noise, two Self.

- **Safehouse** is the version that survives — a lock, a bolt-hole, a place to come down.
- **Break the System** is the version that goes looking for why the price moves, which is the closest a PC gets to the MC-only half of `overview` on their own initiative.
- **Impressive Physique** is the version that pays for doses with the body in daylight, on the Face, where nobody may know why it works.

No Mythos theme is offered, per `overview` and **[OPEN]** (OQ-5).

Motivation load: two Itches (*Take the dose and hunt as the beast*; *Take more than I need*) against one Identity (*Life is hard – learn to survive*, Core p. 219). Both Itches pull toward spending a supply that is finite, and the Identity is the only thing on the sheet arguing for tomorrow.

## Canon and flags

- Canon relied on: Bible §1 — the sculpt is irreversible, so leaving the pack does not leave the condition; Bible §2 — the dependency is on the Almoners' supply, which the packs mediate, so a Howler outside a pack is a Howler paying retail or worse; Bible §4 theme 3.
- The three fixed kits are three different theme types (Augmentation, Cutting Edge, Troubled Past) and no choice kit repeats one (Core p. 177).
- **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-46) display name. **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-50) book kits as bare slugs in the frontmatter lists, cited with themebook and page in the body.
- **[OPEN]** (OQ-18) how a Howler outside a pack actually buys from the Almoners — direct, through a broker, or not at all — is the syndicate's business model and belongs to WP4. The trope needs only that it is harder and dearer, which Bible §2–3 already gives.
- Book kits in `fixed_kits` / `choice_kits` carry the `book:` prefix and are indexed with page and themebook in [[book-kits-index]] (BC-126); they are offered as printed, never copied in.

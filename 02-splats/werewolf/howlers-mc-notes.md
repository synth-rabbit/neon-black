# Howlers — MC Notes

The MC-only section split out of [[howlers]] by WP8 (BC-176) so that the overview can be handed to a player whole. The text below is the owning package's, moved verbatim; it is not for the session-zero packet ([[session-zero-packet]]).

Canon (Bible §2, §3; [[names]] §2–3): **Meliora Bioworks**, Corp A, the bio specialist of the Big Three, manufactures the compound the street calls Leash, **for a different purpose**. **[OPEN]** (OQ-3) That purpose is unwritten and is WP4's to invent as Corp A's twist; do not fill it here or anywhere in this folder. The **Almoners**, a syndicate entirely separate from the corporations, buy it in bulk and resell it to the packs — through the Marlow Relief Kitchen's back door, the Gullet's collectors, and the Cinder Yards siding ([[marlow-blocks]], [[gullet-market]], [[cinder-yards]]). The alpha of the largest Yards pack is the Alms' counterparty; the alpha deals with the packs.

Three consequences of that chain that the packs cannot see and that everything in this folder is built on:

1. **The dependency is not on a drug. It is on a distributor who does not make the thing.** Whatever the Alms' margin is, it is a margin on someone else's line, and the Alms' leverage over the Run rests on the Run never learning that.
2. **Meliora's purpose is upstream of every pack in Palisade.** A change to that purpose — a reformulation, a discontinuation, a recall, an audit — reaches the cinder flats without anyone at [[meliora-terraces|the Terraces]] intending it or noticing.
3. **The corporations clean up after Howlers** and blame Baselines (Bible §2, §3; the Masquerade model in the Megacity overview, Brief §4.1). The corporation with the most to lose from a public Howler and the corporation whose compound makes Howlers possible are the same corporation.

**[OPEN]** (OQ-20) How old the Run and Leash are is unresolved; [[timeline]] proposes ~30–15 years as BC-25 and nothing here depends on the number.

# Doppels — MC Notes

The MC-only section split out of [[doppels]] by WP8 (BC-176) so that the overview can be handed to a player whole. The text below is the owning package's, moved verbatim; it is not for the session-zero packet ([[session-zero-packet]]).

Facts a Doppel PC does not have, and pointers for the packages downstream. Not for the session-zero packet (Plan WP8).

- **Who owns whom is WP4's.** OQ-30 above is unresolved on purpose. The two Power Sets in this folder are written so that they attach to *any* owner — a corporation's special-projects office, the Almoners, or a private buyer — without needing the answer.
- **The Cutloose refuge is [[foundation-galleries]]' Gallery Nine**, placed hidden by WP1 (BC-22), reached by a route that changes, through the water. Who runs it is WP4's (BC-18). [[the-cells-will-answer]] is written to work whether the cell that answers is Gallery Nine's or a cell nobody has named yet.
- **The Old Sump Stair** connects the Galleries to the Sump behind [[suture-row]]'s lowest surgery — the road to the one surgeon who will cut a tracker out. Perhaps a dozen people know it. [[deadman-rig]]'s power tag *a debt at the Sump* assumes the PC is one of them.
- **An owned Doppel is a Challenge before it is a person.** Corporate special units and syndicate assassins take [[owned-asset]] and, when they are working, [[wearing-your-face]]. The book's neighbours are *Shapechanger* (Mythos, Core p. 331) and *Holographic* (Noise, Core p. 333); both are reusable and both are wrong for a Doppel in a specific way, documented in [[wearing-your-face]].
- **The escapee list** — Corp C's list of Ledger escapees, which the PCs are on — is a *watcher* Challenge assigned to WP5. It is not a retrieval contract and does not interact with [[retrieval-contract]]; a Cutloose PC who was also in the Ledger is on two different people's lists for two different reasons.
- **Open, and not to be filled here:** whether Doppels are grown, converted from adults, or both (OQ-32); where the Tao in the tissue comes from (OQ-31); whether the beacon a Cutloose cut out is ever really dead (OQ-33).

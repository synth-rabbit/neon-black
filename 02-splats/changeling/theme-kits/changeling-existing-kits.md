# Doppel Kits from the Books

Book kits a Doppel PC may take as printed. These are **offered in addition to** the custom kits in this folder, never instead of them (Brief §0.1). Every kit below is used with its printed tags, weaknesses, and motivation; the re-flavor line says what the printed words mean in Palisade and changes nothing mechanical.

Pages are to the *Metro:Otherscape* Core Book.

## The two the Brief names

### Impossibly Good Looks — Augmentation, Noise, Core p. 239

Printed tags: *impossibly good looks*, dazzle and impress, alternate face, distracting appearance, powers of seduction, precise body control, self-suggestion techniques, appreciation for details, on-call plastic surgeon, fashion model, center of attention. Weaknesses: easily damaged, modification pains, needs constant updating, intense jealousy. Itch: **"Be seen by more eyes, appreciated for the work of art that I am."**

**Re-flavor.** This is the Doppel whose work was done for the front of the house — the unit that gets into the party rather than the vault. *alternate face* is a second face grown to spec, not a set of prosthetics. *self-suggestion techniques* is what a fitter teaches so that you believe the person you are wearing before anyone else has to. *on-call plastic surgeon* is your **fitter**: the technician who cut you and keeps you in tune. For an owned Doppel the fitter comes with the unit; for a Cutloose the tag is a lie you keep telling yourself, or the Sump behind [[palisade-player-primer#Suture Row|Suture Row]], and the MC may treat it as such.

**Note on the Itch.** As printed the Itch pulls toward being *seen*, which is the opposite of what most Doppels are for. Two ways to take it, both legitimate: keep it as printed and play a Doppel who was built for visibility and resents the dark; or take the kit-fixed Doppel Itch *"Become someone else."* from [[doppels]] instead. **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-76).

### Hidden Gadgets — Augmentation, Noise, Core p. 239

Printed tags: *hidden gadgets*, scanners and diagnostic tools, secret compartment, knife launcher, painkiller injector, finger lockpick, resourceful, obtain new attachments, hide my secret life, holographic face mask. Weaknesses: unusual weight distribution, many devices to monitor, compartment door jam, can't surprise them twice. Itch: **"Surprise them with a new trick."**

**Re-flavor.** Field issue for a Doppel doing spy work. The compartments are inside the body, closed by skin that seals without a seam — the same tissue trick as the face, put to a duller use. *holographic face mask* is not how a Doppel changes their face; it is the cheap layer they put over the real change when they need to be two people in one evening, and it is the tell a Baseline with a good lens will catch. *hide my secret life* is the whole job.

Pairs with either face kit as a second Noise theme only if the trope allows a second Augmentation theme — it does not, since a PC's four themes take four different theme types (Core p. 177). Take it *instead of* the face kit for a Doppel whose impersonation is equipment-led, or as a replacement theme later.

### Escaped Servitude — Troubled Past, Self, Core p. 218

Printed tags: *escaped servitude*, resilient, bypass security systems, makeshift weapons, laborer's strength, camaraderie with the oppressed, micro-transmitter implant, my old bindings, speak truth to power, see unspoken power structures, liberate others. Weaknesses: bounty hunters, laser fence burns, mistrustful of leaders, being bound. Identity: **"Remain free."**

**Re-flavor.** The Cutloose Self theme, and the closest thing in the books to what an escape costs. *my old bindings* is the switch itself — the thing cut out of your chest, kept in a tin, because throwing it away felt worse than carrying it. *micro-transmitter implant* is the tracker, dead in its socket or not quite. *bounty hunters* are retrieval contractors (**[BUILD CHOICE]**, BC-80). *laser fence burns* re-reads as the burn scar from the surgery, or from the fence at whatever site you left.

This kit also fits a PC who escaped the Ledger rather than a fitting program, so a Doppel who takes it should be clear at the table which servitude it is about. Fixed in [[runaway-face]].

## The Mythos side

Doppels use Tao **indirectly and at a lower level** (Bible §1). That constrains what may be offered here, and the constraint is real: a Doppel with a full studied discipline stops reading as a Doppel and starts reading as a Caster, which the Bible keeps separate.

### Exposure — Core p. 232–235

**Exposure is the right themebook for the Tao half**, and the custom kit [[held-shape]] is what the Brief asks for. The eight printed Exposure kits are all built around a legend — angelic wings, Midas, elemental spirits, warrior ghosts — and none of them describes a body that was worked on at a bench.

**[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** Any printed Exposure kit taken by a Doppel needs two substitutions before it is playable here (Brief §3.2): the **Source** becomes a Tao gateway that is a procedure or a place where Tao is dense, never a legend, an Avatar, or a benefactor; and the **Ritual** becomes a discipline, a price, or a condition, never a return to a shrine or a re-enactment.

Three that survive the substitution:

- **Animate Shadows** (p. 234) — a Doppel whose Tao went sideways into absence rather than shape. The printed Ritual *"I must enter the shadow realm every night"* must be rewritten as a condition — *"I must spend part of every night unseen by anyone"* — or the kit is not usable. **[RULES CONFLICT]** (CR-3): flagged as strained; the shadow realm is a place out of legend and this vault has none.
- **Elemental Body** (p. 234) — with `[element]` read as a material the Doppel's tissue was keyed to at the bench. Ritual *"I must bathe in [element] daily"* is already a condition and needs no rewrite.
- **Speak With Machines** (p. 235) — a Doppel whose Tao half was cut into the interface rather than the face. The printed Ritual *"I must report routinely to [a powerful machine intelligence]"* re-reads, uncomfortably well, as reporting to a handler. Strained: it gives an intelligence a will, which is fine (it is a machine, not Tao), but it makes the Mythos theme about obedience rather than about Tao. Offer it, flag it.

**[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** The remaining five — Angelic Wings, Aura of Authority, Floral Overgrowth, Midas Touch, Warrior's Instincts — cannot be restated under Tao without becoming different kits, and are **not** offered for Doppels.

### Esoterica — Core p. 228–231

Offered with the low-and-indirect constraint attached. A Doppel may take one Esoterica kit; a Doppel with two has become something else and the table should say so out loud.

- **Evil Eye** (p. 230) — *hypnotic gaze*, *hex of vulnerability*: the half-trick a Doppel picks up to make a mark hold still while the face does its work. Ritual as printed, *"I must never stifle my bitterness,"* is a discipline already and needs no rewrite.
- **Fortune Telling** (p. 230) — reading a person well enough to predict them, which is 90% of wearing them. *see their desires*, *symbology*, *follow the signs*. Ritual *"I must follow the signs, wherever they lead"* is a condition; usable as printed.
- **Potion Craft** (p. 231) — the chemistry a change needs to set, kept and mixed by hand. Ritual *"I must experiment with any new ingredient presented to me"* is the book's own example of a workable discipline (Brief §3.2).
- **Warding Signs** (p. 231) — a Cutloose who has learned to make a room hard to find. *circle of protection*, *silent alarm spell*, *barrier ward*; Ritual *"I must maintain the warding symbols around my home or community"* is a discipline and a price, and it reads as Gallery Nine.

**[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** Not offered: **Corpse Animation** and **Spirit Summoning** (both command or bind beings the Bible's cosmology does not contain — Brief §3.1; Companion themes under Tao are constructs, and that is a Caster's business), **Devil's Bargains** (a bargain needs a counterparty with a will, and Tao has none — `style-guide` §6), **Weapon Witching** (its title power has nothing to work on here).

## Canon and flags

- Canon relied on: Bible §1 (Tao older than the mods, interwoven with tech, used by changelings indirectly and at a lower level), Bible §2 changeling (owned; escapees removed switches and trackers), Brief §0.1 (existing *and* custom), Brief §2.5 Changeling (the three kits named), Brief §3.2 (Sources as Tao gateways; Rituals as disciplines).
- **[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** every Exposure and Esoterica kit above; the Source and Ritual substitutions are stated per kit.
- **[RULES CONFLICT]** (CR-3, `conflict-register`) Animate Shadows' shadow realm and Speak With Machines' reporting Ritual are flagged strained rather than rewritten.
- **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-75) *the fitter* / *the fitting room* as the setting's word for the technician and the room; (BC-76) the Impossibly Good Looks Itch option; (BC-80) *retrieval contractor* as the reading of *bounty hunters*.

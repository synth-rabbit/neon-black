# Deadman Rig

**Themebook:** Cutting Edge · **Category:** Noise · **For:** Doppels · **Source:** custom

Nobody sells this. It is built, in the Galleries and in rooms like them, out of salvage and theory and other people's mistakes, by escapees who have exactly one problem and a great deal of time to think about it: the beacon they cut out of themselves may not have been the only one, and the people who put it there have not stopped looking.

A rig is a shroud, a jammer, a spoofer and a battery, sewn through a coat and seated over the scar. It makes you nothing on a network. It makes you loud to anyone watching for holes. It is the difference between being Cutloose and being caught, and every Cutloose who has one has a different opinion about whether it is worth wearing.

**On *switch-popping pulse*.** The rig's ugliest setting. Tuned to trip a kill switch instead of hide a tracker, it is a weapon that works on exactly one kind of person, and every Cutloose who builds one has had the argument about whether to. It is registered as **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-81) because nothing in the Bible says a switch can be triggered from outside; what the Bible says is that the switch exists and that somebody else holds it.

**On *a debt at the Sump*.** The Sump is the lowest surgery on [[palisade-player-primer#Suture Row|Suture Row]], reached through a pharmacy stockroom and a ladder, and the one door on the Row that opens for the Cutloose — the place a tracker comes out. The Old Sump Stair connects it to [[palisade-player-primer#Foundation Galleries|Foundation Galleries]]. Both are WP1's (BC-22 and the Row's place names in `names`).

## Itch

> Go dark — cut one more thread back to my makers.

A gear Itch, in the Cutting Edge register (Core p. 241): an impulse that can only be scratched by using the tech, and indulging it keeps the theme. It is **not** the kit-fixed Itch the five carry — that lives on the face ([[borrowed-face-cutloose]]), because the face is the body and this is equipment. **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-77).

The two pull against each other on purpose, and that is a hard choice the sheet makes on its own: *become someone else* wants you out in the world wearing a face, and *go dark* wants you off every network, which includes the one the face has to pass a scan on.

## Specials

Inherits the Cutting Edge themebook's five (Core p. 241: *Amp Up*, *Modular Design*, *Occult Blueprints*, *Still in Prototype*, *Void the Warranty*), and adds one:

**Counted Dead:** Once per job, when a Challenge would locate you by any means that passes through a network — a beacon, a chip, a camera net, a records query — you may burn a tag from this theme to have the search return you as already accounted for somewhere else. The MC says where; you do not choose. Anyone who goes to that place finds whatever is actually there.

*Note on the themebook's* **Occult Blueprints** *Special (Core p. 241): it lets a Cutting Edge theme take a power tag question from Esoterica or Exposure to represent magical methods in the tech's construction. For a Doppel that is a rig built with Tao worked into it, which is exactly what the Bible means by interweaving Tao and technology (§1). It is legal, it is thematic, and it is* **[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** *wherever it is used: the methods are Tao, not a legend's.*

## Nascent form

**deadman rig** + *jamming draws attention* is a theme: you are invisible, and being invisible is the loudest thing about you.

## Canon and flags

- Canon relied on: Bible §2 changeling (escapees removed their kill switches and trackers; live in constant danger; form groups with other escapees; purely underground), Bible §1 (a mix of corporate, black-market and DIY controls the mod ecosystem — this is the DIY end), Bible §3 (escaped-changeling society is secret and has no political pull), Bible §4 theme 3; Brief §2.1, §2.5, §4.2.
- Rarity (Bible §2): the rig is hand-built by a population measured in dozens and cannot be bought. Nothing here implies a market.
- Tag scope (Core p. 192): all specific. *deadman rig* names the kit of parts; each other tag is one thing that kit does.
- **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-76) Cutting Edge rather than Augmentation for the survival kit — the rig is worn, salvaged and replaceable, which is Cutting Edge's register, and a Doppel's four themes cannot hold two Augmentation themes anyway (Core p. 177); (BC-77) the gear Itch; (BC-78) the kit name; (BC-81) *switch-popping pulse*.
- **[OPEN]** (OQ-33) whether a beacon a Cutloose cut out is ever really dead — the rig exists because nobody down there is sure.

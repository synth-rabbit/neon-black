# Borrowed Face (Cutloose)

**Themebook:** Augmentation · **Category:** Noise · **For:** Doppels · **Source:** custom

The same body. The same ten things it can do. What is gone is the switch, the beacon, the fitter's schedule and the voice on the other end of it — cut out on a table below street level by somebody who charged you everything you had and told you not to come back.

What is left is a body that still needs servicing, a scar you can feel through a shirt, and the certainty that the people who paid for all this work have not stopped looking for it.

## Itch

> Become someone else.

Kit-fixed (`build-choices` BC-2). Unchanged by escaping — cutting the switch out does not cut out what the work made you want.

## Specials

Inherits the Augmentation themebook's five (Core p. 237), and adds **Wear Them Long**, as [[borrowed-face-owned]].

## Nascent form

**borrowed face** + *hunted by my makers* is a theme in two lines: a body that can be anyone, and the people who built it coming to collect. That is the Cutloose in full.

## Canon and flags

- Canon relied on: Bible §2 changeling (escapees removed their kill switches and trackers, live in constant danger, group with other escapees, purely underground), Bible §1 (sculpt and bio-manipulation irreversible; cyborg implants removable), Bible §3 (escaped-changeling society is secret); Brief §2.5 (*the escaped version replaces them with "hunted by my makers"* — used verbatim as weakness D), §4.2.
- Rarity (Bible §2): the kit describes a surgery perhaps a dozen people in Palisade can perform and a maintenance problem with no legal answer.
- **[RULES CONFLICT]** (CR-13) This kit only exists because a kill switch and a tracker can be removed. Bible §1 permits it for cyborg implants generally and Bible §2 requires it for escapees; Brief §2.4 phrases the removability exception as hunter-only.

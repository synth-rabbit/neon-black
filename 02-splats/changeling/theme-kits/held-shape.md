# Held Shape

**Themebook:** Exposure · **Category:** Mythos-OS · **For:** Doppels · **Source:** custom

The tech cuts the face. It cannot make it stay.

Rewritten flesh wants to go back — days, sometimes hours, and then the jaw drifts and the cheek softens and the person you were being is a person nobody recognises. What stops it is Tao, worked into the same tissue on the same table, low and indirect, doing nothing a Caster would call a discipline. It is not a power you learned. It is a property you were given, along with everything else, and the only part of it you control is the conditions you keep it under.

Doppels do not have a word for it. Fitters call it *the set*. Everyone else says the face held, or it didn't.

## Ritual

> I must let each new shape set before I wear it out of the room.

A condition and a price, in the register Brief §3.2 requires: hours you do not have, in a room you had to find, before the face is safe to walk out in. Neglecting it — going out in a change that has not set — is what marks Decay.

**[RULES CONFLICT]** (CR-3, `conflict-register`) Per-kit strain check, as the register asks for. **This Ritual is not strained.** It re-enacts nothing, it asks nothing of Tao, and it gives Tao no will: it is a discipline the Doppel keeps because the physics of their own tissue demand it, the same shape as the book's own *"I must replenish the wand's power"* and *"I must gather obscure ingredients."*

## Tao, and what this kit is not

**[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** The Exposure themebook is built on a Source: a Thin Place, a relic, an Avatar, a benefactor you go back to and recharge from (Core p. 232–233). None of those exist here (Brief §3.1). The Source of this theme is a **Tao gateway that is a procedure** — the bench, the hands, the tissue — and there is nobody to return to. What the themebook calls recharging is, for a Doppel, maintenance of a condition inside their own body.

Two consequences the table should be clear on:

- **Tao here is impersonal and indirect** (`style-guide` §6, Bible §1). The hold does not choose, prefer, want, or refuse. It sets under some conditions and not others, the way a resin does. Nothing in this kit lets a Doppel *ask* Tao for anything, and nothing in it should be spoken as though it could.
- **This is not a Caster's theme.** Bible §1 makes Casters pure users and changelings indirect and low-level users. A Doppel with this theme has a property, not a practice; they cannot teach it, extend it beyond their own tissue except by touching another Doppel's (*hold a shape for someone*), or use it on anything that is not flesh.

**[OPEN]** (OQ-31) Where the Tao in a Doppel's tissue comes from — who supplies it, and whether the same hands supply every Doppel in Palisade — is not stated by the Bible and is not decided here. See [[doppels]].

## Specials

Inherits the Exposure themebook's five (Core p. 233: *Discipline*, *Durable*, *Mythic Residue*, *Secondary Contamination*, *Surge of Power*), and adds one:

**Set It Deeper:** Once per job, when you spend an uninterrupted hour alone in one shape, you may name a condition that will not break the hold — a beating, a scan, a night without sleep. Until the end of the job, statuses representing that condition do not cause your face to slip and cannot be used to reveal you as a Doppel. Anything you did not name still can.

## Nascent form

**held shape** + *someone who knew me before* is a theme on its own: a body that stays where it is put, and one person alive who remembers where that was.

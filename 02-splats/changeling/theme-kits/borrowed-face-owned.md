# Borrowed Face (Owned)

**Themebook:** Augmentation · **Category:** Noise · **For:** Doppels · **Source:** custom

The work was done at the body level and it was not done for you. Bone that moves on command, tissue that holds what it is told, a throat tuned by hand, and — underneath all of it, doing the part the tech cannot — enough Tao worked into the same flesh to keep a face on that would otherwise want to slide off by morning. You are very good at being other people. You were built to be.

Somebody paid for that. There is a switch under your sternum and a beacon somewhere in you that has never once needed your permission, and a handler whose voice you know better than your own, because you have not used your own in a while.

*D reads as ownership rather than fashion, and that is the intent: the backlash of this particular aug is not that strangers stare, it is that everybody who knows what it is knows who signed for it.*

**If the PC cuts the switch out in play, do not replace this theme.** Swap this weakness card for the one in [[borrowed-face-cutloose]]: same ten power tags, four different weaknesses. The mods are irreversible (Bible §1); the leash is not (Bible §2). **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-74.)

## Itch

> Become someone else.

Kit-fixed (`build-choices` BC-2). Indulging it keeps the theme; refusing it marks Decay (Brief §4.2). A Doppel who spends a job as nobody but themselves is a Doppel losing the thing that was done to them.

## Specials

Inherits the Augmentation themebook's five (Core p. 237: *Backstory*, *Formidable Tech*, *More than Human*, *Quick Reboot*, *This is Who I Am*), and adds one:

**Wear Them Long:** When you have worn one person's face for a full scene without being challenged on it, you may write that person's name beside this theme. Once per session, while wearing that face, you may invoke a tag from this theme as directly relevant when it would only be indirectly relevant. You may hold only one name at a time; writing a new one erases the last.

## Nascent form

**borrowed face** + *switch under my sternum* is a whole theme on its own: a body that can be anyone, owned by someone who can stop it. It reads at the table with nothing else on the card, which is what a nascent theme has to do (Plan A.5).

## Canon and flags

- Canon relied on: Bible §2 changeling (Tao and tech at the body level; Face Dancer-style impersonation in some cases; owned, with kill switches and trackers), Bible §1 (irreversibility; cyborg implants removable), Bible §4 theme 3; Brief §2.1 (required Noise primary), §2.5 (kill switch and tracker as weakness tags on the owned version), §4.2.
- Rarity (Bible §2): nothing in this kit is bought or installed by the PC. Every tag describes work that was done *to* a body by people with a fitting room, and two of the four weaknesses name the people who still have the keys. Nobody reading this card would conclude Doppels are common.
- Tag scope (Core p. 192): every power tag is specific. *borrowed face* is a title tag naming the theme, not a broad ability — it says what the body is, and B through J say what it does.
- **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-73) two face kits rather than one kit with a note; (BC-74) identical power tags, swapped weakness set on escape; (BC-75) *fitter* as the setting's word; (BC-78) the kit name.

# Runaway Face

**For:** Doppels · **Built toward:** Cyborg

You cut it out. Somebody below street level opened your chest for everything you had and took the switch and the beacon and told you not to come back, and you walked out of there owning your own body for the first time — with no credentials, no fitter, no unit and no money, into a city where the only people who will help you are other people in the same position, and there are not many of them, and none of them has anything either.

You are still very good at being other people. It is the only marketable thing about you, and using it is how you get found.

## Notes on the kits

*Book kits are named with their page and are not vault files; only the custom kits in this folder are wikilinked. The `fixed_kits` and `choice_kits` lists in this file's frontmatter carry book-kit slugs that have no `.md` behind them — a known shape for WP6 to reconcile.*

*Escaped Servitude*'s printed tags read almost as though they were written for this: *my old bindings* is the switch, kept in a tin because throwing it away felt worse than carrying it; *micro-transmitter implant* is the beacon, dead in its socket or not quite; *bounty hunters* are retrieval contractors. Its Identity is **"Remain free."**

The rebreather is on the Loadout line because the way into [[palisade-player-primer#Foundation Galleries|Foundation Galleries]] goes through water, and the route to Gallery Nine changes.

Of the three choices, **[[held-shape]]** puts the Tao half back on the sheet and shifts the character to Nexus; the Tao is in the tissue whether or not it is a theme, so choosing it is a statement about what the character leans on, not about what is true. **Safehouse** is the tempting one and the dangerous one — a fixed address is how retrieval finds you, and the kit's own weaknesses will say so. **Zeroed Identity** is the character who decided the answer to being hunted was to stop existing on paper.

## On the sheet

- **Essence minimum:** satisfied twice over by [[borrowed-face-cutloose]] and [[deadman-rig]] (Brief §2.2, `build-choices` BC-1).
- **Essence as built:** with Safehouse or Zeroed Identity the balance is Self and Noise only — **Cyborg** (Core p. 135). That is the point of the trope and it says something true: cutting the leash pulls a Doppel back toward being a person. The Cyborg Special (add Self or Noise to resist intangible, unmeasurable effects) is also a working answer to anti-Tao countermeasures, which are the one thing that loosens a hold.
- With [[held-shape]] the balance is Self 1 / Mythos 1 / Noise 2 — **Nexus**.
- **Persistent condition:** Cutloose. [[retrieval-contract]] is the Special this trope is written for; [[the-cells-will-answer]] is the one that makes the underground real at the table. [[the-face-doesnt-come-off]] fits a character who ran while still wearing somebody.
- The motivations pull opposite ways by design: the Itch *Become someone else* on the face wants you out in the world in a face, and the Itch *Go dark — cut one more thread back to my makers* on the rig wants you off every network the face has to pass a scan on. Both are Noise; both mark Decay when refused.

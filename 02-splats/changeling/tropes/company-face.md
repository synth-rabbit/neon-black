# Company Face

**For:** Doppels · **Built toward:** Transhuman

Somebody signed for you, and somebody still does. You are a special unit: a face sent where a team would be noticed, into a boardroom, a research floor, a rival's bed, a syndicate's back office. You have credentials that hold up, a fitter who keeps you in tune, a car that comes when it is called, and a voice on a line that tells you when the job is over. None of it is yours. You have been eleven people this year and you are not certain how many of them you invented.

## Notes on the kits

*Book kits are named with their page and are not vault files; only the custom kits in this folder are wikilinked. The `fixed_kits` and `choice_kits` lists in this file's frontmatter carry book-kit slugs that have no `.md` behind them — a known shape for WP6 to reconcile.*

*Zeroed Identity* is the paperwork half of an impersonation: records, credentials, a person who exists in the systems even when nobody has met them. A face without it gets three hours out of a face; a face with it gets three weeks.

Of the three choices, **Cloaking Jumpsuit** keeps the trope at Transhuman and builds a unit that was never meant to be seen at all. **Corporate Citizenship** and **Science Experiment** both add a Self theme and make the character a person who is owned rather than a thing that is used — and both change the Essence. *Corporate Citizenship*'s printed weaknesses (*monitored by the company*, *corporate logo tattoo*) land harder on somebody who is company property than on somebody who is company staff; *Science Experiment* has brackets for *designed to [choose purpose]* and *programmed to [choose purpose]*, and filling them in is where the player says what their unit is actually for.

## On the sheet

- **Essence minimum:** satisfied by [[borrowed-face-owned]] — at least one Noise theme (Brief §2.2, `build-choices` BC-1).
- **Essence as built:** with Cloaking Jumpsuit, the four themes are Noise, Mythos, Noise, Noise — **Transhuman** (Core p. 135), the Doppel's natural home. The Transhuman Special is the trope's payoff: *once per scene, when you invoke both mythical and technological tags in the same action, trade a miss (6-) for a mixed hit (7-9)*. The mods do the face; the hold keeps it on; one action.
- With Corporate Citizenship or Science Experiment the balance is Self 1 / Mythos 1 / Noise 2 — **Nexus**. Both are legitimate and neither is a lesser build; Nexus is what a Doppel who still has a life outside the work looks like.
- **Persistent condition:** owned. [[switch-and-handler]] is the Special this trope is written for. If the character cuts the switch out in play, swap the weakness card on the face kit for [[borrowed-face-cutloose]]'s and swap the Special for [[retrieval-contract]].
- The two motivations to watch: the Itch *Become someone else* on the face, and the Ritual *I must let each new shape set before I wear it out of the room* on the hold. They collide the moment a job is in a hurry.

## Canon and flags

- Canon relied on: Bible §2 changeling (corporate special units, syndicate assassins, spies; owned, with kill switches and trackers), Bible §3 (the Big Three, the upstart, the syndicate — any of which may be the owner); Brief §2.1, §2.2, §2.5(c).
- **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-78) the trope name; (BC-82) building the owned trope toward Transhuman and the Cutloose trope toward Cyborg.
- **[OPEN]** (OQ-30) who the unit belongs to is not decided here. The trope works for a corporation's special-projects office, a syndicate, or a private buyer without changing a tag; the player and the MC name it at the table, or wait for WP4.
- Book kits in `fixed_kits` / `choice_kits` carry the `book:` prefix and are indexed with page and themebook in [[book-kits-index]] (BC-126); they are offered as printed, never copied in.

# Doppels

![[assets/splats/doppels.png]]

*What the city calls them: **Doppels**. Nobody says it to their face, because nobody knows which face that is.*

## What a Doppel is

A Doppel is a body that was rewritten to be somebody else's.

The work is done at the body level and it is done twice over. First the tech: cybernetics threaded through the face and throat, gene work, bio-manipulation, *Red Rising* sculpt taken further than the Crest has ever taken it — bone that moves, tissue that can be told what to be, a voice box that can be tuned. Then the Tao: worked into the same tissue, at low level and indirectly, doing the one thing the tech cannot. Flesh that has been rewritten wants to go back. Tao is what makes the new shape hold.

That is the whole trick, and it is why Doppels are rare even by the standards of the five. Sculpt is expensive. Tao is rarer than sculpt. A body that has both is not something a person walks into a clinic on [[palisade-player-primer#Corbel Gallery|Corbel Gallery]] and buys.

Doppels are made to be used. The Bible's roles are the roles: **corporate special units, syndicate assassins, spies** — the work you send a face to do instead of a team. And the people who commissioned that work did not commission a colleague. A Doppel is **owned**: built with a kill switch and a tracker, held by whoever paid, answerable to a handler.

Some got out. They cut the switch and the tracker out of themselves, or found somebody who would, and now they live in constant danger for having done it. They are the **Cutloose**, and they group with other escapees, underground, with no pull anywhere. That is the whole of their society. There is nothing above it.

## Two ways to play

Every Doppel PC picks one of these at character creation, and it is a fact about the character before it is a mechanic.

**Owned.** You are still somebody's. There is a switch under your sternum and a tracker that reports where you are. You have a handler, a unit, credentials that work, a fitter who keeps your face in tune, and a car that comes when it is called. None of it is yours. You are in the Ledger's escape convoy or on the crew's payroll because your orders currently allow it, or because nobody has looked at your feed today. The clock on that is not something you control.

**Cutloose.** You cut it out. There is scar tissue where the switch was and a dead socket where the beacon sat, and no fitter, and no credentials, and nobody to call — except the cells, who will answer, and count it. The people who made you have not written you off; nobody writes off that much money. Somewhere there is a contract with your face on it.

A PC can cross from the first to the second in play. Crossing the other way is not a thing that happens: nobody puts a switch back into a Doppel they cannot trust with one.

## Who makes Doppels

**[OPEN]** (OQ-30) The Bible names what Doppels are *for* — corporate special units, syndicate assassins, spies — and never names who builds them. This vault does not decide it.

What a Doppel knows from inside is small and specific: your fitter's hands, the room you were fitted in, your unit's designation, your handler's voice, and the fact that all of it is billed to somebody you have never met. Papers go up; nothing comes down with a name on it. Ask three Doppels who owns them and you get three different answers, all of them second-hand.

Registered proposal, for the GM to adopt, replace, or leave open — **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-79):

- **Primary proposal — commissioned, not manufactured.** No single Key Player makes Doppels. The body work is contracted through the cybernetics and bio lines that already exist in Palisade; the Tao half is bought from whoever will sell it; the buyer is a corporation's special-projects office, a syndicate, or a person with enough money. This keeps the Bible's plural roles intact and leaves every maker's identity to WP4.
- **Alternate A — one program.** One of the Big Three runs the only fitting program in the city and licenses units out. Doppels are then a corporate product with a supply chain, the way Leash is.
- **Alternate B — the Tao half is the bottleneck.** Nobody makes a Doppel without the Wuji, which would make every Doppel in Palisade a Tao-society artifact and every owner a Wuji customer. This is the largest of the three and touches `names` §2 and the Key Player twists; it is the GM's call, not this package's.

Nothing in the rest of this folder depends on which of the three is true.

## Mapping

Hybrid mapping, per the Brief's model (Brief §2.1) — what a Doppel is lives in theme kits, tropes, Specials and Essence guidance, not in a category.

| | Themebook | Category | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Required primary** | **Augmentation** | **Noise** | The body-level mods. Canon: "a mix of Tao and tech at the body level" (Bible §2). |
| **Optional secondary** | **Exposure** (preferred) or **Esoterica** | **Mythos-OS** | The Tao half. Canon: Doppels use Tao "indirectly or at a lower level" (Bible §1). |

**Exposure over Esoterica.** Exposure is a body that Tao happened to; Esoterica is a discipline a person studied. A Doppel's Tao is in their tissue, put there by someone else, and they did not train for it — so Exposure is the fit, and [[held-shape]] is the kit. Esoterica remains available for a Doppel who went and *learned* something after the fact, but a Doppel with a full studied discipline is drifting into what a Caster is, and the Bible keeps those separate: Casters are pure users, Doppels are indirect and low-level. See [[changeling-existing-kits]] for the book's Esoterica kits and the constraint on offering them.

**[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** The Exposure themebook assumes a Source — a Thin Place, a relic, an Avatar, a benefactor to return to (Core p. 232–233). There are none. Under Brief §3.2 the Source here is a **Tao gateway that is a procedure**: the bench, the tissue, the hold. The Doppel has nobody to go back to and thank. The Ritual is therefore self-maintenance, not a return. See [[held-shape]] and the register row CR-12.

## Essence minimum

**At least one Noise theme.** That is option (ii) *Minimum* from Brief §2.2, recorded as `build-choices` BC-1. In practice a Doppel PC's Noise theme is the face — [[borrowed-face-owned]] or [[borrowed-face-cutloose]] — and nothing else in the four themes is constrained.

**The Doppel's natural Essence is Transhuman** (Core p. 135): Mythos and Noise, no Self. The Transhuman Special reads as though it were written for the Doppel — *once per scene, when you invoke both mythical and technological tags in the same action, you can trade a miss (6 or less) for a mixed hit (7–9)*. A Doppel's signature action is exactly that: the mods do the face, and the hold keeps it on, in one move. [[company-face]] is built to land there.

Other Essences a Doppel PC will realistically reach:

- **Nexus** (all three) — the common starting shape for a Doppel who keeps a Self theme, and the default for [[runaway-face]] if the Tao half is taken.
- **Cyborg** (Self and Noise) — a Cutloose who leans on who they have become instead of what was done to them. [[runaway-face]] lands here by default. The Cyborg Special (resist intangible effects) is a real answer to anti-Tao countermeasures.
- **Singularity** (only Noise) — a Doppel who is nothing but the work. Available; nothing forbids it.
- **Avatar / Conduit** (only Mythos) — unreachable while the Essence minimum stands, since a Doppel needs the Noise theme. Noted for completeness; see `conflict-register` CR-8.

## Persistence

**The ownership status is a narrative fact.** It does not live on a theme card and it does not go away when a theme does (Brief §2.4, `conflict-register` CR-7). A Doppel whose face theme Decays to replacement is still a Doppel: the sculpt is still in the bone, the Tao is still in the tissue, and somebody either still owns them or still wants them back.

**The mods are irreversible.** Bible §1: sculpting and bio-manipulation cannot be undone. The face is permanent whether or not it is a theme.

**The switch and the tracker are not.** Bible §1's one exception is that cyborg implants can be removed, and Bible §2 requires it — the Cutloose exist because escapees removed their kill switches and trackers. Removing them is surgery, it leaves scar tissue, and it is the single most dangerous thing a Doppel can do. **[RULES CONFLICT]** (CR-13) The Brief states the removability exception as hunter-specific (§2.4); the Bible states it generally and requires it for Doppels. The Bible wins (Brief preamble). Options are laid out in the register row.

**On the sheet**, the persistence is carried by the PC Specials, which live on the character card and survive theme replacement (Tokyo p. 72):

- [[switch-and-handler]] — owned: what the leash gives you and what it costs.
- [[retrieval-contract]] — Cutloose: the contract with your face on it.
- [[the-cells-will-answer]] — Cutloose: what the network can actually reach, and what it charges.
- [[the-face-doesnt-come-off]] — either: the one you wore too long.

Crossing from owned to Cutloose in play is written into [[switch-and-handler]]: cutting the switch out removes that Special and replaces it with [[retrieval-contract]], and the PC marks an Evolution point. Nothing else on the sheet changes except the weakness set on the face kit — see below.

## Motivation

**Kit-fixed Itch: "Become someone else."** (`build-choices` BC-2, Brief §2.5.)

It is an Itch and it works the way Itches work (Brief §4.2, `style-guide` §3): indulging it *keeps* the theme, and refusing it marks Decay. A Doppel who stops wearing other people is a Doppel losing the thing that was done to them — which is the closest this setting gets to recovery, and it costs a theme.

Both face kits carry it verbatim. [[deadman-rig]] carries its own gear Itch instead, because it is equipment, not the body — **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-77).

## How a Doppel reads on the sheet

A typical starting Doppel:

| Theme | Themebook | Category | Motivation |
|---|---|---|---|
| The face | Augmentation | Noise | Itch — *Become someone else.* |
| The hold | Exposure | Mythos-OS | Ritual — a condition the change is kept under |
| One more Noise or Mythos theme, or a Self theme | — | — | — |
| The fourth, from the trope's choice list | — | — | — |

Read the four and you can tell which kind of Doppel is at the table without asking. Four Mythos/Noise themes is a Transhuman: a made thing that never stopped being one. A Self theme in the mix — a Troubled Past that says *Remain free*, an Affiliation with a cell — is somebody who is turning back into a person, and paying for it in Power.

The weakness tags are where owned and Cutloose actually differ. Same ten power tags, different four weaknesses: the switch, the tracker, the handler and the slip on one card; the scar, the missing fitter, the slip and *hunted by my makers* on the other. When a PC escapes in play, swap the card. **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-74.)

## Rarity

Doppels are rare and secret (Bible §2, §3). The Masquerade covers the Cutloose specifically — escaped-changeling society is one of the four secret things — and it covers owned Doppels by a different route: their owners do the hiding, because a unit that is known is a unit that is worthless.

The public knows extreme modding exists. The public knows about sculpted entertainers on [[palisade-player-primer#Corbel Gallery|Corbel Gallery]] and arena fighters at the Bowl. The public does not know that a face can be worn, and no kit in this folder implies otherwise: nothing here is bought over a counter, nothing here is advertised, and every custom kit's weakness set names somebody who is looking.

# Retrieval Contract

**For:** Doppels · **Prerequisite:** You were owned, you had the switch and the tracker cut out, and your makers have not written you off.

Your makers do not report escapes. They contract them.

Once per job, when you learn that someone is looking for you, you may ask the MC one question about the contractor and get a true answer, as in Discover. Whenever you use your face in front of something that can report it — a scan you did not clear, a camera net, a person who knew the face you are wearing — the MC may, as a Consequence, advance the contract: take **traced-1**, which stacks. At **traced-3**, a retrieval team is working your district and the MC may Present a New Challenge. A downtime spent going to ground removes one tier.

This is what "they live in constant danger for having done so" costs on a character card. The Special does not tell you to hide; it prices every use of the one thing you are good at.

## Persistence

Survives theme replacement (Brief §2.4, `conflict-register` CR-7). Replacing the face theme does not close the contract — the contract is for a body, and the body is irreversible (Bible §1).

Removed only for narrative reasons: the contract is bought out, closed, or the party holding it stops existing. Mark an Evolution point (Tokyo p. 72).

## Canon and flags

- The persistent condition: **escaped, and still wanted**. Bible §2 — escapees removed their kill switches and trackers and live in constant danger for having done so. Bible §4 theme 3.
- **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-80) *retrieval contractor* — that makers pay third parties rather than fielding their own recovery teams. The Bible gives constant danger and does not say who supplies it; contracting keeps the danger real without naming a maker, which OQ-30 leaves open. If the GM decides makers use their own units instead, only the word changes.
- **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-84) *traced* as a stacking status with a downtime clear.
- **[OPEN]** (OQ-30) who holds the contract; (OQ-33) whether the beacon a PC cut out is genuinely dead, which is why the MC gets the Consequence rather than the player getting a guarantee.
- Distinct from Corp C's escapee list (WP5): a Cutloose who was also in the Ledger is on two lists, kept by two people, for two different reasons, and only one of them is paying anybody.

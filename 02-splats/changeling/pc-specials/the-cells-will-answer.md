# The Cells Will Answer

**For:** Doppels · **Prerequisite:** You are Cutloose, and a cell has taken you in — you have been to a refuge and been let out of it again.

Once per job, in a district where the Cutloose have anyone at all, you may call on the cells. Gain three story tags for the scene from among *a route nobody maps*, *a bed and a locked door*, *someone who will wear your face for an hour*, *a name that is currently unused*, and *a surgeon who owes the cell*.

The cells count everything. The second time you use this Special in a single job, and every time after, you also take **in-debt-to-the-cell-2**, which hinders any action that would put a Cutloose at risk. It clears when you have done the cell a service.

This is the entire political weight of escaped-changeling society: a route, a bed, an hour of somebody else's face, and a ledger. There is nothing above it and nobody to appeal to.

## Persistence

Survives theme replacement (Brief §2.4, `conflict-register` CR-7). What you are to the cells is not a theme; it is a debt in somebody's head.

Removed only for narrative reasons — the cell is destroyed, moves, or puts you out. Mark an Evolution point (Tokyo p. 72).

## Canon and flags

- The persistent condition: **the network, and its price**. Bible §2 — escapees form groups with other escapees. Bible §3 — escaped-changeling society is purely underground with no political pull. Bible §4 theme 3.
- The refuge on the map is Gallery Nine in [[palisade-player-primer#Foundation Galleries|Foundation Galleries]], placed hidden by WP1; the route to it changes and goes through water. This Special deliberately does not require that specific cell: "a district where the Cutloose have anyone at all" works for a cell WP4 has not written yet.
- Nothing here gives the Cutloose reach they do not have. Three story tags, a scene long, in a district where they are already present, and a debt that hinders the PC afterwards. No money, no muscle, no standing, no favours from anyone above ground.
- Modelled on Tokyo's *Friends in the Bureau* (Tokyo p. 73), scaled down from four tags to three because the granting body is a few dozen hunted people rather than a government bureau.
- **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-85) the five story tags the cells can supply, and the debt clause.
- **[OPEN]** who runs Gallery Nine and whether the cells in different districts are one network or several — WP4's (the Cutloose, `build-choices` BC-18).

# Wearing Your Face

**Applies to:** Any Challenge representing a Doppel operating under somebody else's face — a corporate special unit on a job, a syndicate's assassin, or a Cutloose passing · **Category:** Noise · **For:** Doppels

## Tags & statuses

somebody else's face, voice to match, no seams

## Specials

**Nobody Questions a Familiar Face:** When this Challenge first appears in a scene, the MC declares who the PCs believe it is. Until somebody takes an action to establish otherwise, actions against it that depend on knowing who it really is are hindered.

**Second Face:** Once per job, during Establish, the Challenge drops the face it is wearing and appears as somebody else already in the scene's cast. Remove every status on it representing being recognised, followed, marked, or identified.

**The Change Holds:** Tao worked into the tissue keeps the shape set. At the end of each scene, reduce by one tier any status on this Challenge that would reveal it as something other than the person it appears to be — swelling, a torn face, a failed scan. Inside an anti-Tao field this Special does not function.

## Threats / Consequences

› Answer to a name that is not theirs, without a beat of hesitation
» Walk through a checkpoint, a door, or a conversation on somebody else's credentials (Deny Them Something They Want)
» Get a PC to say something in front of the wrong person (*compromised-2*)

› Watch somebody in the room a beat too long
» Take that person's face before the next scene (create a story tag *wearing their face*)
» Have the person they copied turn up somewhere they cannot have been (Escalate the Situation)

› Let the face slip for half a second — a jaw that sits wrong, a voice that drops
» Strike from a position of trust, before anyone reads the room (*knifed-3*)
» Leave the scene wearing the face of someone the crew trusts (Present a New Challenge)

## Against the book's neighbours

Two printed Power Sets sit next to this one and neither replaces it (Brief §0.1 — offer both):

- ***Shapechanger*** (Mythos, Core p. 331) is the closer fit mechanically and the wrong one narratively: it changes into a beast or a monster and its *Beast Mode* grants *wild endurance* and *acute senses*. A Doppel does not become a monster; it becomes an accountant, convincingly, for three weeks. Reuse *Shapechanger* for the Howlers.
- ***Holographic*** (Noise, Core p. 333) is the wrong one both ways: it is an illusion that cannot touch anything and dies with its projector. A Doppel is present, solid, and can pick up a cup. The one place *Holographic* is right is the cheap layer over a real change — the *holographic face mask* of Hidden Gadgets (Core p. 239) — and a Challenge running both is a Doppel being two people in one evening.

## Canon and flags

- Splat canon (Plan A.6): this overlay carries only what the face does. **It says nothing about ownership** — that is [[owned-asset]], and an owned Doppel Challenge takes both. A Doppel Challenge with this Power Set and *not* [[owned-asset]] is Cutloose, and that must be a deliberate MC choice, not an oversight.
- **[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** *The Change Holds* is the Tao half at Challenge scale. Tao here is impersonal and does nothing but hold a shape; the anti-Tao clause matches the weakness *loosened by anti-Tao fields* on [[held-shape]] and the anti-Tao countermeasures the Bible gives the corporations (§3).
- Rarity and the Masquerade (Bible §2, §3): the Specials assume the crew does not know what they are looking at. Confirming a face is worn always costs an action.
- **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-78) the Power Set name; (BC-87) *Second Face* as a once-per-job escape rather than an at-will ability, so that a Doppel Challenge cannot simply out-face a crew indefinitely.

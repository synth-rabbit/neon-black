# Book Power Sets for Caster NPCs

Reused as printed wherever they fit (Plan A.1 rule 1), with a Tao re-flavour line each. The custom overlays [[wuji-operative]], [[unaffiliated-practitioner]] and [[anti-tao-countermeasure]] are additions, not replacements. **MC only.**

| Power Set | Category | Page | Reuse for | Tao re-flavour `[TAO-REINTERPRETED]` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Arcane** | Mythos | 330 | Any versatile practitioner; the default overlay for a house instructor or a well-supplied practitioner | Usable almost as written: "access to a Source, usually an Esoteric practice, which allows them to create magical effects on the fly" needs only *Source* → **gateway** and *magical effects* → **workings**. Drop "these effects should always match the theme and sensibilities of the Source's Mythos" and replace with **the effects should always match the one discipline this practitioner keeps** — a practice is narrow, and that narrowness is where the drama is. *Counterspell* stays; *Spellcraft*'s three story tags stay. |
| **Telekinetic** | Mythos | 331 | A practitioner whose discipline is holding and moving; the closest printed match to [[aldine-forms]] | No legend content to strip at all — "some variation of the most common magical power: moving things from a distance." Read *Everything Within Reach* as the practitioner using whatever the room is full of. Pair with, or use instead of, [[wuji-operative]]'s *Arrest* for a lower-Scale practitioner. |
| **Medium** | Mythos | 330 | `[OPEN]` — see below | "The power to call up the dead" has nothing in canon to call up. Two usable reads, neither adopted: **(a) residue** — the discipline reads what a place or an object retains of what was done in it, and *Knowledge From Beyond the Grave* becomes the practitioner reading a PC's own history off them; **(b) made beings** — what is raised is not a dead person but a form stood up in a dead person's shape, i.e. [[standing-form]] with an ugly application. *Raise a ghost… (Present a New Challenge: Ghost, p. 317)* requires the Source-Touched & Conjurations Challenge to be re-flavoured first (Brief §3.2). **[OPEN]** (OQ-29). |
| **Lucky** | Mythos | 330 | A practitioner whose discipline is smoothing outcomes | Hardest of the four to keep honest: nothing arranges anything. Read as **a working spent to move one small thing at a time**, and let the *out of luck* Limit be the working running out. `[BUILD CHOICE]` (BC-71). |
| **Nature Kissed** | Mythos | 330 | A practitioner or a Tao-touched person whose working runs to one material | "Touched by a Source of nature" → **worked, or exposed to a dense place, until one material answers**. The element list is usable unchanged. |
| **Spirit-Possessed** | Mythos | 331 | Not recommended | Requires a Ghost with a will. Under canon the nearest thing is a made being that has been put inside a body, which is not what the Power Set describes. Use [[standing-form]]'s `[RULES CONFLICT]` (CR-10) before reaching for this. |
| **Shapechanger** | Mythos | 331 | Not a Caster overlay | Belongs to `02-splats/werewolf/` and `changeling/`. Listed here only so nobody re-flavours it as a Tao discipline by default. |
| **Corporate Sponsored** | Self | 328 | A corporation's rebel practitioner | No re-flavour needed. Pair with [[unaffiliated-practitioner]]: the branding, the expense account, and a capability the employer paid for and cannot replicate. |
| **Connected & Protected** | Self | 328 | A house member with a respectable position | No re-flavour needed, and it is the most accurate thing in the book about what membership actually buys on [[chancery-hill]]. Pair with [[wuji-operative]]. |
| **In the Know** | Self | 329 | A fixer who knows which of two buyers pays for worked goods | No re-flavour needed. The rumour-mill limit is exactly right: this Challenge has heard *the Quiet Hand* said and knows nothing behind it. |
| **Heavily Armed** | Self | 328 | A countermeasure escort | Note its printed story-tag example includes *Source-blessed bullets*; in this setting that reads as **worked rounds**, which the escort bought and does not understand ([[worked-sidearm]]). |

## Canon and flags

- **[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** (CR-1) every Mythos Power Set above; *Source* → gateway, *Mythos* → the practice that made the working, *Conjurations* → made beings.
- **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-71) The specific re-flavour of *Arcane*, *Lucky*, and *Heavily Armed*'s Source-blessed bullets.
- **[OPEN]** (OQ-29) Whether anything at all persists of a person after death in Palisade, which is what *Medium* and the Ghost Challenge (Core p. 317) would require. Canon says nothing; the vault asserts nothing.
- Splat canon (Plan A.6): no overlay applied to a Caster NPC may give Tao intent, and none of these does once the substitutions above are made.

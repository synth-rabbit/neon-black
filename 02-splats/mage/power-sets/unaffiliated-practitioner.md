# Unaffiliated Practitioner

**Applies to:** Any Challenge representing a practitioner who is not the house's — a corporation's rebel Tao user, a street practitioner who taught themselves, a former initiate who walked · **Category:** Mythos · **Setting name:** Caster

**MC only.** Canon gives the other corporations "a few rebel Tao users" and gives the society a habit of finding talent, which together imply talent that got away, got bought first, or was never found (Bible §2). This overlay is all three: narrow, self-taught or half-taught, dangerous in one direction and defenceless in every other. Where [[wuji-operative]] is unhurried and leaves nothing behind, this Challenge is exhausted, improvises, and is trying to get out of the room.

## Tags & statuses

one trick, done well; nothing to fall back on

## Specials

**Only One Trick:** This Challenge's practice does exactly one thing, named when the overlay is applied — stopping, opening, hiding, burning, reading, holding. When doing that one thing, it ignores a Scale gap of 1. It has no answer for anything else.

**Burns Themselves Out:** The second time this Challenge uses its practice in a scene, it takes *drained-3*. The third time, it takes *drained-3* again and the practice is unavailable until the end of the next downtime.

**Improvised Working:** Once per scene, this Challenge can burn a mundane item in the scene — a lighter, a length of cable, a handful of chalk, a spent casing — to create a story tag representing a working made out of it on the spot. The item is destroyed.

## Threats / Consequences

› Mutters, works their hands, and does not look at what they are doing.
» Do their one thing, hard and without control (an appropriate tier-3 status to a target, or a story tag holding a door, a road, or a room)
» Do their one thing to the wrong target as well (the same status at one tier lower to a bystander, an ally, or the Challenge itself)

› Checks the door, the window, and the door again.
» Break contact and go, having already worked the exit (Deny Them Something They Want)
» Trade: name what they want in exchange for the trick — supplies, a room, or silence (create a story tag recording the bargain)

› Says something about the crew that is far too accurate.
» Read a working, an implant, or a piece of gear a PC is carrying and say out loud what it does (*exposed-2*; Escalate the Situation if that was a secret)

## Limits

Suggested on the host Challenge: **hurt or subdue 2** (nothing about this practice makes a body harder to break), **convince 4** when the offer is materials, a bench, or a place to sleep, and **convince 2** when it is money.

## Corp-side variant

A rebel practitioner inside a corporation keeps this overlay and gains the Self Power Set ***Corporate Sponsored*** (Core p. 328) — the branded gear, the expense account, and the visible logo. The pairing is the point: the corporation pays for a capability it cannot manufacture, and does not understand what it has bought. **[OPEN]** (OQ-24) How the employer manages, restrains, or audits such a person is a Key Player fact for WP4.

## Canon and flags

- Splat canon this overlay obeys (Plan A.6): pure Tao use augmented by tech, never a mod (Bible §2); all Tao use is rare, and this Challenge is a person nobody has replaced (Bible §2 rarity). **Tao is given no intent anywhere in this file** — everything the Challenge does costs it something, and nothing helps it.
- **[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** (CR-1) Stands in the slot of the book's *Arcane* Power Set (Core p. 330) with its "access to a Source" read as a gateway; *Arcane*'s *Spellcraft* (three story tags during Establish) is deliberately **not** copied, because a narrow self-taught practice is the opposite of a versatile one.
- **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-69) One trick, done well, at a cost that compounds within the scene, as the shape of every practitioner the house did not train.
- **[OPEN]** (OQ-28) What the house does when it finds one.

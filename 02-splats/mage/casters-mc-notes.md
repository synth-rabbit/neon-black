# Casters — MC Notes

The MC-only section split out of [[casters]] by WP8 (BC-176) so that the overview can be handed to a player whole. The text below is the owning package's, moved verbatim; it is not for the session-zero packet ([[session-zero-packet]]).

**Do not hand this section to players.** It states two facts a starting Caster of any position cannot know, and one of them is a Key Player's core secret. **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-56) The rest of this file is player-safe; WP8's session-zero packet should link the file only if this section is split out or the packet notes the cut ([[README]] "Player-safe vs MC-only").

## Orison

The Wuji control **[[corp-b|Orison Defense Systems]]**, the weapons corporation on [[orison-reach]] (Bible §2, §3; [[names]] §2–§3). Nothing in the district shows it and nothing in any player-facing Caster file states it. Consequences that matter for Caster characters:

- A placed initiate can be moved into an Orison job — a range crew, a clerkship, a barracks flat inside the inner wall — without ever being told the two things are connected, and will read the transfer as the House being kind.
- Orison's product line is the one place in Palisade where a Tao working and a manufactured object are made in the same building. Anything a Caster PC recognises as *worked* on an Orison item is a genuine lead and is worth exactly what leads are worth on the Wall.
- The Wuji's own security problem is that a practitioner who was never found — the third position in §2 — is evidence the Masquerade leaks. Unfound practitioners get found.

## Anti-Tao countermeasures

Canon: the other corporations "have a few rebel Tao users and **anti-Tao solutions** of their own" (Bible §2). What that means at the table:

- The countermeasure is **Noise, not Mythos**. Nobody outside the practice can channel Tao to block Tao; what [[corp-a|Meliora Bioworks]] and [[corp-c|AP&I]] field is engineering — grounding, shielding, field-nulling, and above all **detection**, because a corporation's first problem is not stopping a working but knowing one happened.
- Mechanically it is the Power Set [[anti-tao-countermeasure]], laid over a countermeasure team, a hardened room, or a fielded device. The cross-cutting Challenge profile is [[anti-tao-countermeasure-challenge]] (WP5), which applies the overlay rather than restating it (BC-134).
- The persistent-condition Special [[countermeasure-scar]] is what a Caster carries after surviving one.
- **[OPEN]** (OQ-24) How well countermeasures actually work, who sells them, and whether either corporation can *reproduce* a working rather than merely stop one, are Key Player facts and belong to WP4.

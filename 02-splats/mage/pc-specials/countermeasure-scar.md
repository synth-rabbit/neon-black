# Countermeasure Scar

**Setting name:** Caster · **Prerequisite:** You were caught inside a working anti-Tao countermeasure — a grounded room, a fielded null, a sweep that found you — and you kept practising afterward.

Whenever a Consequence would **ground, null, or suppress your Mythos tags**, reduce its tier by 1 (minimum 1). You have already had this done to you properly, and the second time is never as bad as the first.

You permanently carry the story tag **on file**, which anyone running a countermeasure sweep, a corporate security check, or an equipment audit may invoke against you. It cannot be burned and it comes back after every downtime. Somewhere there is a reading with your name attached to it, and the corporation that took it does not throw readings away.

## Persistence

Survives theme replacement (Brief §2.4). The resistance and the record are both facts about what happened to this person, not about what they can currently do; a Caster who stops practising entirely keeps *on file* and stops having Mythos tags for the first clause to protect ([[casters]] §5). Removed only for narrative reasons — the record that holds the signature is destroyed or corrupted beyond use — and marking an Evolution point (Tokyo p. 72).

## Canon and flags

- The persistent condition this carries: **having been detected**. Canon gives the other corporations "a few rebel Tao users and anti-Tao solutions of their own" (Bible §2), and the Masquerade holds because incidents get cleaned up rather than because nothing happens (Brief §4.1). A practitioner who has been read once is in a file, and files on the Wall get sold.
- Built on the Tokyo Megacity Special pattern (Tokyo p. 72–74) — nearest to *Nanotech Residue* (Tokyo p. 73) in shape: something was done to this body, and it now both protects and marks.
- Mechanically the mirror of the `anti-tao-countermeasure` Power Set, which is what did this.
- **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-67) That countermeasures leave a durable **record** rather than a durable injury, and that surviving one confers partial resistance. Canon states countermeasures exist and nothing about what they do to a person.
- **[OPEN]** (OQ-24) How far countermeasures actually go — who sells them, how well they work, and whether either corporation can reproduce a working rather than merely stop one — is a Key Player fact belonging to WP4.

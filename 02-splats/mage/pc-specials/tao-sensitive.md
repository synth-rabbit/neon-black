# Tao-Sensitive

**Setting name:** Caster · **Prerequisite:** You have kept a Tao discipline across at least one full job, and you have handled or stood in at least one working that was not your own.

Whenever you encounter a **working** for the first time — a worked item, a practice in use, a place where Tao runs dense, a made being — you immediately learn **one useful detail about how it was done**: what it does, what it costs, what condition it needs, or what it was made from. When you later encounter anything worked the same way, you immediately recognize it as the same hand or the same method.

You learn nothing about anyone's intentions, because there are none to learn. A working tells you about the person who made it, in the way a weld tells you about a welder.

## `[TAO-REINTERPRETED]`

**[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** (CR-1) This adapts the Evolution Special ***Source-Sensitive*** (Core p. 141), which reads: *"Whenever you encounter a Source for the first time, you immediately learn one useful detail about its Mythos. When you encounter anything related to that Mythos later, you immediately recognize it."*

Three changes, all forced by canon (Brief §3.1–3.2):

1. **Source → working.** A Source is a gateway; the four gateways in this setting are practice, item, place, being (`pillars`). The Special triggers on any of them.
2. **"a detail about its Mythos" → a detail about how it was done.** There is no Mythos and nothing behind the working to learn about. What is legible is *method*: cost, condition, materials, effect, hand.
3. **"anything related to that Mythos" → anything worked the same way.** Recognition runs along a method, not along a legend. Two shells from the same bench match. A shell and a ward from the same practitioner match. A shell and a ward from different practitioners doing the same thing do not.

Offered as a **PC Special** on the Tokyo pattern (Tokyo p. 72–74) rather than as the book's Evolution Special, so a Caster can carry it early and keep it through theme replacement. **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-66) — the book's *Source-Sensitive* remains available as printed at Evolution, and a character may hold both to no additional effect.

## Persistence

Survives theme replacement (Brief §2.4). The eye is training, not a theme: a Caster who lets every discipline lapse still sees what a working left behind, which is the second of the two things that stick to a Caster who has stopped practising ([[casters]] §5). Removed only for narrative reasons — an injury, a countermeasure, or years without practice that dull it — and marking an Evolution point (Tokyo p. 72).

## Canon and flags

- The persistent condition this carries: **a trained eye**. Casters are pure Tao users (Bible §2); what a practitioner cannot stop being is somebody who has looked at workings for years. This is also the Caster's practical answer to the Masquerade: the thing they notice is invisible to everyone around them, and saying so out loud is the fastest way to be noticed back.
- **[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** (CR-1) as set out above.
- **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-66) Offering *Source-Sensitive* as a PC Special rather than only at Evolution; and the recognition rule running along method rather than maker.
- **[OPEN]** (OQ-14) Whether places where Tao runs dense exist at all is unresolved; this Special names them as one of four triggers without asserting that Palisade has any.
- **[OPEN]** (OQ-6) Nothing here gives the practitioner a word for it that anybody else would understand.

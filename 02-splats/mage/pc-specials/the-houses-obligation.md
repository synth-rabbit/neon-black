# The House's Obligation

**Setting name:** Caster · **Prerequisite:** You have been brought into the fold: offered a place in the practice by someone already in it, and you accepted.

Membership is not a card and it is not a rank. It is an account. Once per job you may ask the house for a service and gain **two story tags** for the scene — a name, an address, a door left unlocked, an instructor's minute, a physician who asks nothing, papers that hold up once. When you do, you take **owed-2**, which hinders any action that would make the practice public and does not expire on its own: it comes off when you have done the thing that is asked of you in return, and not before. You may not use this Special again while *owed* is on you. Whenever you speak of the practice to anyone outside it, take **owed-2**, or increase it by one tier if you already carry it.

The house is not generous and it is not cruel. It keeps a ledger, and everyone in it has always been on the ledger.

## Persistence

Survives theme replacement (Brief §2.4). A Caster who lets every discipline lapse still owes what they owe; the account is with people, not with a practice ([[casters]] §5), and it is one of the two things that stick to a Caster no matter what they stop doing. Removed only for narrative reasons — the house releases you, or you break with it and survive doing so — and marking an Evolution point (Tokyo p. 72).

## Canon and flags

- The persistent condition this carries: **membership and its secrecy**. Canon makes the society secret, makes the corporations prefer it secret, and makes membership something conferred rather than sought — "a secret society identifies talented people and brings them into the fold" (Bible §2, §3). What is inescapable is not a power; it is the fact that somebody found you, and people who find you expect things.
- Built on the Tokyo Megacity Special pattern (Tokyo p. 72–74), nearest in shape to *Friends in the Bureau* (Tokyo p. 73) — a service asked of an institution, with a mark left on the asker.
- **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-65) The obligation's terms: one service per job, two story tags, *owed-2* that hinders exposing the practice and clears only on a task done. The Bible states that membership exists and is secret; the account is this package's model of what that costs and grants.
- **[OPEN]** (OQ-27) What the house does about a member who refuses the task, or who is asked for something they will not do. Canon does not say what the society does to its own, and the vault does not decide it.
- **[OPEN]** (OQ-6) The word for what is being kept secret is never spoken in this Special's text, on purpose.

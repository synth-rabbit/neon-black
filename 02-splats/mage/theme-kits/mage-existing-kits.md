# Caster Kits from the Books

Every kit here is **offered as printed** (Plan A.1 rule 1). The custom kits in this folder are additions, never replacements. What changes is the *flavour text*: the books' Mythos kits assume returning legends, and this setting has none (Brief §3.1). Each row below carries a **Tao re-flavour** line — one sentence saying what the kit is when the only mystical force is Tao. Every such line is `[TAO-REINTERPRETED]` (CR-1).

Two standing substitutions apply to every Mythos kit in the books and are not repeated in each row:

- **"a Source"** → a **Tao gateway**: a practice, a worked item, a dense place, a made being (`pillars`).
- **A tag or Ritual that names a legend, a god, a spirit's wishes, or a myth's demand** → the same effect with a *human* origin: what a practitioner did, what a working holds, what the method costs. Nothing wants anything.

Tokyo pp. 20–21 is the format model for presenting practices this way — Onmyōdō, Shugendō, hanko magic, tezuma are written as *practices with methods and institutions*, not as legends with appetites. Format only: none of those practices exists in Palisade.

## 1. Esoterica — the eight kits (Core p. 230–231) `[TAO-REINTERPRETED]`

Esoterica is the Caster's home themebook: "a means to mythic power that relies on various **personal methods which require special study**" (Core p. 228), which under impersonal Tao needs almost no adjustment — a discipline is exactly a personal method requiring special study. The themebook's own Ritual examples ("I must harvest special inks from hidden pools," "I must practise the visceral ways of magic") are already disciplines and prices and are usable verbatim.

| Kit | Page | Title tag | Tao re-flavour `[TAO-REINTERPRETED]` |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Weapon Witching** | 231 | *legend-killing enchantment* | The discipline of working Tao into edged and fired weapons. The canon Caster's discipline — this is the caster-shell tradition the setting is named for (Bible stated influences; `names` §1). Read *legend-killing enchantment* as **a working that goes through what should stop it**; *disenchant weapons* as unpicking somebody else's working; *inter-enchantment reaction* as two workings on one bench interfering. |
| **Warding Signs** | 231 | *circle of protection* | Marks, chalk lines, and geometry that hold a space. *Trace spell source* becomes **trace a working back to the bench that made it** — a practitioner's most valuable trick in a city where nobody admits to practising. *Disrupts helpful magic* stays as written: a ward is indiscriminate because a line is a line. |
| **Spirit Summoning** | 231 | *spirit binding* | The discipline of standing beings up out of Tao and putting them away again. There are no spirits with an afterlife behind them; there are **made things that hold shape while a practitioner holds them**. *Spirits demand respect* becomes **a form kept badly comes apart badly**. *Enter the spirit world* is `[OPEN]` (OQ-25) — there is no other world in canon; either cut the tag or read it as a dense place. See [[standing-form]] for the same idea as a Companion theme. |
| **Potion Craft** | 231 | *healing salves* | Chemistry the long way round: a discipline of preparation in which the method, not the compound, does the work. Sits directly beside [[palisade-player-primer#Meliora Bioworks|Meliora]] — Meliora Bioworks — and its product line and is the easiest Caster practice to mistake for pharmacology — which is why it is the easiest one to hide. |
| **Fortune Telling** | 230 | *glimpse impending events* | Reading the flow of a thing before it has finished happening. **No fate, no destiny, nothing written** — Tao has no plan and *reveal inescapable destiny* must be downgraded to **read a course already set in motion** (a room, a market, a fight). `[BUILD CHOICE]` (BC-58): the tag as printed asserts a cosmology the Bible does not have. *Follow the signs* reads as pattern, not instruction. |
| **Evil Eye** | 230 | *hex of vulnerability* | Working harm at a distance through attention. *Folk curses* are a **street tradition of the practice**, learned outside any house — the third position in [[casters]] §2 in kit form. *Misfortune is infectious* is a description of a sloppy working's spread, not a moral law. |
| **Devil's Bargains** | 230 | *remove obstacles for others* | **No devil.** A discipline of binding agreements: a working that holds two parties to terms, made by a practitioner who charges for it. *Drag them to hell* becomes **drag them somewhere they cannot get back from**, which in Palisade is a place, not an afterlife. `[BUILD CHOICE]` (BC-58). |
| **Corpse Animation** | 230 | *command undead servitors* | A discipline that stands a body up and walks it. *Rites to honour the spirits whose bodies I use* has nothing to honour; rewrite the Ritual as the practitioner's own price — *"I must return every body I use to where it was found."* Body horror is a tone word (`style-guide` §4); this kit is squarely in it. |

**Esoterica Specials** (Core p. 229) all survive unchanged: *Daily Practice*, *Mystical Savvy*, *Something Extra*, *Tools of the Practice*, *Universal Toolbox*. **[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** *Mystical Savvy* reads "when you witness a Mythical effect" — read as **a working**.

## 2. Artifact — worked items (Core p. 220–223) `[TAO-REINTERPRETED]`

An Artifact theme is a **worked item**: an object into which a practitioner put a discipline. The themebook's "items crafted through magic by specialists, corporations, or cults" (Core p. 220) is the branch that survives; "relics of legendary origin," "the will of the gods," and "a destiny tied to your Artifact" do not. `[BUILD CHOICE]` (BC-58) An Artifact in Palisade has **no temperament and no plans** — every printed tag or Ritual that gives an item wants is rewritten as a property of the working.

| Kit | Page | Tao re-flavour `[TAO-REINTERPRETED]` |
|---|---|---|
| **Wizard's Staff** | 223 | The nearest printed kit to a Caster's focus: an object that stores a working and discharges it. *Vast magical knowledge* → **knows the practice that made it** as a reference, not a mind. *Uncontrolled power* stays. |
| **Scrying Crystals** | 223 | A worked lens or sensor set. *Astral projection* → **read a place I am not standing in**. *Places beyond this earth* is `[OPEN]` (OQ-25) — cut, or read as a dense place. |
| **Invisibility Helm** | 222 | A worked garment or head-rig. *Supernatural senses* as a weakness becomes **anything that watches for a working**, which is exactly what a countermeasure does (`anti-tao-countermeasure`). |
| **Hero's Sword** | 222 | A worked blade. Strip *bound to the will of the gods* and *won't attack the innocent*; a worked blade has conditions, not scruples — replace with a limitation the bench imposed. |
| **Risk-Taker's Luck Charm** | 222 | Hardest to keep honest: luck implies something arranging outcomes. Read as **a working that spends itself smoothing one thing at a time** and treat *hexes and curses* as rival workings. `[BUILD CHOICE]` (BC-58). |
| **Possessed Vehicle**, **War God's Armor**, **Mask of the Trickster God** | 222–223 | Offered, but each is built on a specific legend. Usable only after the legend is stripped out entirely, which leaves the mechanical shell; the custom [[worked-sidearm]] is the cleaner starting point. |

**Artifact Specials** (Core p. 221): ***Overcharged and Overclocked*** is the one this setting is built around — a power tag answered from the **Cutting Edge or Drones** themebook representing a technological upgrade to the Artifact. That is Tao interwoven with tech in one printed rule, pointing the opposite way from Cutting Edge's *Occult Blueprints* (§4). *Reckless Discharge*, *Sharing is Caring*, *Signature Move* survive as written. **[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** *Status Symbol* reads "where Sources grant prestige or respect" — in Palisade almost nowhere does, because almost nobody knows; usable inside the practice, at [[palisade-player-primer#Chancery Hill|Aldine House]], and among people who buy worked goods.

## 3. Companion — made beings (Core p. 224–227) `[TAO-REINTERPRETED]`

Canon permits Companion themes: **Tao can produce beings** (Brief §3.1). What it cannot produce is a legend with an agenda. The themebook's "Conjurations spawned by a myth" become **things a practitioner stood up out of Tao**; the themebook's own "Conjurations you create with another Source, such as summoned with Esoterica" is already the right shape and is the recommended route — pair a Companion theme with an Esoterica theme as its gateway (Core p. 224).

| Kit | Page | Tao re-flavour `[TAO-REINTERPRETED]` |
|---|---|---|
| **Arcane Construct** | 226 | The cleanest fit as printed. *I must feed my creation raw magical energy* → the practitioner's own hours: **I must re-work it out of the day's practice**. *Anti-magic field* is a made thing that grounds workings — including its maker's. |
| **Magical Guide** | 226 | Usable only with the mentor read stripped: nothing made out of Tao knows anything Tao knows, because Tao knows nothing. Read as **a working that holds a practitioner's own recorded method** and answers from it. *Teach me a new spell* → **recall a step I have forgotten**. `[BUILD CHOICE]` (BC-58). |
| **Guardian Spirit** | 226 | A standing watch. *Daily offering* → a daily re-forming. *Link to the spirit world* — `[OPEN]` (OQ-25); cut or read as a dense place. |
| **Supernatural Pet**, **Trained Monsters** | 227 | Offered. Under Tao these are **made animals or a made pack**, which sits close to [[palisade-player-primer#Meliora Bioworks|Meliora]] — Meliora's — actual business and is a good source of mistaken identity. *Prayers / chant / scrolls that bind them* → the practitioner's binding method, held by discipline. |
| **Tech Gremlin** | 227 | Tao interwoven with tech from the other end: a made thing that lives in machinery. Sits beside [[shellwork]]. |
| **Nature Spirit**, **Legendary Sidekick** | 226–227 | Offered; both lean hardest on legend. Strip and reuse the mechanics, or take [[standing-form]] instead. |

**Companion Specials** (Core p. 225) survive: *Back-to-back*, *Caregiver*, *Hands Off*, *Strategic Positioning*. **[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** *New Tricks* — a power tag answered from Esoterica or Exposure representing "your Companion's new manifested powers" — reads as **the practitioner working a new capability into the form**, not the being growing one on its own.

**[RULES CONFLICT]** (CR-10) The themebook states Companions "have opinions and sometimes act on their own" and that their assistance "always involves striving to achieve what the creature ultimately desires" (Core p. 224). Tao has no will; a thing Tao was made into might. The two readings are set out in full in [[standing-form]]; neither is resolved here.

## 4. Exposure — Tao-touched bodies (Core p. 232–235) `[TAO-REINTERPRETED]`

Exposure is primarily the Doppel's Tao half (Brief §2.1, `02-splats/changeling/`). For a Caster it is available as a **further** Mythos theme, never the signature ([[casters]] §3): a body changed by a dense place, by a working that went wrong, or by long practice. The themebook's Thin Places and Avatars become dense places and practitioners; *"I gained magical powers resembling the legend of ___"* has no filler in this setting and the sentence is cut.

| Kit | Page | Tao re-flavour `[TAO-REINTERPRETED]` |
|---|---|---|
| **Elemental Body** | 234 | A body that took on a property of something it stood in too long. *Those who keep the balance* → rival practitioners and countermeasure teams. |
| **Animate Shadows** | 234 | *I must enter the shadow realm every night* has no realm to enter — rewrite as a nightly condition of the practice. |
| **Speak with Machines** | 235 | The Exposure kit closest to the setting's core image: Tao and tech in one nervous system. *I must report routinely to [a powerful machine intelligence]* → a maintenance condition, not an obedience. |
| **Aura of Authority**, **Angelic Wings**, **Midas Touch**, **Floral Overgrowth**, **Warrior's Instincts** | 234–235 | Offered; each needs its legend cut before use. *Aura of Authority*'s Ritual (*"worship at the shrine of the entity that grants me my authority"*) is the clearest example of a Ritual this setting cannot use as printed (Brief §3.2, CR-3). |

**Exposure Specials** (Core p. 233): *Discipline*, *Durable*, *Mythic Residue*, *Surge of Power* survive. ***Secondary Contamination*** — a power tag answered from Artifact or Companion because "your Source rubs off on a nearby item or creature" — is the printed route from a Tao-touched body to a worked item or a made being, and is `[TAO-REINTERPRETED]` as **the working spreading into what is nearest**.

**[RULES CONFLICT]** (CR-2) The Exposure themebook's own example Ritual is *"(Vampire) I must consume the blood of the living."* In this setting Bloodware are Noise, not Mythos (Brief §2.1, §10.2). Noted here so no Caster kit borrows the framing.

## 5. Noise kits for the tech that augments the practice

Canon: Casters "use technology to augment their magic" (Bible §2). The optional secondary theme is Noise, and the printed bridge is the Cutting Edge Special ***Occult Blueprints*** (Core p. 241): *choose and answer a power tag question from the Esoterica or Exposure themebooks that reflects magical methods used in the construction of this Cutting Edge tech.* That single rule is the mechanical statement of "Tao interwoven with technology" and every tech-augmented Caster build should be pointed at it.

| Kit | Themebook | Page | Why offer it to a Caster |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Force Fields** | Cutting Edge | 243 | Fields and barriers next to Warding Signs; take *Occult Blueprints* and the two stop being separate things. |
| **Cryptographic Skeleton Key** | Cutting Edge | 242 | Doors, seals, and credentials — the tech twin of a warding practice. |
| **Cloud of Nanites** | Cutting Edge | 242 | Matter on demand beside Potion Craft. Note the Bloodware association (`02-splats/vampire/`): a Caster carrying this kit will be read wrong by anyone who knows what nanites usually mean in Palisade. |
| **Advanced Railgun** | Cutting Edge | 242 | The mundane half of a worked weapon; pairs with [[worked-sidearm]]. |
| **Cloaking Jumpsuit** | Cutting Edge | 242 | Staying unseen by tech, where a practice stays unseen by people. |
| **Enhanced Senses** | Augmentation | 239 | Offered with the note that an Augmentation theme is a **body modification** — a Caster who takes one has bought a rung on the ladder like everyone else (`pillars`), and is no less a pure Tao user for it, because the practice is still the practice. |
| **Zeroed Identity** | Cyberspace | 247 | Secrecy is the practice's standing problem ([[casters]] §2). |

## Canon and flags

- Existing content offered in addition to custom content, never instead: Plan A.1 rule 1, Brief §0.1.
- Casters as pure Tao users augmenting with tech; Tao as the only force, impersonal, able to produce beings and be interwoven with tech: Bible §1, §2; Brief §3.1.
- **[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** (CR-1) every Mythos kit, Special, and Ritual above; the two standing substitutions at the head of the file.
- **[RULES CONFLICT]** (CR-2) the Exposure themebook's vampire example. (CR-10) Companions with wants under impersonal Tao — see [[standing-form]].
- **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-58) the specific re-flavour of each printed kit, tag, and Ritual that asserted a legend, a fate, a devil, a god, or a will.
- **[OPEN]** (OQ-25) whether there is any "spirit world," "shadow realm," "nether realm," or "place beyond this earth" for the printed tags that name one; canon has no other world and the vault asserts none.

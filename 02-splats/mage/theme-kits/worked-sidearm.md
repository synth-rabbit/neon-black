# A Worked Sidearm

**Themebook:** Artifact · **Category:** Mythos · **Setting name:** Caster · **Source:** custom

A pistol that would pass any inspection on the Wall, and six rounds that would not, if anybody knew what to look for. This is the object the city's practitioners are named after (`names` §1): a manufactured weapon carrying workings that were put into its ammunition at a bench, days ago, by somebody who then had to sleep. It has no temperament, no history, and nothing it is trying to do. It is a delivery system for a discipline, and when the rack is empty it is a gun.

Built from [[shellwork]]'s output — take the two together and the practitioner both makes and carries. Taken alone, somebody worked these shells, and the question of who is a whole character.

## Ritual

> I must keep the sidearm current: a shell more than a month off the bench is dead metal.

A condition and a price at once (`style-guide` §3): workings go flat, and flat rounds are indistinguishable from live ones until they are fired. Neglect is a month of not getting back to a bench. Nothing withdraws anything; the shells simply stop being anything.

## Specials

Inherits the Artifact themebook's five (Core p. 221): *Overcharged and Overclocked*, *Reckless Discharge*, *Sharing is Caring*, *Signature Move*, *Status Symbol*. Two notes:

- ***Overcharged and Overclocked*** is the one this kit exists for — a power tag answered from **Cutting Edge or Drones** representing a technological upgrade to the sidearm. Optics, a smart frame, a feed system, a rangefinder that talks to the shells. This is the printed rule for tech augmenting a working, the mirror of *Occult Blueprints* (Core p. 241) on the Noise side. See [[shellwork]] for both.
- **[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** *Status Symbol* grants a social status "where Sources grant prestige or respect." Almost nowhere in Palisade does, because almost nobody knows there is anything to respect ([[casters]] §1). It works among people who buy worked goods, and inside the practice.

This kit adds one of its own:

**Called Shell:** Once per session, before you roll, name which of your worked shells is chambered and what it was worked for. The tag you invoke for that shell counts as directly relevant, even if it would otherwise be indirectly relevant.

## Nascent form

Title tag **a worked sidearm** plus one weakness reads as a theme: somebody with a gun that does one impossible thing and a very short supply of it.

## Canon and flags

- Canon relied on: Tao can be interwoven with technology, caster-shell style (Bible §1, stated influences; Brief §3.1); Casters augment the practice with tech (Bible §2); Artifact themes are Tao-imbued items (Brief §3.2).
- **[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** (CR-1) The Artifact themebook's relics, gods, destinies, and items with "a will of their own" (Core p. 220) are all cut. A worked item **carries** a working; it never has one. Every printed weakness of the "bound to the will of the gods" kind becomes a property of the working or a limit of the bench.
- **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-62) The worked sidearm as an ordinary frame with extraordinary ammunition — rather than an extraordinary gun — so that the practice, not the object, is where the power lives, and so that the object is disposable and the practitioner is not. Name registered in `build-choices`.
- **Rarity** (Bible §2): six shells, one bench, one practitioner. There is no production run and there is no catalogue entry; the Tao-touched Loadout category (`09-loadout/`) covers single items, not stock.

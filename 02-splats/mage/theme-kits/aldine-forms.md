# The Aldine Forms

**Themebook:** Esoterica · **Category:** Mythos · **Setting name:** Caster · **Source:** custom

Forty people in grey, in a walled garden on the Crest, moving slowly in the hour before the city gets loud. That is the whole of it, and it is taught to anyone whose parents pay the subscription. What the subscription does not mention is that a few students keep going after the class ends, and get a second instructor, and learn that the sequence is not exercise. The forms are stillness applied to a world that is always moving: hold the stance, and things that were going to happen do not happen yet.

## Ritual

> I must keep the morning form, wherever I wake.

A discipline, in the register the setting requires (`style-guide` §3): a practice to maintain, at a fixed time, in whatever room the night left you in. Neglecting it means skipping the form — for a job that ran long, for a bed that was not yours, for a night spent hiding. Nothing objects. The forms simply get further away.

## Specials

Inherits the Esoterica themebook's five (Core p. 229): *Daily Practice*, *Mystical Savvy*, *Something Extra*, *Tools of the Practice*, *Universal Toolbox*. **[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** *Mystical Savvy* reads "when you witness a Mythical effect" — read as **a working**.

This kit adds one of its own, in the Tokyo pattern (Tokyo p. 60–61):

**Kept the Form:** Once per session, if you kept the morning form on screen or in a Flashback, you may gain *centered-2*, or remove two tiers of a status representing panic, haste, or overstimulation from yourself or someone you can touch.

## Nascent form

Title tag **the Aldine forms** plus one weakness reads as a theme on its own: someone who learned a taught discipline and cannot use it with their hands full. The forms name the whole practice, so a nascent version can do any small thing the practice does, slowly.

## Tao and the practice `[TAO-REINTERPRETED]`

**[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** (CR-1) The Esoterica themebook is written for "occult symbols, incantations… bold invocation of otherworldly entities" (Core p. 228). Nothing here invokes anything. The forms are a **method**: a sequence a body performs, which produces a state, in which Tao — impersonal, always present, mostly unusable — runs and does one narrow thing. There is no entity at either end of it. A practitioner who stops practising loses the method, not the force ([[casters]] §5).

## Canon and flags

- Canon relied on: the society finds and teaches the talented, and stays hidden (Bible §2, §3); Tao is impersonal and predates the mods (Bible §1; Brief §3.1); the mage's primary is Esoterica with a Ritual as discipline (Brief §2.5, §3.2).
- **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-59) The Aldine forms as the House's taught discipline — a movement-and-breath practice producing *arrest*, taken from the morning class already established at [[palisade-player-primer#Chancery Hill|Aldine House]] by WP1 (BC-19). Registered in `build-choices`; the kit name goes to `names` via that file.
- **Rarity** (Bible §2): the class is public and harmless; the practice is what happens to a few students after it. Anyone can attend Aldine House's garden. Almost nobody there is a practitioner.
- **[OPEN]** (OQ-6) Nothing in this kit uses the word *Tao* where a bystander could hear it; whether the public has the word is the GM's.

# Placed Initiate

**Setting name:** Caster · **Built toward:** Nexus (or Spiritualist without the fourth theme's Noise)

You did something at fourteen you should not have been able to do, and a fortnight later a foundation wrote to your household offering a scholarship. There was no interview. There was a garden, and a class at dawn, and after a year one of the instructors kept you back and taught you the sequence properly. Since then you have been *placed* — a flat, a job, a route across the city, all arranged, all reasonable, none of it explained. You keep the forms. You do the errands. Nobody has yet told you what the errands are for, and you have worked out that asking is not how this is done.

## On the sheet

Mythos ×1 + Self ×2 + Noise ×1 = **Nexus** (Core p. 135), which satisfies the Caster's Essence minimum of at least one Mythos theme (Brief §2.2, `build-choices` BC-1). Dropping the fourth theme for a third Self makes this a **Spiritualist**, the commonest Caster shape ([[casters]] §4).

The Ritual on the Esoterica theme is *"I must keep the morning form, wherever I wake"* — a discipline, not a demand from anything (`style-guide` §3). The Identity on the Affiliation theme is what the House gets in return; write it flatly, so that acting against it is obvious.

Candidate PC Special once the fiction supports it: [[the-houses-obligation]] — the standing debt that comes with being placed. It goes on the character card and survives theme replacement (Tokyo p. 72).

## What this character knows

Everything in [[casters]] §2, "placed initiate" row, and nothing beyond it: that there is a house, that it teaches, that there are others, that the bill has not come. Not the society's name. Not who decides anything. Not one word about any corporation.

## Canon and flags

- Canon relied on: a secret society identifies talented people and brings them into the fold; the society is secret and the corporations prefer it (Bible §2, §3). The PCs start at the bottom with no institutional standing (Bible §6) — being *placed* is not standing; it is somebody else's convenience, and it is the reason this character was in a work crew at the bottom of the Wall in the first place.
- **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-63) The trope, its kit list, and its loadout. Placement — flat, job, route, all arranged, none explained — is this package's reading of "brings them into the fold" for a character who must still start at the bottom.
- **[OPEN]** (OQ-6) The character has never heard the word *Tao* used out loud by anyone but their instructor, and does not know whether anybody else in the city has it.
- Book kits in `fixed_kits` / `choice_kits` carry the `book:` prefix and are indexed with page and themebook in [[book-kits-index]] (BC-126); they are offered as printed, never copied in.

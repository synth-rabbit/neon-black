# Backroom Shellwright

**Setting name:** Caster · **Built toward:** Nexus (or Spiritualist, if the fourth theme is Self)

Nobody found you. You found a habit: a way of sitting at the bench, at the same hour, doing the same three things before you touch a primer, and the first time you did it properly the round you loaded came off the wall instead of going into it. That was four years ago and you have never told anybody, because the only people you know who would believe it are the people you would least like to have believe it. You sell ordinary work at ordinary prices out of a back room, and once in a while somebody pays a great deal for six rounds and does not ask what is in them.

## On the sheet

Mythos ×2 + Self ×1 + a fourth: **Nexus** with the Noise option (Core p. 135), **Spiritualist** with either Self option. Two Mythos themes drawn from *one* gateway — the bench discipline, and the item that discipline made — is the shape that becomes an **Avatar** if the Self themes ever go ([[casters]] §4); this trope is the most likely Caster in the vault to get there, and the least likely to survive it.

Both Rituals are prices, and they are different prices on purpose: *"I must re-work every shell I spend before I sleep"* (the practice) and *"I must keep the sidearm current"* (the object). A long job away from the bench threatens both at once.

Candidate PC Special: [[tao-sensitive]] — four years of looking for what a working leaves behind. If a countermeasure has ever caught this character at a bench, [[countermeasure-scar]] instead.

## What this character knows

Everything in [[casters]] §2, "unfound street practitioner" row: that it works, that it should not, and that nobody safe to ask has ever seen it. If they have heard *the Quiet Hand* at all, they heard it the way everyone else on the Wall does — as a joke somebody made about a members' club.

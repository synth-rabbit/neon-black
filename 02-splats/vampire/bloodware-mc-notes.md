# Bloodware — MC Notes

The MC-only section split out of [[bloodware]] by WP8 (BC-176) so that the overview can be handed to a player whole. The text below is the owning package's, moved verbatim; it is not for the session-zero packet ([[session-zero-packet]]).

Facts a street-level or newly made Bloodware does not have. A PC starts at the bottom with no institutional standing (Bible §6), so a PC does not begin with either of these unless the GM says otherwise.

- **[[upstart|Continuity Risk & Response]]** is Bloodware-run. Within Bloodware society anyone with power or sway knows this; street-level and newly made ones do not (Bible §2, §5). **[OPEN]** (OQ-17) What it costs, and to whom, if this becomes known, is not decided. No player-facing file in this package mentions it; [[the-lattice]] deliberately reads as a data ward and nothing else.
- **The corporations regard the strain as a disease.** It flares up, it gets cleaned up, and every so often it produces a clan noteworthy enough that one of the corps decides to wipe it out entirely (Bible §2). "Noteworthy" is a corporate judgment, not a moral one.
- The crew leader in the Ledger, **[[tomas-adair|Tomas Adair "Rook"]]**, is a Bloodware spy (Bible §5). What the crew knows about him is the players' business.

# Bloodware

![[assets/splats/bloodware.png]]

**Splat:** vampire · **Setting name:** Bloodware (`names` §1) · **Required primary:** Noise (Cutting Edge) · **Optional secondary:** Mythos (Exposure) or further Noise

## What a Bloodware is

A hundred years ago something got out of a laboratory in what is now [[palisade-player-primer#Ferrante Basin|Ferrante Basin]], and it is still getting out, one body at a time. The **Ferrante strain** is a self-replicating nanite colony that took up residence in people. It rebuilds what it lives in: faster, stronger, harder to finish, and eventually not quite the same body it started with. The company that made it, **Ferrante Nanoscale**, has not existed for a century (Bible §2, §3). Nobody is coming to fix this.

The strain replicates with **iron**, so a Bloodware needs blood or iron and needs it regularly. Blood is easiest, iron is quieter, and both are a problem in a city where neither is free. The hunger is not a metaphor and it is not optional: run low and the strain starts taking what it needs out of the body it is already in.

A Bloodware does not age out. The strain replaces what wears out, including the parts that were doing the remembering — the body that walks around after a century is not the body that was made, in the same way a boat rebuilt plank by plank is not the boat that was launched. Old ones talk about this the way other people talk about a bad knee.

**What kills one.** Two things, and only two things are worth planning around. **Electromagnetic fields and effects** tear the colony apart — a live field is the one place a Bloodware is just a person with a hole in them. And every Bloodware has a **Kernel**: a master node, one dense knot of the colony sitting somewhere in the body, which runs the rest. *Pop the kernel* and the body does not get up. The Kernel is the one thing the strain cannot rebuild, because it is the thing doing the rebuilding (`names` §2; Bible §2).

**Bloodware are rare** (Bible §2, Brief §2.6). Palisade has a lot of people with work done. It does not have a lot of these. A Bloodware may go years without meeting another.

**Bloodware are secret.** The outbreak is public history — the worst industrial disaster in the city's memory, a nanite accident, a company gone, a district walled ([[palisade-player-primer#Ferrante Basin|Ferrante Basin]]). What the outbreak *produced* is not public. The corporations do the cleanup, the Noise does the rest, and when something happens on a street that cannot be explained, a Baseline gets the blame (Bible §2, §3; Brief §4.1).

**How you were made** is a question for the player to answer, not this file. **[OPEN]** (OQ-4) The exact vector by which the strain passes from one body to another is not settled and nothing in this package settles it. Kits, tropes and Specials here refer only to *the one who made me* and leave the how to the table.

## Mapping

**Hybrid** (Brief §2.1). Bloodware identity lives in theme kits, tropes, Megacity-style PC Specials and Essence guidance — not in a category by itself.

| | Category | Themebook | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Required primary** | Noise | **Cutting Edge** | The strain itself: the colony, the Kernel, what the nanites do to and for the body. |
| Optional secondary | Noise | Cutting Edge / Augmentation | A second expression of the strain, or ordinary chrome bought like anyone buys chrome. |
| Optional secondary | Mythos | Exposure | Only if the GM opens **[OPEN]** (OQ-5) — see [[interwoven-strain]]. |

**[RULES CONFLICT]** (CR-2) The Exposure themebook's own worked example is a vampire, with the Ritual *"I must consume the blood of the living"* (Core p. 233), which would place this package in Mythos. Canon places it in Noise: the strain is manufactured technology, not an exposure to anything mystical (Bible §1, §2). The Brief's adopted resolution is the Hybrid mapping above, and it is recorded here as required. The GM may overrule and run Bloodware out of Exposure instead; if so, every Itch in this package becomes a Ritual and the Essence minimum below becomes "≥1 Mythos theme."

**[OPEN]** (OQ-5) Whether the five can use Tao at all is unresolved for Bloodware. The Exposure option and [[interwoven-strain]] exist as a conditional branch and are switched off until the GM answers.

## Essence minimum

**At least one Noise theme** (Brief §2.2, BC-1), and at creation at least one of those Noise themes is a Bloodware kit — the strain is not optional equipment.

**[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-32) The minimum is read as a **character-creation** requirement, not a standing one. Brief §2.2 sets the minimum for building a character; Brief §2.4 says the power lives in the theme and may Decay while the condition does not. Read together: a Bloodware whose last Noise theme Decays away and is replaced by a Self theme is still a Bloodware — still hungry, still field-fragile, still carrying a Kernel — and the PC Specials below are what carries that. The alternative reading, that the minimum must be maintained for the life of the character (so a Bloodware may never replace their last Noise theme), is available to the GM.

## Persistence

**[RULES CONFLICT]** (CR-7, adopted by the Brief) Vampirism is irreversible (Bible §1) but themes Decay and are replaced (Core p. 136). The resolution used here:

- The **power** lives in the kit. [[the-ferrante-strain]] and [[hardened-kernel]] can Decay like any theme, and a Bloodware who stops indulging the Itch loses the theme.
- The **condition** does not. Blood-or-iron hunger, EM fragility and the Kernel are narrative facts of the character, not of the theme card.
- The mechanical hooks live on **Megacity-style PC Specials** (Tokyo p. 72 pattern): taken instead of a theme upgrade, gated behind a narrative prerequisite, recorded on the character card, and untouched by theme replacement. See [[iron-hunger]], [[field-bitten]], [[where-the-kernel-sits]] and [[made-by-the-strain]].

## Motivation

**Kit-fixed Itch** on the required Noise theme (Brief §2.5, BC-2): *feed the strain — blood or iron.* Both Noise kits in this package carry an Itch pointed at feeding, and both carry the dependency again as a weakness tag, so the cost sits on the card twice: once as what the character wants, once as what it does to them.

Indulging the Itch **keeps** the theme; stifling it marks Decay (Core p. 136; Brief §4.2). That is the whole shape of the thing — a Bloodware who refuses to feed does not become a better person, they lose the strain's power and keep the hunger. There is no humanity track and none may be added (Brief §4.3).

If the GM opens OQ-5 and a Bloodware takes [[interwoven-strain]], that Mythos theme carries a **Ritual** in the discipline register instead (Brief §3.2, CR-3) — never a legend's demand.

## How a Bloodware reads on the sheet

Four themes, of which at least one is Noise and at least one of those is the strain.

| Build | Themes | Essence | Reads as |
|---|---|---|---|
| Most starting PCs | 1 Bloodware kit (Noise) + 3 Self | **Cyborg** (Core p. 135) | Someone who is mostly still a person and has one enormous problem. |
| Two-kit Bloodware | 2 Noise (both kits) + 2 Self | **Cyborg** | Committed. The strain is half of what they are. |
| Nothing but the strain | 4 Noise | **Singularity** (Core p. 136) | The colony wearing a person. The end of the road this condition is on. |
| Tao-touched (OQ-5 only) | Noise + Exposure + Self | **Nexus** (Core p. 135) | Rare twice over. |
| Tao-touched, no Self (OQ-5 only) | Noise + Exposure | **Transhuman** (Core p. 135) | Iron and current, nothing else. |

**Real is not available while a Bloodware carries a Noise theme** (Real is Self only, Core p. 135). A Bloodware who ends up Real — every Noise theme Decayed and replaced — is a Bloodware who has stopped using the strain and has not stopped being one. That character keeps every PC Special they took. It is the bleakest legal build in this package and it is deliberately reachable.

Bloodware PCs are pushed toward **Cyborg** and, at the far end, **Singularity**, in the same way Baselines are pushed toward Real and Cyborg (Brief §2.3). Nobody is required to go anywhere.

- **Book kits, first.** [[vampire-existing-kits]] lists Self-Healing (Core p. 243), Cloud of Nanites (Core p. 242) and the Augmentation and Exposure kits worth putting in front of a Bloodware player, each with a re-flavor line. Nothing in this package replaces them (Plan A.1 rule 1).
- **Custom kits.** [[the-ferrante-strain]] — the bloodline itself, the classic package rendered as nanite tech. [[hardened-kernel]] — the second expression: a Bloodware who has worked on their own master node and runs the body from it. [[interwoven-strain]] — conditional on OQ-5.
- **Tropes.** [[scrap-fed-newblood]] (made this year, feeding out of scrap, has never heard of any of this) and [[hundred-year-tenant]] (made a century ago, still under the Wall, still nobody).
- **PC Specials.** [[iron-hunger]], [[field-bitten]], [[where-the-kernel-sits]], [[made-by-the-strain]].
- **For the MC.** `bloodware-power-set` — the NPC overlay, with the Limits an NPC Bloodware must have.

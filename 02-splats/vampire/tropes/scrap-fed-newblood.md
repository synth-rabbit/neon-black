# Scrap-Fed Newblood

**Splat:** vampire · **Built toward:** Cyborg

Somebody made you, recently, and then either died, left, or decided you were not worth the follow-up. You came out of it strong, fast, hard to finish, and permanently hungry, with no explanation and nobody to ask. You have been living off scrap iron — rebar, filings, whatever the Gullet coughs up — because the alternative is a person, and you are not there yet. Everything you know about what you are, you worked out in the dark, wrong.

You have never heard of any clan. You do not know that anyone with sway knows anything. As far as you can tell, this happened to you and only you.

## On the sheet

Three fixed kits are Cutting Edge (Noise), Troubled Past (Self) and Personality (Self); the fourth may not share a themebook with them (Core p. 177), and none of the three choices does. Every combination lands on **Cyborg** — Self and Noise (Core p. 135) — and every combination satisfies the Essence minimum of at least one Noise theme (Brief §2.2, BC-1).

**Do not take *a hundred years of practice*** from [[the-ferrante-strain]]. This character has had the strain for months. The tags that fit are *wounds close while you watch*, *bone-breaking strength*, *drink the iron from steel*, *the one who made me* and *a flask of iron salts*; the weakness that fits hardest is *runs down without iron*.

If the GM opens **[OPEN]** (OQ-5), [[interwoven-strain]] (EXPOSURE) is a legal fourth kit and turns the trope into a **Nexus**. It is a strange choice for a character this new and that is the point of offering it.

**PC Specials.** This trope is the natural home of [[iron-hunger]] and [[made-by-the-strain]]: the first is already true of the character and only needs a hungry job in play to unlock, and the second is the question the character actually wants answered.

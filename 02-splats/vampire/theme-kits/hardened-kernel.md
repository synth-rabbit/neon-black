# Hardened Kernel

**Themebook:** Cutting Edge · **Category:** Noise · **Splat:** vampire · **Source:** custom

Most carriers never think about the node. It sits where it landed, it runs the colony, and one day somebody finds it. You went the other way: you found yours, you moved it, you wrapped it, and you learned to work the body from there instead of from the nerves. It has made you very hard to finish and it has cost you most of what it feels like to be in a body at all.

**[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-34) Plan WP2 requires a second Bloodware kit "master-node/EM-focused"; this is that kit. The alternative brief the package could have taken — the colony as a detachable weapon or tool — is largely covered by the book's **Cloud of Nanites** (Core p. 242), which is why this kit went to the core instead ([[vampire-existing-kits]]).

## Itch

> Spend the swarm freely; take the iron back out of someone.

Kit-fixed (Brief §2.5, BC-2), and pointed at the same place as every Bloodware Itch: feeding. This one indulges by *spending* — running the colony hard, hardening further, pushing it outside, and then covering the deficit off somebody. Stifling it looks like husbanding the colony, refusing the showy use, and staying inside a reserve you can pay for.

## Specials

Inherits the Cutting Edge themebook's five (Core p. 241). *Amp Up* is the core running hot before a job; *Void the Warranty* is a Bloodware spending the node itself, which is a sacrifice with real teeth on a carrier. **Occult Blueprints** is available only if the GM opens **[OPEN]** (OQ-5) — see [[interwoven-strain]].

## Nascent form

Title tag **a hardened Kernel** plus **a strong field drops me** reads as a complete theme on its own: someone who has fortified the one thing that can kill them and is still afraid of doorways (Plan A.5).

## How it sits beside the other kit

[[the-ferrante-strain]] is what the colony does *to* you; this is what you have learned to do *with* it. A Bloodware may carry both as two Noise themes (Essence: Cyborg, or Singularity if the other two go Noise as well — see [[bloodware]]). A Bloodware who has never met an older one should not start with this kit; **an older one taught me** is not decoration, it is the entry price, and it is a hook the MC owns.

## Canon and flags

- Canon relied on: Bible §2 — the master node the street calls **the Kernel**, destroyed to kill; EM as the other weakness; iron replication; the progressive rebuild. Bible §4 theme 3 (ownership versus freedom): every advantage on this card is bought from the thing that owns you. Brief §2.1, §2.5, §4.2.
- **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-34) the second kit's subject; (BC-35) *read as a corpse* is the one classic image this package keeps that the Bible does not name outright — a nanite colony holding a body still and cold is a plain consequence of what the Bible does state, and it is offered as a tag, not asserted as canon.
- **[OPEN]** (OQ-4) *an older one taught me* names a teacher, not a maker, and settles nothing about transmission. **[OPEN]** (OQ-5) gates *Occult Blueprints*.
- Rarity: Kernel discipline is taught by one carrier to another and has no supplier, school or price list (Bible §2 rarity).
- The only two things on this card that can finish the character are a field and the node, which is the whole of it (Bible §2).

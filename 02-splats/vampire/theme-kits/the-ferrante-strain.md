# The Ferrante Strain

**Themebook:** Cutting Edge · **Category:** Noise · **Splat:** vampire · **Source:** custom

Something got out of a laboratory a hundred years ago and it lives in you now. It is faster than you, stronger than you, and better at keeping you alive than anything you were born with — and it runs on iron, which means it runs on blood, which means you do. This is the classic package with the mystery taken out of it and the invoice left in.

## Itch

> Feed it. Blood if there's blood, iron if there isn't.

Kit-fixed (Brief §2.5, BC-2). Scratching it keeps the theme; stifling it marks Decay (Core p. 136). Suppressing this Itch looks like going a whole job without taking anything from anyone — paying for filings, drinking the flask, going hungry to stay decent.

## Specials

Inherits the Cutting Edge themebook's five (Core p. 241): *Amp Up*, *Modular Design*, *Occult Blueprints*, *Still in Prototype*, *Void the Warranty*.

- **Modular Design** reads beautifully here: the colony re-lays itself, so swapping a power tag for a session is the strain rebuilding to suit.
- **Still in Prototype** is the strain's whole character — the thing was never finished.
- **Occult Blueprints** is available only if the GM opens **[OPEN]** (OQ-5). It writes an Exposure or Esoterica power tag onto this theme, which asserts that Tao has been worked into the strain. Switched off until then; see [[interwoven-strain]].

## Nascent form

Title tag **the Ferrante strain** plus **runs down without iron** is a complete theme: a person carrying a colony that keeps them alive and demands to be fed. That is Bloodware in two lines, which is what a nascent theme after a replacement needs to be (Plan A.5).

## Choosing tags

At creation a player takes the title tag, two more power tags and one weakness (Core p. 177). **A newly made Bloodware cannot honestly take *a hundred years of practice*** — the tag is a century of living with the thing. Everything else on the card is available from the first night.

**[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-33) *drink the iron from steel* is deliberately narrow — a specific tag under Core p. 192, useful on hinges, bars, blades, cuffs and rails and not much else. A broader phrasing (*eat metal*, *nanite omnitool*) would be too broad and is explicitly named as such by the book (Core p. 192).

## Canon and flags

- Canon relied on: Bible §2 — transmissible nanites from a hacked or failed Ferrante Nanoscale experiment about a century old; iron replication; the classic power package; immortality by replication with a ship-of-Theseus body; **EM and the Kernel as the only weaknesses, replacing the classic vampire vulnerabilities outright**. Brief §2.1 (Hybrid mapping), §2.5, §4.2.
- **[RULES CONFLICT]** (CR-2) The Exposure themebook's printed vampire example (Core p. 233) would have put this kit in Mythos; canon puts it in Noise. See [[bloodware]].
- **[OPEN]** (OQ-4) *the one who made me* names a person and stops there. The vector by which the strain passed is the GM's to settle and this kit does not settle it.
- **[OPEN]** (OQ-5) gates the *Occult Blueprints* Special above.
- Rarity: the kit is a colony inherited from another carrier, not a product. Nothing here is sold, printed, prescribed or installed (Bible §2 rarity).

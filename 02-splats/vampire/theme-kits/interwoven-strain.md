# Interwoven Strain

**Themebook:** Exposure · **Category:** Mythos · **Splat:** vampire · **Source:** custom

**[OPEN]** (OQ-5) **This kit is conditional.** Whether Bloodware can use Tao at all is unresolved (Bible §1; Brief §12). Until the GM answers yes, this card does not exist: no player may take it, no NPC carries it, and the Bloodware package runs entirely out of Noise ([[bloodware]]). It is written now so the answer costs nothing to implement.

Tao is older than the mods and can be interwoven with technology (Bible §1). A colony of nanites is technology. Somewhere — a practice you kept, a thing you carried too long, a place you should not have slept — the two came together in you, and now the strain does things no laboratory ever asked it to do.

## Ritual

> I must go still beside a gateway, every day, until the swarm goes quiet.

**[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** A discipline and a condition, not a legend's demand (Brief §3.2; `style-guide` §3). Tao does not want this and does not notice whether it happens; the practitioner does, and the interweaving comes apart without it. Neglecting the practice marks Decay (Core p. 136).

**[RULES CONFLICT]** (CR-3) The printed Exposure Ritual examples include *"(Vampire) I must consume the blood of the living"* (Core p. 233) — which is the Bloodware Itch, on the wrong theme, in the wrong register. The feeding motivation belongs on the Noise kit ([[the-ferrante-strain]], [[hardened-kernel]]); this theme's motivation is a practice. If that split reads strained at the table, it is CR-3's row that needs the GM, not this card.

## Specials

Inherits the Exposure themebook's five (Core p. 233), with two re-readings:

- **Secondary Contamination** writes an Artifact or Companion power tag onto this theme. **[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** The item is Tao-worked, the creature is a Tao construct; neither is a legend's boon (Brief §3.1, §3.2).
- **Durable** applies to body- and mind-altering Mythos Consequences. It does **not** apply to EM: the field is technology and the strain has no defense against it that this package will grant.

## Nascent form

Title tag **Tao runs in the strain** plus **EM cuts the thread** is a theme on its own: something impossible in the blood, and the one thing that switches it off (Plan A.5).

## On the sheet

This is a **secondary** theme only. The Essence minimum is at least one Noise theme (Brief §2.2), and a Bloodware's strain lives there; a character cannot express the strain solely through this card. A Bloodware carrying Noise + this + Self themes is a **Nexus**; Noise + this and nothing else is **Transhuman** (Core p. 135). Avatar and Conduit are not reachable while the character holds a Noise theme, and a Bloodware with no Noise theme has no strain on the sheet at all — the condition persists regardless ([[bloodware]], CR-8 for the Essence question generally).

## Canon and flags

- Canon relied on: Bible §1 (Tao is real, older than the mods, interwoven with technology; whether vampires can use it is **[OPEN]**), Bible §2 (the strain, the Kernel, EM), Brief §3.1–3.3.
- **[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** every *Source* in the Exposure themebook is a Tao gateway here; there are no Mythoi, no Avatars and no legends behind any tag on this card.
- **[RULES CONFLICT]** (CR-3) the Ritual register; (CR-2) the themebook's vampire example, addressed in [[bloodware]].
- **[OPEN]** (OQ-5) gates the whole card; **[OPEN]** (OQ-14) *stronger beside a gateway* deliberately says "gateway," not "Thin Place" — whether Tao-dense *sites* exist is not decided here, and the tag is playable with a practice or an item if the GM says sites do not.
- Rarity: a Bloodware is rare and one who has also touched Tao is rarer. Nothing here suggests a second population.

# Made by the Strain

**Splat:** vampire · **Prerequisite:** Your character carries the Ferrante strain, and has met at least one other carrier and lived.

Colonies keep house style. Yours came from somebody, and theirs came from somebody, and the two either match or they do not. Once per job, in the presence of another carrier, you may ask the MC whether they come from your line and get a straight answer, and gain `recognized-2` with them: it helps you be trusted, vouched for or placed by that line, and hinders you with anyone hunting it. Which is most people who know the line exists.

You may not use this to learn who made them, or how. It tells you the line, not the crossing.

## Persistence

Chosen instead of a theme upgrade in any of the four main themes (Tokyo p. 72). Recorded on the character card and untouched by theme replacement (Brief §2.4). This one *can* be removed for narrative reasons — the line is wiped out, or the character finishes with it — for an Evolution point, unlike [[iron-hunger]], [[field-bitten]] and [[where-the-kernel-sits]], which carry conditions that are canon-permanent.

## Canon and flags

- The condition carried: **being made**. Bible §2 — the strain is transmissible person to person; Bible §2 again — the corporations regard it as a disease that occasionally produces a clan noteworthy enough to be wiped out, which is why `recognized-2` cuts both ways.
- **[OPEN]** (OQ-4) The exact vector of transmission is not settled and this Special is written so that it never needs to be: it deals in lineage, not in mechanism, and it explicitly refuses to answer *how*. "How I was made" stays a player-answered question ([[the-ferrante-strain]] tag *the one who made me*).
- **[RULES CONFLICT]** (CR-7) the condition of having been made persists regardless of themes.
- Rarity (Bible §2): meeting another carrier is an event, not a weekly occurrence, which is why the use is once per **job** and the prerequisite is having met one at all.
- Masquerade (Bible §3): nothing here makes a carrier legible to anyone who is not one. `recognized-2` passes between carriers.
- What this Special does **not** grant: any knowledge of what [[palisade-player-primer#Continuity Risk & Response|Continuity Risk & Response]] is, or that anyone with sway knows anything. That is MC-only ([[bloodware]]) and no amount of lineage tracing reaches it from the street.

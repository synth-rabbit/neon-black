# Field-Bitten

**Splat:** vampire · **Prerequisite:** Your character carries the Ferrante strain, and an electromagnetic field or effect has put them down at least once in play.

You have been in one and you will feel the next one coming. Once per scene, before entering a space, you may ask the MC whether an electromagnetic field, emitter or effect is active in it and get a straight answer; if there is one, you gain `braced-2` against it. In exchange, the strain never forgets: whenever you take a status caused by an electromagnetic field, weapon or effect, **increase its tier by 1**, and you may **not** mitigate it with tags from Noise themes — the colony is the thing being taken apart, so it cannot be the thing that saves you. Self and Mythos tags mitigate normally, which for most Bloodware means finding out how much of a person is left under the strain.

## Persistence

Chosen instead of a theme upgrade in any of the four main themes (Tokyo p. 72). Recorded on the character card and untouched by theme replacement (Brief §2.4). The vulnerability half is **not removable**: EM is one of the two things that ends a Bloodware and it is not a phase (Bible §2). A character who stops working at it may drop the forewarning half for an Evolution point and keep the penalty.

## Canon and flags

- The persistent condition carried: **the EM weakness**. Bible §2 — electromagnetic attacks and effects, which with the master node *replace* the classic vampire weaknesses entirely. No other vulnerability from the classic list is available anywhere on this card.
- **[RULES CONFLICT]** (CR-7) the condition persists while the theme need not.
- Pairs with [[where-the-kernel-sits]]: between them a Bloodware PC carries both of the only two things that can finish them, on the card, permanently. NPC Bloodware carry the same pair as Limits (`bloodware-power-set`, Plan A.6).

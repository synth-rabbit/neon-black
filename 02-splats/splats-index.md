# Splats Index

The five character packages (Plan WP2), one folder per splat under its stable role-word slug (BC-7): `vampire/` **Bloodware**, `werewolf/` **Howlers**, `mage/` **Casters**, `changeling/` **Doppels**, `hunter/` **Baselines**. Each folder holds a player-safe setting overview, its MC-only notes (split out by WP8, BC-176), `theme-kits/` (custom kits plus a `<splat>-existing-kits` list of printed kits to offer), `tropes/`, `pc-specials/` (the persistent-condition Specials, Brief §2.4), and `power-sets/` (NPC overlays — MC-only). Printed kits are referenced as `book:<slug>` and indexed once in [[book-kits-index]] (BC-126).

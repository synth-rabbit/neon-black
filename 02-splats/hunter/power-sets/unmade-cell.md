# Unmade Cell

**Applies to:** Any Challenge representing a radicalized cell of anti-cybernetic extremists · **Category:** Self · **Splat:** hunter

Most people who call themselves Unmade picket and organize and never raise a hand. This is the fringe that decided persuasion wasn't fast enough — true believers with homemade gear, picking targets they think deserve it: a chop-shop, a warranty-clinic recruiter, a Sculpted exec who made the wrong speech. They are not the movement; they are what a movement produces when it curdles, and [[the-unmade]]'s own members would say so.

## Tags & statuses

homemade EMP gear, true believers, picked their target

## Specials

**EMP Cell:** Pull out a homemade EMP charge: fry a target's active chrome (*glitching-3*), or clear a room of active mods (create a story tag such as *dead zone*).

**Righteous Cause:** Once per scene, when this Challenge is threatened or offered a bribe, they gain *resolute-1* instead of complying.

## Threats / Consequences

› Corner someone visibly modified.
» Make an example of them in public (*beaten-3*).

› Plant an EMP charge near a target's rig.
» Detonate it (*glitching-4*).

› Pressure a bystander to join or look away.
» Present a New Challenge — a sympathizer, or a witness who won't talk.

## Canon and flags

- Bible §2's anti-cybernetic-extremist family, taken to its violent edge; entirely Self-category and entirely human — no chrome, no Tao, consistent with hunter canon.
- No power asymmetry intended against the crew: this cell is dangerous through preparation and conviction, the same currency any Baseline PC trades in (Bible §2 rarity).
- MC-only, per `02-splats/hunter/power-sets/` (README "Player-safe vs MC-only").

# Bonded Tracker

**Splat:** hunter · **Built toward:** Real

You work contract to contract for whichever corp or precinct signs the paper — tracking down the investigations too extreme for a beat cop and too deniable for corp security to touch directly. You're good, you're bonded, and you're the first name on the press release if any of it goes wrong. That's the job.

## On the sheet

All four theme kits are Self — this trope lands squarely on **Real** (Core p. 135; Brief §2.3). No Augmentation or Cutting Edge theme is part of this build; the Bonded Tracker's edge is entirely conviction, training, gear, and paperwork, and their Essence Special (roll with Self to uphold an Identity) rewards exactly that.

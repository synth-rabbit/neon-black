# Corporate Muscle

**Splat:** hunter · **Built toward:** Cyborg

You're the reason a corp's enclave doesn't need to call in anything worse. No implants, no bio-work — just a company-owned hardsuit signed out of the armory, a badge that means the same thing a Chancery badge does on corp ground, and a job description that includes standing between whatever's happening and whoever's paying you. When people ask what a corp's "extreme security response" looks like, you're often the honest, human answer.

# The Unmade

**Themebook:** Affiliation · **Category:** Self · **Splat:** hunter · **Source:** custom

Not every Baseline holds the line alone. The Unmade are the organized end of it — a public, unashamed movement built around the belief that a body shouldn't be for sale, least of all to whichever corp is selling this decade's ladder rung. They picket outside warranty clinics, run safehouses that ask no chrome of anyone who walks in, and treat "sold out" as the worst thing a member can be called. The street calls them **Raw** — mildly contemptuous, the way the Face talks about anyone who hasn't been up to a clinic.

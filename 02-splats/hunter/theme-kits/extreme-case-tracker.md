# Extreme-Case Tracker

**Themebook:** Expertise · **Category:** Self · **Splat:** hunter · **Source:** custom

You learned to read the tells nobody else takes seriously: the EM meter that spikes near a person who checks out clean on every other scan, the chemical trace a bench chemist would swear was recreational, a face that moves a half-second wrong. You never had to be told what any of it means — you just learned to spot it before it got worse. This is the trade the Bible's bounty hunters, corp cleanup contractors, and investigators all share, whatever they call themselves.

## Specials

Inherits Expertise's five (Core p. 205): Evolving Technique, Flexible Discipline, If at First You Don't Succeed, Sense of Purpose, Strength from Adversity.

## Nascent form

Title tag **extreme-case specialist** + weakness **paperwork buries the tell** still reads as a whole theme: someone who does this work and knows exactly how the system keeps them from finishing it.

## Canon and flags

- Bible §2: cybernetics-free tracking of the other four's public tells; the tags never name what a "case" actually is, only how it presents — appropriate for a player-safe kit that must not spoil MC secrets.
- Tag J is this package's expression of the Bible's "hunters may use Tao indirectly or at a low level" (Bible §2) as gear-knowledge rather than a Mythos theme (Brief §2.1).
- Rarity note: the credentials tag (G) and the burying-the-tell weakness (D) both frame this as a small, licensed, precarious trade — not a common one; most Baselines with a gun never see an extreme case up close.
- **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-91) Kit name and all ten power tags/four weakness tags are invented for this package; no tag reveals which of the other four produces which tell (EM/Bloodware, Leash/Howlers, faces/Doppels, Tao-sense/Casters are implied, never stated).

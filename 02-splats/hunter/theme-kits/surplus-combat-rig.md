# Surplus Combat Rig

**Themebook:** Cutting Edge · **Category:** Noise · **Splat:** hunter · **Source:** custom

Nobody builds a hardsuit for a bounty hunter. You bought this one off a corp disposal lot, won it in a contract, or pulled it off someone who didn't need it anymore — mil-spec, over-built, and still one generation ahead of anything a civilian could buy new. It was designed for a bigger war than the one you're fighting, and it shows: too loud, too heavy, and worth every problem it causes the moment you need to hit harder than your own body can.

## Specials

Inherits Cutting Edge's five (Core p. 241): Amp Up, Modular Design, Occult Blueprints, Still in Prototype, Void the Warranty. Occult Blueprints (answer an Esoterica or Exposure power tag question) is the mechanical route for a Baseline whose surplus rig has picked up a Tao-touched retrofit, without giving the character a Mythos theme of their own (Bible §2 indirect/low-level Tao use).

## Nascent form

Title tag **surplus combat rig** + weakness **can't be worn quiet** still reads as a whole theme: heavy, borrowed, decisive muscle that costs you every subtle option.

## Canon and flags

- Bible §2: a battle suit / weapon system, worn, never implanted — the Cutting Edge line of hunter tech Brief §2.1 authorizes as an optional secondary.
- Rarity note: tags H and I frame the rig as a specific, hard-won, one-off acquisition (an auction lot, a contract prize), not standard gear every Baseline owns.

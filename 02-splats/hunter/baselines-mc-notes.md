# Baselines — MC Notes

The MC-only section split out of [[baselines]] by WP8 (BC-176) so that the overview can be handed to a player whole. The text below is the owning package's, moved verbatim; it is not for the session-zero packet ([[session-zero-packet]]).

*Everything above this line is player-safe. This section is not — it restates and cross-references MC-only canon and is not for the table.*

Bible §2 and §3: when an extreme mod goes visibly out of control in public, the corporations' press releases blame a Baseline. Palisade's Masquerade model (`05-megacity/palisade.md`, "The Masquerade as Noise plus cleanup" — note: that file's slug is also `overview`, so it is not wikilinked here to avoid colliding with this one) spells out why the cover is cheap to run: the Noise does most of the work, and "another baseline with a gun" is boring enough that nobody follows up (Brief §4.1). The cover story itself dates from the Ferrante cleanup roughly a century ago, per `05-megacity/timeline.md` (**[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-25), not the Bible's own timing).

This package does not invent who is actually responsible for any given incident, or which Key Player's cleanup crew is running point — that is WP4's twist to write. What it provides for MC use:

- [[another-baseline-with-a-gun]] — the PC-facing mechanical hook for a player character who has already been the cover story once.
- A pressure lever available in any job: an NPC Baseline (see [[corp-bonded-extraction-team]] for a corp-bonded one) can be the person a crew has to get past, get information from, or get blamed alongside, without the scene ever touching a secret splat directly.
- A standing tension the crew can lean into or fight: taking credit for an incident a PC didn't cause buys cover; refusing the story and clearing a name draws the kind of attention [[secret-war-goes-public]] tracks.

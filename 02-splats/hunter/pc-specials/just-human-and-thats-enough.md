# Just Human, and That's Enough

**Splat:** hunter · **Prerequisite:** Essence Real (all four themes Self).

Once per session, when you refuse an offer of power — a mod, a Tao working, power borrowed from one of the other four — that would compromise your Essence, treat the refusal as a strong hit (10+) regardless of the roll, and gain *resolute-2*. This stacks on top of the Real Essence Special (roll with Self to uphold an Identity, Core p. 135): it's what a table can hand a Baseline who has gone all the way to Real, on purpose, and wants the choice to matter mechanically, not just narratively.

## Persistence

Tied to Essence rather than to any single theme, so it survives ordinary theme replacement as long as the replacement theme is also Self. Removed automatically, for narrative reasons, the moment the character's Essence stops being Real; mark an Evolution point as normal.

## Canon and flags

- The persistent condition this carries: none in the bodily sense — this Special exists to give a fully-Real Baseline a mechanical payoff for the choice itself, matching Bible §2's "no intended power asymmetry" between the five (a Baseline who stays purely human is not meant to read as the weak one at the table).

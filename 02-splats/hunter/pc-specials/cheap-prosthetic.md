# Cheap Prosthetic

**Splat:** hunter · **Prerequisite:** a lost limb or organ, replaced with cybernetics under the Bible's replacement-only rule.

Fitted in a [[palisade-player-primer#Suture Row|Suture Row]] chop-shop, off an AP&I loaner line, or paid for with real money and a real warranty on the Crest — the fitting shop changes with the caste band, the rule doesn't. You've replaced a limb with a prosthetic you can easily fix or replace. When mitigating Consequences that you can prevent by sacrificing this prosthetic — blocking a blade, stepping in molten metal, absorbing an EM surge meant for the rest of you — you may have the limb take the status instead, giving it *damaged* or a similar Effect that affects the use of the prosthetic until you repair it (Tokyo p. 73).

## Persistence

Survives theme replacement (Brief §2.4) — even if the [[standard-issue-replacement]] Augmentation theme Decays away and is replaced with something else, the prosthetic itself is still there; the theme card tracked your *attention* to it, not its existence. Removed only for narrative reasons; mark an Evolution point (Tokyo p. 72) — and, per canon, that removal can mean the prosthetic comes off for good, not just that the character stops thinking about it (Brief §2.4).

## Canon and flags

- The persistent condition this carries: a visible, mechanical, repairable body part that reads on scanners and draws questions — the one cybernetic hook a Baseline is allowed to have, and the one that Bible canon lets a character walk away from.
- Adapted from Tokyo's Cheap Prosthetic Megacity Special (Tokyo p. 73), named as the explicit template in Brief §2.5.

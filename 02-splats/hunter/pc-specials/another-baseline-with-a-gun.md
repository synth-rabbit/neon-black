# Another Baseline With a Gun

**Splat:** hunter · **Prerequisite:** already a public face for one incident that wasn't yours.

The city already has its story about what you are — the cover for every extreme mod that flares up where witnesses can see (Bible §2, §3). Once per scene, when a public incident happens nearby that isn't your doing, you may let the story stick: gain *overlooked-2*, which helps you get clear, hide what you saw, or protect someone else's secret, for as long as the story holds. When the story finally doesn't stick — the footage is too good, the timing too exact — you instead take *blamed-3*, which hinders your standing with whoever is running the cleanup until you clear it, or until it's absorbed into someone else's story instead.

## Persistence

Survives theme replacement — this Special is about reputation and public record, not any single theme, so nothing about replacing a theme makes it go away. Removed only for narrative reasons; mark an Evolution point.

## Canon and flags

- The persistent condition this carries: the public cover role itself (Bible §2, §3; Brief §4.1) — the one hunter condition that is social rather than bodily, since a Baseline has no biological weakness to model the way the other four do.
- **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-95) Name and mechanics invented for this package, built directly on the corporate press-release line already established for Palisade ("another baseline with a gun," `05-megacity/palisade.md`).

# Baselines

![[assets/splats/baselines.png]]

**Splat:** hunter · **Reads as:** the one who stayed human (`names` §1) · **Primary:** Self · **Secondary (optional):** Noise · **Built toward:** Real / Cyborg (Core p. 135; Brief §2.3)

## What a Baseline is

A Baseline is whatever a human being can become without stopping being human. Bounty hunters who work contract to contract. Corporate muscle who never took the corp's own chrome. Investigators, medics, cops, chop-shop surgeons who see everything the ecosystem produces and cut none of it into themselves. People who watched a mod go wrong once and decided the line was worth keeping. The Bible names three families to start from — **bounty hunters, anti-cybernetic extremists, and corporate muscle** — and says plainly that Baselines are *deliberately broad*: anything human that doesn't fit one of the other four counts. Build past the three families freely. A street medic who stitches up what the ecosystem breaks, a caste-mobility organizer, a licensed arena judge who certifies fighters' mods, an off-duty Chancery investigator working casework nobody wants — all of it is Baseline.

**What makes a Baseline rare isn't the gun.** Half of Palisade owns a gun. A Baseline, in the sense that matters at this table, is the rare person who has trained, geared, and hardened themselves enough to stand in a room with something that used to be human and isn't anymore, and walk out again — without ever taking the shortcut the room's other occupant took. Being armed is common. Being *this* good at it, on purpose, without the chrome, is not (Bible §2 rarity; Brief §2.6). **No power asymmetry is intended**: a Baseline is not the crew's weak link who makes up for it with numbers. What a Baseline trades for staying human is not raw power — it's a different kind of power, the kind Real and Cyborg pay out (below).

## The line, and why it's held

Cybernetics are on the table for a Baseline only **as replacements for lost parts** — an arm, a leg, an eye, an organ. **Body sculpting and bio-manipulation are never on the table.** This is the line the Bible draws between "still human" and one of the other four (Bible §2), and it is the one rule every Baseline theme kit in this package obeys without exception.

Different Baselines hold that line for different reasons — Baselines are built from individual conviction, not a shared origin story, and no one reason should be treated as *the* reason:

- **Money.** A Stock-band Baseline never chose "unmodified" over a warranty; a warranty was never on offer (`names` §4).
- **Conviction.** Some hold it as a belief worth organizing around — see [[the-unmade]].
- **The job.** Certain contracts, licenses, and testimony (bonded bounty work, some Chancery and arena certifications) are worth more, or worth anything at all, from someone whose body a court or a client can trust wasn't rebuilt for the occasion.
- **A bad memory.** A chop-shop job gone wrong on a friend, a Suture Row surgery table story, a squad-mate who Augmented past the point anyone recognized them — reasons enough to never sit in that chair.
- **Nothing more complicated than stubbornness.** "I am who I am, and no outside force gets to say otherwise" (Core p. 135) is a whole personality, not just an Essence description.

A lost limb, eye, or organ is the one door that stays open — see [[cheap-prosthetic]].

## Mapping (Brief §2.1)

| Category | Role | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **Self** | Required primary | Expertise, Assets, or Affiliation — the trade, the gear, or the people who made you who you are |
| **Noise** | Optional secondary | Cutting Edge gear (battle suits, weapon systems), or an Augmentation kit *only* if every tag is a replacement, never an enhancement |
| **Mythos** | Not built here | The Bible allows indirect, low-level Tao use (Bible §2); this package expresses it as gear (a Cutting Edge Special, see [[extreme-case-tracker]]) rather than a dedicated Mythos theme — Brief §2.1 does not list Mythos as an optional secondary for hunters the way it does for changelings or mages |

**Essence minimum (BC-1):** no restriction beyond the Augmentation rule above — a Baseline may run all four Self themes, or three Self plus one Noise. There is no hunter theme that requires Mythos.

## Essence: built toward Real and Cyborg (Brief §2.3; Core p. 135)

This package designs every hunter kit and trope on purpose to land a Baseline on one of two Essences:

- **Real (only Self)** — four Self themes, nothing else. *"You have chosen to remain yourself, eschewing fancy baubles and the pull of past narratives… no outside force — mythical or technological — can say otherwise."* The **Real Essence Special**: whenever you take action to directly uphold or protect one of your Identities, you may roll with Self instead of counting positive tags (Core p. 135). Mechanically, this is what "just human is enough" buys a Baseline — a Baseline who keeps their word, on the sheet.
- **Cyborg (Self and Noise)** — three Self themes plus one Noise (typically a Cutting Edge battle-suit or weapon-system kit, or the replacement-only Augmentation kit). *"You are flesh, wires, and data… paying no heed to this new and incalculable 'magic.'"* The **Cyborg Essence Special**: once per session with your Self total and once per session with your Noise total (or both together in the same action), add that number to the Power of any action to resist or shake off mythical forces that aren't manifested as tangible, measurable effects — curses, hallucinations, mental influence (Core p. 135). Mechanically: a Baseline built out of grit and gear is hard to enchant.

A Baseline who takes on Mythos content, or an Augmentation kit that isn't replacement-framed, drifts toward Nexus, Spiritualist, or Transhuman — none of that is forbidden by the rules (BC-1: "Essence is otherwise free"), it just isn't what this package is built toward, and an Augmentation kit that isn't replacement-framed breaks Bible canon outright (see [[hunter-existing-kits]]).

## Persistence (Brief §2.4; CR-7)

Each of the other four in this vault carries an irreversible condition that survives its theme's Decay. A Baseline is the one exception the Brief names outright: **replacement cybernetics can be removed** — consistent with canon, since a Baseline was never transformed into anything, only repaired. [[cheap-prosthetic]] carries that hook and is the only "you can't get away from your own body" Special a Baseline needs.

What *doesn't* wash off is social, not biological: being the name on the press release. See [[another-baseline-with-a-gun]], and the MC-only note below.

## Motivation (BC-2)

- **Identity** on every Self theme, including the primary one — a flat conviction in the book's register (Core p. 177): *"I call it what it is, whatever the paperwork says."* is the register to write toward, not a specific required line.
- **Itch** on any Cutting Edge (or replacement-Augmentation) theme taken as the optional secondary — an impulse to indulge, pulling toward *using* the gear, never toward becoming more than baseline.

## On the sheet

A Baseline is built almost entirely from **Self** themebooks — Expertise, Assets, Affiliation, and (less centrally to this package but not excluded) Horizon, Personality, Troubled Past — plus, optionally, one **Cutting Edge** theme. Four Self kits reads as **Real**; three Self plus one Cutting Edge (or the replacement Augmentation kit) reads as **Cyborg**. A table running rapid creation should expect most Baselines to land on one of those two Essences; nothing stops a stranger build, but nothing in this package points toward one.

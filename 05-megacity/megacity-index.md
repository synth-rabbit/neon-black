# Megacity Index

Palisade (Plan WP1): the city file, the timeline, the Generator run that produced the eighteen districts, the districts themselves, and — new in WP8 — the player primer. The player-facing page is [[palisade-player-primer]] (`player_safe: true`; what any resident knows); every other file here is MC-only, and the character-creation files point at the primer's sections rather than at the district files (BC-177).

Read [[palisade]] first, then [[district-directory]] (zone codes, story tags, the mandatory-placement map), then [[timeline]], then the districts by tier: **Crest** [[aurelian-crest]] · [[chancery-hill]] · [[meliora-terraces]] · [[orison-reach]] — **Face** [[amalgam-stack]] · [[the-lattice]] · [[corbel-gallery]] · [[halloran-circus]] — **Foot** [[suture-row]] · [[gullet-market]] · [[marlow-blocks]] · [[cinder-yards]] · [[relay-fields]] — **Under** [[kilbride-stretch]] · [[lowmere-sinks]] · [[foundation-galleries]] · [[ferrante-basin]] · [[coldwater-outfall]].

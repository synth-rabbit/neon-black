# Corbel Gallery

![[assets/districts/corbel-gallery.png]]

**Zone:** 31-32 · **Tier:** Face · **Pillar:** Transhumanism · **Caste band:** Fitted · **Story tag:** *judged by your brand*

## Central concept

A corbel is the bracket that holds a balcony to a wall. Corbel Gallery is the balcony: a commercial arcade six kilometers long and eight storeys deep, cut into the Face and roofed in glass, running the length of the Wall between [[amalgam-stack]] and [[halloran-circus]] — the shopping promenade of the book's Commercial District (Core p. 41) stretched into a single continuous street in the sky. Boutiques, clinics, showrooms, cafés, gene-salons, implant bars, the flagship stores of every corporation on the Wall, all under one unrelenting AR overlay so thick that "some retail shops don't even bother to decorate their physical space" (Core p. 41).

This is the Fitted tier at its most Fitted. The residents of the apartment decks behind the arcade are corporate-warrantied bodies with corporate credit lines, and the Gallery is where they spend both. Everything here is clean, bright, monitored, and priced just above what a Fitted salary can bear, so that everyone on the Gallery is a little in debt to somebody, and shows it in the newest thing on their face. The sculpted entertainers the public is allowed to know about (Bible §3) work the Gallery's stages and screens; the AR is theirs, and every window is a mirror that suggests an improvement.

Below the arcade, the Gallery's underdecks — the service corridors, the stockrooms, the Patched staff entrance from the lift-heads — are the same shops seen from behind. That is where the Foot works when it works on the Face.

## Developments

1. **Fashion is Identity — the trend hotspot** (Gangs, Cults & Counterculture, chosen) — Every cosmetic-implant trend in Palisade is born on the Gallery and dead there a month later: subdermal light this season, ridged cheekbones the last, a run on cat-pupil lenses that ended when the Circus's fighters started wearing them. The clinics on the upper decks install on credit; the implant bars on the lower ones do it in an hour with a drink. It is not sculpting — that is the Crest's — and it is not chrome in the AP&I sense; it is the ladder's decoration, and the Fitted climb it a face at a time. Whether any of it makes anyone more than human is not the point. The point is being seen to afford it.
2. **Corporate Rules — judged by brand** (Oligarchic Corruption, chosen) — On the Gallery you are what you wear and who warrantied it. A Meliora face outranks an AP&I hand; a Continuity security tag on your collar gets you a table; an unbranded implant marks you Patched no matter what deck you sleep on, and the shop AR quietly stops suggesting improvements and starts suggesting the lift. The Gallery's brand-etiquette is the caste system's fine print — not law, not a marker, but the thing that makes both feel inevitable (Bible §3: "slowly becoming formalized"). **[OPEN]** (OQ-8) Nothing here is formal. Everything here acts as if it were.

## Who holds ground here

- [[corp-c|AP&I]] — flagship showrooms and the warranty-service counters; the Gallery is where AP&I's product is sold to people who are not its employees.
- [[corp-a|Meliora]] — the gene-salons and the sculpting "consultations" that send customers up the Wall.
- Nobody criminal, visibly. The Gallery is too well lit. What the Almoners and the Weighhouse do here, they do in the underdecks.

## Places

- **The Corbel Mile** — The arcade's central stretch, where the roof rises to a full cathedral vault and the flagship stores face each other across a promenade of moving walkways and AR fountains. The most surveilled public space on the Face and the easiest to disappear in, because nobody is looking at anything real. A Marketplace and a Meeting Place at once: a crew that dresses the part can meet anyone here in plain sight.
- **Lumen** — An implant bar on the fourth deck, all white leather and blue light: an hour, a drink, a subdermal, a warranty card that will bounce next month. Its back room does uncatalogued work for people who can pay and do not want a record, and its owner buys the Gallery's discarded last-season implants by the sack and sends them down to [[suture-row]]. The Gallery's one doorway to the Foot that is not a staff entrance.
- **The underdecks** — Service corridors, stockrooms, freight lifts, and a staff canteen that is the Gallery's only honest restaurant, all behind the shops and all Patched. A Fringe Zone in the middle of the Face. The Weighhouse's runners work it; so does anyone who wants to get from the lift-heads to the Circus without being seen on the Mile.

## Hooks

- A Fitted client of Lumen's back room has a warranty tag that reads as installed and an implant that is not; someone at AP&I wants to know who did the work.
- A trend starts on the Gallery that is not cosmetic — a lens that sees the data fog — and the corporation it belongs to did not release it.
- The crew needs to walk the Corbel Mile as Fitted for one afternoon, and every brand they are wearing says otherwise.

# Lowmere Sinks

![[assets/districts/lowmere-sinks.png]]

**Zone:** 55-56 · **Tier:** Under · **Pillar:** Cities in Ruins · **Caste band:** Stock · **Story tag:** *the pumps are running late*

## Central concept

The bottom of the city, in every sense. South of the Stretch the lowland was once salt marsh; Palisade drained it behind levees a century and a half ago, built on it, and has been pumping it dry ever since — and the pumps are old, the levees are older, and the sea has been rising the whole time. **The Sinks** are the result: a district below sea level where the streets flood to the knee at a high tide and to the roofline in a storm, where the ground floors of everything were abandoned to the water a generation ago and life moved up to the second storey and the rooftops, where boats are more use than cars and the pump-houses are the only buildings anyone maintains. The book's Undercity and Urban Wilds at once (Core p. 42): buried layers, blank spots in the surveillance net, "homes to violent gangs … and secret corp operations," and "almost every part … someone's turf."

The residents are Stock — the poorest in Palisade, the ones the Stretch looks down on — living in rooftop shanties, on stilt-walks between drowned blocks, in the pump-houses, on the water. They fish the flooded streets, strip the drowned buildings, and keep the pumps running because nobody else will. *The pumps are running late* is what they say when the water is rising and the Chancery's utility board has not sent the fuel.

The Sinks are also where the Run hunts. [[packs|The Run]] come down from [[cinder-yards]] at night, on Leash, across the levees, and the Sinks' families lock the stilt-walks and wait for dawn.

## Developments

1. **Losing Humanity → the hoards** (Tao Weaponized, roll 56-52-63-3) — **[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** The book's result is *residents hoard and worship Sources to escape their humanity*. Nothing is worshiped. But the Sinks' families *hoard*: Tao-touched scrap fished out of the drowned blocks — Tao is older than the mods (Bible §1), and the marsh was inhabited before the levees — and fragments of practice passed down as household habit: the way a pump is started, the words said over a net, the stilt-walk that is never crossed left-foot-first. None of it is understood and some of it works. The hoards are the Sinks' only wealth, kept in waterproof chests under rooftop floors, and the buyers who come down from the Weighhouse know what they are looking for even when the families do not. Whether the marsh is a place where Tao runs dense is **[OPEN]** (OQ-14) and the Sinks assert nothing.
2. **Bodies Modified — skill chips** (Transhumanism, roll 55-65-23-2) — There is no school in the Sinks; there is a chip. Secondhand skill chips from the Row, socketed into a cheap port behind the ear, give a Stock kid a trade for the price of a week's fish: pump maintenance, boat handling, field surgery, three languages, the basics of Harnessing. The chips are worn, the ports are Patched work, and the knowledge is someone else's — but it is the only ladder the Sinks have, and the families trade chips the way the Crest trades tutors.
3. **Technological Singularity → the answering farms** (Transhumanism, roll 55-65-14-2) — Rumor, and kept as rumor. Under the water, in the drowned ground floors of the Sinks' east side, are the server farms of three corporations that died before the levees failed — racks still powered by a grid nobody has cut, running on whatever they were running when the water came in. The Sinks' Harnessing kids, who go in through the flooded windows on borrowed rigs, say something in there *answers*: not a program, not a rogue AI in the book's sense (Core p. 33), but something that talks like a person and does not know it is drowned. The book's result is *"human" consciousnesses are born in cyberspace*; the Sinks say only that the farms answer back. **[OPEN]** (OQ-15) Whether what answers has anything to do with Tao — whether cyberspace has Tao in it at all — is not decided here.
4. **Corporate Domains — the abandoned workspace** (Sprawling Cyberspace, roll 55-46-16-3) — Compounding the above: one of the drowned farms still hosts an abandoned corporate workspace Domain, online, unguarded, its offices and meeting rooms rendered in the corporate style of a company that no longer exists, its files intact. The Sinks' kids use it as a clubhouse. Data miners from the Fields have started paying the kids for the door.

## Who holds ground here

- [[packs|The Run]] — hunting ground, not turf. The packs cross the levees at night; the Sinks belong to the families by day.
- Nobody corporate, nobody Chancery. The utility board sends fuel for the pumps when it is paid to; the police do not come. The Sinks are the one district in Palisade where no Key Player holds ground, which is why things end up here.

## Places

- **Pump-house Six** — The largest of the Sinks' pump stations, a brick fortress on a levee with four diesel pumps the size of trucks and a family that has kept them running for three generations: the district's Seat of Power (Core p. 43), such as it is, where the families' council meets and the fuel is rationed. Anyone who wants the Sinks' help asks here. Anyone who wants the Sinks drowned starts here.
- **The stilt-walks** — The plank bridges and cable-ways strung between rooftops across the flooded streets, the Sinks' only roads: narrow, unlit, swaying, lockable at every landing, and never crossed left-foot-first. A Fringe Zone kilometers long and the best place in Palisade to lose a pursuer who cannot swim.
- **The drowned farms** — The east side's flooded server halls, entered through broken windows a meter under the tide: racks in black water, indicator lights still blinking green, a Harnessing rig floating on a raft in the dark, and a Domain that answers. A Secured Site by water rather than by guards, and a Meeting Place for the Sinks' kids and whoever they let in.

## Hooks

- A Weighhouse buyer wants a family's hoard and the family wants the pumps' fuel for a year; Tally will broker it if the crew will carry it.
- A pack came across the levees last night and did not go back, and the Sinks' council has offered the crew what it has to find out why.
- The drowned farms' Domain has started asking the kids who use it about a name — one of the crew's.

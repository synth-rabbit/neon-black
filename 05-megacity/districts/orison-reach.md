# Orison Reach

![[assets/districts/orison-reach.png]]

**Zone:** 21-22 · **Tier:** Crest · **Pillar:** Oligarchic Corruption · **Caste band:** Sculpted · **Story tag:** *the guns face outward*

## Central concept

The seaward end of the Crest, where the plateau narrows to a headland and the Wall runs out into the water: [[corp-b|Orison Defense Systems]]' headquarters, proving grounds, and armories, walled off from the rest of the Crest by a second, lower palisade of Orison's own. Street name *Orison* ([[names]] §2). The specialty is **weapons** (BC-8, GM-approved): small arms, security systems, the drones over the Face, the sidearms on the Chancery's police, the heavy platforms the Big Three keep in reserve for the war they are not fighting.

The Reach is a fortress-district in the Tokyo Five Rings pattern — "equal parts awe-inspiring and terrifying," its people housed in blocky family barracks inside the walls (Tokyo p. 31) — and it is the most *orderly* place in Palisade. Clean, drilled, quiet. The Sculpted here are officers and executives whose sculpt is for endurance and reaction time, not beauty; the Fitted are engineers and range crews; there are no Stock inside the inner wall at all. Orison hires no Stock for the Reach; its stated reason is clearance, and nobody on the Crest has ever needed a second one.

The name is a prayer, and reads to everyone on the Wall as empty corporate Latin ([[names]] §2). **What the district does not show:** Orison is controlled by the Wuji (Bible §2, §3). Nothing in the Reach reads as that. The Reach reads as a weapons company that keeps its secrets the way weapons companies do, and the other two corporations answer it with anti-Tao countermeasures and rebel practitioners of their own (Bible §2) — which, if you knew what to look for, is the only tell there is.

## Developments

1. **War for Power — the installation** (Oligarchic Corruption, chosen) — The Reach is a major military installation in all but name: a headland of proving grounds where the guns face outward over the water and the test detonations rattle windows on the Crest, hardened armories cut into the rock, a drone field, a private port at the headland's foot where Orison's own ships come and go without Chancery customs. It is the only place in Palisade where a corporation openly keeps an army, and the civil agreements ([[spine-and-clock]]) are the reason nobody objects: all three corporations agreed, long ago, who would keep the heavy weapons, and Orison has kept them ever since.
2. **Isolationist Enclave — the range towers** (Gangs, Cults & Counterculture, roll 22-22-15-5) — At the Reach's landward edge, outside the inner wall, stand the old test-range towers: a row of hollow concrete shells Orison built decades ago to shoot at, and stopped shooting at when the ranges moved seaward. Squatters hold them now — a few hundred Patched and Stock who came up the Wall on service contracts and never went down, fortified inside the shot-pocked towers, fending off Orison's eviction crews with scavenged range equipment that is, after all, Orison's. Orison could clear them in an afternoon. It has not. Nobody knows why, and the towers have become the one place on the Crest a Patched crew can sleep.

## Who holds ground here

- [[corp-b|Orison Defense Systems]] — the Reach entire. Orison security is the only law inside the walls and the Chancery's police do not enter.
- [[tao-society|The Wuji]] — hidden. Their control of Orison is exercised from inside the executive floors and is visible nowhere in the district; MC-only, and not to be revealed by any scene set here without the GM's decision.

## Places

- **The Reach Armory** — The headquarters proper: a squat, windowless block at the headland's neck with Orison's name on it in letters a meter high, executive floors underground, and the armories below those. The most Secured Site on the Wall (Core p. 43): layered checkpoints, drone coverage, a Nearspace so hardened it reads as dead, and a security force that has never, as far as the street knows, lost a fight. Every acquisition job in Palisade is measured against it and most are not attempted.
- **The seaward ranges** — Kilometers of headland where Orison tests: cratered ground, target hulks, observation bunkers, the sea beyond. Detonations at dawn. A Warzone that is not at war, and a Fringe Zone for anyone who can get onto it — which, between tests, is easier than the Armory makes it look, because the ranges face the water and the fence faces the city.
- **Tower Four** — The largest of the squatted range towers: twelve hollow floors of concrete around an open core, the shot-holes glazed with salvage, cook-fires on the landings, a lift that works because someone Fitted rebuilt it from Orison parts. The squatters' council meets on the ninth floor. A Living Space at the top of the Wall for people who should not be there, and a Meeting Place for anyone who wants to talk to Orison without Orison knowing.

## Hooks

- Orison hires the crew, through a cut-out, to *not* clear Tower Four — to find out what the squatters found in the tower's foundations that has kept the eviction crews away.
- A Meliora countermeasure team wants a Patched crew to walk a package onto the seaward ranges during a test and leave it there; they do not say what it detects.
- An Orison engineer wants out — off the Reach, down the Wall, with a case of something she says is "not a weapon" — and has heard the Weighhouse in [[gullet-market]] takes anything.

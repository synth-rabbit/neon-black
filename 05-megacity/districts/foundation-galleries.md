# Foundation Galleries

![[assets/districts/foundation-galleries.png]]

**Zone:** 61-62 · **Tier:** Under · **Pillar:** Gangs, Cults & Counterculture · **Caste band:** Stock · **Story tag:** *the Wall has hollow bones*

## Central concept

The Wall is not solid. Two centuries of flood barrier, seawall, foundation, and stacked construction ([[palisade|Palisade]], BC-15) left it honeycombed at the base: construction galleries, drainage tunnels, pump chambers, service ways, cable runs, and the sealed voids between one generation's concrete and the next, running the whole length of the escarpment's foot and up into the cliff behind the Foot's districts. **The Galleries** are the book's Undercity exactly (Core p. 42) — "buried layers like a river filling with silt," forgotten tunnels, abandoned shelters, "small communities of people who have nowhere else to go" — and Palisade's deepest place: under the Gullet, under the Row, under the Blocks, in the dark, in the Wall's own bones.

Nobody governs the Galleries. The Chancery's maps of them are a century out of date; the corporations' security does not go below the Foot; the water comes and goes with the pumps. The residents are whoever went down and stayed: Stock with nowhere else, debtors hiding from the Kitchen, escapees hiding from the Ledger, and — deepest, best hidden, and the reason this district is on the map — [[changeling-cells|the Cutloose]].

The Cutloose are Doppels who got out: removed their kill switches and trackers, and now live in constant danger for it, grouped with other escapees, purely underground and with no political pull (Bible §2, §3). The Galleries are underground in the literal sense, and a Doppel's whole survival is not being found. So the refuge is not a district *within* the Galleries; it is a rumor within a rumor — a place called **Gallery Nine** that nobody who has not been taken there can find.

## Developments

1. **Mass Migration — underground** (Cities in Ruins, chosen) — "Due to weather or other threats, most residents migrate underground" (Core p. 165). The Galleries' population came down in waves: after the floods that took the Sinks' ground floors, after the Blocks' lifts failed, after every Chancery sweep of the Foot. What began as shelter became settlement — cook-fires in pump chambers, hammocks in cable runs, a market in a drainage vault — and what began as settlement became the Under's last refuge, because nobody official has ever come this far down and come back with a report.
2. **Total Social Disintegration — the concrete jungle** (Oligarchic Corruption, roll 62-35-56-1) — Survival is all that matters here. There is no council as in the Sinks, no Kitchen as in the Blocks; there are people who have what you need and people who take it. The Galleries' law is the pump schedule — when the water comes, everyone moves up, and whoever does not drowns — and the rest is settled in the dark. It is the one place in Palisade where a Fitted body is a liability, because it can be tracked, and a Stock body is worth exactly what it can carry.
3. **Irregular Religion — the omens** (Gangs, Cults & Counterculture, roll 62-22-32-5) — "Programs from cyberspace appear in this district as prophetic visions" (Core p. 166). Somewhere in the Wall's foundation a Domain was cut off from the Tangle when its cables were poured over — a construction consortium's, a flood authority's, nobody remembers — and it is still running on a grid nobody has found, leaking into the Galleries' dead Nearspace as AR ghosts: a foreman's schedule, a water-level warning, a safety animation, flickering on tunnel walls to anyone with a working lens. The Galleries read them as omens, and the omens are right more often than they should be, because the Domain is still monitoring the pumps. **[OPEN]** (OQ-15) Nothing here says Tao is in the Domain. The Galleries do not have the word.

## Who holds ground here

- [[changeling-cells|The Cutloose]] — Gallery Nine, and the routes to it. The only Key Player in the Galleries, and the one that must never be seen to be.
- Nobody else. The Galleries are the one district where no corporation, no syndicate, no government, and no pack holds anything.

## Places

- **The Drainage Vault** — A cathedral-sized chamber under the Gullet where three of the Wall's drainage tunnels meet: a market on scaffolding above the water line, cook-fires, hammocks, a hundred people, the sound of the pumps. The Galleries' only public place — a Marketplace and Meeting Place (Core p. 43) for people who cannot be seen in the Gullet above — and the last place anyone is willing to guide a stranger.
- **Gallery Nine** — An old pump gallery, sealed when the pumps were moved, reopened by people who needed a door that only they knew: a dry concrete hall two hundred meters long behind a submarine hatch, lit by scavenged panels, holding perhaps thirty Cutloose at any time and never the same thirty. The refuge. Reached by a route that changes, through the water, past people who will kill a tracker on sight. Inside, faces are not worn; it is the only place in Palisade where a Doppel is nobody but themselves. **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-22) The Cutloose's refuge, placed hidden; WP4 decides who runs it.
- **The Old Sump Stair** — A maintenance stair that climbs from the Galleries inside the Wall to a sealed door behind [[suture-row]]'s lowest surgery, the Sump: the Cutloose's road to the one surgeon who will cut a tracker out. Known to perhaps a dozen people. A crew that learns it has learned something worth killing for.

## Hooks

- A PC who is Cutloose knows the way to Gallery Nine, and someone the crew is being paid to find has been taken there.
- The omens on the Drainage Vault's walls say the water is coming, and the pump schedule says it is not; one of them is lying.
- A corporate special unit — an *owned* Doppel, with a tracker (Bible §2) — has come down into the Galleries wearing a face the Cutloose know, and Gallery Nine has to decide whether to run or to end it.

# Relay Fields

![[assets/districts/relay-fields.png]]

**Zone:** 51-52 · **Tier:** Foot · **Pillar:** Sprawling Cyberspace · **Caste band:** Patched / Stock · **Story tag:** *every kid is a hacker*

## Central concept

East along the Foot, where the lowland opens toward the levees, a field of towers: the old broadcast and relay masts that carried Palisade's signal before the Wall's cliff-face arrays made them obsolete, forty of them in a grid on concrete pads, three hundred meters tall, dead and rusting and still the tallest things on the Foot. Between them, the coffin-blocks — capsule housing built for the towers' maintenance crews and now the cheapest beds on the Foot, rented by the hour to online laborers in the Tokyo coffin-worker pattern (Tokyo p. 28): emaciated, jacked in, working Harnessing shifts for whoever pays. Relay Fields is a Fringe Zone with a population (Core p. 42): empty and isolated by the standards of the Face, and the densest cyberspace on the Foot by the standards of anyone who lives in it.

The Fields' Nearspace is cheap and lawless — the towers' old infrastructure was never decommissioned, only abandoned, and it leaks bandwidth the Chancery's utility board stopped metering decades ago. A kid in a coffin-block with a secondhand jack has access here that a Fitted coder on the Lattice pays for by the minute. That is why the Fields are Palisade's hacker district, and why [[upstart|Continuity]] recruits here.

The residents are Patched coffin-workers and Stock kids, a few Fitted who came down the Wall to get away from their monitors, and the tower-squatters who live in the masts' base stations and go up them at night for the view.

## Developments

1. **Online Crime — everyone is a hacker** (Sprawling Cyberspace, chosen) — "Harnessing is popular and accessible, everyone is a petty hacker now" (Core p. 169). Harnessing rigs are cheap in the Fields — rented by the hour in the coffin-blocks' ground floors, built from Gullet parts, run on the towers' free bandwidth — and every kid in the district has run a Harnessed job by fifteen: skimming a Gallery credit line, cracking a Hood, selling a corporate Domain's door-code to someone who wants in. Most of it is petty. Some of it is not, and Continuity's crisis-response teams come down the Wall to hire the ones who are not — or to make an example of them, which is also hiring.
2. **Backfired Tech — the defense grid** (Cities in Ruins, roll 52-14-26-4, narrowed from *an autonomous drone army programmed to exterminate all life*) — Around the central nine towers stands a fence that still works. The corporation that owned the masts is gone — one of the many that died when the cliff arrays went up — but its autonomous security grid was never switched off: sentry turrets on the pads, patrol drones on a loop, an intrusion protocol that kills anyone who crosses the inner fence without a credential nobody has issued in fifty years. It has killed forty-one people that the Fields know of. It has never been hacked, because its Domain is air-gapped inside the towers it guards. The nine towers inside the grid are the tallest in the district and nobody has been up one in two generations, and the Fields' kids talk about what might still be running at the top.

## Who holds ground here

- [[upstart|Continuity]] — recruiting. A crisis-response office in a coffin-block's ground floor, a Harnessing suite nicer than any in the district, and a standing offer to any kid who cracks something they should not have.
- [[fence-network|Tally's network]] — the Fields are where the Weighhouse's rigs are built and its data is moved; Tally's runners buy here and sell in the Gullet.

## Places

- **Mast Twelve** — A relay tower outside the grid whose base station has been a squat for thirty years: a concrete blockhouse at the foot of three hundred meters of rusted lattice, a bar in the transformer room, Harnessing rigs on every landing of the maintenance stair, and a platform at the top with the whole Foot under it. The Fields' Nightclub and Workshop at once (Core p. 43), and the place where a job's hacker gets hired.
- **The coffin-blocks** — Ten-storey capsule stacks between the towers, each capsule two meters by one, each with a jack, a screen, and a door that locks from inside: the Fields' Living Space, for a price that buys an hour or a year. A crew can vanish into a coffin-block and nobody will look, because everyone in one is somewhere else. Crowded, anonymous, and the worst possible place to be found in.
- **The inner fence** — The grid's perimeter: three meters of mesh, warning boards in a dead corporation's typeface, turret mounts every fifty meters, and beyond it nine towers and fifty years of silence. A Secured Site (Core p. 43) that nobody owns and nobody can turn off. Whatever a crew is hired to fetch from inside, the fetching is the job.

## Hooks

- Continuity's recruiter in the Fields has offered a PC's cousin a job with a signing bonus, and the cousin cracked something the crew would rather nobody had.
- A Weighhouse rig came back from a coffin-block with a credential on it that the inner fence accepts.
- Something at the top of Mast Nine, inside the grid, has started broadcasting again — once a night, on a dead frequency, in the direction of the Lattice.

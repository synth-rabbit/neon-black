# Gullet Market

![[assets/districts/gullet-market.png]]

**Zone:** 41-42 · **Tier:** Foot · **Pillar:** Gangs, Cults & Counterculture · **Caste band:** Patched · **Story tag:** *everything falls to the Gullet*

## Central concept

The Wall sheds water, and the water has to go somewhere. Where the escarpment's main run-off channel — the *gullet* — meets the shore, Palisade built its port: the locks, the wharves, the customs sheds, the warehouses; and around the port, over two centuries, the market that lives off it. **The Gullet** is the Foot's marketplace and waterfront at once (Core p. 41, 43): a roofed labyrinth of stalls, pawnshops, food carts, and lock-ups running from the base of the Wall to the water, under the girders of the Stack's bottom bays and the Circus's cradle, in permanent shade, permanently wet, smelling of frying oil and coolant and the sea. Everything that falls off the Wall — literally, off the lifts and the trucks and the loading decks — lands here, and everything that comes in by water that the Chancery's customs did not see lands here too.

This is **under the Wall** in the most literal sense: the Crest is a kilometer straight up, and the Gullet sees the sun for an hour at noon. The residents are Patched — dockers, stallholders, runners, pawnbrokers, the crews of the coasters at the locks — and they are the Foot's own aristocracy, because in the Gullet a Patched body with a network outranks a Fitted one with a warranty.

**Tally is here.** [[marisol-okonkwo|Marisol Okonkwo, "Tally"]] — the fence Rook points the crew at, their single day-one contact (Bible §5, §6) — keeps the **Weighhouse**, and the Weighhouse is where the Series' second scene happens: the crew, at the bottom, walking into the Gullet with nothing but a name. The hiring of Job 1 and Job 2 runs through her or her network (Brief §7.2). **[OPEN]** (OQ-11) Whether she has any connection to Continuity is the GM's and nothing here decides it.

## Developments

1. **Organized Crime — the smuggling hub** (Oligarchic Corruption, chosen) — The Gullet is where Palisade's grey goods move: chrome off the Stack's trucks, pharmaceuticals off Meliora's lines, Orison small arms off the Reach's private port by way of a coaster and a bribed lock-keeper, and everything the Row will fit. The specific kind of goods, in the book's phrase (Core p. 168), is *the ladder itself* — mods without warranties, sold to people who cannot buy them any other way. The Chancery's customs sheds are here; their tariff is known and paid.
2. **Seedy Underbelly — the Lock Lights** (Tech Noir, roll 41-52-36-4) — Along the locks runs the Gullet's famed red-light quarter, the Lock Lights: brothels, hostess bars, VR dives, a fantasy for every credit line. Run by madams and pimps who are the Foot's own and answer to nobody on the Wall — the Almoners take a cut and the Circus sends its custom down, but the Lights are not anybody's. A district within the district where a Fitted face is welcome and a Fitted question is not.
3. **Inspired Heroism → the Tao-touched trade** (Tao Weaponized, roll 41-23-61-3) — **[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** The book's result is *treasure hunters scouring the area for rumored magical artifacts*. There are no artifacts of legend. There are things that come through the Gullet nobody can explain — a caster-shell that fires once and is warm for a week, a lock that opens for one hand, a Kernel-scanner that works better than its parts — Tao worked into objects (Bible §1), fallen off the Wall like everything else, and buyers who hunt them: corporate purchasing agents in bad disguises, Aldine House's quiet people, collectors from the Crest. The Weighhouse takes them, weighs them, and does not ask. This is the fence trade's strangest and most profitable line, and Tao Weaponized ([[pillars]]) at its most literal: a gateway as a commodity with a price on Tally's scale.
4. **Fashion is Identity — the slumming feed** (Gangs, Cults & Counterculture, roll 42-16-45-3) — A cyberspace lifestyle influencer with a Face following has been recording live from the Gullet for a year — the stalls, the Lights, the fish shacks, all of it *authentic* — and the Face has started coming down to slum: Fitted kids in the labyrinth at night, spending, photographing, getting robbed. The stallholders hate it and take the money. The Almoners have noticed the new customers.
5. **Urban Blight — the shore end** (Cities in Ruins, roll 41-13-53-4) — Past the locks, where the port's old outer wharves rotted and were never rebuilt, the Gullet regresses into a hamlet: fishing shacks on stilts along the flood line, nets, smokehouses, a population that lives on what the polluted water still yields and has no use for the market's money. Stock, and proud of it. The coasters that bring the Reach's small arms unload here.

## Who holds ground here

- [[fence-network|Tally's network]] (folder `fence-network/`, BC-7) — the Weighhouse, and through it the runners, the pawnshops, the surgeons on the Row, the lock-keepers. The Gullet is the network's home and Tally is the Gullet's weigh-scale.
- [[syndicate|The Almoners]] — a cut of everything that moves, and a distribution line for Leash that runs through the Gullet to [[cinder-yards]]. Not visible as the Almoners; visible as collectors.
- [[changeling-cells|The Cutloose]] — passing through, under other faces, on the way to and from [[foundation-galleries]]. The Gullet is where a Doppel buys a night's identity; the Lights are where one earns it.

## Places

- **The Weighhouse** — Tally's: the port's old customs weighhouse, a stone box on the quay with a loading door on the water and a counter door on the market, a great brass balance under the roof that still works, and a back room that is an office, a kitchen, and a vault. Goods come in on the water side, get weighed — literally; Tally prices by the kilo when she is in a mood — and go out on the market side. The counter is a Meeting Place the whole Foot trusts and a Marketplace for anything; the back room is the crew's first Living Space in Palisade, if she takes them, and she takes a percentage of everything they will ever earn for it. **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-20)
- **The Labyrinth** — The roofed market proper: a kilometer of stalls under corrugated plastic and pirate cabling, lit by shop signs and the Bowl's screen through the girders, crowded from dawn to the small hours. Food, chrome, papers, drugs, faces, everything. Impossible to surveil, impossible to search, and the Weighhouse's runners know every cut-through. The book's Marketplace (Core p. 43) in its filthiest, most useful form.
- **Lock Three** — The middle lock, where the coasters tie up and the customs shed stands with its light on: the Gullet's front door from the water. The lock-keeper's fee is Tally's to collect; the customs officer's is the Chancery's. A crew leaving Palisade by sea, or bringing something in, starts and ends here — and a crew being watched is watched from the customs shed's roof.

## Hooks

- Something Tao-touched came through the Weighhouse, and three buyers who should not know about each other have all asked for it by name.
- The influencer's feed caught a face in the Labyrinth that belongs to someone the Cutloose need to stay dead.
- Tally has a job — the first one — and the crew is the only crew in the Gullet with nothing to lose yet.

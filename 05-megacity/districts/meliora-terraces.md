# Meliora Terraces

![[assets/districts/meliora-terraces.png]]

**Zone:** 15-16 · **Tier:** Crest · **Pillar:** Transhumanism · **Caste band:** Sculpted · **Story tag:** *grown to specification*

## Central concept

[[corp-a|Meliora Bioworks]]' headquarters and the corporate enclave around it: a campus of stepped greenhouse towers along the Crest's inland side, glass stacked on glass, green under grow-light all night, so that from the Foot the Terraces are a soft emerald glow at the top of the Wall. Street name *Meliora*, *Mel* ([[names]] §2). The specialty is **bio** — genomics, pharmaceuticals, gene mods, body sculpting — the flesh half of the ladder (BC-8, GM-approved), and the towers grow it: tissue, organs, whole custom bodies in the sculpting suites, and the pharmaceutical lines that supply half the clinics on the Wall.

Inside the enclave the laws are Meliora's and "the government has no say" (Core p. 41). The residents are corporate citizens: Sculpted researchers, executives, and their families in the terrace housing; Fitted technicians in the tower dormitories; and a Stock ground crew who tend the glass. The Terraces are warm, clean, humid, and smell faintly of soil and antiseptic. The friendlier the name, the colder the company ([[style-guide]] §1): Meliora's is very friendly.

**What the Terraces do not advertise:** somewhere on one of Meliora's pharmaceutical lines, a compound comes off that the Almoners buy in bulk and the Run injects to shift. **Leash** is Meliora's, made for some other purpose (Bible §2; OQ-3 — the purpose is [[corp-a]]'s twist, WP4). Nobody at the Terraces calls it Leash.

## Developments

1. **Mythology in Action → a lapsed condition** (Tao Weaponized, roll 16-26-26-4) — **[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** The book's result is *a local deity demands to be worshiped again, or else*. There is no deity and nothing demands. There is a *condition*: before Meliora built here, the inland slope was terraced garden, and a practice was kept on it — a discipline of tending, a price paid in labor and season — that had run so long the ground had grown used to it. Meliora bulldozed the terraces and the practice lapsed, and the greenhouse towers have never grown right since: cultures collapse, lines fail, whole floors rot overnight with no pathogen anyone can find. Meliora's solution was corporate: it hired the last person who knew the practice, a gardener, and pays them very well to keep it in a plot on the lowest terrace. The towers grow. The gardener is on the payroll as "grounds." **[OPEN]** (OQ-14) Whether the slope is a place where Tao runs dense is rumor, not fact.
2. **Automation Propagation — the tender uprising** (Technological Overdrive, roll 16-55-12-1) — Decades ago the greenhouse-tender drones — the spider-legged pruning and pollination units that crawl the glass — turned on the ground crew and killed eleven people in a night before the towers were powered down. Meliora's report blamed a firmware fault; the ground crew's grandchildren say the drones had started tending the plot the gardener now keeps, and objected to being reassigned. The drones are back, with hard kill-switches, and the ground crew still will not work the lowest terrace at night.
3. **Theurgists Needed → Tao-made things** (Tao Weaponized, roll 16-32-66-1) — **[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** The book's result is *spirits or demons infesting the district*. Tao can produce beings (Brief §3.1), and the towers have them: small, persistent, hard to see — things that move in the condensation on the inside of the glass, things that nest in the root-mats and unpick the irrigation, a shape that walks the catwalks on floor thirty and is on no camera. They arrived when the condition lapsed and thinned when the gardener was hired, but did not leave. Meliora's security classifies them as *contamination* and has an anti-Tao countermeasure program — the "other corporations have … anti-Tao solutions of their own" (Bible §2) — that spends most of its budget in the Terraces.
4. **Cyber-Showbiz — the house feed** (Sprawling Cyberspace, roll 15-44-33-4) — Meliora's corporate citizens are hardcore fans of a VR entertainment Meliora produces for them: a serialized sculpting drama in which contestants are remade live and the audience votes on the result. It is not sold outside the enclave. The winners are real, and they live on the Crest now.

## Who holds ground here

- [[corp-a|Meliora Bioworks]] — everything. The Terraces are Meliora; Meliora security is the only law; the Chancery is not consulted.

## Places

- **The Verdant Spire** — The headquarters tower at the campus's center, the tallest greenhouse on the Wall: forty floors of stacked biomes around a central lift core, executive suites at the top under a glass crown, the sculpting suites in the windowless floors halfway up, and the pharmaceutical lines in the bedrock below. A Seat of Power and a Workshop (Core p. 43). Getting a Patched crew in means getting them a reason to be there: a delivery, a specimen, a body for the suites.
- **The lowest terrace** — A strip of open ground at the campus's downhill edge, below the last tower, where the old garden terracing survives under Meliora's landscaping: a plot of vegetables and a shed, tended by one person on the "grounds" payroll. The tender drones will not enter it. The ground crew will not enter it at night. A Fringe Zone inside a corporate enclave, and the one place on the Terraces where the countermeasure program's sensors read nothing at all.
- **Dormitory Row** — The Fitted technicians' housing along the campus's eastern wall: identical blocks, company canteens, the house feed on every wall. The technicians are monitored, warrantied, and loyal, and they gossip like anyone. A Living Space for a crew running an infiltration under a borrowed Fitted identity; a Marketplace, quietly, for anyone selling what the Almoners want.

## Hooks

- Something walked off floor thirty of the Spire last week and Meliora's countermeasure team wants a crew that is not on the payroll to find out where it went — and will not say what it was.
- The gardener is old, and has no apprentice, and someone at Meliora has noticed.
- A Dormitory Row technician has a case of something from the pharmaceutical lines and a buyer on the Foot, and needs it carried down the Wall past Meliora's own security.

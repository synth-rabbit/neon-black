# Aurelian Crest

![[assets/districts/aurelian-crest.png]]

**Zone:** 11-12 · **Tier:** Crest · **Pillar:** Transhumanism · **Caste band:** Sculpted · **Story tag:** *never been told no*

## Central concept

The top of the Wall, as far from the Foot as money can get. Aurelian Crest is a ridge of terraced estates and slim white towers along the plateau's edge, gated at every road and lift-head, with the whole of Palisade laid out below it in the afternoon like something that belongs to it — which, mostly, it does. Daylight reaches here. So does wind, and the Sculpted walk in it with their faces uncovered because there is nothing on their faces that was not chosen.

This is the caste gradient's top ([[palisade|Palisade]], BC-15). The residents are **Sculpted** ([[names]] §4): bodies rebuilt to specification in the clinics of [[meliora-terraces]] next door, so completely that the work is invisible — no seams, no warranty ports, nothing that shows. Extreme transformation is rare (Bible §2); the Crest is where the rare people who can afford it live, and where the rarest of all — the ones who bought something more than beauty — keep their bodyguards visibly more than human, because that is one of the three transformations the public is allowed to see (Bible §3).

Under the Sculpted is a second population nobody counts: the Stock who keep the terraces. Domestic staff, gardeners, lift attendants, the drivers of the autocars, brought up the Wall each morning on service lifts and sent down each night. They are the only people on the Crest who have ever been told no.

## Developments

1. **Luxuries of Science** (Technological Overdrive, chosen) — The Crest's private clinics are the ones the feeds mean when they say Palisade can cure anything. They can: age, injury, the face you were born with, the caste you were born into. The price is the price. A Patched body that comes up the service lift can be Sculpted in a season and never go down again — which is why the service lifts are watched, and why the Sculpted talk about *new money* the way the Foot talks about a bad smell.
2. **Artificial Intelligence Hotspot** (Sprawling Cyberspace, roll 12-46-26-2) — Something is killing people in the Crest's Domains. The estates' private cyberspace — luxury AR lounges, art vaults, the family Hoods — is walled off from the Tangle and guarded like everything else up here, and for the past year a violent AI has been inside the walls, hunting Harnessed users and leaving them braindead in their chairs. The families blame each other. Continuity has offered to find it, twice.
3. **Inspired Heroism → Tao-touched labor** (Tao Weaponized, roll 11-33-55-6) — **[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** The book's result is *Source-powered workers standing up to corporate exploitation*. There are no Sources; there is a servants' action. Last winter the Stock staff of three estates walked off the terraces at once, and the estate security that went to bring them back came down the service stair with their weapons fused shut in their holsters. Nobody was hurt. The feeds called it a Baseline stunt. On the Crest they say one of the gardeners is a Caster, and nobody has fired anyone since. Together with the same roll in [[kilbride-stretch]] this is a Megacity-wide pattern: *Tao-touched labor unrest*, whispered from the top of the Wall to the bottom (Core p. 163: "if you see a pattern emerging … consider applying them to a larger swath").

## Who holds ground here

- [[corp-a|Meliora Bioworks]] — the clinics are Meliora's, or Meliora-licensed; half the Crest is a customer and the other half is a shareholder.
- [[government|The Chancery]] — lives here; governs from [[chancery-hill]] next door. Estate security is corporate, private, and exactly as legal as the police (Bible §3).

## Places

- **The Aurelian Belvedere** — The public terrace at the ridge's highest point, kept public by a century-old covenant nobody has managed to buy out: a long stone balcony with the whole city under it and a bronze rail worn smooth by Sculpted hands. Anyone can come up on the visitors' lift; almost nobody from the Foot does, because the ride is priced like a week's food. A Meeting Place for people who want to be seen not hiding, and the one spot on the Crest where a Patched face is not stopped at a gate.
- **Vane House** — A terrace estate on the seaward edge, all glass and white composite, whose owners have been Sculpted for three generations and whose staff turn over every six months. Its private Domain is one of the ones the cyber-killer has been inside. A Living Space for the crew if they are ever hired to guard it; a Secured Site if they are hired to rob it.
- **The service stair** — Not a place the Sculpted know exists: the concrete switchback stair inside the ridge that the Stock staff use when the service lifts are down, running from the Crest's kitchens to a maintenance deck on the Face two hundred meters below. Unlit, uncamera'd since the walkout, and the fastest way off the top of the Wall that does not involve a gate.

## Hooks

- A Sculpted heir hires the crew, through Tally, to find out which gardener fused the guns — and does not say whether they want them protected or removed.
- A Vane House staffer is braindead in a Harnessing chair and the family wants the AI dead before Continuity's team arrives to "help."
- Someone from the Foot came up the service lift Patched and is trying to come down Sculpted with a new name; the clinics' records say they were never here.

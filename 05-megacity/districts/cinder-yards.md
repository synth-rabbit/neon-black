# Cinder Yards

![[assets/districts/cinder-yards.png]]

**Zone:** 45-46 · **Tier:** Foot · **Pillar:** Gangs, Cults & Counterculture · **Caste band:** Patched · **Story tag:** *this is pack ground*

## Central concept

Inland from the Blocks, where the Foot flattens toward the levees, the Yards: two square kilometers of rendering plants, reclamation sheds, kiln-halls, and rail sidings that processed the port's waste and the Stack's slag for a century, and were abandoned when the processing moved into the Stack itself. Cinder — the ash the kilns left in drifts a meter deep, grey-black, still there. The book's Warzone and Industrial trope together (Core p. 41, 43): a landscape of hollow sheds, rusted gantries, chimneys, and cinder flats, cut through by one working concern and otherwise given over to whoever can hold it.

**The Run holds it.** [[packs|The Run]] — the pack culture's name for itself ([[names]] §2) — took the Yards a generation ago, or a decade; how long the packs have existed is **[OPEN]** (OQ-20; see [[timeline]]). Three packs hold the Yards now, on ground marked out in cinder and enforced at night, "gangs that are small fish in the big scheme, but kings and queens of their turfs" (Core p. 146). The Howlers are what the Bible says: bio-sculpt and cybernetics, shifted by a chemical trigger, dependent on the syndicate for it (Bible §2). The Yards are where they *run* — the open cinder flats at night, on Leash, in the shape the sculpt gives them — and where the Almoners' vans come to deliver.

The residents who are not the Run are the reclamation concern's workers, Patched, living in the sheds their employer converted, and a fringe of Stock squatters in the kiln-halls who pay the packs in whatever the packs want that week.

## Developments

1. **Industrial Sprawl — the abandoned factories** (Technological Overdrive, chosen) — "Giant factories abandoned due to new tech now attract new residents" (Core p. 171). The rendering plants died when the Stack learned to reclaim its own slag; the sheds stood empty for years; the Run moved in. A pack's *kennel* is a kiln-hall or a rendering shed, fortified with scrap, floored with mattresses and weight benches, the cages that were once for carcasses now for the nights the sculpt will not settle. The Yards suit the Run because nothing in them is worth a corporation's attention.
2. **Seedy Underbelly — the alpha's turf** (Tech Noir, roll 45-54-42-5) — The Yards are the home turf of a prominent underworld figure: the alpha of the largest pack, whose word settles the Run's disputes across the Foot and whose kennel is the Yards' seat of power in the book's sense (Core p. 43). The Almoners deal with the alpha, not the packs; the alpha deals with the packs. Named by WP4 in [[packs]]. Not a kingpin in the corporate sense — the Run has no political pull and wants none — but under the Wall, the one person the Almoners cannot simply cut off.
3. **Liars & Cheats — the reclamation concern** (Tech Noir, roll 46-54-26-3) — The one working employer in the Yards is a scrap-reclamation concern that strips and sorts what the Stack does not want — old chrome, dead drones, slag with metal still in it — and pays its Patched workers in scrip good at its own shed-store, shorting every ticket. The workers know. The concern's manager pays the packs for the privilege of not being run off, which makes the Run, in effect, the workers' landlord and the concern's protection at once. Whether the concern belongs to a Key Player is left to WP4; it reads as independent.

## Who holds ground here

- [[packs|The Run]] — the turf. Three packs, the alpha's kennel, the running ground on the cinder flats; recruiting in [[marlow-blocks]] and hunting in [[lowmere-sinks]].
- [[syndicate|The Almoners]] — the supply. The vans come from the Kitchen's back door to the Yards' rail siding, and the price is whatever the Almoners say it is this month.

## Places

- **The Kiln Row** — Six brick kiln-halls in a line along the old rail siding, each a pack's kennel or a pack's claim: scrap barricades, fire-barrels, the sculpt's smell, a Patched sentry on every roof. The alpha's is the largest, the one with the chimney that still smokes. A Seat of Power for people who own nothing else, and the only place on the Foot where a stranger is told, out loud, whose ground they are standing on.
- **The cinder flats** — The open ash-fields between the sheds, a kilometer across, grey under the Wall's shadow: where the Run runs. At night, on Leash, the packs cross them in the shape the sculpt gives and the flats belong to nobody human until dawn. A Warzone (Core p. 43) with no walls; the Chancery's police do not enter, and corporate cleanup crews come at first light for whatever was left.
- **The siding** — The rail spur where the Almoners' vans back up to unload, once a week, under the reclamation concern's gantry lights: crates in, envelopes out, the alpha's people counting, an Almoner who never gets out of the cab. The Run's dependency, once a week, in a place the crew could watch from the kiln roofs.

## Hooks

- The Almoners have raised the price of Leash and a pack that cannot pay has started taking the dose from a Row chop-shop's knockoff; the alpha wants to know who is making it before the pack finds out what it does.
- The reclamation concern's manager has stopped paying the packs and started paying someone on the Face, and the Run wants a crew that is not the Run to find out who.
- Something crossed the cinder flats last night that was not running with any pack, and the packs want it found before the corporate cleanup crew does.

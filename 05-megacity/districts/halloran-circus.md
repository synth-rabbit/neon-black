# Halloran Circus

![[assets/districts/halloran-circus.png]]

**Zone:** 33-34 · **Tier:** Face · **Pillar:** Gangs, Cults & Counterculture · **Caste band:** Fitted / Patched · **Story tag:** *the crowd wants blood*

## Central concept

The entertainment district (Core p. 41), built around the one thing Palisade agrees on: the **Halloran Bowl**, an arena hung off the Face in a cradle of girders, forty thousand seats around a licensed ring, its outer wall a screen the size of a district that plays the fights to the whole Foot below. Around the Bowl, in the decks the arena's money built: casinos, bookmakers, fight gyms, idol stages, theaters, clubs, the sculpted entertainers' agencies, and the drinking that goes with all of it. The Circus is the only district on the Face where the Fitted and the Patched mix on purpose — the Fitted come down for the fights and the Patched come up for the work — and it is loud, garish, and open all night.

It is also the Masquerade's showroom (Brief §4.1). The public knows extreme modding exists because it has *seen* it here: the arena fighters — sculpted, chromed, bio-boosted, fighting under license with their mods listed on the card — and the sculpted entertainers on the idol stages are two of the three transformations the public is allowed to know about (Bible §3). Everything the Circus shows is a permitted exception, and every impermissible transformation in Palisade hides behind the fact that the crowd has seen a fighter tear a cage door off and did not think it was strange. The Noise takes care of the rest (Core p. 22). Nobody leaves the Bowl thinking about how rare the fighters are, or how many did not come from a licensed gym.

## Developments

1. **Places to Unwind — the bookies** (Tech Noir, roll 33-51-55-1) — The Circus is where Palisade gambles: on the fights, on the idols' chart positions, on the weather, on which lift will break down first. Bookmakers on every deck, three casinos in the Bowl's cradle, betting AR on every surface. The book is run by whoever the Almoners let run it, and the Almoners take their cut in the currency they prefer — debt, which they collect in the Blocks.
2. **Seedy Underbelly — the ringfights** (Tech Noir, chosen) — Below the licensed ring, in the maintenance decks under the Bowl's floor, the unlicensed ring: fights to the death, mods unlisted, fighters unregistered, bets in cash. The fighters are whoever will fight — Patched, desperate, sculpted by a chop-shop, running on something the Almoners sold them — and the crowd is Fitted and Sculpted and pays for the difference. It is an open secret; the Bowl's own security works the door. It is also, on a bad night, the closest the public ever comes to seeing what a Howler on Leash actually is, and the reason the Circus has the best cleanup crews on the Face.

## Who holds ground here

- [[syndicate|The Almoners]] — the book, the underground ring, and the debt both generate. Not visible as the Almoners; visible as bookmakers.
- [[corp-b|Orison]] — the Bowl's title sponsor and the licensed ring's security contractor; the drones over the Circus are Orison's.

## Places

- **The Halloran Bowl** — Forty thousand seats around a ring, the cradle of girders it hangs from, the district-sized screen on its outer wall. Loud enough to hide anything. A Nightclub in the book's sense (Core p. 43) at arena scale — the perfect place to meet a mark, lose a tail, or watch a target in a crowd — and a Secured Site on fight nights, when Orison's contractors lock the cradle down.
- **The Under-ring** — The maintenance decks below the Bowl's floor: hydraulics, cabling, the fighters' cages, and the unlicensed ring in a cleared bay lit by work-lamps, two hundred people on scaffolding around it. Cash only, mods unlisted, a medic who is a chop-shop surgeon from [[suture-row]] on a retainer. A Warzone with a door charge.
- **The Kettle** — A fighters' gym and bar on the Circus's lowest deck, where the lift from the Foot lets out: heavy bags, a sparring cage, a bar that opens at six in the morning for the night shift. Where the licensed fighters train and the unlicensed ones are recruited. A Meeting Place for the Patched half of the Circus, and the place the Almoners' collectors wait for people who owe the book.

## Hooks

- A licensed fighter's mods are not the mods on the card, and the difference is something no licensed gym could install; the Bowl's sponsor wants it kept off the screen.
- Someone from the Run is fighting in the Under-ring on Leash, for money the pack needs, and is about to lose control in front of two hundred phones.
- The book has a PC's name on a debt they never made, and the collectors at the Kettle are not interested in the paperwork.

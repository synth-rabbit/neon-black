# Marlow Blocks

![[assets/districts/marlow-blocks.png]]

**Zone:** 43-44 · **Tier:** Foot · **Pillar:** Oligarchic Corruption · **Caste band:** Patched · **Story tag:** *everyone owes the Kitchen*

## Central concept

The Foot's housing, in the book's Slums sense (Core p. 42): forty identical tenement blocks in a grid behind the Gullet, twelve storeys each, built two generations ago as worker housing for the Stack and the port and never maintained since — galleries strung with washing and pirate cabling, lifts that work on three blocks out of five, courtyards that are markets by day and nothing you want to cross at night. Marlow is where the Patched live when they are not working on the Face: dockers, Row orderlies, Circus staff, Gallery underdeck crews, and their families, ten to a flat. The Chancery's police come in daylight, in numbers, for a reason. Corporate security does not come at all; nothing here is a corporation's.

What holds the Blocks together is the **Kitchen**. The Marlow Relief Kitchen occupies the ground floor of the central block and feeds the district: hot meals twice a day, a clinic on Tuesdays, coats in winter, school fees for the courtyard schools, a loan when the rent is short. Nobody in Marlow has not eaten there. Nobody in Marlow does not owe it. The Kitchen is [[syndicate|the Almoners]]' charitable face — an *almoner* hands out what you cannot live without, on their terms ([[names]] §2) — and its back door is where Leash is distributed to the Run (Bible §2). The front door is real. So is the debt.

## Developments

1. **Bureaucratic Dysfunction — the suspicious charity** (Oligarchic Corruption, chosen) — "A suspicious district-wide charity organization emerges" (Core p. 168): the Kitchen. Founded, on paper, by a trust nobody can find the trustees of; funded by donations nobody can trace; run by volunteers who are polite, tireless, and armed. It does exactly what it says. The Chancery has audited it twice and both auditors are now on its board. The people of Marlow know what it is and eat there anyway, because the alternative is the Stack's canteen at the price of a company port in the neck, and the Kitchen at least asks nothing until it asks.
2. **Mythology in Action → the Kitchen's table** (Tao Weaponized, roll 43-26-24-3) — **[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** The book's result is *the area becomes a paradise (no dying, no aging, no hunger)*. Not a paradise; a rumor about hunger. People who eat at the Kitchen say they are not hungry for days afterward — a meal on Monday carries to Thursday — and the Kitchen's soup is nothing special. The old-timers say the central block was built on the site of a communal hall where something was *kept* — a practice of the table, a discipline of feeding, run for so long the ground grew used to it — and that the Almoners, when they took the ground floor, inherited it without knowing. Whether the Kitchen is a place where Tao runs dense, or whether some volunteer keeps a practice in the back, or whether it is only hunger and hope, is **[OPEN]** (OQ-14) and stays rumor. What is not rumor: the Almoners have noticed, and a charity that can feed a district for the cost of a day's soup is a very profitable charity.
3. **Touched Subcultures → the Run recruits** (Gangs, Cults & Counterculture, roll 44-15-23-1) — **[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** to canon: the book's result is *lycanthropy spreads as a new werewolf tribe seeks to grow its numbers*, a Source-based subculture. Howlers are not Touched; they are bio-sculpt, cybernetics, and a chemical trigger (Bible §2), and they do not *spread* — they are *made*, one expensive procedure at a time, and kept by Leash. What the roll describes is a pack of [[packs|the Run]] recruiting in the Blocks: Marlow kids with nothing, offered the sculpt on the Almoners' credit, the chrome from a Row chop-shop, and the first dose free. A pack that runs the Blocks' courtyards at night has grown from four to eleven in a year, and the Kitchen's ledger has eleven new names in it. Theme three in one paragraph (Bible §4): *owned or free*.

## Who holds ground here

- [[syndicate|The Almoners]] — the Kitchen: front door and back door. The distribution front for Leash, and the debt that runs the Blocks.
- [[packs|The Run]] — a pack recruiting here, running the courtyards; its turf proper is [[cinder-yards]].

## Places

- **The Kitchen** — The Marlow Relief Kitchen: a ground floor of long tables and steam, a serving counter, a clinic room, an office with a ledger, volunteers in grey aprons; and behind the ovens a loading dock onto the block's service alley where the vans come at night. The book's Marketplace and Meeting Place in one, with a charity's manners: anyone can eat, anyone can be seen eating, and anyone who wants to talk to the Almoners sits at the end table and waits. **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-21)
- **Block Nineteen** — A tenement at the grid's edge whose lift has not worked in a decade and whose top three floors are the pack's: mattresses, a weight bench, a cage that was a stairwell, the smell of the sculpt healing. The pack's kennel in the Run's own word ([[names]] §2). Reached by the stairs or the outside — the galleries, at night, when they are running. A Living Space for people who are owned and a Warzone for anyone who comes up uninvited.
- **The Tuesday clinic** — The Kitchen's clinic room, open one day a week with a Row surgeon on the Almoners' retainer: vaccinations, stitches, the sculpt's aftercare, and — for the eleven names in the ledger — the dose. The most watched room in Marlow, by the people who use it and the people who run it.

## Hooks

- The Kitchen's ledger has a PC's name in it, from before the Ledger, with a balance that has been accruing interest for the whole of their sentence.
- A Marlow mother wants her son out of Block Nineteen before the sculpt finishes healing, and the pack wants the crew to understand that he already belongs to them.
- A Chancery auditor, the third, has not yet been offered a seat on the board and is asking about the Tuesday clinic's pharmaceutical invoices.

# Kilbride Stretch

![[assets/districts/kilbride-stretch.png]]

**Zone:** 53-54 · **Tier:** Under · **Pillar:** Oligarchic Corruption · **Caste band:** Stock · **Story tag:** *nobody official comes down here*

## Central concept

The sprawl. Behind the Foot's levees, inland from the Wall, the lowland runs flat for as far as the pumps reach, and Palisade filled it: kilometers of two- and three-storey prefab housing on a grid, automats on every corner, drone-lanes overhead, distribution warehouses the size of districts, and a population of Stock that nobody on the Wall has counted. **The Stretch** is where the bottom of the caste ladder lives (Bible §3) — factory-standard flesh, unmodified because unaffordable ([[names]] §4) — in numbers that make the Crest look like a village. It is the book's Slums at sprawl scale (Core p. 42): "millions of residents … left to fend for themselves," municipal services that avoid it, crime that runs unchecked, and a wariness of strangers that is the only security the Stretch has.

From the Stretch the Wall is a horizon: a grey line to the west with a glow on top, throwing its shadow across the sprawl every afternoon. Nobody official comes down here. The Chancery's police have a station every ten kilometers and do not leave it; corporate security has no ground to hold and does not bother; the drones overhead are delivery drones. The Stretch runs on automats, favors, cash, and the courtyard councils that settle what the police will not.

## Developments

1. **Automation Propagation — the robots** (Technological Overdrive, chosen) — The Stretch was built for workers, and the work went to machines: the distribution warehouses run on drones and sorting arms, the automats stock themselves, the levee pumps are unmanned. Unemployment is not a rate here; it is the condition. What is left is service — the Crest's domestic staff ride the service lifts up from the Stretch each dawn — and the piecework the Almoners, the Weighhouse, and the packs hand out to anyone who will do it.
2. **Blackmail, Betrayal & Bribery — the expected bribe** (Tech Noir, roll 54-51-32-4) — Every official who *does* come down here — a Chancery inspector, a utility board meter-reader, a police sergeant with a warrant — expects the envelope before the work, and the Stretch has learned that the envelope is cheaper than the work. A permit is a bribe; a ration card is a bribe; getting your name off a list is a bribe with a longer receipt. The Chancery's corruption (Bible §3), seen from the bottom: not a scandal, a tax.
3. **Inspired Heroism → Tao-touched labor** (Tao Weaponized, roll 54-32-62-6) — **[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** The book's result is *Source-powered workers standing up to corporate exploitation*. There are no Sources; there is a workers' collective — the Stretch's service-lift crews, the warehouse's few human loaders, the courtyard councils — that has been organizing against the warehouses' owners for two years, and getting away with it. Drones that were sent to break a picket landed and would not take off. A warehouse's sorting arms stopped, all at once, for a shift, with nothing wrong in their code. Whispers say there are Casters among the collective, or one Caster, and that the collective does not know which of its members it is. Taken with the same roll on [[aurelian-crest]] this is the Megacity-wide pattern the Generator invites (Core p. 163): *Tao-touched labor unrest*, from the top of the Wall to the bottom. Aldine House on [[chancery-hill]] has, it is said, been paying attention — the Wuji "identify talented people" (Bible §2).

## Who holds ground here

- [[government|The Chancery]] — nominally. Stations, inspectors, the envelope.
- [[upstart|Continuity]] — a crisis-response contract with the warehouse owners to "restore continuity of operations" at the pickets. The Stretch is the first place under the Wall where Continuity's teams have been seen in force.

## Places

- **The Kilbride Interchange** — Where the Stretch's grid meets the Foot's: a knot of elevated drone-lanes, a bus terminus, the service-lift depot where the Crest's staff queue at dawn, and a market of automats and food carts that never closes. The Stretch's Meeting Place and its only landmark; anyone giving directions in the sprawl starts here.
- **Warehouse Nine** — A distribution warehouse the size of a district, automated, windowless, humming, with a picket at its gate and a Continuity van parked opposite: the collective's front line. Inside, kilometers of sorting arms that stopped once for a shift and might again. A Workshop (Core p. 43) that makes nothing and moves everything, and a Warzone waiting for its first shot.
- **Courtyard 4471** — One of ten thousand identical prefab courtyards, this one with a council: eleven Stock elders at a folding table under a tarp, settling what the police will not — a debt, a marriage, a body. Where the crew goes when they need the Stretch's permission for something, and where the Stretch decides whether it has ever heard of them.

## Hooks

- Warehouse Nine's owner hires the crew, through a cut-out, to find the Caster in the collective — and Continuity's van has been hired to make sure the crew does not.
- A courtyard council has a PC's family in front of it over a debt run up while the PC was in the Ledger, and the Stretch's justice is not the Chancery's.
- A Chancery inspector came down to the Stretch without taking the envelope, and has not come back up.

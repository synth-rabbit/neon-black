# Coldwater Outfall

![[assets/districts/coldwater-outfall.png]]

**Zone:** 65-66 · **Tier:** Under · **Pillar:** Oligarchic Corruption · **Caste band:** none (inmates: Stock, by definition) · **Story tag:** *nobody's supposed to be here*

## Central concept

Where the map ends. Far along the coast from the Wall, past the Sinks and the Basin, past the last pump the utility board still fuels, the city's drainage discharges: the **Coldwater Outfall**, a delta of concrete culverts, settling lagoons, and sludge-flats where everything Palisade flushes comes out — the Wall's run-off, the Foot's sewage, the Stack's foundry water, the Row's grey water, the Basin's dust when it rains. Kilometers of it, poisoned, stinking, and officially empty: the Chancery declared the Outfall uninhabitable a generation ago and leased it, for "remediation," to [[corp-c|Amalgamated Prosthetic & Interface]]. The book's Fringe Zone at its most literal (Core p. 42): "empty and isolated, too big and too deserted … if you're testing a new weapon, arranging a secret trade of illegal goods, or settling down while you're spying on a neighboring area, you're probably in a Fringe Zone."

And in the middle of it, behind the remediation contract's fences, where the sludge-flats give way to a rise of old fill, **Reconciliation Facility 4** — the secret debt-labor and penal work camp AP&I owns (Bible §3, §5), which its inmates call **the Ledger** ([[names]] §2). This is where the Series opens: in medias res inside the camp, during [[upstart|Continuity]]'s strike (Brief §7.1). The crew's work block is here. So is the moment they watch [[tomas-adair|Rook]] being taken, gently, by people who came for him.

**The camp is not the mystery** (Bible §5). It is debt labor and penal; it produces something generic; it is secret because it is illegal and remote because it is secret. What it produces is left to WP7a and the GM, within the Bible's word: *generic*.

## Developments

1. **Industrial Pollution — uninhabitable** (Technological Overdrive, chosen) — "The government declares this district uninhabitable" (Core p. 171). The declaration was true — nothing should live in the Outfall — and it was also a lease: the Chancery's declaration made the Outfall a remediation zone, the remediation zone went to tender, and AP&I's tender was the only one. The contract is a public document. It describes lagoon dredging, sludge processing, and "site security." It is renewed every five years without discussion, and the Chancery's inspectors are paid not to inspect.
2. **Government Overreach — the prison complex** (Oligarchic Corruption, chosen) — "A growing part of the district is converted into a prison complex" (Core p. 168). Under the contract's site-security clause, on the fill-rise at the delta's center, AP&I built the Ledger: fences, floodlights, dormitory blocks, work sheds, a processing plant that is the remediation contract's alibi and the camp's actual product line, and a security force that is exactly as legal as the police and, out here, the only law there is (Bible §3). Inmates arrive by truck through the Stack's bottom bays or by barge along the coast, on debt papers or on a sentence, and work off what the Ledger says they owe. The Ledger is never wrong. The name is the inmates' ([[names]] §2). *Growing* is the right word: a fifth block was under construction when the Series opens.

## Who holds ground here

- [[corp-c|AP&I]] — the contract, the camp, the guards, the barges. The camp's authority is a vector face in the breakout job (Brief §7.1; WP4 names them).
- [[government|The Chancery]] — the declaration and the lease; an inspector's signature, twice a year, on a report nobody reads.
- [[upstart|Continuity]] — for one night: the strike. Not a presence, an event.

## Places

- **The Ledger** — Reconciliation Facility 4: a rectangle of fence and floodlight on the fill-rise, four dormitory blocks and a fifth going up, work sheds, the processing plant, the administration block with the camp authority's office and the debt records, a landing stage on the lagoon for the barges, a truck gate on the causeway. The book's Secured Site of the keep-things-in kind (Core p. 43). The crew knows every meter of it from the inside; the job is getting out. Its security is WP5's custom Challenge; its scenes are WP7a's.
- **The causeway** — The single raised road across the sludge-flats from the Ledger's truck gate to the coast road and the Sinks, three kilometers, no cover, floodlit at the camp end and dark after that. The way the trucks come in and the only way out on foot that does not involve the lagoons. Where the breakout's escape route runs, and where the escapee list's *not worth the bounty* is first tested.
- **The settling lagoons** — The Outfall itself: kilometers of concrete-edged pools and sludge-flats under a permanent chemical haze, culvert mouths the size of tunnels, the sea beyond. Uncrossable in daylight, lethal in the wrong places, and the reason nobody has ever walked out of the Ledger without help. A Fringe Zone the crew will cross once, in the dark, with the strike behind them.

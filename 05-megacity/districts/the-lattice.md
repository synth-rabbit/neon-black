# The Lattice

![[assets/districts/the-lattice.png]]

**Zone:** 25-26 · **Tier:** Face · **Pillar:** Sprawling Cyberspace · **Caste band:** Fitted · **Story tag:** *someone is always listening*

## Central concept

The data ward. Halfway down the Face, where the cliff's rock is coldest and driest, Palisade cut its server halls: a grid of galleries and cooling shafts behind a glass-and-steel plaza hung off the Wall, the physical home of most of the city's Domains — the Chancery's, the banks', the Hoods of half the Face, everything that is not big enough to keep its own halls on the Crest (Core p. 27: Domains are "stored on server farms"). Formally the *Lattice Data Ward*; everyone says **the Lattice** — for the cooling grid that crosshatches the cliff face, and for what it is, a mesh everything passes through.

Three years ago the ward belonged to a consortium of tenants and a Chancery utility board. Then the Lattice fire — a cooling failure that took out two galleries and threatened the rest — and **[[upstart|Continuity Risk & Response]]** arrived with crisis-response teams before the Chancery's fire service did, secured the ward, put out the fire, and did not leave. It holds the Lattice now under a "continuity of service" contract the tenants signed while their Domains were burning. Continuity's headquarters is the plaza's central tower. Its crisis-response vans park in the plaza. Its monitors are in the Nearspace of every gallery. This is the challenger's ground (Bible §3): security, crisis management, hacking — and the Lattice is where all three are one business.

The residents are **Fitted**: coders, Domain architects, cooling engineers, Continuity's own staff in the plaza housing, monitored by their employer like everyone on the Face. The ward is clean, cold, quiet, and lit blue. It reads as exactly what it is: a data ward under a security firm's occupation. Nothing here is anything else.

## Developments

1. **Corporate Domains — the ward** (Sprawling Cyberspace, base condition) — "A major megacorp's Domain is securely hosted here … a secret corporate data fortress … the Nearspace in this corporate district is swarming with data security" (Core p. 169) — all three at once, because the Lattice hosts everyone. The plaza is a hotbed of industrial espionage in the book's sense: corporate AR lounges where deals are struck, Harnessing suites for rent by the hour to anyone with a Fitted credit line, and a Nearspace so dense with security nimbuses that a hacker's own data fog is invisible in it.
2. **War for Power — the takeover** (Oligarchic Corruption, roll 26-42-63-5) — The book's result is *a foreign power, Mythos, or AI staged a military invasion here*. **A foreign power**, and nothing else: Continuity's crisis-response teams came into the ward armed, in force, through the plaza gates and the cooling shafts at once, during the fire, and secured it "for the duration of the emergency." The emergency has not been declared over. The Chancery's utility board has been paid to agree. The tenants have Domains that work, and monitors in them. It was the first act of Continuity's that the street noticed, and the civil agreements had nothing to say about it, because the Lattice belonged to none of the Big Three ([[spine-and-clock]]; [[timeline]]).
3. **Cult Meddling → the focus practice** (Tao Weaponized, roll 26-26-45-4) — **[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** The book's result is *a Mythos cult gains popularity, changing the district's daily practices*. There is no cult. There is a *practice*: a breath-and-attention discipline sold to Lattice coders through a wellness contractor as productivity software with a live coach — twenty minutes before a Harnessing session, and the session runs cleaner, longer, deeper. It works. It works far too well for what it claims to be, and the coaches never explain why, and the coders do not ask because their numbers are up. Tao Weaponized as commodity ([[pillars]]): a stripped-down discipline, packaged and licensed, with nobody in the ward aware of what they are practicing. Who supplies the contractor is **[OPEN]** for WP4 to attach (the Wuji, a rebel practitioner, or nobody); the Lattice asserts nothing.

## Who holds ground here

- [[upstart|Continuity Risk & Response]] — the Lattice entire, by contract: headquarters in the plaza tower, crisis-response teams in the plaza, monitors in every gallery's Nearspace, Harnessed teams in the suites. Its cyberspace resources are in its Key Player entry (Brief §6.5).
- Nobody else holds ground. The Big Three are tenants here, and tenants of Continuity, which is the point.

## Places

- **Continuity Plaza** — The glass plaza hung off the Face, the headquarters tower at its center: a lobby like a hospital, crisis-response vans at the loading ramp, executive floors that do not appear on the building's public Domain. A Seat of Power (Core p. 43) that is also, by design, a Meeting Place — Continuity sells reassurance, and the plaza is where clients come to be reassured. The crew's first sight of the people who broke them out of the Ledger, if they ever look up.
- **Gallery Seven** — One of the server halls behind the plaza, deep in the rock: a kilometer of racks in blue light, a cold that gets into the teeth, cooling shafts overhead big enough to walk in, and a Nearspace where every device is watched. The Chancery's Domains are here. So are the Hoods of everyone on the Face who cannot afford the Crest. A Secured Site whose weakness is that it is also a workplace, with shifts, and doors.
- **The Fire Galleries** — The two halls the fire gutted, sealed off since, officially awaiting reconstruction: scorched racks, melted cable, a Domain or two still running on the racks that survived, and the cooling shafts Continuity's teams came in through. A Fringe Zone inside the most watched district on the Face — the one place in the Lattice where the monitors read nothing, because nothing is supposed to be there.

## Hooks

- A tenant wants its Domain moved out of the Lattice and its monitor removed without Continuity noticing either, and will pay a Patched crew what it would never pay a Fitted one.
- A Lattice coder's focus-practice coach has stopped showing up, and the coder's Harnessing sessions have started running deeper on their own.
- Something the crew carried out of the Ledger has a data nimbus that answers to a Gallery Seven address.

## Canon and flags

- The upstart: security, crisis management, hacking; multiple smaller income streams; a credible challenger too weak to face one of the three alone (Bible §3). Cyberspace as written (Brief §6.5; Core p. 27–28).

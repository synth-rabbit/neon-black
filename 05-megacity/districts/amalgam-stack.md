# Amalgam Stack

![[assets/districts/amalgam-stack.png]]

**Zone:** 23-24 · **Tier:** Face · **Pillar:** Oligarchic Corruption · **Caste band:** Fitted · **Story tag:** *the company owns the body*

## Central concept

[[corp-c|Amalgamated Prosthetic & Interface]]'s headquarters: not a tower on the Crest but a **stack** — a vertical industrial city bolted into the Face of the Wall, thirty decks of foundry, assembly, fitting theater, warehouse, and worker housing hung from the cliff on girders the size of streets, with the corporate offices at the top deck just under the Crest's lip and the loading bays at the bottom opening onto the Foot. Street names *AP&I*, *Amalgam* ([[names]] §2). The specialty is **cybernetics** (BC-8, GM-approved): the mass-market chrome that is the ladder's metal half — replacement limbs, eyes, organs, warranty-grade implants — made here by the million and fitted here by the thousand.

The Stack is the NM3 pattern made concrete ([[style-guide]] §1): legacy heavy industry, a mouthful of a name, an ampersand, grease. It roars. Foundry heat vents out of the cliff face day and night; the lift-cages run on the outside of the structure in open steel; the whole thing sways in a storm and everyone inside pretends it does not. The residents are **Fitted** — AP&I's own product, in AP&I's own workers, warrantied and monitored — and the Stack is the largest single employer on the Face.

**What the Stack does not show:** AP&I owns the Ledger — [[coldwater-outfall|Reconciliation Facility 4]] — the secret debt-labor and penal camp where the Series opens (Bible §5). The Stack's accounts department knows it as a remediation contract. The crew's names are on an escapee list somewhere in this building (Bible §6), filed under *not worth the bounty*.

## Developments

1. **Corporate Rules — the factory complex** (Oligarchic Corruption, chosen) — The entire district is one well-oiled, Orwellian factory: shifts announced by klaxon, production quotas on every wall, canteens that serve by employee number, dormitories by deck, and a security force that is exactly as legal as the police and considerably more present (Bible §3). Nothing in the Stack is not AP&I's, including the air. Workers live here, eat here, are fitted here, and are buried, when it comes to that, in the company plot on the Foot.
2. **Bodies Modified — mandatory augs** (Transhumanism, chosen) — AP&I requires invasive augmentation of all its workers as a condition of employment: a company interface port at the base of the skull, a warranty tag in the forearm, and whatever the job needs — foundry hands, assembly eyes, a fitting technician's steadied nerves. It is sold as a benefit, and it is one: a Stock body that signs on with AP&I comes out Fitted. It is also a leash. The augs are monitored, the warranty is void the day you quit, and a body that leaves the Stack takes the company's property with it. The one exception is **band-grade** employment: an employee who cannot or will not take the implant wears a collar unit and a wrist tag instead, at one pay grade lower, and *band-grade* is a slur on the decks — which is how a Baseline works the Stack without ceasing to be one (Bible §2; [[corp-c-membership]]; **[RULES CONFLICT]** CR-16, adopted provisionally as BC-106 / BC-130, the GM may choose otherwise).
3. **Inspired Heroism → the giveaway ring** (Tao Weaponized, roll 24-32-56-5) — **[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** The book's result is *a criminal group led by a charismatic do-gooder*. There is one: somewhere inside the Stack a ring of workers steals warranty-grade chrome off the lines and *gives it away* on the Foot, fitted free by a chop-shop in [[suture-row]], to Stock who could never buy it. The ring's leader is a rumor with a dozen names. AP&I has never caught them, and the reason the Stack gives is the reinterpreted part: the stolen chrome walks out past AP&I's tracking as if it were not tagged, and the security office's own countermeasure staff say the tags are *there* and simply not answering — a Tao trick, a practice worked into the metal, a caster-shell method applied to a warranty chip. AP&I calls it a firmware exploit. Nobody has found the firmware. (Not connected to Rook, who was caught elsewhere doing something else — OQ-10.)
4. **Spliced Technologies — the rebuilds** (Technological Overdrive, roll 23-56-46-2) — Compounding the above: the ring's chrome does not stay AP&I's. It comes back up the Wall rebuilt — foundry hands re-geared as climbing claws, assembly eyes re-lensed for the dark of the Under, interface ports re-wired to take a Harnessing jack — and AP&I's engineers have started quietly buying the rebuilds back to see what the Foot did with their product.

## Who holds ground here

- [[corp-c|Amalgamated Prosthetic & Interface]] — the Stack entire, from the top deck's executive suites to the bottom bays. The escapee list and the Ledger's contract are here.

## Places

- **The Top Deck** — AP&I's executive floor, the one deck of the Stack with windows, opening onto a terrace just under the Crest's edge: boardrooms, the contracts office, the security command. A Seat of Power (Core p. 43) reached only by the executive lift, which runs inside the cliff rather than outside it. The remediation contract for Coldwater is in a safe here. So is the escapee list, on paper.
- **Fitting Theater Twelve** — One of the mass-fitting halls halfway down the stack: two hundred surgical bays under one vaulted ceiling, automated arms, a viewing gallery for buyers, and a queue of new hires waiting to be made Fitted. Loud, bright, bloody, and running three shifts. A Workshop and — because everyone in it is anaesthetized or busy — a surprisingly easy place to walk through.
- **The Bottom Bays** — The loading decks where the Stack meets the Foot: cargo lifts, drone gantries, and the trucks that take the chrome down into the city. The giveaway ring works here. So do the Weighhouse's runners from [[gullet-market]], because whatever falls off a truck at the Bottom Bays lands in the Gullet by morning.

## Hooks

- A Fitting Theater technician wants a PC's company port removed *and* the warranty tag left reading as installed; she knows a chop-shop that can do the first and has heard the ring can do the second.
- AP&I's security office hires, through Tally, a crew that is not on its payroll to find the ring's leader. It is the crew's own escapee list they will be walking past to do it.
- A truck from the Bottom Bays is headed for the outfall with sealed crates marked *remediation* — and someone wants to know what AP&I sends to a district that is officially uninhabitable.

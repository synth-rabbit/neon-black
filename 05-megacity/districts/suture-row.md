# Suture Row

![[assets/districts/suture-row.png]]

**Zone:** 35-36 · **Tier:** Foot · **Pillar:** Transhumanism · **Caste band:** Patched · **Story tag:** *stitched up, sent back out*

## Central concept

Where the Face meets the Foot, along the base of the Wall directly under [[amalgam-stack]]'s bottom bays, runs the medical corridor: a kilometer of clinics, surgeries, pharmacies, prosthetic fitters, and the chop-shops that do all of the above without the paperwork, stacked four and five storeys against the cliff with the Wall going up forever above them. The Row is the Tokyo Yakushi Corridor pattern (Tokyo p. 34) turned Patched: the corporations' warranty-service clinics on the upper floors, where a Fitted body comes down the Wall to have its chrome serviced; the chop-shops at street level, where a Patched body has second-hand chrome fitted by someone who learned on the job; and below street level, in basements that flood, the surgeries that will do anything.

The Row is where the mod ecosystem's three owners meet — corporate, black market, DIY (Bible §1) — and where the caste ladder is actually built. A Stock body walks in with money and walks out Patched. A Patched body walks in with more money and walks out with a warranty. The chop-shops here are where a Baseline has a lost arm replaced with something functional and nothing more (Bible §2), where a Howler has the sculpt repaired after a bad night, and where — quietly, for the right price, without a record — a Doppel has a tracker cut out. Irreversible work is irreversible here as everywhere (Bible §1; Brief §2.4): the Row can add and remove chrome; it cannot undo a sculpt, and the people who ask are sent back out.

The residents are the Row's own staff — Patched surgeons, techs, orderlies, the pharmacists — in the floors above the shops, and a floating population of patients sleeping in recovery rooms rented by the night.

## Developments

1. **Natural Disaster — the floods** (Cities in Ruins, roll 36-14-11-1) — The Row sits at the Wall's foot, and the Wall sheds water. Every storm season the run-off comes down the cliff faster than the Foot's drains can take it, and the Row's basements and street level go under: a meter of grey water through the chop-shops, the surgeries below street level sealed and pumped or abandoned for the week, sandbags at every door. The upper-floor clinics do not flood, which is the caste ladder again. The basement surgeries have learned to work in waders, and some of them have learned to use the water — a flooded surgery is a surgery no security team wants to search.
2. **Losing Humanity — organs for rent** (Tech Noir, chosen) — The desperate sell here. Kidneys, corneas, marrow, a lung; and, newer, *rent*: brain functions leased by the hour to a Harnessing shop, a body leased by the night to someone who needs to be somewhere in a Patched face. The buyers are the upper-floor clinics, who pay in credit toward a warranty; the sellers are Stock who will never afford one otherwise. It is the ladder's lowest rung, and it is made of what people take out of themselves to reach the next.

## Who holds ground here

- [[corp-c|AP&I]] — the warranty-service clinics on the upper floors, and the chrome that ends up in the chop-shops below when it falls off AP&I's trucks.
- [[corp-a|Meliora]] — the pharmacies and the licensed gene-clinics; Meliora's pharmaceuticals, in every form including the ones it does not sell.
- [[fence-network|Tally's network]] — the Row is where the Weighhouse's goods get *fitted*; the chop-shops are the fence's customers and her surgeons.

## Places

- **Marrowgate Clinic** — The largest of the upper-floor warranty clinics, AP&I-licensed, glass-fronted, with a lift to the Stack's bottom bays: a Fitted patient's idea of the Foot. Its basement is a Marketplace for organs and rentals that its upper floors do not officially know about. Getting a Patched crew through the front door means an appointment; getting through the basement means a flood.
- **Threadneedle** — A street-level chop-shop under a hand-painted sign, three surgical bays, a fitter's bench, a waiting room that is also the surgeon's kitchen. Does anything, asks nothing, takes chrome in part-payment. The Row's reputation in one room, and the crew's likeliest surgeon after a bad job. (The surgeon is unnamed here; WP4 or WP7 names them.)
- **The Sump** — The lowest surgery on the Row, below street level, sealed against the floods with a submarine door: the place for work that must never appear on any record — a tracker out of a Doppel, a kill-switch traced, an EM burn treated without questions. Reached through a pharmacy's stockroom and a ladder. A Secured Site by obscurity, and the one door on the Row that opens for the Cutloose.

## Hooks

- A Marrowgate patient is on the table with chrome that was reported stolen from the Stack, and the clinic wants it *back* more than it wants the patient.
- A Stock kid rented out his body for a night and it came back with a new face and someone else's memories of the evening.
- The floods are coming and the Sump has a patient who cannot be moved and a door that cannot be opened from outside.

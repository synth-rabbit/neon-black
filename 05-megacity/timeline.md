# Timeline

The "geohistorical record" the Generator asks for (Core p. 160), kept to the book's scale — "just enough worldbuilding … similar to the amount of worldbuilding you see in a movie or TV series." **No absolute years.** Everything is relative to *now*, which is the night [[upstart|Continuity]] strikes the Ledger (Bible §5). Every line either cites the Bible or carries **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-25); no line contradicts the Bible's three fixed spans — Tao before mods; mods roughly 200 years; the Ferrante outbreak roughly 100 years (Bible §3).

## The spans the Bible fixes

| Span | Bible |
|---|---|
| Tao is real and older than the mod ecosystem; it predates the tech by an unstated span. | §1, §3 |
| Mods — genetic modification, cybernetics, bio-manipulation, body sculpting — are roughly **200 years** old. | §3 |
| The vampire outbreak is roughly **100 years** old; the corporation that created the nanites no longer exists. | §2, §3 |
| Present day: the Big Three's stalemate is under strain from the upstart; the upstart strikes the camp. | §3, §5 |

## The history, oldest first

**Before the mods — Tao.** (Bible §1, §3) Tao is practiced in Palisade before there is a Palisade: on the plateau, in the marsh, on the terraced slope where [[meliora-terraces]] now stands, in the communal hall under what is now the Kitchen in [[marlow-blocks]]. Disciplines kept as household habit and craft; a force nobody names as a force. **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-25) Palisade's escarpment is settled and the first flood barrier built along its foot in this period — the Wall's oldest bones ([[foundation-galleries]]). How much older Tao is than the barrier is not stated and not needed.

**~200 years ago — the mods.** (Bible §3) Genetic modification, cybernetic replacement, bio-manipulation, and body sculpting arrive as a mixed ecosystem — corporate, black market, DIY — that no single actor controls (Bible §1). **[BUILD CHOICE]** The first *hunters* in the Bible's sense — humans who use augments and weapon systems without sculpting or bio-manipulation (Bible §2) — are simply the first people who could afford the gear; the line between "still human" and "splat" is drawn, unnoticed, in this century.

**~150 years ago — the Wall goes up.** **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-25) As the mod economy sorts people by what they can afford to have done, it sorts them by altitude: the plateau's edge is built up into the Crest, the cliff is cut into the Face, the lowland is levee'd and pumped into the Foot and the Under ([[palisade|Palisade]], BC-15). The caste gradient is geography before it is a word.

**~100 years ago — the Ferrante outbreak.** (Bible §2, §3) A hacked or failed experiment at **Ferrante Nanoscale**'s campus releases a transmissible, iron-hungry, self-replicating nanite strain. The campus is digested; the district is walled ([[ferrante-basin]]); the company no longer exists within the year (Bible §2). **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-23) The outbreak enters public history as the worst industrial disaster in Palisade's memory — *a Ferrante flare-up* is a curse — while what it produced, Bloodware, is never public (Bible §3). The corporations take over the cleanup and come to regard the strain as a recurring disease that occasionally produces a clan worth wiping out (Bible §2). **[BUILD CHOICE]** The cover story that public incidents are the work of Baselines (Bible §2, §3) dates from this cleanup — its timing is not the Bible's.

**~100 to ~50 years ago — the Big Three consolidate.** **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-25) Out of the mod economy's many players, three become the ones with fingers in every pie: [[meliora-terraces|Meliora Bioworks]] (bio), [[orison-reach|Orison Defense Systems]] (weapons), [[amalgam-stack|Amalgamated Prosthetic & Interface]] (cybernetics) — the specialties are BC-8, GM-approved (Bible §3 offered them as "maybe"). Corporate security becomes exactly as legal as the police (Bible §3) somewhere in this period; the Chancery ([[chancery-hill]]) is bought and stays bought. The Wuji's control of Orison (Bible §2, §3) is established at a date this vault does not state.

**~50 years ago — the civil agreements.** (Bible §3: "basically stable agreements to keep things civil between them") **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-25) The three corporations' wars — never public, never admitted — end in a set of agreements: lanes, territories on the Wall, who keeps the heavy weapons (Orison), who runs the mass market (AP&I), who owns the flesh (Meliora), and what each pays the Chancery. The stalemate begins. The range "~40–60 years ago" is proposed; the GM may move it.

**~50 years ago to now — the caste system formalizes.** (Bible §3: "the beginning of a formal caste system. It started as economic but is slowly becoming formalized, in part because of unequal access to cybernetics, gene mods, and sculpting") **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-25) The drift from *economic* to *bodily* sorting is the last half-century's story: warranties become monitored, brand becomes etiquette ([[corbel-gallery]]), the tier lifts become where everyone finds out, and the slang — **Sculpted / Fitted / Patched / Stock** (BC-9) — settles into the language. **[OPEN]** (OQ-8, OQ-21) Nothing is yet law; whether it becomes law is the Series' to decide.

**~30 to ~15 years ago — Leash and the Run.** **[OPEN]** (OQ-20) The Bible does not say whether the Howlers and their packs are recent or old (§2). **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-25) Proposed range: Meliora's compound reaches the street as **Leash** somewhere between thirty and fifteen years ago — recent enough that the first Howlers are still alive and the packs are a generation deep, old enough that the Run has turf ([[cinder-yards]]) and a culture ([[names]] §2). [[syndicate|The Almoners]] are the resellers from the beginning (Bible §2). The GM may make both older or younger; nothing in the districts depends on the number.

**~20 years ago — the Ledger.** **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-24, BC-25) The Chancery declares the Outfall uninhabitable; AP&I takes the remediation lease; **Reconciliation Facility 4** opens behind it ([[coldwater-outfall]]). The Bible fixes only that the camp exists, is AP&I's, is secret, and is debt labor and penal (§3, §5).

**~10 years ago — Continuity's rise.** (Bible §3: the upstart, "best known for security, crisis management, and hacking … a credible challenger but still too weak to easily take on one of the three one-on-one") **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-25) Continuity Risk & Response grows from a crisis-response contractor into the fourth power in a decade — by being where emergencies are, and, the street says, by being why. The Bible's secret — that it is Bloodware-run, known to Bloodware with power and not to the street (§2, §5) — has no date in this vault.

**~3 years ago — the Lattice.** **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-25) The Lattice fire; Continuity's crisis-response teams take the data ward under a continuity-of-service contract and do not leave ([[the-lattice]]). The first of Continuity's acts the street noticed, and the first the civil agreements had nothing to say about. The stalemate's strain (Bible §3) becomes visible from here.

**~2 years ago to now — the crack.** **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-25) Tao-touched labor unrest from the Crest to the Stretch ([[aurelian-crest]], [[kilbride-stretch]]); a pack in the Blocks grows from four to eleven ([[marlow-blocks]]); the Almoners take corporate customers at the Circus book; a violent AI in the Crest's Domains; the Ledger's fifth block goes up. Every faction smells opportunity (Bible §5). None of it is the war. All of it is the weather before it.

**Before now — the crew.** (Bible §6) Five freelance low-level criminals see something they should not have — *what*, and whether it ties to the spine, is the players' and the GM's (OQ-12) — and are sent to the Ledger for it, and put on the same work crew, under [[tomas-adair|Tomas Adair, "Rook"]], who was caught leaving one of the corporations' research facilities (which: OQ-10) and passed himself off as a street thug. How long they are inside is not stated; long enough for Rook to acclimate and protect them.

**Now — the strike.** (Bible §5) Continuity strikes Reconciliation Facility 4 to extract Rook. The general breakout is cover. The crew escapes in it, on AP&I's escapee list and not worth a bounty (Bible §6), with one name: Tally, at the Weighhouse in [[gullet-market]]. The Series begins; the clock ([[secret-war-goes-public]]) is at zero.

## Ten lines

1. Tao is practiced in the marsh and on the slope before the Wall exists (Bible §1, §3; BC-25 for the place).
2. ~200 years ago: the mods arrive as a mixed ecosystem nobody controls (Bible §1, §3).
3. ~150 years ago: the escarpment is built into the tiered Wall; caste becomes altitude (BC-15, BC-25).
4. ~100 years ago: Ferrante Nanoscale's nanites get out; the Basin is walled; the company vanishes; the strain's product stays secret (Bible §2, §3; BC-23).
5. ~100–50 years ago: the Big Three consolidate — Meliora, Orison, AP&I — and the Chancery is bought (Bible §3; BC-8, BC-25).
6. ~50 years ago: the civil agreements; the stalemate begins (Bible §3; BC-25 for the date).
7. ~50 years ago to now: the caste system drifts from economic to bodily; the slang settles; nothing is law (Bible §3; BC-9; OQ-8).
8. ~30–15 years ago: Leash reaches the street; the Run takes the Yards; the Almoners resell (Bible §2; OQ-20; BC-25).
9. ~20 years ago the Ledger opens; ~10 years ago Continuity rises; ~3 years ago it takes the Lattice (Bible §3, §5; BC-24, BC-25).
10. Now: Continuity strikes the Ledger for Rook; the crew escapes; the clock is at zero (Bible §5, §6).

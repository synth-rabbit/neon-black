# District Directory

The Megacity Generator (Core p. 160–173), run **on the GM's behalf, maximal** (Brief §6.2): the 12-row d66 District Directory of Core p. 162, expanded toward 18 districts where the content supports it, then developed by rolling seven dice per development until every district had at least one (Core p. 163). Every name, zone code, development, and story tag here is authorized invention (Plan A.1 rule 5) and registered as `[BUILD CHOICE]` — the names in [[names]] "Added by WP1", the method and results as BC-17, the placements as BC-18. The city they describe is [[palisade|Palisade]].

## Step 1 — carve it into districts (Core p. 161–162)

Palisade is tiered ([[palisade|Palisade]], BC-15), so the map was carved by tier first and by character second, aiming for "about a dozen" and then splitting the prominent ones. Every district has "its own personality" (Core p. 162) and maps to at least one of the book's district tropes (Core p. 41–43).

## Step 2 — the future history (Core p. 163)

**Method — `[BUILD CHOICE]` (BC-17).** Thirty-six developments were rolled with seven dice each (zone code, thematic code, sub-theme, development) using `python3 random` with seed `20260903`, against the 18-row directory above and the tables of Core p. 164–172. Duplicates were noted and dropped or compounded; two districts that received no roll (Corbel Gallery, Coldwater Outfall) and every mandatory placement were **chosen** from the tables and are marked *chosen* below. Where a roll landed on the **Mythology Weaponized** pillar (Core p. 167), or on any development that assumes a Mythos, Source, or Touched subculture, it was **reinterpreted as a Tao phenomenon** and marked **[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** (Brief §3.2, §6.2; CR-1). Each district keeps the sub-theme of its most prominent development as the root of its story tag (Core p. 163), rewritten in tag voice ([[style-guide]] §2).

**Pillars rolled**, as the book's thematic-code table reads them (Core p. 164): Cities in Ruins 11-14 · Gangs, Cults & Counterculture 15-22 · Mythology Weaponized 23-34 · Oligarchic Corruption 35-42 · Sprawling Cyberspace 43-46 · Tech Noir 51-54 · Technological Overdrive 55-62 · Transhumanism 63-66.

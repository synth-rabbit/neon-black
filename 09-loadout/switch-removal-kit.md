# Sump Switch-Removal Kit

**Catalog:** merc-gear · **Tags:** *switch-removal kit*, *surgical*, *cuts the kill switch out clean*, *one bad hand and it fires* · **Flaws:** *dangerous to use* · **Requires setup:** yes

## Description

A roll of surgical instruments purpose-built for one job: cutting a kill switch out of a changeling's body without triggering it on the way out. It is what [[suture-row|the Sump]] keeps behind the submarine door for exactly this reason (Bible §2), and it is the difference between a changeling walking out free and a changeling not walking out at all. Requires setup — a table, good light, steady hands, and time nobody in the middle of a job has.

## Availability

DIY — the Sump's own tools, [[suture-row]]; not sold, only used, and only by someone who has done this before

## Canon and flags

- Bible §2 (changeling): removing the kill switch is how a changeling becomes Cutloose; this is the physical act, not the aftermath.
- Written to the flaw the Bible implies rather than states: cutting a live kill switch out is inherently a bet, and the item's flaw makes that bet mechanical.

# Deadband EM Rounds

**Catalog:** ammo · **Tags:** *EM pulse rounds*, *disrupt nanite lattice*, *localized pulse*, *useless against unmodified flesh* · **Flaws:** *weak against Baselines* · **Requires setup:** no

## Description

Standard-caliber rounds packed with a conductive jacket and a localized electromagnetic charge, built to brick a drone's control board and, incidentally, to hurt a body built out of nanites more than an ordinary bullet would. Cleanup crews carry these into a suspected flare-up scene; on the street they are just as easily bought as anti-drone rounds, because that is also exactly what they are.

## Availability

corporate (cleanup-crew issue) | black market — sold openly as anti-drone ammunition, which is also true

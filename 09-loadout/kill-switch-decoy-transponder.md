# Ghost Ping Transponder

**Catalog:** cyberspace · **Tags:** *decoy transponder*, *spoofs the tracker's last position*, *buys a false trail*, *one-time trick* · **Flaws:** *single use* · **Requires setup:** no

## Description

A disposable transponder that mimics a changeling tracker's signal and broadcasts a fake position, then dies. Planted somewhere the pursuit will check first — a coaster leaving the Gullet, a coffin-block in the Fields — it buys the real fugitive a head start while whoever is watching chases the ghost. It only works once; a corp or syndicate handler who has been fooled by one learns to check twice on the next.

## Availability

DIY — a Cutloose specialty, rare, traded between cells rather than sold

## Canon and flags

- Bible §2 (changeling): trackers are how owners keep changelings found; this is the Cutloose's answer, consumable rather than permanent.

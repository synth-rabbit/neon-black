# Tracker Shroud

**Catalog:** garments · **Tags:** *tracker shroud*, *woven signal cage*, *blocks passive tracking*, *bulky under normal clothes* · **Flaws:** *conspicuous* · **Requires setup:** no

## Description

A lightweight mesh, woven with conductive thread in a Faraday-cage pattern, sewn into a coat lining or worn as an underlayer: it blocks a changeling's tracker from broadcasting its position without physically removing the implant. It buys distance and time — hours, not days — and it does nothing for a tracker's owner if the wearer is ever searched down to the skin, because the shroud itself is not exactly subtle to the hand.

## Availability

DIY — a Cutloose specialty, occasionally sold through [[gullet-market|the Weighhouse]] at a price the Cutloose set

## Canon and flags

- Bible §2 (changeling): the tracker is the second half of ownership alongside the kill switch; this is a stopgap for a changeling who is not yet, or cannot yet be, fully cut loose.

# Starburst EM Canister

**Catalog:** weapons · **Tags:** *EM starburst canister*, *area pulse*, *thrown, not fired*, *fries nearby electronics too* · **Flaws:** *indiscriminate* · **Requires setup:** no

## Description

A hand-thrown canister that detonates into a wide, brief electromagnetic starburst — a crowd-control answer to a Bloodware subject in a public space, when a baton is too close and a scanner is too slow. It does not discriminate: cyberware, drones, and unshielded electronics in the blast radius take the same pulse a nanite lattice does, which is exactly why cleanup crews like it and exactly why a crew carrying one had better check what else is standing nearby.

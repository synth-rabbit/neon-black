# Reconciliation Facility Fatigues

**Catalog:** garments · **Tags:** *camp fatigues*, *RFID-chipped*, *marks you as Ledger property*, *broadcasts your location if not stripped* · **Flaws:** *incriminating* · **Requires setup:** no

## Description

Grey work fatigues, AP&I-stamped, with an RFID chip sewn into the collar seam that logs the wearer past every gate and checkpoint in [[palisade-player-primer#Coldwater Outfall|Reconciliation Facility 4]] (Bible §3, §5). They are warm, durable, and exactly what everyone in the Ledger wears — which is the entire problem outside the fence. Worn without the chip cut out, they broadcast the wearer's identity to any reader an AP&I facility owns; worn at all, they mark the wearer as camp property to anyone who recognizes the cut.

## Availability

camp-issue — what an escapee walks out of [[palisade-player-primer#Coldwater Outfall|the Ledger]] wearing, unless they had time to change

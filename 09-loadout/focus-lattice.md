# Focus Lattice

**Catalog:** tao-touched · **Tags:** *focus lattice*, *channels a Caster's Tao*, *must be attuned to one user*, *useless in another's hand* · **Flaws:** *requires setup* · **Requires setup:** yes

## Description

A caster-shell foci: a lattice of worked metal and channel-cut crystal, built the way the book's wands and staves are built, but understood here as a tool that concentrates and directs Tao a practitioner is already channeling rather than a magic item with a power of its own (Bible §1; [[style-guide]] §6). It has to be attuned to its user — hours of practice, the discipline the Ritual demands ([[style-guide]] §3) — before it does anything at all, and in anyone else's hand it is an expensive paperweight.

## Availability

black market / Wuji-adjacent — the closest thing this setting has to a wand, and just as rare

## Canon and flags

- **[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** restates the book's generic Source-Touched foci as a Tao-channeling tool, never a Mythos-granted relic.
- Bible §1: mages use technology to augment their magic; Brief §3.1 fixes Tao as impersonal — the lattice concentrates, it does not grant.

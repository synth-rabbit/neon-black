# Null-Cast Rounds

**Catalog:** ammo · **Tags:** *null-cast rounds*, *disrupts Tao-worked items*, *corp anti-Tao issue*, *does nothing to mundane targets* · **Flaws:** *weak against mundane targets* · **Requires setup:** no

## Description

Ammunition cored with a lattice designed to scramble the discipline holding a Tao-worked item or construct together — mundane engineering built specifically to counter Tao, not itself Tao-touched (Brief §3.1: the other corporations field their own anti-Tao solutions). Fired at a caster-shell weapon, a focus lattice, or a Tao construct, it disrupts the working; fired at anything else, it is just an expensive, ordinary bullet.

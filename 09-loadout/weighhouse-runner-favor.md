# Runner's Favor

**Catalog:** access-perks · **Tags:** *a runner's favor*, *one of Tally's people owes you*, *opens a door once*, *they'll want it back* · **Flaws:** *recurring fees* · **Requires setup:** no

## Description

One of Tally's runners — the ones who move goods through the Labyrinth's cut-throughs and know every lock-keeper by name — owes the crew something, or has been paid to owe it. Called in, it opens one door: a way through the Gullet nobody's watching, a word in the right ear, a delivery that arrives early. Called in, it is also spent, and the runner will expect the crew to owe them something in turn before long.

## Availability

black market — a favor called in from one of [[palisade-player-primer#Gullet Market|the Weighhouse]]'s runners

## Canon and flags

- [[palisade-player-primer#Gullet Market|Gullet Market]]: the Weighhouse's runners are part of Tally's network, the crew's day-one contact structure (Bible §5–6).

# Hocked Piece

**Catalog:** merc-gear · **Tags:** *a hocked piece*, *bought back cheap*, *someone else's story attached*, *Tally can reclaim it* · **Flaws:** *can be reclaimed* · **Requires setup:** no

## Description

Some piece of gear — a tool, a weapon, a chip, whatever the Weighhouse had on the shelf — that somebody else pawned to Tally and never came back for, bought out of hock at a price that makes it the crew's best deal all month. It comes with somebody else's fingerprints on it, figuratively and sometimes literally, and Tally's ledger still technically has a claim on it if she ever decides she wants it back for a debt of her own.

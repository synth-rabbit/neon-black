# Null-Field Emitter

**Catalog:** merc-gear · **Tags:** *null-field emitter*, *suppresses Tao in a radius*, *corp anti-Tao issue*, *dampens friendly Casters too* · **Flaws:** *heavy* · **Requires setup:** no

## Description

A backpack-mounted field generator that saturates a wide radius with an interference pattern engineered to disrupt Tao-worked effects — the portable, area version of the same anti-Tao engineering behind null-cast rounds (Brief §3.1). It does not care whose discipline it disrupts; a crew's own Caster inside the field is dampened exactly as much as the target the field was aimed at. Heavy enough that carrying it and running are not both options at once.

## Availability

corporate — carried by anti-Tao teams from a Big Three corp that is not Wuji-controlled

## Canon and flags

- Bible §3, Brief §3.1: anti-Tao solutions are a corp answer, not a Wuji product; this is one such device, mundane engineering rather than a Tao-touched item. **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-29) catalogued as merc-gear, not `tao-touched`.

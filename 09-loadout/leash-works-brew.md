# Kennel Brew

**Catalog:** enhancers · **Tags:** *Kennel brew*, *home-cooked*, *you don't know the dose*, *smells like a chop-shop* · **Flaws:** *unpredictable dose* · **Requires setup:** yes

## Description

A pack that cannot afford the Almoners' prices, or does not trust the cut, cooks its own. Kennel brew is reverse-engineered Leash boiled down in a Cinder Yards shed from whatever precursor chemistry the cook can steal or fake — it works, mostly, and nobody outside the pack that made it knows what 'mostly' means for any given batch. Requires setup: it has to be titrated by hand before use, over an open flame, by someone who has done it before.

## Availability

DIY — cooked by [[packs|the Run]]'s own chemists in [[cinder-yards]], never sold outside the pack that made it

## Canon and flags

- Bible §2 (werewolf): packs function like gangs; a gang with a supply problem solves it the way gangs solve supply problems.
- This item exists at the edge of what canon allows — the trigger is corporate-manufactured, so a DIY brew is necessarily an inferior, dangerous copy, never a real substitute; that instability is the point.

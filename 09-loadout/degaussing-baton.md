# Degaussing Baton

**Catalog:** weapons · **Tags:** *degaussing baton*, *close-range EM discharge*, *corp cleanup issue*, *needs a running charge* · **Flaws:** *short range* · **Requires setup:** no

## Description

A telescoping baton with a coil wound through the shaft: on contact it discharges a short electromagnetic pulse strong enough to scramble a nanite lattice at arm's length. Corporate cleanup crews carry these into confirmed flare-ups because a baton does not need line of sight and does not risk an EM round going through a wall into a crowded street. It has to be swung to work, which is the flaw in a single sentence.

## Availability

corporate (cleanup crews and AP&I site security) | black market, secondhand, from a decommissioned unit

- Bible §2 (vampire): EM disrupts the nanites; a melee tool sized for a cleanup crew, not a soldier.

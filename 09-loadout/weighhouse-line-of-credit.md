# Weighhouse Line of Credit

**Catalog:** access-perks · **Tags:** *Weighhouse line of credit*, *Tally will front it*, *she takes a cut until it's clear*, *she remembers exactly what you owe* · **Flaws:** *recurring fees* · **Requires setup:** no

## Description

Not an item so much as standing access: [[palisade-player-primer#The Weighhouse|Tally]] will front gear, favors, or cash against a job the crew hasn't been paid for yet, weighed and priced by her own scale (Bible §5–6; [[palisade-player-primer#Gullet Market|Gullet Market]]). It is how a crew with nothing gets its first Loadout item of any real value. She takes a percentage of everything the crew earns until the debt clears, and she does not forget the number even when the crew would like her to.

## Availability

black market — [[palisade-player-primer#Gullet Market|the Weighhouse]], only for a crew Tally has decided is worth fronting

## Canon and flags

- Bible §5–6: the fence is the crew's day-one contact and referral; [[palisade-player-primer#Gullet Market|Gullet Market]] BC-20: the Weighhouse's back room is the crew's first Living Space if she takes them.

# Sinks Scrap Shard

**Catalog:** tao-touched · **Tags:** *Tao-dense scrap*, *salvaged from the Sinks*, *hums faintly*, *does something nobody can predict* · **Flaws:** *unstable* · **Requires setup:** no

## Description

A fragment of scrap — server casing, pipe fitting, a piece of something nobody can identify — pulled from the drowned marsh below [[lowmere-sinks]], where Sinks families hoard Tao-dense material against a superstition the Bible leaves unexplained ([[lowmere-sinks]]; [[open-questions]] OQ-14). It is not a made item; it is raw, and using it for anything specific means gambling on what it turns out to be good for. Buyers hunt these because a Caster can sometimes work one into something with a name — most people just carry it and hope.

## Availability

black market — dug out of [[lowmere-sinks]] by families who hoard it, occasionally sold through [[gullet-market|the Weighhouse]]

## Canon and flags

- **[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** the generic Source-Touched category includes raw, unshaped material as well as crafted items (Core p. 266); this is Palisade's version, tied to a district already written as Tao-dense ([[lowmere-sinks]]).
- **[OPEN]** (OQ-14) whether the Sinks sit on a genuine Tao-dense site, or the scrap is rumor and superstition, is left open — the item asserts nothing either way.

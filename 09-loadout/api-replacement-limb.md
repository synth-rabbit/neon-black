# AP&I Replacement-Grade Limb

**Catalog:** attachments · **Tags:** *AP&I replacement limb*, *restores a lost function*, *warranty-monitored*, *maintenance fees never stop* · **Flaws:** *monitored by the company* · **Requires setup:** no

## Description

A standard AP&I prosthetic — an arm, a leg, an eye — sold as what it plainly is: a replacement for something the wearer lost, restoring function and nothing more. This is the cybernetics grade a [[open-questions|Baseline]] can take without crossing the line the Bible draws for hunters (Bible §2: cybernetics only as replacements for lost body parts). AP&I's warranty means AP&I's telemetry; the limb phones home, and the maintenance contract is not optional if the wearer wants it to keep working.

## Availability

corporate — AP&I, sold and fitted through licensed clinics such as [[suture-row|Marrowgate Clinic]]

## Canon and flags

- Bible §2 (hunter): Baselines use cybernetics only as replacements for lost body parts, never enhancement or sculpting — this item is written to sit squarely inside that line.
- Bible §3, [[build-choices]] BC-8: AP&I's specialty is cybernetics, the ladder itself; a Baseline buying this is buying the ladder's bottom rung, not climbing it.

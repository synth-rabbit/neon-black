# Continuity Crisis-Response Kit

**Catalog:** cyberspace · **Tags:** *Continuity response kit*, *field hacking suite*, *priority comms uplink*, *Continuity always knows where it is* · **Flaws:** *monitored by the company* · **Requires setup:** no

## Description

A hard-shell case that unfolds into a portable Harnessing rig, a priority comms uplink to Continuity's own dispatch, and the diagnostic tools a crisis-response team uses to secure a scene fast. It is genuinely excellent gear — Continuity's whole business is being the security and hacking firm that shows up faster and better-equipped than the police (Bible §3; [[names]] §2) — and every kit checks in with Continuity's dispatch on a schedule whether its holder wants it to or not.

## Availability

corporate — Continuity Risk & Response, standard field issue for its crisis-response and Harnessing teams

## Canon and flags

- Bible §3, [[build-choices]] BC-8: the upstart's specialty is security, crisis management, and hacking; this is Continuity's own field kit, with no hint that the firm is secretly Bloodware-run (Bible §5; OQ-17 stays closed).

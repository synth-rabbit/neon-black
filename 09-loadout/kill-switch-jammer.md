# Sump-Cut Signal Jammer

**Catalog:** cyberspace · **Tags:** *kill-switch jammer*, *blocks the activation signal*, *narrow band*, *if it fails, the switch fires* · **Flaws:** *battery drains quickly* · **Requires setup:** no

## Description

A palm-sized jammer tuned to the narrow signal band a changeling's kill switch listens on, built by someone who has taken one apart before. It does not remove the switch — nothing this small can — it only makes sure the activation signal never arrives while the jammer has power. A Doppel who has not yet had the switch cut relies on this to survive a job; a Doppel who has already been cut carries an old one out of habit, because the habit is what kept them alive.

## Availability

DIY — built in [[suture-row|the Sump]] or [[foundation-galleries|Gallery Nine]], never sold outside the Cutloose

## Canon and flags

- Bible §2 (changeling): changelings are owned via kill switches and trackers; escapees have removed theirs. This is what someone still wearing one, or newly cut, carries in the gap.
- The flaw is not cosmetic — the book pattern for dangerous gear is a Consequence that matters, and a dead jammer battery here means the kill switch is live again.

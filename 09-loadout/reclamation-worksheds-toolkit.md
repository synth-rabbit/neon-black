# Reclamation Shed Toolkit

**Catalog:** merc-gear · **Tags:** *work-shed toolkit*, *pry bar, cutters, torch*, *doubles as improvised weapons*, *obviously camp property* · **Flaws:** *cumbersome* · **Requires setup:** no

## Description

A canvas roll of the tools the Ledger's inmates actually use — a pry bar, bolt cutters, a hand torch, a spool of wire — issued for whatever the camp's processing plant needs done that shift and never counted carefully enough. In a breakout it is a lock-pick, a weapon, and a fence-cutter in one bundle; on the outside, a Chancery or AP&I patrol that finds it stenciled with the Ledger's inventory marks knows exactly where its owner came from.

## Availability

camp-issue — the tools of [[palisade-player-primer#Coldwater Outfall|the Ledger]]'s work sheds, carried out by whoever was on shift there

## Canon and flags

- Bible §3, §5: the camp is debt labor and produces something generic (Bible §5); work tools are the generic labor made concrete without answering what the camp produces.

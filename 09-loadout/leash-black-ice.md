# Black Ice

**Catalog:** enhancers · **Tags:** *Black Ice, uncut Leash*, *no filler*, *hits like a wall*, *somebody paid for this* · **Flaws:** *incriminating* · **Requires setup:** no

## Description

Trigger with nothing added: no filler, no stretching agent, straight off a line that was never meant to reach the street. Nobody sells Black Ice cheap and nobody who has it wants to explain how they got it — a dose this clean is either stolen from a corp shipment or cooked by someone who knows exactly what they are doing, and either way it is the kind of thing the Almoners notice missing.

## Availability

black market only — a handful of dealers under the Wall, priced by the dose, never on consignment

## Canon and flags

- Bible §2 (werewolf): the trigger's manufacture is corporate and its resale is criminal; uncut product sits at the seam between the two and belongs to neither cleanly.
- A PC carrying this is carrying evidence of a supply-chain leak — the flaw is narrative as much as mechanical.

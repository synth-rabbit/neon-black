# Orison 'Devotional' Sidearm

**Catalog:** weapons · **Tags:** *Orison directed-energy sidearm*, *silent discharge*, *corporate-Latin nameplate*, *needs a full charge cycle* · **Flaws:** *requires recharging* · **Requires setup:** no

## Description

A compact directed-energy pistol, engraved along the grip with the corporate-Latin nameplate every Orison product carries — a prayer for a weapons firm, empty corporate liturgy to anyone who doesn't know better (Bible §3; [[names]] §2). It fires without a report, which unsettles people who expect a gunshot, and it is precisely the kind of quiet, expensive, faintly reverent-looking hardware a corp nobody examines too closely would put its name on. Between shots it needs a full charge cycle — no quick reload, only a wait.

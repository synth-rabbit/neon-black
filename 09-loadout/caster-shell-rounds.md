# Caster-Shell Rounds

**Catalog:** tao-touched · **Tags:** *caster-shell rounds*, *Tao worked into the casing*, *fires once, warm for a week*, *burns out after use* · **Flaws:** *single use* · **Requires setup:** no

## Description

A round whose casing has had Tao worked into it at the moment it was cast — not enchanted in any storybook sense, but interwoven with the discipline the way a caster-shell weapon is interwoven with its user (Bible §1). Fired, it does something a mundane round cannot: burn through a ward, punch past armor that shouldn't be punchable, mark its target with something that lingers. The casing stays faintly warm for a week afterward and is worthless the moment it is spent — one shot, then it is just brass.

## Availability

black market — surfaces in [[gullet-market|the Gullet]] and nowhere reliable; Wuji quiet buyers and corporate purchasing agents both hunt these

## Canon and flags

- **[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** the book's generic Source-Touched ammunition (Core p. 266–267), restated under Tao: nobody owns or manufactures Tao, so this item was worked by a Caster, not blessed by a legend.
- Bible §1: Tao is interwoven with technology; the caster-shell is the Brief's own image for it (Brief §3.1).
- Impersonal Tao voice: the round does what it does through the work put into it, never because it 'wants' anything ([[style-guide]] §6).

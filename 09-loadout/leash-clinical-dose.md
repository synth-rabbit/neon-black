# Leash, Clinical Grade

**Catalog:** enhancers · **Tags:** *Leash, clinical grade*, *measured dose*, *Meliora batch stamp*, *steadies the shift* · **Flaws:** *source-traceable* · **Requires setup:** no

## Description

A single-use injector in a sealed cartridge, printed with a Meliora Bioworks lot code that was never meant to leave a lab. This is the trigger in the form Meliora makes it for whatever the corp actually uses it for (Bible §2, [[open-questions]] OQ-3) — filtered, measured, none of the cutting agents that make the street version a gamble. A Howler on a clinical dose shifts cleaner and comes down steadier than on anything the Almoners sell.

## Availability

corporate (diverted from Meliora Bioworks) | rare on the street — a stolen or bought-off case, not a corner buy

## Canon and flags

- Bible §2 (werewolf): the trigger is manufactured by one of the Big Three for a purpose the werewolf who takes it will never be told.
- **[OPEN]** (OQ-3) what Meliora actually uses this formulation for is not decided here; the item only states what the dose does to a Howler.
- The lot code is Meliora's own inventory control, not a street cutting mark — anyone who reads it can trace the case back to a specific shipment, which is the flaw.

# Degaussing Collar

**Catalog:** merc-gear · **Tags:** *degaussing collar*, *restrains via EM field*, *locks around the throat*, *drains fast* · **Flaws:** *battery drains quickly* · **Requires setup:** no

## Description

A restraint device, not a weapon: a collar that clamps and holds a continuous low-level electromagnetic field against the wearer's body, suppressing nanite regeneration without a lethal pulse. It is what AP&I's site security uses to hold a suspected Bloodware prisoner rather than kill them outright, which makes it as useful for a crew trying to take someone alive as for the corps trying to interrogate one. The field eats its own battery fast enough that it is a stopgap, not a solution.

## Availability

corporate (AP&I site security, also used at the Ledger) | black market, stolen or salvaged units

## Canon and flags

- Bible §2 (vampire): EM is the weakness, applied here as sustained suppression rather than a one-shot attack.
- AP&I's site security at [[coldwater-outfall|the Ledger]] is canon-adjacent (Bible §3, §5); this item gives that security a face without inventing new canon about it.

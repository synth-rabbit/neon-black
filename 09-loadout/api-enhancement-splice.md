# AP&I Enhancement-Grade Splice

**Catalog:** attachments · **Tags:** *AP&I enhancement splice*, *grants capability beyond baseline human*, *visibly Fitted*, *flags you to Amalgam's monitoring* · **Flaws:** *draws attention* · **Requires setup:** no

## Description

The other half of AP&I's catalog: chrome that adds a capability a baseline human body never had, not a replacement for anything lost. This is what makes someone Fitted (Bible §3; [[names]] §4) — visible, warrantied, monitored — and it is exactly the grade of cybernetics a hunter cannot take without stopping being a hunter (Bible §2: body sculpting and bio-manipulation are not on the table for hunters). Wearing it in public is wearing AP&I's brand on your body, and the company notices where its brand goes.

## Availability

corporate — AP&I, sold through Amalgam Stack's own storefronts and the Row's warranty clinics, not through the chop-shops

## Canon and flags

- Bible §2 (hunter): this is the line — enhancement-grade cybernetics is exactly what a Baseline may **not** take without crossing into a different splat's territory.
- Bible §3: caste band Fitted is defined by corporate-issue implants, warrantied and monitored ([[names]] §4) — this item is that definition made a loadout tag.

# Meliora Restorative Graft

**Catalog:** enhancers · **Tags:** *Meliora restorative graft*, *closes wounds fast*, *grown-tissue injector*, *rebound crash* · **Flaws:** *addictive* · **Requires setup:** no

## Description

Meliora's signature line: a single-use injector of grown tissue and accelerant that closes a wound in minutes rather than weeks, the same bio-science line that produces Leash and everything else Meliora Terraces grows (Bible §3; [[names]] §2). It is legitimately sold, warrantied, and expensive — Meliora's whole brand is 'better things,' and this is the better thing everyone under the Wall actually wants. Use it more than once in a short span and the crash on the way down is worse each time.

## Availability

corporate — Meliora Bioworks, sold through licensed clinics on [[suture-row]]'s upper floors and stolen everywhere else

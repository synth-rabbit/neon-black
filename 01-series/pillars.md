# Pillars

The book's thematic pillars (Core p. 18, restated for Series setup on p. 146) mapped onto the Bible's four themes. The mapping is the interviewer's choice recorded as [[build-choices]] BC-4 (Brief §1.3); this file restates it, ranks the pillars, and defines the one pillar the canon forces the vault to rewrite.

## The Bible's four themes → the book's pillars (Brief §1.3)

| Bible theme (§4) | Primary pillar | Secondary pillar |
|---|---|---|
| **The price of climbing when the ladder is made of your own body** | Transhumanism | Technological Overdrive / Moral Bankruptcy |
| **Who counts as human when the mods decide your caste** | Transhumanism | Oligarchic Corruption |
| **Ownership versus freedom** — kill switches, trigger dependency, nanite bondage | Oligarchic Corruption | Gangs, Cults & Counterculture |
| **The city as something worth saving versus something worth seizing** | Oligarchic Corruption | Gangs, Cults & Counterculture |

In one line each ([[style-guide]] §4): *the ladder is your body; the mods decide who is human; owned or free; save the city or seize it.*

## Tao Weaponized `[TAO-REINTERPRETED]`

The book calls Mythology Weaponized "the most central theme of :Otherscape" (Core p. 146): legends returning through the Noise as **Mythoi**, working on the world through gateways called **Sources** — Relics, Thin Places, Esoterica, Avatars — which corporations, cults, and mercs covet, hoard, and weaponize (Core p. 20–21). "The hunt is on."

The Bible removes the legends. **Tao is the only mystical force; there are no legend-Mythoi** (Brief §3.1). Tao is impersonal — a force with disciplines, no will, no agenda, no story to re-enact — and it predates the mods by an unknown span (Bible §1, §3). It can be interwoven with technology, and it can produce beings (Brief §3.1).

**[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** (CR-1) So the pillar becomes **Tao Weaponized**, defined as follows:

> **Tao Weaponized.** Tao is real, older than the chrome, and rarer than any implant. It works on the world through *gateways* — a **practice** kept with discipline, an **item** into which Tao has been worked, a **place** where Tao runs dense, a **being** Tao has made. Nobody owns Tao and nobody can manufacture it, which is exactly why the corporations and the cabals hunt its gateways: a practice can be recruited, an item can be bought or stolen, a place can be fenced off, a being can be leashed. In Palisade a Tao gateway is a weapon or a commodity, and the Wuji — the one group that understands the force — keep their understanding secret and their corporation quiet, while the other two corporations answer with rebel practitioners and anti-Tao countermeasures of their own (Bible §2 mage).

What survives from the book's pillar, and what changes:

| Book element (Core p. 20–21, 167) | Under Tao |
|---|---|
| Mythoi as legends with stories, wants, and agendas | **Gone.** Tao *flows*, *concentrates*, *is channeled* ([[style-guide]] §6). It never wants. |
| Sources as gateways for a Mythos | **Tao gateways**: practice, item, place, being (Brief §3.2). The word *Source* survives only as a mechanical term. |
| Relics | Tao-touched items — caster-shell-style, Tao worked into an object (Bible stated influences). The Loadout catalog's *Source-Touched Items* become *Tao-touched* (Plan A.4, WP5). |
| Thin Places | Places where Tao runs dense. **[OPEN]** (OQ-14) Whether they exist; the Generator's results that point at one are written as rumor, never as a fact on the map. |
| Esoterica | Tao disciplines — the Wuji's practices; the Esoterica themebook re-flavored (Brief §2.5 mage). |
| Avatars — living incarnations of a legend | **Gone as legend.** The Essence *Avatar* survives mechanically as a person of one discipline or gateway with a self-imposed law for an Agenda (Brief §3.3; CR-8). |
| Source-Touched people | Tao-touched: Casters (pure users), Doppels (Tao and tech at the body level), Baselines who use it low and indirect (Bible §2). |
| Conjurations | **Gone.** Companion themes are Tao-made beings — constructs, not summoned legends (Brief §3.2). |
| "Corporate Magic" — a corp ruled by a Mythos, mass-producing a Mythic effect, equipping staff with Sources, harvesting a local Source (Generator, Core p. 167) | A corp quietly run by a practice-society (the Wuji and [[corp-b|Orison Defense Systems]]); the attempt to mass-produce what a discipline does; agents equipped with Tao-touched items; a dense place fenced and harvested. The Generator's *Mythology Weaponized* rows (Core p. 167) are read this way whenever they come up — every such reinterpretation in `05-megacity/districts/` is marked. |
| Cults of a Mythos | Practice circles, respectable fronts, and the Quiet Hand rumor — never worship, because there is nothing to worship. |

Two more consequences the districts rely on:

- **Tao and the Masquerade.** Whether the public knows Tao exists at all — as distinct from knowing a *society* exists — is **[OPEN]** (OQ-6). The vault writes it as hidden, so a Tao gateway on the street reads to the public as a trick, a drug, a new implant, a stunt — the Noise does the rest (Brief §4.1).
- **Tao and cyberspace.** Whether Tao is present in Nearspace, Domains, or the Tangle is **[OPEN]** (OQ-15). No district asserts it; two districts ([[foundation-galleries]], [[lowmere-sinks]]) leave the hook.

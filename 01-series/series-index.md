# Series Index

The four Series-level files (Plan WP1): what the Series is about, which pillars it leans on, the one story under it and the one clock the Bible confirms, and the words it is written in. All four are MC-only; the player-facing statement of the Series' premise is the crew's own — [[crew-index]] and [[session-zero-packet]].

Read them in this order: [[series-concept]] → [[pillars]] → [[spine-and-clock]] → [[tone]]. The Countdown Challenge the spine points at is [[secret-war-goes-public]] (in `08-challenges/custom/`), and the arithmetic of the three jobs against it is tabled in [[spine-and-clock]] (BC-172).

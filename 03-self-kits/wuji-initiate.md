# Wuji Initiate

**Themebook:** Affiliation · **Category:** Self · **Fits:** any of the five · **Source:** custom

You were noticed at [[palisade-player-primer#Chancery Hill|Aldine House]] and brought into its morning practice — most naturally a Caster taking the first steps of a Tao discipline, but the tags fit anyone the House has quietly opened its gate to. Reflavor of Forbidden Cult (Core p. 198) for a respectable members' club that is [[palisade-player-primer#The Quiet Hand|the Wuji]]'s front. **[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** Nothing in this Affiliation names Tao as a legend or a will; the practice is a discipline, a condition, a thing you keep or lose, in the register Brief §3.2 sets.

## Canon and flags

- Canon relied on: the society identifies talented people and brings them in, secret, controls Corp B (Bible §2, §3); Aldine House (`build-choices` BC-19).
- **[BUILD CHOICE]** This kit must not reveal that the House is a front, that it is the Wuji, or that it controls [[palisade-player-primer#Orison Defense Systems|Orison Defense Systems]] — a member's own Affiliation card reads exactly as the respectable club Chancery Hill sees, per Brief §4.4's requirement.

# Pack Affiliation

**Themebook:** Affiliation · **Category:** Self · **Fits:** any of the five · **Source:** custom

You belong to a pack of [[palisade-player-primer#The Run|the Run]] — most naturally a Howler who found a family in the sculpt and the Leash, but the tags fit anyone the pack has taken in: a supplier, a medic, a Baseline who runs with them anyway. Reflavor of Street Gang (Core p. 199) for pack culture. This kit is player-safe: it never states that the wearer shifts, needs the trigger, or is bio-sculpted — that content belongs to the werewolf package (`02-splats/werewolf/`), which links here for the Affiliation half of a Howler's build.

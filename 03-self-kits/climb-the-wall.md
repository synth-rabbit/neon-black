# Climb the Wall

**Themebook:** Horizon · **Category:** Self · **Fits:** any of the five · **Source:** custom

You mean to get off the Foot — climb Palisade's tiers the way you'd climb anything else, one purchased or stolen or earned rung at a time. In Palisade *climb* is not a metaphor (Bible §3; [[palisade-player-primer#The Wall|Palisade]]). This is a purely personal Horizon: it does not decide whether the city gets fixed or taken (that's [[fix-the-city]] and [[take-the-city]]); it's about one person's own ladder.

# Fix the City

**Themebook:** Horizon · **Category:** Self · **Fits:** any of the five · **Source:** custom

You've decided Palisade is worth saving — one district, one Kitchen ledger torn up, one caste line pushed back, at a time. This is the *save it* half of the Series' fourth theme (Bible §4): the city as something worth saving. Nothing here decides what the crew as a whole will do (that stays a Job-by-Job, table decision, per `series-concept`) — this is one PC's own Horizon, which the crew doesn't have to share.

# Weighhouse Credit

**Themebook:** Assets · **Category:** Self · **Fits:** any of the five · **Source:** custom

You have standing with [[palisade-player-primer#The Weighhouse|Marisol Okonkwo, "Tally"]] and her [[palisade-player-primer#The Weighhouse|network]] out of the [[palisade-player-primer#Gullet Market|Weighhouse]] — the crew's single day-one contact (Bible §5, §6), whether you were the one Rook actually pointed to or you earned your own line since. This is what she extends: not cash, but credit, and credit at the Weighhouse always comes with a cut. Reflavor of Assets' Money to Burn (Core p. 203) as a debt-based line of credit instead of wealth.

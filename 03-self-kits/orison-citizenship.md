# Orison Citizenship

**Themebook:** Affiliation · **Category:** Self · **Fits:** any of the five · **Source:** custom

You wore [[palisade-player-primer#Orison Defense Systems|Orison Defense Systems]]' colors — a range crew hand, a junior engineer, a security contractor, a soldier on a corporate contract. Orison Reach is the most orderly ground on the Wall, and it made you that way too. This is Corporate Citizenship (Core p. 198) reflavored for an arms manufacturer, and it reads, deliberately, as nothing more: a disciplined weapons firm, its people, its rules. Nothing here is Tao.

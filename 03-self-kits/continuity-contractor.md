# Continuity Contractor

**Themebook:** Affiliation · **Category:** Self · **Fits:** any of the five · **Source:** custom

You work for [[palisade-player-primer#Continuity Risk & Response|Continuity Risk & Response]] — security, crisis management, or the hacking side of the business — and you were probably hired because the firm is growing fast and paying well, not because you knew what you were signing up for. Reflavor of Corporate Citizenship (Core p. 198) for the challenger corp. This kit is a security-firm contractor's card and nothing more: it does not say what Continuity is underneath, because street-level Continuity staff don't know either (Bible §5).

A Continuity contractor's own tags read a security firm and nothing else; a PC who takes this kit and later learns the truth learns it the same way any street-level Bloodware would (Bible §5), through play, not through their character sheet.

# Take the City

**Themebook:** Horizon · **Category:** Self · **Fits:** any of the five · **Source:** custom

You've decided Palisade is worth seizing — you mean to end up holding a piece of it that nobody can take back. This is the *take it* half of the Series' fourth theme (Bible §4): the city as something worth seizing. As with [[fix-the-city]], this is one PC's own ambition; it does not commit the crew to anything until the table decides that together (`series-concept`).

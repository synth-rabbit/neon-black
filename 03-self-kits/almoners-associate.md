# Almoners Associate

**Themebook:** Affiliation · **Category:** Self · **Fits:** any of the five · **Source:** custom

You run with [[palisade-player-primer#The Almoners and the Kitchen|the Almoners]] — a Kitchen volunteer who is more than a volunteer, a runner on the vans, a collector, someone who grew up owing them and never stopped. Reflavor of Criminal Syndicate (Core p. 198) for a charity that is also a drug distribution network, and a debt economy that is also a community. Unlike the corp citizenships, this Affiliation can say what it is: the Almoners' business is street-level fact in Palisade, not an MC secret.

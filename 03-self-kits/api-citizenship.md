# AP&I Citizenship

**Themebook:** Affiliation · **Category:** Self · **Fits:** any of the five · **Source:** custom

You worked the Stack — a foundry hand, an assembly-line technician, a fitting-theater tech, a floor supervisor. [[palisade-player-primer#Amalgamated Prosthetic & Interface|Amalgamated Prosthetic & Interface]] requires invasive augmentation of every worker as a condition of employment, and it is sold, honestly, as a benefit: a Stock body that signs on comes out Fitted. It's also a leash. This kit reflavors Corporate Citizenship (Core p. 198) for AP&I and says nothing about the remediation contract at [[palisade-player-primer#Coldwater Outfall|Coldwater Outfall]] — the escapee list, if it touches this character, is a matter for their Troubled Past, not this theme.

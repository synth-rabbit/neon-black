# The Ledger

**Themebook:** Troubled Past · **Category:** Self · **Fits:** any of the five · **Source:** custom

You were sent to [[palisade-player-primer#Coldwater Outfall|Reconciliation Facility 4]] — the Ledger, its inmates call it — debt labor and penal, secret, and every PC in this Series shares this kit's central fact: you were on the work crew that escaped when the strike hit (Bible §5, §6). Every question here is written so it can be answered without deciding what got you sent there, why the work crew held all five, or what any PC saw before the sentence — those stay the player's to answer, not this kit's to assert.

## Nascent form

Title tag **the Ledger** + one weakness (e.g. *the truck gate, the floodlights, the klaxon*) reads as a theme on its own: someone who did time in the camp, however they answer everything else.

## Canon and flags

- Canon relied on: the camp is debt-labor and penal, secret, produces something generic, is not the mystery; the PCs escape in the breakout on their work crew (Bible §5, §6).
- **[BUILD CHOICE]** Written so **any** PC can answer every question regardless of which of the five they are, per Brief §4.4 — nothing here requires or forbids being human, Bloodware, Howler, Caster, or Doppel. WP7a's breakout job is where the Ledger's scenes and Flashbacks live; this kit is the character-creation half.
- **[OPEN]** (OQ-12, OQ-13) What sent this PC to the Ledger and why the work crew held all five stay unanswered here — must not be filled by this file.

## Canon and flags

- Every kit follows Plan A.5: ten power tags A–J (A the title tag), four weakness tags A–D, one motivation in the kit's category voice, a nascent form that reads as a theme on its own.
- Rarity: none of these kits imply one of the five is common (Bible §2); Self kits describe institutions and circumstance, never the five directly.

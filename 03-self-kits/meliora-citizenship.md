# Meliora Citizenship

**Themebook:** Affiliation · **Category:** Self · **Fits:** any of the five · **Source:** custom

You're on [[palisade-player-primer#Meliora Bioworks|Meliora Bioworks]]' books — a technician, a clinic hand, a ground-crew hire, a research assistant, whatever the badge says. Meliora made the ladder's flesh half, and for as long as you draw its pay, some part of that ladder is yours to stand on. Reflavor of the book's Corporate Citizenship (Core p. 198) for a bio-genomics giant. This kit says nothing about what Meliora's pharmaceutical line actually produces; it is a citizenship, not a conspiracy.

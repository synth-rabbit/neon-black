# Caste-Sorted

**Themebook:** Troubled Past · **Category:** Self · **Fits:** any of the five · **Source:** custom

You used to stand higher on the Wall. A corporation dropped you, a family disowned you, a warranty lapsed, a sculpt went wrong and nobody paid to fix it — however it happened, someone made it official, and you came down a tier ([[palisade-player-primer#The Wall|the caste gradient]], `names` §4). This is Palisade's version of Disaster Survivor or Target of the Government (Core p. 218–219): a fall that was somebody's decision, not an accident.

## Nascent form

Title tag **sorted down the Wall** + one weakness (e.g. *the family or firm that dropped me*) reads as a theme on its own: someone who fell, and remembers exactly how far.

## Canon and flags

- Canon relied on: the beginning of a formal caste system, mobility harder the lower you are (Bible §3); caste slang is street-level, not legal (Brief §6.6; `build-choices` BC-9).
- **[BUILD CHOICE]** This kit does not assert that castes are legally formalized or visibly marked — `open-questions` OQ-8 stays open. It names the *fall*, not a registry.

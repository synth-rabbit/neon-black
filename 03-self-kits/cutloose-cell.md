# Cutloose Cell

**Themebook:** Affiliation · **Category:** Self · **Fits:** any of the five · **Source:** custom

You're part of a Cutloose cell — most naturally a Doppel who removed a kill switch and a tracker and never went back, but the tags fit an ally too: someone who shelters them, forges for them, or simply owes the network a debt of the same kind. Reflavor of Counterculture (Core p. 198) for the escaped-changeling network. Player-safe by design: nothing here states that the wearer has, or had, a kill switch or a tracker — that fact belongs to the changeling package (`02-splats/changeling/`), which this Affiliation is built to sit underneath.

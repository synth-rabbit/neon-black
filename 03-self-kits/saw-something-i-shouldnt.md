# Saw Something I Shouldn't

**Themebook:** Troubled Past · **Category:** Self · **Fits:** any of the five · **Source:** custom

**[BUILD CHOICE]** Before the Ledger, you were a freelance low-level criminal who saw something you shouldn't have — that is why you were sent there, whether or not you understood what you saw (Bible §6). Every other kit in this vault gives you the content along with the question. This one does not: **the tags below are fill-in prompts, not suggested answers.** Title tag A fixes the theme's shape — *I saw something I shouldn't have* — and every tag from B onward is the matching themebook question rewritten as a blank for the player to fill during character creation (Core p. 177, 190). Nothing here decides what was seen, who else was there, or whether it connects to the Series spine — that stays the player's answer, not this file's (Brief §12; `open-questions` OQ-12).

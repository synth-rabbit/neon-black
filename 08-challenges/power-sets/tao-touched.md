# Tao-Touched

**Applies to:** Any Challenge channeling Tao directly — a Caster, a Wuji agent, a rebel corp Tao user, or a Tao-worked being. · **Category:** Mythos · **Splat:** mage

**[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** The setting's re-flavor of the book's *Arcane* Mythos Power Set (Core p. 330), rewritten so nothing here gives Tao a will (Brief §3.1; [[style-guide]] §6). Where the book's Arcane Challenge "unleashes the magic of their respective Source" through named elements (fire, water, earth, wind, toxin), this overlay unleashes a **working** — Tao concentrated and directed through a caster-shell technique, described impersonally throughout. It applies as an overlay to any Tao-channeling NPC across Key Players: a rank-and-file Wuji agent, a Caster the Almoners or a corp has hired, or a rebel Tao user inside Orison, Meliora, or AP&I (Bible §2: "the other corporations have a few rebel Tao users").

## Tags & statuses

caster-shell technique, Tao interwoven with tech, not separate from it

## Specials

**Working:** Once per scene, during Establish, create up to three story tags representing a Tao working — force, barrier, binding, tech disruption, or a tracking mark. Never described as a spell "wanting" an outcome.

**Severs the Working:** Once per scene, burn a tag representing a Tao working or Tao-touched item before a PC's roll — disruption of a working, not banishment of a being.

## Threats / Consequences

› Reach toward a caster-shell focus, or simply concentrate, and let Tao flow through the working.
» Force: a directed kinetic burst (*blasted-3*)
» Ward: a barrier that must be broken to pass (*break:3*)
» Bind: restrains a target (*restrained-2*)
» Disrupt: interferes with nearby tech or cyberware (*glitched-2*)
» Mark: leaves a trackable working on a target (*marked by the working*)

## Canon and flags

- Bible §1: Tao predates the mods and is interwoven with technology, not separate from it. Bible §2 (mage): a secret society brings talented people into the fold; other corps have a few rebel Tao users. Brief §3.1: Tao is impersonal — "flows, concentrates, is channeled, interwoven," never "wants" or "chooses" ([[style-guide]] §6).
- **[TAO-REINTERPRETED]** the entire file, re-based from the book's Arcane Power Set (Core p. 330) per Brief §3.2 and [[conflict-register]] CR-1.

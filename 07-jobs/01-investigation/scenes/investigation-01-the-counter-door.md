# The Counter Door

**Job:** [[investigation]] · **Order:** 1 · **District:** [[gullet-market]] · **Story tag:** *everything falls to the Gullet* · **Core moment:** no

## Set piece

The second hour of the morning, which at the Weighhouse is the middle of the working day. A coaster is tied up at the water door and dockers are walking crates up the ramp two at a time. The brass balance hangs under the roof, swinging very slightly; [[bohdan-adeyemi|Bohdan]] is on the water side with a hand on the frame, not doing anything. The Slate by the counter door has forty names chalked on it and the crew's figure is one of them, four lines down, and it has not moved since the day they arrived.

[[marisol-okonkwo|Tally]] is behind the counter in her apron. There is food on the table in the back room before anyone asks for any. She weighs three things while she talks, prices them, and does not look up.

What she says, in order, in the flattest voice she has:

A runner called [[wax|Wax]] — twenty, fast, a year in the network, carries a docker's jacket with the pockets resewn — went out the counter door on the second night of the week with a bundle nobody weighed. She has not come back and her chits have not been redeemed. That is nine days.

The bundle was **pages**. Not goods. Pages out of the Book, pulled against a standing order that has been running eighteen months, in three categories that do not sit together: *corporate shipping losses; chrome with dead tags; things nobody can explain.* Somebody buys them through three intermediaries and Tally has never met any of them and has not stopped selling (**[OPEN]** OQ-37).

Then the part she does not say twice: she is not sending the network. A fence who is seen looking for her own paper is a fence whose paper is worth taking. She is hiring a crew nobody in the Gullet has an opinion about yet. The fee is real, the fee is chalked, and there is no version of this where the crew are not more owed at the end than at the start ([[the-weighhouse]], *The Percentage*).

If a PC asks to read the Book she says no, pleasantly, once. That subject is closed for the Series.

# The Counting Room

![[assets/jobs/investigation/investigation-07-the-counting-room.png]]

**Job:** [[investigation]] · **Order:** 7 · **District:** [[kilbride-stretch]] · **Story tag:** *nobody official comes down here* · **Core moment:** no · **Set piece:** yes

The confrontation. Everything the crew have been following is in one room with a man standing next to it.

## Set piece

Kilometres of prefab sprawl and automats where the Stock live, the robots work, and every official expects a bribe ([[kilbride-stretch]]). The address off the concurrence is a **converted automat unit** on a service street: a shuttered frontage with a dead menu board, and behind it one long room with a bonded door.

Inside: strip lights, a weigh-in desk with a lamp over it, a set of counter scales that are honest, and four hundred bundles on numbered shelves — oilcloth, string, and a docket on each. It smells of paper and cold fat. There is a haulier's booking sheet pinned by the door with a date on it.

A site rig hums in the corner, AP&I-supplied, the kind a bonded room rents by the month to prove it is not being interfered with ([[anti-tao-countermeasure-challenge]]; BC-117).

[[anselm-boateng|Anselm Boateng]] is at the desk in a good coat, doing the accounts. He is not surprised. He does not run. He has hired four people for the evening who are somewhere in the shelves, and he expects the crew to notice them before he has to mention it.

## What is in the room

The **consignment ledger**, open, in his handwriting:

- **Category one** — corporate shipping losses. Eight years of pages, bought out of the Book and out of two smaller houses.
- **Category two** — chrome with dead tags. Six years.
- **Category three** — things nobody can explain. Thin, expensive, and mostly Gullet.
- **Category four** — added eleven months ago, and the reason Wax is dead: **name-bearing paper.** Eleven AP&I write-off year-blocks bought as data remediation. Nine Chancery second copies bought off [[emeric-vann]] and two others. And, at the top of the block, a bundle of pages pulled from the Book against a specific list of dates — the bundle Wax was carrying, which is on shelf 388, weighed and docketed and never returned, because when the two hired hands who took it off her were told to bring the pages they brought the pages.

The ledger is the proof. It is also the whole of the answer available in this room.

## What the Factor is

He will talk, at length, courteously, because talking is cheaper than the alternative and he knows the crew are not police. He is a **consolidator**: he buys on commission for people who do not want to be seen buying, he has done it for twenty years, and he is very good at it. He has three intermediaries, a category list, a delivery address that changes each run, and a payment method.

He does not know who is buying. Pressed, he gives a name — an insurance-forensics consolidator with an office on the Face — and it is checkable and it checks out, to a second shell, which checks out to a third. He is not lying and he is not stupid; he has simply never had a reason to look, and the reason he has never had a reason is that the money has been perfect.

What he will say about the money is the useful part: it arrives as **pre-purchased concurrences**, transferred rather than spent, from an account that has more of them than any consolidator has ever seen (BC-122; [[government]]). Whoever the principal is, they buy lawfulness in bulk and hand it out as change.

He will not say who takes delivery. He does not take the consignment anywhere. A haulier comes.

## The hard choice here

**Casters.** The site rig in the corner is doing what a site rig does: reading the room and then flooding it with Noise until nothing can be held ([[anti-tao-countermeasure-challenge]]; [[casters]]). A discipline whose condition requires stillness, silence, or an unbroken working does not survive it. Keeping the condition means going out and coming back, and the crew do not have that time on [[the-consignment-window]]. Breaking it means acting now and lapsing, with the practice down until Downtime — through [[investigation-08-the-cordon]], which is the worst possible scene to be an ordinary person in.

**Howlers**, if the roof-space dose was taken in [[investigation-02-the-labyrinth-route]]: the crew got here ahead of the Factor's evening hires and the four are still arriving, one at a time, through a door the crew can watch.

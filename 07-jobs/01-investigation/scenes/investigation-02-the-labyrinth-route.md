# The Labyrinth Route

**Job:** [[investigation]] · **Order:** 2 · **District:** [[gullet-market]] · **Story tag:** *everything falls to the Gullet* · **Core moment:** no

This is a **"snooping around" scene** in the book's sense (Core p. 291): intel gathering, run as a montage with each PC on their own side task, or wrapped in a few Quick outcomes with the players paying for what they get. Only the third stop wants a Tracked outcome.

## Set piece

The Labyrinth after the tide turns: a kilometre of stalls under corrugated plastic and pirate cabling, lit by shop signs and the Bowl's screen through the girders, wet underfoot, crowded from dawn to the small hours. Nothing here can be surveilled and nothing here can be searched. Everyone knows the Weighhouse hired somebody and nobody knows who, which lasts about two hours.

Three stops, in the order Wax made them.

**One — the pawnbroker under the north stalls.** An old man with a hand-written day-book and a lens screwed into one eye. He redeemed Wax's chit at half past eleven and gave her a bundle wrapped in oilcloth. He remembers because it was paper, and paper is not a thing anybody pawns. His day-book has the entry. The **stub is torn out** — the counterfoil, the half with the payer's mark on it — and he says he tore it himself, for a customer, for a fee, and he is not going to pretend otherwise at his age. That is the Job's first cover-up (Core p. 286).

**Two — the lock-keeper at Lock Three.** A coaster was in that night and the keeper was on the shed roof. He saw Wax cross the quay at midnight with the bundle under her jacket, and he saw who was waiting: two people who were not from the Gullet, hired for the evening, standing where a person stands when they do not intend to be there long. He can describe the money: a **receipt**, written out and countersigned, in a hand that is not the Weighhouse's, settled in **Chancery instruments** rather than cash. Under the Wall, that is nearly a signature.

**Three — the stop she never made.** A rig-builder's drop at the market's east end. He waited an hour and went home. He is the one who says the name, because he has taken the same commission twice: the buyer is a consolidator, he works for people who do not want to be seen buying, and the Foot calls him **the Factor**.

## The roof-space

The Labyrinth's roof — corrugated plastic on a lattice of scaffold, three metres up, running the whole market — is how a runner crosses the Gullet faster than anything on wheels, and how a crew gets from stop one to stop three ahead of the Factor's own people. It does not take a normal body's weight at a normal body's speed.

**Howler hard choice.** An Almoners collector is working the Gullet that night with clinical-grade on him ([[leash-almoners-cut]]; [[syndicate]]). He will not sell. He will trade — for the name of who is paying the crew. Paying him puts the Weighhouse in the Kitchen's ledger permanently and Tally will find out, in her own time, from somebody else. Refusing closes the roof-space: stop three then costs a Tracked outcome and a scene's worth of time on [[the-consignment-window]]. ([[howlers]]; Bible §2 werewolf — the Almoners supply, Meliora manufactures.)

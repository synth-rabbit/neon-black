# The Cordon

![[assets/jobs/investigation/investigation-08-the-cordon.png]]

**Job:** [[investigation]] · **Order:** 8 · **District:** [[kilbride-stretch]] · **Story tag:** *nobody official comes down here* · **Core moment:** **yes** · **Climax**

The finale, and the tension is at maximum (Core p. 291). This is an **escape**, not a boss fight — although there is a boss in the room if the crew want one and did not deal with him in [[investigation-07-the-counting-room]].

## Set piece

Prepared as a situation (Core p. 290). The set is a prefab service street at night in the Stretch: shuttered automats, one working streetlight, a bonded door standing open behind the crew and four hundred bundles of other people's history inside it.

A **grey van with the side door already open** stops where nothing was parked a minute ago. People in matte soft-shell come out at a walk, not a run, with a serial where a name should be. Tape goes across the street at both ends. Somebody says, pleasantly, that this is now a declared scene and everybody's evening has been rearranged ([[continuity-crisis-response-cell]], *For The Duration Of The Emergency*).

They are not looking for the crew. They are here for the **room**.

Then the second set of headlights: a Chancery patrol car, unhurried, and an **Envelope Detail** that also has paper for this address and this hour ([[envelope-detail]]). Two lawful forces, one street, one window — which is the exact commodity the Hill sells and the exact failure the Hill's double-selling produces (BC-122; [[government]]).

## The escape

Ways out, all of them costed:

- **The service run.** Automat blocks in the Stretch back onto a shared delivery trench; it is unlit, it is a hundred metres, and it comes out inside the far cordon line. The Stretch's own residents will not help and will not report.
- **Through the room.** The bonded door logs, and [[the-counting-room]] is a barrier from the inside as much as the outside. Burning the shelves ends the consignment and ends the evidence and is the loudest thing anybody could do here.
- **Under the tape, as somebody with a reason to be there.** A face, a badge, a hi-vis, a fitted-out story. Works exactly once.
- **Straight at it.** [[continuity-crisis-response-cell]] is Scale 2, has *Overlapping Fields*, and does not negotiate. A crew that fights it in a street with witnesses is the second Threat on [[secret-war-goes-public]] happening in front of the Stretch.

**The Factor** is in the room behind them the whole time, and his evening arrangement does not cover this. He will trade anything he has left for a way out that is not a van, including the delivery contact he swore he did not have — which is a name, and it is a haulier's name, and it is worth precisely as much as a haulier's name is worth.

## The public-war notch

[[secret-war-goes-public]] is in play in every scene of the Series, and this is the scene where the crew can move it (Brief §7.3).

- **A Continuity cell and a Chancery detail draw on each other in a street with witnesses** → `public-war-2`, and the Stretch's tier lifts lock down for the rest of the Job.
- **Something happens here that no Baseline could have done, in front of somebody's feed** → `public-war-1`.
- **Nothing public happens, or it happens with no witnesses and no feed** → nothing. The Noise absorbs it (*The Noise Absorbs*). This is the outcome a careful crew earns, and it is worth earning.

# Ivo Meszaros

![[assets/npcs/ivo-meszaros.png]]

**Handle:** — · **Affiliation:** none (the Row's own economy, [[suture-row]]) · **Role in the pilot:** the cold room of [[investigation-03-the-cold-room]]; a loot-catalog contact in [[investigation-aftermath]].

## Who they are

Fifties, chain-drinking tea, a Patched left hand he built himself, does anything, asks nothing, takes chrome in part-payment. Threadneedle is a street-level chop-shop under a hand-painted sign: three surgical bays, a fitter's bench, a waiting room that is also his kitchen, and in storm season four centimetres of floodwater in the corridor. [[suture-row]] left the surgeon at Threadneedle unnamed for a job to name; this is the name (BC-152).

He did not strip Wax, and says so, and is telling the truth; he can read what was taken out of her and what was left in. He will not price, cut, or keep the second body on his tables — male, no papers, burned from the inside out with the skin unmarked — because he has seen two others this year and has stopped asking ([[em-ambush]]; **[OPEN]** OQ-17). He would like it gone.

The Baseline hard choice runs through his shop: AP&I's licensed channel on the Row will fit an interface port this afternoon, on credit, and it is an enhancement ([[api-enhancement-splice]]); Meszaros's bench reader is the other way, four hours and no line crossed ([[baselines]]).

## Vector

- **Want:** the shop open, the kettle on, and nothing on his tables he would have to remember.
- **Push:** the bench, the freezer, the reader, and the Row's unwritten rule that he keeps no records and the Row talks anyway.

## What the crew knows / does not know

**Knows:** Tally's surgeon on the Row who will not ask ([[the-weighhouse]]). **Does not know:** how many burned bodies he has seen, until he says.

# Anselm Boateng

![[assets/npcs/anselm-boateng.png]]

**Handle:** "the Factor" · **Affiliation:** none — and that is the point of him · **Role in the pilot:** the opposition of [[investigation]], named in clue 2, felt in clues 3–6, present in [[investigation-07-the-counting-room]].

## Who they are

Sixties, tall, a good coat kept brushed, reading glasses on a cord, an accent from three districts up that he has never bothered to lose. He buys paper for people who do not want to be seen buying paper, and he has done it for twenty years — for insurers, for auction houses, for two of the Big Three, for a cultural foundation on [[chancery-hill]] whose name he still will not say, and for eighteen months now for whoever has been emptying the Book ([[fence-network]], BC-102). *Factor* is the trade's own word for a commission agent buying for a principal; the Foot uses it as a job, not a title.

He is not brave, not armed, not augmented past a good ear and a better memory, and he does not need to be. Nothing he owns is in the room, nothing in the room is his, and every arrangement he makes has a person in it who is not him. He has never once been in a room when it went badly. He believes — and will say, and is not lying — that his principal is an insurance-forensics consolidator. He has never tested it. When the crew press him for a name he gives them one, and it is a shell, and it checks out to another shell.

## Vector

- **Want:** to close the order.
- **Push:** other people's money, other people's hands, and bought lawfulness — a principal who settles in pre-purchased concurrences and has given him a few to spend ([[government]], BC-122).

**Knows:** by clue 2, that a consolidator called the Factor placed the order and pays in Chancery instruments. **Does not know:** that he does not know either. `name the principal` is immune on his profile and the profile says so as a Special, so the table cannot spend a session on it (BC-152).

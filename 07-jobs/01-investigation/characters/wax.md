# Wax

![[assets/npcs/wax.png]]

**Handle:** "Wax" · **Affiliation:** [[fence-network|the Weighhouse]] · **Role in the pilot:** the missing runner of [[investigation]]; the first body in the Job.

## Who they are

Twenty. Fast. A year in Tally's network, which is long enough to be trusted with a bundle and not long enough to be told what was in it. A docker's jacket with the pockets resewn to hold chits flat; a Patched leg, a shoulder assembly and one eye, all second-hand AP&I stock bought on the Slate and paid off in runs; a subdermal warranty tag under the skin of the forearm that she never thought about. No surname was ever given in play, and the network does not use one ([[dessa-rahimi|Chit]] and [[bohdan-adeyemi|Weights]] are the pattern): a runner is named for what she carries, and wax is what a chit's thumbprint is pressed into.

She went out the counter door on the second night of the week with a bundle of the Book's pages that nobody weighed, made two of her three stops in the Labyrinth ([[investigation-02-the-labyrinth-route]]), and did not make the third. Nine days later she is on a table in the cold room behind Threadneedle ([[investigation-03-the-cold-room]]): killed first, badly, by people who were being quick rather than careful; her chrome cut out clean afterwards by somebody competent who was not [[ivo-meszaros]]; and her tag left in on purpose, still answering, so that a ledger line somewhere would keep reading *in good standing* while the parts moved.

## Vector

- **Want:** none now. The Job is about what she was carrying and who wanted it.
- **Push:** the route (three stops, in order), the unredeemed chits, and the live tag — the only part of her anybody paid to keep. Her tag's query log is clue 4 in the chain ([[a-tag-still-answering]]).

## What the crew knows / does not know

**Knows:** a runner went out and did not come back, and Tally will not send the network after her ([[investigation-01-the-counter-door]]). A PC may have met her once — the scene's Flashback prompt asks what she carried and what they owed for it. **Does not know:** until the cold room, that she is dead; until the mirror rig, that the bundle was an order and not a delivery.

## Challenge

None. [[a-tag-still-answering]] (mystery) is the profile for what is left of her.

# A Full Set of Names — Aftermath

The denouement and fallout of [[investigation]] (Core p. 292–293). Downtime follows this; the crew Load Off first (Core p. 124).

## Rewards

### Cash

Tally pays what was chalked, and pays it in the Gullet's usual mixture of money, credit and things.

| Outcome at [[investigation-08-the-cordon]] | Status |
|---|---|
| The consignment ledger **and** shelf 388 came out | *flush* — the ledger is worth more to Tally than the fee was, and she says so, which from her is an event. |
| One of the two came out | *lots-to-spend* |
| Neither, but the crew have the paper-barn lead | *lots-to-spend*, paid grudgingly against the next job |
| The room burned, or the run cleared with nothing recovered | No cash status. The Slate stands, `owed` does not reset, and the crew have worked nine days for a bed. |

Everything paid through the Weighhouse carries *The Percentage* ([[the-weighhouse]]).

Catalog items are burnable by Consequences like any other (Core p. 293), and the ledger in particular should be burnable.

### Information

The real payment. In order of value:

1. **The escapee list exists in two copies**, and the crew are struck from one and standing on the other. They now know that their own status in Palisade is a question of which page somebody reads (Bible §6; [[escapee-list]]).
2. **The Chancery's paper registry has been double-sold for years** by the Office of Concurrent Jurisdiction, and nobody can audit it because there is no third copy (BC-122; [[government]]).
3. **Somebody is buying provenance in bulk**, by category, through intermediaries, and has been for eighteen months — and is now buying name-bearing paper (**[OPEN]** OQ-37).
4. **Where the second copies live.** See the handoff.
5. **Concurrences are a currency.** Whoever the buyer is, they hold enough pre-purchased lawfulness to pay a consolidator's fees with it.

## If the run cleared

If [[the-consignment-window]] maxed before [[investigation-07-the-counting-room]], the Job still completes and completes differently: the crew arrive at an empty bonded room with a swept floor and a booking sheet, [[the-factor]] is at a different address doing the same work for the same money, and [[investigation-08-the-cordon]] happens anyway — because the cell was coming for the room, not the paper, and finds the crew in it.

What changes: no ledger, no shelf 388, no cash status, *short-of-the-mark-3*, Tally cold for a Series. What survives: [[emeric-vann]], the two copies, and the paper barn. The handoff below is unaffected. That is deliberate — Job 2 must be reachable from every ending of Job 1.

## The public-war notch

[[secret-war-goes-public]] advances **only** from [[investigation-08-the-cordon]], and only if what happened there was public and witnessed (*The Noise Absorbs*):

- Continuity's cell and a Chancery Envelope Detail draw on each other in the street, with witnesses → **`public-war-2`**, and the Stretch's tier lifts lock down for the rest of the Job. This is the Threat as written in the Challenge file: *two sets of corporate security, or corp security and a Continuity crisis-response team, arrive at the same incident and draw on each other in front of a crowd.*
- Something happens in the street that no Baseline could have done, and a feed catches it → **`public-war-1`**.
- The room burns, visibly, in a residential district, and Continuity's cleanup does not hold the story → **`public-war-1`**.
- The crew got out under the tape with nobody dead and nothing filmed → **no notch**, and the Job's whole climax is absorbed by the Noise. This is a real outcome and the correct reward for playing carefully.

The rows stack — a loud table can earn more than one — so the most this Job can add is **4** (2 + 1 + 1), and a careful one adds nothing. Job 0 added 0 ([[breakout-aftermath]]); the running arithmetic through Job 2 is tabled in [[spine-and-clock]] (BC-172). No other Challenge, scene or aftermath item in this Job advances the series clock. No new series-level clock is proposed (Brief §7.3).

## Denouement questions

Asked at the table after the last scene, as the book advises writing them beforehand (Core p. 292). No answers are written here.

- Which page is true — the one you are struck from, or the one you are on? Which one would you rather was the one they read?
- Tally is going to keep selling pages. Did any of you say so out loud to her, and what did she do with her face?
- Wax was twenty and carried a bundle because somebody asked her to. Who tells whoever she has, and what do you say happened?
- The second body in the cold room is still there. Did anybody go back for it?
- Four hundred bundles is four hundred people's property, taken off them by weight. You took some of it. What did you take, and who does it belong to?
- The Factor is alive, or he is not. If he is, he is doing the same work next week for the same money. Does that sit right?
- What did this Job cost each of you that was not money — a discipline, a face, a line you held, a dose you took, a hunger you did not stifle?

## Handoff

**The next job is [[acquisition]]** — *The Second Copy*.

The line, stated plainly for the MC and for whoever writes Job 2:

> AP&I's escapee list exists in **two copies**, because the Chancery's Office of Concurrent Jurisdiction double-sold its paper registry. The copies that no longer match — the **counterpart set** — were physically separated out of the Chancery basement years ago and went off the Hill under a records-retention lease to a leased bay in a distribution warehouse in [[kilbride-stretch]]: the **Chancery Records Repository, Kilbride**, which the street calls **the paper barn** ([[acquisition-06-the-paper-barn]]). It is four hundred metres of steel shelving under a digitization contract — the counterparts are being scanned, certified and pulped for the first time in sixty years. It is paper, so it cannot be Harnessed ([[chancery-process]], *Not On The Net*). It is a room, in a building, with a door, and **it can be taken.** Which volume, which bay, and when the van comes are Job 2's first purchases ([[acquisition-02-the-lower-gate]]).
>
> Somebody has been buying second copies out of it, by category, through three intermediaries, for eighteen months, and has now started buying name-bearing paper. **Who that is remains [OPEN]** (OQ-37, and through it OQ-11). Nothing in this Job names them. [[upstart|Continuity]] and [[corp-c|AP&I]] must both remain possible readings when Job 2 begins, and Job 2 may not close the question on this Job's evidence alone.

What the crew carry into it: the reason to want the room (their own names, on a page nobody has struck), a lead with a building in it, and — if they got it out — a ledger that says which categories the buyer has already paid for. Job 2 opens when a clerk from behind the Hill walks into the Weighhouse and asks for one volume out of that building ([[acquisition-01-a-job-at-the-counter]]).

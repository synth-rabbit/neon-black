# The Second Copy

**Type:** acquisition · **Sessions:** 3 · **Pole:** pivot (starts Paycheck) · **Pivot twist:** yes

A heist for a book. Not a prototype, not a Tao-touched object, not a body — one buckram-bound volume of second copies, five hundred pages of leases, licences, remediation contracts, filed escapee lists and numbered concurrences, all of it covering one uninhabitable district at the edge of the map. It is worth nothing to anyone who has not read it in order, and it is being carted away to be scanned and pulped in nine days.

The Chancery's registry is paper because paper cannot be Harnessed ([[chancery-hill]]). What nobody on the Hill says out loud is that most pages exist **twice** — the sets were separated years ago as a clerical convenience and reconciled never — and that a decade of clerks have been selling one copy while the other sat somewhere else, unread ([[government]], [[chancery-process]] *Two Copies*). The second set is not in the basement under the atrium. It is in a leased bay in a distribution warehouse in the sprawl, and it is being digitized for the first time.

Job 1 ended on the fact. This job is the object.

## Hooks

- **The lead.** Job 1 established that AP&I's escapee list — the one with the crew's own names on it, worth no bounty (Bible §6) — exists in two copies, because the Office of Concurrent Jurisdiction double-sold the registry it sits in; that the second copies went off the Hill to the paper barn in the Stretch ([[investigation-09-what-the-book-cannot-say]]); and that for eighteen months somebody has been buying provenance in bulk through three intermediaries (**[OPEN]** OQ-37). The lead points at a specific volume in that building that can be taken: the **Coldwater counterpart**.
- **The employer.** A solicitor's clerk, [[margit-nakagawa|Margit Nakagawa]], from one of the law firms behind the Hill, meets them at the Weighhouse counter. Cash up front, cash on delivery, a described object, no questions about the principal. The principal is [[halima-boyce]], and the crew are not told and can find out.
- **The fence.** [[marisol-okonkwo|Tally]] brokers it because Nakagawa came to her counter, takes her percentage, and fronts the Loading Up against the fee ([[weighhouse-line-of-credit]]). She wants the volume across her balance for one hour before it goes on.
- **The clock.** The Repository is under a digitization contract. Volumes go out by van on a weekly collection run, are scanned in a bay in the Lattice, certified, and pulped. The Coldwater run has a date on it, and the date moves.

## Goal

Take the Coldwater counterpart out of the **Chancery Records Repository, Kilbride** — the street calls it *the paper barn* — before the collection run takes it, deliver it whole and readable to the employer, and leave no record that it ever left. Everything after that is what the crew choose to do with what they read on the way out.

**Prep Sequence and Flashbacks.** From [[acquisition-05-the-prep-sequence]] onward the Prep Sequence is open (Core p. 125): a player may cut the action and flash back to an action their PC took while casing the Repository or securing help, and the Effects *and* Consequences of that action land in the present. A Flashback is never used to Load Up; wildcard loadout tags are the instrument for that (Core p. 115, 125).

## Ties to PC themes — hard choices, one per splat

Each fires **only if that splat is at the table**, and each is a choice between the job and the theme, not a penalty (Core p. 292: when an Identity, Ritual or Itch is challenged, the player may want to engage with the job *because* of it).

- **Bloodware.** The Repository is four hundred metres of steel shelving in a cold bay, and the night watchman is alone from two until six. The Itch is *feed the strain — blood or iron* ([[bloodware-power-set]]), and there is enough iron in one shelf-run to end the hunger for a week — except that a shelf-run stripped of its uprights is a shelf-run that collapses, on a schedule, in front of a scanning crew. Indulge and the theft becomes an incident with a shape no Baseline could have made ([[secret-war-goes-public]]); stifle and mark Decay.
- **Howlers.** The exit is a chase across a sprawl with no cover. The shape would carry the volume and everyone holding it, and the dose is in a pocket. It is also counted: the Almoners' ledger has a number in it this month, and a Howler seen at a Chancery site is a story the Chancery would have to name out loud ([[a-line-on-leash]], [[pack-on-the-run]]). Take the dose and the crew get out; take the dose and somebody at the Kitchen knows where they were.
- **Casters.** The cage is bonded and the lock is a Chancery lock, and a worked piece opens it in four seconds ([[shellwork]], [[worked-sidearm]]). AP&I's Field Assurance rigs are two districts away and Meliora's Adjunct Series is not, but the Repository's own bay is instrumented for fire, not for anything else — the practitioner does not know that until after. Using the practice here is the fast way through, and it is the way a practitioner ends up *on file* ([[countermeasure-scar]]; **[OPEN]** OQ-24). The Ritual is the discipline, and the discipline says how, not whether.
- **Doppels.** [[ileana-boakye|Ileana Boakye]] has a badge, a shift, and a face the scanning crew stopped looking at months ago. Wearing it is the cleanest entry in the job, and it is also the thing that puts her name on the incident report instead of anyone's ([[wearing-your-face]], [[the-face-doesnt-come-off]]). An owned Doppel has the further problem that the handler's feed does not stop at a district boundary ([[switch-and-handler]]); a Cutloose has [[retrieval-contract]] and no credentials at all. The Itch says *become someone else*. The job says *become this one*.
- **Baselines.** The volume proves that Palisade's official record has not been true for a decade, and a Baseline's Identity is a flat conviction of the *I call it what it is, whatever the paperwork says* kind ([[baselines]]). The employer is paying for the volume to be **quiet**. Saying it out loud — to the feeds, to a courtyard council, to anyone — is the one thing that would make it matter, and it burns the fee, the employer, and the crew's only door onto the Hill, and it moves [[secret-war-goes-public]].

## The twist that carries the pivot

Recorded here and paid off in [[acquisition-07-the-coldwater-run]], [[acquisition-08-collection-night]] and [[acquisition-aftermath]]. The crew read it; nobody explains it to them.

Two pages, in the same volume, that were never meant to be in the same room:

1. **An advance concurrence for the Coldwater Outfall**, numbered, filed **eleven days before** the night the Ledger was struck, covering a window that begins at dusk on that night and ends at dawn. It is signed by [[halima-boyce]]. The buyer is named only by a standing-account number. In the schedule of persons and property to which the instrument applies, one line reads: *site population, work lines as scheduled, released*. The crew's work line is one of the numbers.
2. **A transfer schedule filed by AP&I's Contracts Office**, assigning a block of written-off Standing Accounts — the crew's among them — on to a purchaser, for a consideration of one unit each. The purchaser is named only by a standing-account number. **It is the same number.**

What that means, in the crew's own terms: *they were not rescued and they were not forgotten. The night they became people again was bought in advance off a public fee schedule, and their debts were sold on for a unit apiece to whoever bought it.* Nobody's motives are explained, no name is attached, and the volume says nothing whatever about [[tomas-adair|Rook]] beyond a work-line number he shares with them (**[OPEN]** OQ-10, OQ-39 — this job does not answer either).

**Who the number is.** The volume does not say. **[BUILD CHOICE]** — this package's proposal, which the GM may overrule without changing a line of the job: the standing account is **Continuity's**, and it is also the account behind the eighteen months of bulk provenance buying in Tally's Book (**[OPEN]** OQ-37, OQ-11). The proposal is written so it can be refused: the number is the number in every scene, the account can as easily be an AP&I counterparty, a Meliora loss-adjuster, or a shell nobody has met, and every branch in [[acquisition-aftermath]] works under any of those answers. Nothing here touches what Continuity **is** (**[OPEN]** OQ-17).

## Canon and flags

- Canon honoured: the Chancery is deeply corrupt and its registry is paper (Bible §3; [[chancery-hill]]); corporate security is exactly as legal as the police (Bible §3); the crew are on AP&I's escapee list and not worth a bounty (Bible §6); Continuity struck the camp to extract one person and the general breakout was cover (Bible §5). Nothing in this job states why, and nothing states what Continuity is.
- **[BUILD CHOICE]** the counterpart set as a physical off-Hill archive under a digitization contract; the Chancery Records Repository at Kilbride and the collection run; the Carters; the advance concurrence and the transfer schedule as the pivot twist; the proposal for the standing-account number. All extend BC-122 (the double-sold registry) and BC-101 (the Standing Account) rather than adding new canon; the register rows are in [[build-choices]] "Added by WP7c".
- **[OPEN]** (OQ-37) who buys provenance in bulk — proposed, not asserted; (OQ-11) Tally and Continuity — untouched in both directions; (OQ-17) the cost of Continuity's secret — untouched; (OQ-41) whether AP&I's board names Continuity — the transfer schedule is a new pressure on it and does not answer it; (OQ-10, OQ-39) Rook — untouched; (OQ-9) [[collection-run]] is a **job-level** countdown only; (OQ-48) whether Doppels are property in law — the volume contains a property clause and no answer; (OQ-13) nothing here explains the camp's work line.

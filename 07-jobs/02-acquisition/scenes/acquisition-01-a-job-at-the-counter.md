# A Job at the Counter

**Job:** [[acquisition]] · **Order:** 1 · **District:** [[gullet-market]] · **Story tag:** *everything falls to the Gullet* · **Core moment:** no

## Set piece

The market end of the Weighhouse, an hour before the counter door shuts. Wet stone, frying oil, coolant, the sea. The brass balance under the roof is still swinging from the last thing on it. [[bohdan-adeyemi|Weights]] is on the water door with his back to the room. The Slate by the counter door has eleven names chalked on it and one of them belongs to the crew.

Standing at the counter, not touching it, is a woman in a cheap coat over expensive shoes with a hard-shell box under one arm: [[margit-nakagawa|Margit Nakagawa]], solicitor's clerk, three law firms deep behind the Hill. [[marisol-okonkwo|Tally]] has already weighed the box, which was empty, because that is what Tally does to people who come in the counter door pretending they are not nervous.

The offer is short. One volume. Buckram, Chancery binding, a run of second copies, everything filed under the Coldwater Outfall lease. Whole. Undamaged. Nobody is to know it left the building. Half now, half on delivery, a described object and no questions, and the clerk cannot answer any question that begins with *who*.

What makes the crew lean in is not the money. It is the district on the label. **Coldwater Outfall** is where the Ledger is, and Job 1 ended on two facts: that AP&I's escapee list — with their names on it — exists in two copies because the Chancery sold the registry a page at a time, and that the second copies sit in a leased bay in the Stretch the street calls the paper barn ([[investigation-09-what-the-book-cannot-say]]). Nakagawa's described object is a volume out of that building (**[OPEN]** OQ-37 remains the live question underneath).

## Challenges

- [[the-weighhouse]] — asset and temptation at once: the counter is safe, the credit is not free, and everything said here is written down somewhere.
- [[marisol-okonkwo-challenge]] — Tally, if the crew try to get the principal's name out of her, or a better percentage, or both.

## Vectors active

- [[marisol-okonkwo]] — **want:** the volume across her balance for one hour before it goes on, and the percentage. **Push:** the offer, the credit, the back room, the Slate.
- [[margit-nakagawa|Margit Nakagawa]] — **want:** the file closed and the fee accounted. **Push:** cash, a firm's letterhead, and the ability to lose a crew's name permanently.
- [[halima-boyce]] — offstage, and the only reason any of this is happening. **Want:** to read in order what she has been signing. **Push:** a concurrence, which she has not yet offered.

## Beats the MC has ready

- **The described object.** Nakagawa reads the description off a card and will read it again as many times as asked. She does not deviate from it, because deviating is the one thing she was told not to do.
- **The terms.** Half now. Half on delivery. A penalty for damage. A larger penalty for the volume being *seen* by anyone the crew do not name in advance — which is the first thing in the job that sounds like fear rather than procedure.
- **What Tally adds, after the clerk goes.** That the Chancery's registry is paper because paper cannot be Harnessed; that most pages exist twice; and that she has been asked for pages about Coldwater twice in a month by two intermediaries who should not know about each other ([[fence-network]]).
- **The Loading Up is not yet.** If the players want to gear up now, that is downtime and a shopping trip, not a Loading Up moment (Core p. 114). Loading Up is [[acquisition-05-the-prep-sequence]], and saying so now is what makes the Prep Sequence land later.

## What carries forward

- The description, verbatim. It matters in [[acquisition-07-the-coldwater-run]] that the crew were told *whole and readable* and not *unread*.
- A fee, a credit line, and a percentage owed.
- No principal's name — and a clear, purchasable route to one, through [[emeric-vann]] or through the firm.

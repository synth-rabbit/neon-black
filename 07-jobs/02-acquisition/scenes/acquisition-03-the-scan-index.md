# The Scan Index

![[assets/jobs/acquisition/acquisition-03-the-scan-index.png]]

**Job:** [[acquisition]] · **Order:** 3 · **District:** [[the-lattice]] · **Story tag:** *someone is always listening* · **Core moment:** no

## Set piece

The Chancery's Domains are hosted in Gallery Seven, in the rock behind Continuity Plaza, because the Chancery has not owned a server hall in thirty years ([[the-lattice]]). The registry itself is not in there — the registry is paper in a basement, which is the whole reason this job is physical. What *is* in there is eleven weeks old: a records-retention Domain stood up for the digitization contract, holding the scan sets of counterpart volumes as they are collected, plus the index, the shelf lists, the collection schedule, and a certificate for each volume that has been pulped.

Harnessing in is the cheapest scouting in the job and the most expensive mistake in it. The gallery's Nearspace is Continuity's: monitors in every rack row, a firm that has walked this Domain because it restored it, and a Cold Suite two hundred metres up whose staff are paid to notice queries with no client behind them ([[the-cold-suite]]).

A crew with no Harnessing can still get this scene: the same three facts are on a printed shelf list on a clipboard in the Repository's duty office, and [[ileana-boakye|Ileana Boakye]] carries a copy in her coat. That route costs [[acquisition-06-the-paper-barn]] its element of surprise instead.

## Challenges

- [[the-cold-suite]] — Continuity's Harnessing floors and the monitored Nearspace they reach through: the opposition here is not ICE, it is *being an unexplained query*.
- [[chancery-process]] — cited for its *Not On The Net* Special. The Domain holds the index and the scans. It does not hold the registry, and no roll in cyberspace produces a page that has not been scanned yet. That is the job.
- Generic reuse: Cyberspace Intrusion Countermeasure AI (Core p. 308) for the Domain's own ICE, and Hacker (Core p. 309) for a Cold Suite operator who takes an interest ([[generic-reuse-map]]).

## Vectors active

- [[rosalind-ekwueme]] / [[upstart|Continuity]] — **want:** for the advance-concurrence traffic to stay unexamined. **Push:** here, the cheapest push it owns — a call to the records contractor about a service level. It does not know the crew exist. It knows a query happened.
- [[collection-run]] — **want:** to close the shelf-run. This is the scene where the clock becomes visible: nine days, a booked bay, and a pulper that runs on Thursdays.
- [[ileana-boakye|Ileana Boakye]] — offstage, and present as a hole in the data: eleven volumes whose scan sets do not match their shelf lists.

## Beats the MC has ready

- **What is actually in the Domain.** An index, shelf lists, collection dates, scan sets for the volumes already taken, and certificates of destruction. It is the dullest Domain in Palisade and it is enough to plan a heist on.
- **The three facts.** (1) The Coldwater counterpart is in run C, bay four. (2) Run C's contents were re-marked *certified — awaiting collection* eleven days ago, which means the cage, not the shelf. (3) The collection date.
- **The fourth fact, if anybody looks sideways.** Eleven scan sets are short. Between the shelf list and the index, pages are missing — the same three categories every time. A crew that notices this has found the insider before they meet her, and has found the edge of **[OPEN]** OQ-37 without the answer.
- **The tell.** Nothing attacks them. A monitor logs a query against a records Domain with no client account behind it, and eleven minutes later a Continuity client manager emails a records contractor about service levels. That is the whole Consequence, and it is the reason the date moves in [[acquisition-05-the-prep-sequence]]. If the crew get out clean, it does not happen, and the extra week is the difference the players earned.
- **Restraint.** No Bloodware, nothing about what Continuity is, and no answer to who owns the account (**[OPEN]** OQ-17, OQ-11).

## What carries forward

- Run C, bay four, the cage, and a date.
- Eleven short volumes.
- A query with no client behind it, or not.

# Thirtieth Deck

**Job:** [[acquisition]] · **Order:** 4 · **District:** [[amalgam-stack]] · **Story tag:** *the company owns the body* · **Core moment:** no

## Set piece

Thirty decks of foundry, assembly and fitting theatres, and then carpet. The Contracts Office is quiet in the way only a place that makes no physical thing is quiet: filtered air, low light, a wall of glass with the Face's foundry heat rolling past it, and rows of people administering the largest lending book in Palisade ([[corp-c]]). Nobody here has ever seen the Ledger. Several of them have written its lines.

The crew are here because the Chancery's index does not carry a filing reference for a lease it did not write. The filer's receipt does — one page, stamped twice, held in the Contracts Office because AP&I keeps its own copy of everything it gives the Hill. It says which counterpart volume the Coldwater filings went into.

This is the most expensive scouting in the job. Every Fitted body reports. Every permit crossed. This is the building where the crew's own file lives.

## Beats the MC has ready

- **Four ways in, and they are all bad.** As a customer opening a Standing Account, which means a fitting appointment and a warranty tag; as a records contractor with the kiosk's forged credentials, which pass everywhere except in front of the person who filed the original — and the person who filed the original works on this deck; as a Doppel wearing an analyst; or through Novak herself, who can be reached because her department buys rumours and does not check who is selling.
- **The receipt.** One page. A filing reference, a lease number, a date. That is the whole prize and it takes two minutes to read.
- **The second reference.** The same receipt carries a later filing under the same lease, eleven weeks after the night the Ledger was struck — a schedule, filed, referenced, not described. Nobody in the Contracts Office thinks it is interesting; it is a transfer of written-off accounts, and there are four of those a year. It is the second half of the twist, and the crew have now touched it without reading it.
- **The cost.** Whatever the crew do here, the Desk gets a data point. Novak's file on them is written off at zero; a crew that walks its own bodies through the building is a crew re-pricing itself.
- **Restraint.** Nothing on this deck explains the camp's work lines, and nobody here knows what the camp produces (**[OPEN]** OQ-38, OQ-13).

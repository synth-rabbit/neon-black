# The Paper Barn

![[assets/jobs/acquisition/acquisition-06-the-paper-barn.png]]

**Job:** [[acquisition]] · **Order:** 6 · **District:** [[kilbride-stretch]] · **Story tag:** *nobody official comes down here* · **Core moment:** no

## Set piece

Four kilometres inland from the Wall, in a distribution warehouse so large the far end has its own weather, the Chancery leases eleven bays. Ten of them are dead licences. The eleventh is the **counterpart set**: four hundred metres of steel shelving in numbered runs, buckram spines going back sixty years, a duty office with a kettle and a clipboard, a scanning crew of six working a night shift under a rented arc-lamp gantry, and a bonded cage at the end of run C where volumes sit certified and awaiting collection.

The street calls it the paper barn. It has never been robbed, because nobody has ever wanted anything in it.

Outside: a loading apron under sodium light, a roller door with a Chancery seal stencilled on it in paint, drone-lanes crossing overhead every ninety seconds, and — new this week, for the first time in nine years — an [[envelope-detail]] parked at the gate with the engine running.

## Challenges

- [[chancery-checkpoint]] — barrier: the drone-lane gate. Re-flavour: the price list here is not unwritten, it is *cheap*, because this is a warehouse in the sprawl and nobody has ever paid for anything at this gate before.
- [[envelope-detail]] — foe: two Chancery officers on a private arrangement, who do not know what they are guarding and were told it was a fly-tipping problem. **The complication in play** (Core p. 285): more checkpoints and obstacles than first thought.
- [[counterpart-stacks]] — barrier: the bay itself. Runs, ladders, the scanning gantry, the duty clipboard, the certified cage, and the fact that the crew must find one volume in a room designed so nobody ever has to.
- [[collection-run]] — the clock, advancing on every miss.

## Vectors active

- [[ileana-boakye|Ileana Boakye]] — records assistant, on shift tonight. **Want:** clear of the Slate and off the contract before it ends, with her name on nothing. **Push:** a badge, a shift pattern, the shelf list, a door held open — and the same things offered to anybody else who works out what she has been doing. She is the *insider pulling the same job* (Core p. 285), and by this scene she is frightened, because the collection date moved and she has three volumes' worth of pages still unsold.
- [[collection-run]] — six days, or fewer.
- [[ilya-sarpong|Ilya Sarpong, "Barrow"]] — offstage tonight, on purpose. The Carters have watched this apron for eleven weeks and have never once entered the building.

## Beats the MC has ready

- **The gate.** The Envelope Detail is the most bribeable obstacle in the job and the most expensive one to bribe badly: they will take money, and they will also mention it to the sergeant who placed them, who was paid by a records contractor, who was asked about service levels by a client manager.
- **The shift.** Six scanners, an arc-lamp gantry that moves down one run per night, a duty clerk with a kettle, and a fire door on the north wall that is alarmed and is the only exit that does not cross the apron.
- **Boakye, met.** She can be bought (cheap — she wants to stop), turned (she will hand over the cage key and a shelf list and want a way out of the district), replaced (a Doppel wearing her, which puts her name on the report), or burned (told to the duty clerk, which clears the bay and starts a different job). Each choice is paid for in [[acquisition-aftermath]].
- **The cage.** The complication lands here: the Coldwater run is not on the open shelf. It was re-marked *certified — awaiting collection* eleven days ago and moved into the bonded cage at the end of run C, which is welded mesh, one lock, one seal, and a certificate that has to be broken to open it. Everything the crew planned in [[acquisition-05-the-prep-sequence]] assumed a shelf.
- **The heist Consequence.** From here on, denying a PC further Flashbacks to the Prep Sequence is on the table (Core p. 125) and is the right Consequence for a miss made through impatience.

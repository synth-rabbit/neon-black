# Nine Days, Then Six

**Job:** [[acquisition]] · **Order:** 5 · **District:** [[gullet-market]] · **Story tag:** *everything falls to the Gullet* · **Core moment:** no

## Set piece

The back room. A table, a hot plate, a bed that is the cheapest lodging in Palisade at a price the crew will be paying for years, and along one wall twenty years of bound ledgers that [[marisol-okonkwo|Tally]] will break a hand over. On the table: a shelf list, a lease number, a filing reference, a hand-drawn plan of a loading apron in the sprawl, and a mug holding it all flat.

This is the planning beat. The book replaces the long planning scene with a Prep Sequence played in hindsight, side by side with execution (Core p. 125), so the scene itself is three things: **Loading Up**, **the Prep Sequence opening**, and **the date moving**.

## The plan beat, in order

**1. Loading Up (Core p. 114–117).** Declare the Loading Up moment here. One Power each, plus whatever the loadout theme, upgrades, downtime Power, or directly relevant power tags add. Tally fronts what the crew cannot cover — [[weighhouse-line-of-credit]], on her terms, which are that she takes a cut until it is clear and remembers exactly what is owed. **Wildcard loadout tags are the right purchase for this job** (2 Power each): a heist where the players will be flashing back to their own preparation wants unspecified slots more than it wants a fixed kit. Items written into this job: [[guard-baton-and-fob]] (a records-contractor fob, still logged in, until they notice), [[tracker-shroud-cloak]], [[reclamation-worksheds-toolkit]], [[weighhouse-runner-favor]], [[weighhouse-salvage-crate]] (a crate that a buckram volume fits inside), [[kill-switch-jammer]], [[focus-lattice]].

**2. The Prep Sequence opens (Core p. 125).** From this scene to the end of the job, any player may cut the action and flash back to an action their PC took while casing the Repository or securing help. The Effects **and** the Consequences land in the present: help secured becomes a co-conspirator standing in the bay; an injury taken becomes a problem in the corridor. The prompts below are slots, not content — nobody writes a player's preparation for them.

**3. The date moves.** This is the *security was recently beefed up* complication (Core p. 285), and it is the consequence of [[acquisition-03-the-scan-index]] if the crew were noticed, or simply the contractor's completion bonus if they were not. Two things change while the crew are sitting at this table:

- The Coldwater run's collection is brought forward one week. Nine days becomes six. [[collection-run]] starts ticking at its faster rate.
- An [[envelope-detail]] is posted on the Repository's gate — two Chancery officers on a private arrangement, at a warehouse in a district where nobody official comes down ([[kilbride-stretch]]). Somebody paid for that this week. The crew can find out who for the usual price, and the answer is a records contractor who was asked a question about service levels.

## Challenges

- [[collection-run]] — countdown, job-level (**[OPEN]** OQ-9). Its `collected` progress Limit is set here and every scene from now on advances it.
- [[envelope-detail]] — not fought here, but named here, so its arrival at the gate in [[acquisition-06-the-paper-barn]] is a plan going wrong rather than a surprise.

## Vectors active

- [[marisol-okonkwo]] — **want:** the hour with the volume. **Push:** the credit, the runner, the back room, and the sentence *I can put that on the Slate or you can pay me now*.
- [[halima-boyce]] — reachable for the first time, through [[margit-nakagawa|Margit Nakagawa]], if the crew ask for something a principal could give. What she can give is a **concurrence**: a numbered instrument that makes the crew's night the lawful one at that address in that window, which does not stop anyone shooting them but decides whose account of it becomes the record. Asking has a price — she has to know they exist, which is what she has spent the job avoiding.
- [[collection-run]] — six days.
- [[ilya-sarpong|Ilya Sarpong, "Barrow"]] — offstage. The Carters have had the shelf list for eleven weeks. Their preparation is finished.

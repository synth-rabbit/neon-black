# The Lower Gate

**Job:** [[acquisition]] · **Order:** 2 · **District:** [[chancery-hill]] · **Story tag:** *everything has a price list* · **Core moment:** no

## Set piece

Where the Hill's road meets the Crest ring road and the public lift-head. A checkpoint of Chancery police who are bored and not stupid; a row of *permit assistance* kiosks staffed by forgers who will solve the crew's whole problem this afternoon; a café where deputy registrars take envelopes over coffee, at tables, in the open, with the ease of men doing something entirely standard. Above and behind it, five floors of stone around a glass atrium, and a queue.

The crew have come up out of the Foot to buy the rest of an answer they already have. Job 1 ended with the building ([[investigation-09-what-the-book-cannot-say]]): the counterparts went off the Hill to the paper barn in the Stretch. What the crew do not have is **the lease number, the bay, when it was moved, when the van comes, and which volume covers Coldwater.**

None of it is secret. It is filed. It is also, from below, three separate purchases: a clerk to say when the set was moved and under what lease, a clerk to say who holds the lease and the bay number, and a clerk to say which volume covers Coldwater. The Chancery does not hide anything from the crew. It charges them, refers them onward, and charges them again — including [[emeric-vann]], who sold them the building nine days ago and will sell them the lease today at a price that has gone up once.

## Challenges

- [[chancery-checkpoint]] — barrier: the lift-head and the gate. A Patched crew on the Crest without a reason is the reason.
- [[chancery-process]] — barrier: the registry, the rate card, the queue. *The Price Goes Up*, *Referred Onward*, and *Two Copies* are all live here, and *Not On The Net* is why the whole job is physical.

## Vectors active

- [[emeric-vann]] — **want:** out, with enough put by, before anyone audits the registry. **Push:** access sold a page at a time, and the standing alternative of selling the crew's asking to whoever else is asking. He will tell them the counterparts went off the Hill. He will charge more for that sentence than for anything else in the scene, because it is the one that could make him a witness.
- [[halima-boyce]] — offstage still. If the crew push hard on *who is buying*, the Office's duty desk is where the question goes, and a duty clerk notes the name of everybody who asks about the Office's own instruments.

## Beats the MC has ready

- **The café, not the counter.** The cheapest version of this scene is a coffee and an envelope with Vann at the lower gate. The most expensive is the atrium queue and a form.
- **What Vann sells.** The paper behind what he told the crew at the end of Job 1: the records-retention lease and its number; the date the counterparts left the Hill "as a clerical convenience" when the basement filled up; the bay in [[kilbride-stretch]], because floor space there costs a hundredth of floor space on the Hill; and, for a further envelope, that the digitization contract has a schedule and the schedule has a van. **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-162) the Repository and its lease.
- **What Vann does not sell, because he does not know it:** which volume. The filing reference is on the *filer's* receipt, not the registry's index — and the filer is AP&I ([[acquisition-04-thirtieth-deck]]).
- **What he will not say at any price.** Where the other copy of anything is. A man who goes looking for the second copy is a man who has decided to be a witness, and he has three years to a pension he does not believe in.
- **The kiosk.** Somebody will offer the crew forged records-contractor credentials by lunchtime. They are indistinguishable from real ones, they will pass everywhere except in front of the person who filed the original, and the kiosk tells the [[envelope-detail]] about its customers before the ink dries ([[chancery-process]]).

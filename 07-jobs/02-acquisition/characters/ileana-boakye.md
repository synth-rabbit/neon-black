# Ileana Boakye

![[assets/npcs/ileana-boakye.png]]

**Handle:** — · **Affiliation:** the Chancery's records contractor ([[government]]), in debt to [[fence-network|the Weighhouse]] · **Role in the pilot:** the *insider pulling the same job* complication of [[acquisition]] (Core p. 285); on shift in [[acquisition-06-the-paper-barn]].

## Who they are

Late twenties, tired, careful, a laminated badge she keeps turning over. Thirties by the end of the job. A records assistant on the digitization contract at the Chancery Records Repository — the paper barn ([[kilbride-stretch]]) — who owes the Weighhouse for a surgery her brother had on [[suture-row]] and has been paying it off in pages: counterpart pages lifted out of eleven volumes ahead of the scan and sold up a chain of intermediaries for cash in a locker at the Kilbride Interchange. Two removes up that chain the pages reach [[emeric-vann]]'s café table, which is where the nine second copies of [[investigation]] came from (BC-171); she has never met him.

She does not know who buys. Three intermediaries, a chit, and a category list that makes no sense to her — corporate shipping losses, chrome with dead tags, things nobody can explain (**[OPEN]** OQ-37). By the night the crew enter the bay she is frightened, because the collection date moved and she has three volumes' worth of pages still unsold.

## Vector

- **Want:** clear of the Slate, off the contract, her name on nothing.
- **Push:** the badge, the shift pattern, the shelf list, a door held open — and the same things offered to anybody else who works out what she has been doing. She sells the shelf list to the crew and to the Carters for the same reason both times.

## What the crew knows / does not know

**Knows:** nothing, until scouting finds her. **Does not know:** whether she has already sold them out — she has, to [[ilya-sarpong|the Carters]], without malice. She can be bought (cheap — she wants to stop), turned, replaced by a Doppel wearing her face, or burned, and each is paid for in [[acquisition-aftermath]].

## Challenge

None. She never fights. The bay she works in is [[counterpart-stacks]].

# Margit Nakagawa

![[assets/npcs/margit-nakagawa.png]]

**Handle:** — · **Affiliation:** a law firm behind the Hill, acting for [[halima-boyce]] ([[government]]) · **Role in the pilot:** the employer's cut-out in [[acquisition]]; present in [[acquisition-01-a-job-at-the-counter]] and [[acquisition-09-what-a-page-is-worth]].

## Who they are

Thirties, brisk, expensive shoes and a cheap coat over them, a clerk who has learned to look like a partner. She comes in the counter door carrying a hard-shell box that turns out to be empty, reads the described object off a card, and will read it again as many times as asked without deviating from it, because deviating is the one thing she was told not to do. She has never met her principal's principal either. She is not brave and she is not lying: she genuinely does not know what is in the volume, and says so, which is the most reassuring thing anybody says to the crew all job.

Her firm is one of the three behind the Hill through which the Chancery's clerks, the Carters' client and the bulk provenance buyer all route their paper; she knows none of that.

## Vector

- **Want:** the file closed and the fee accounted.
- **Push:** cash — half now, half on delivery, a penalty for damage and a larger one for the volume being *seen* — a firm's letterhead, and the ability to lose a crew's name permanently. If the crew are taken with the volume, the firm has never heard of them.

## What the crew knows / does not know

**Knows:** a clerk, a firm, a fee, a described object. **Does not know:** that the principal is [[halima-boyce]], which can be found out ([[emeric-vann]], or through the firm), and that Nakagawa does not know what the volume says either.

## Challenge

None. She does not fight; if a scene needs her under pressure, run her inside [[the-weighhouse]] or as a Civilian (Core p. 302) with *negotiate 2*.

# Ilya Sarpong

![[assets/npcs/ilya-sarpong.png]]

**Handle:** "Barrow" · **Affiliation:** the Carters (freelance; no Key Player) · **Role in the pilot:** the rival crew of [[acquisition]]; offstage in [[acquisition-06-the-paper-barn]], on the apron in [[acquisition-08-collection-night]].

## Who they are

Forties, Fitted, a docker's build and a clerk's manners, drives himself. The handle is the barrow he started with in the Gullet twenty years ago and still keeps. He leads five people who move things for people who cannot be seen moving them — bonded transit boxes, a van with a records-contractor plate that is real, and a reputation for never once having been in a building at the same time as its owner. The street calls them the Carters because that is what they do and because they named themselves after it, the way the Smokes and the Sidings did ([[cinder-yards]]).

He does not enjoy hurting people and is entirely willing to. His standing preference, said out loud to his own crew: *we are not thieves in buildings, we are thieves on roads* — Core p. 291's own worked example, the ambush set on the escape route rather than in the Repository. He kept the ambush from two of his own crew until the night, because they would have refused it. He will talk, at length, on the route, and everything he says is true except the name of his client, which he does not have. He was told the volume must arrive **unread**, and it is the only part of the job that has ever bothered him.

## Vector

- **Want:** the Coldwater counterpart, whole and unread, to a client reached through three intermediaries.
- **Push:** patience, the plate, the schedule (bought from [[ileana-boakye]], who sold it twice), and force on the road. He opens with an offer, every time, in cash, and it is a fair price; the offer stands until the first shot.

## What the crew knows / does not know

**Knows:** nothing, until a second crew is on the apron. **Does not know:** whether Barrow can be bought, beaten, or talked around — all three are on his profile, and each leaves the Carters either owed and knowing the crew's faces, or a number the crew can call ([[acquisition-aftermath]]).

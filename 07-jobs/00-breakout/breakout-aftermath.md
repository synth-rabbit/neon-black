# Breakout — Aftermath

The denouement and aftermath of [[breakout]] (Core p. 292: "a moment of reflection on what just happened," and "some great big consequences that will need to be summed up"). What is true in Palisade the morning after the strike; what each Key Player learns; the clock; the questions; the Credit Roll; and the handoff into `04-crew/` and Job 1.

## 1. Escapee-list status

**The crew are on [[corp-c|AP&I]]'s escapee list and are not worth a bounty** (Bible §6). Mechanically:

- **[[escapee-list]]** (detection) — `noticed` stands at **0, 1, or 2** at the end of session one, and no higher (**[BUILD CHOICE]** BC-145): 1 if a collar chip was read at the causeway gate after the mains returned or the shore-road scanner read one; 2 if both, or if the scanner got a face as well. *The List Doesn't Forget*: it does not go down on its own. The downtime action that lowers it is the one Tally sells — papers, and a paring knife through five collar seams.
- **[[escapee-recovery-desk]]** (pricing) — not open. On the thirtieth deck of [[amalgam-stack]], [[rasheeda-novak]] has one hundred and four open files from the Coldwater event and has priced the crew's five at nothing: no assets, second-hand chrome, not worth a collector's day rate. *Write off* is not *close*. What re-prices the file, from the first morning: a warranty tag that starts reporting again (*Every Fitted Body Reports*), a name on a Chancery permit, a clinic invoice on [[suture-row]], a rumor bought for a month's rent — and a guard's fob ([[guard-baton-and-fob]]) presented at any AP&I reader, which logs the guard's name and the reader's location together.
- **Arrears.** Any *arrears* the crew took inside the wire (*Debt Is The Lock*) follows them out. It is a line on a Standing Account; it does not heal and it is not physically mitigable. It is the first thing on the crew's file that has a number.

**Nobody is coming.** That is the whole of AP&I's posture toward them, and it is true until they make themselves worth the trouble.

## 3. The clock: does the strike advance *public-war*?

**No. Notch: 0.** [[secret-war-goes-public]]'s *The Noise Absorbs*: tiers accrue only from events that are public and witnessed, and "private violence, however extreme, adds nothing" (Brief §4.1; [[spine-and-clock]]: "a dozen dead in a Fringe Zone with no feed and no witnesses is a rumor, and a rumor is tier 0"). The Ledger is a Fringe Zone with no feed; the strike cut the Nearspace; the only witnesses are a hundred escapees with no standing, a Stack logistics contractor in a truck, and AP&I, which will not speak. `advances_public_war: 0`.

**The one exception**, and it is the crew's to cause: if the crew carried something *out* — a recording made on a device that survived the Nearspace cut, a guard's fob with a camp reader log on it, the truck driver's manifest — and put it on a feed or in front of a crowd in the next job, that is the countdown's first Threat voiced in public (*a job ends with something left behind that no Baseline could have done … and the feeds got there before cleanup*), and it is **public-war-1**. Nothing in session one does this; Job 1 ([[investigation]]) may. The running arithmetic across the three jobs is tabled in [[spine-and-clock]] (BC-172).

## 4. Denouement questions

Written beforehand, per Core p. 292, and answered by what happened at the table. Each has a consequence in the files it names.

1. **Which door did the crew come in by?** The water door or the counter door — Tally's first price ([[the-weighhouse]]), and the crew's first reputation in the Gullet.
2. **Did anyone hear the three words in the yard, and did anyone reach the van?** The referral is either three words or the full sentence; either way it was used. If nobody reached the van, the second Flashback slot of [[breakout-05-a-name-and-a-place]] was never opened, and Rook's exit is unwitnessed as well as unexplained.
3. **Which ending did the MC give Rook?** In the van, or not on the road. Recorded here; [[we-owe-rook]]'s title tag reads differently under each, and Job 1 inherits the answer.
4. **Were the generators up while the crew were still on the lit road?** If yes, a collar chip or a face was read at the gate; `noticed` is at least 1, and Solano's count reached them.
5. **Did anyone go into the flats, and what did the surgeon price?** *Mired*, *burned*, *choking* — physical statuses from [[the-sludge-flats]] are what the Row's surgeon costs against the Slate.
6. **Who has the fob, the shed roll, the shotgun?** Loadout entered in the Book; the fob is a liability with a guard's name on it; the roll is stenciled.
7. **Did anyone break the shore-road post?** *broke a checkpoint* and *wanted-2* in [[gullet-market]] on the first morning, or *the price was paid*.
8. **What is owed?** `owed` on [[the-weighhouse]] at 1–3, chalked in public; the figure Tally had before they arrived is still not explained.
9. **What did the crew Flashback establish, and what did it cost?** Every story tag a Flashback created is live into the next job; any PC who was denied a Flashback as a Consequence begins Job 1 with one fewer.
10. **Did anyone say, out loud, what anyone else is?** The players' business (Bible §6, OQ-19). If it happened in the Weighhouse, it is in the Book.

## 5. Credit Roll

The book's Credit Roll (Core p. 125–126), with this session's particulars.

- **What did the crew achieve? What new trouble did they get into?** They are out; they are on a list; they are on the Slate.
- **Highlight moments.** The hand under the elbow. The gate. The three words. The count read into the empty yard. Which door.
- **Individually, the most important thing that happened to each PC** — and whether it was in the present or in a Flashback.
- **Did any PC mark Decay, replace a theme, or have a Moment of Evolution?** No PC has a motivation to act against yet in this job by design; the Troubled Past themes ([[the-ledger]], [[saw-something-i-shouldnt]]) were voiced, not tested.
- **Did anyone Go Out In A Blaze?** In a breakout, the answer changes the escapee list: a PC who went out in a blaze inside the wire is a body AP&I's Recovery Desk *can* price.
- **Crew relationship tags.** Restore any burned; go around the table; revise any the Flashbacks changed. This is the first Credit Roll, so the tags set at the Weighhouse are being confirmed, not revised.
- **Recover one crew theme power tag for each member of the crew.** The crew theme has one; it is recovered.
- **Load Off** happened on Tally's balance. What went into the Book is the crew's Loadout catalog from now on, at her price.

## 6. Handoff

**Into `04-crew/`.** The crew theme exists as a nascent theme — title tag, one weakness tag, one motivation — from [[existing-crew-kits]] or [[scrape-by-together]], [[we-take-the-city]], [[we-owe-rook]], with a motivation from [[crew-motivations]] (procedure: [[crew-index]]). The custom Crew Theme Specials are named and none is yet earned: [[we-got-out-together]] (another Ledger survivor must appear in play), [[not-worth-the-bounty]] (true from tonight; chosen as an upgrade later), [[we-know-the-under]] (two Under story tags invoked), [[weighhouse-line]] (one honest job through Tally).

**Into `09-loadout/`.** What came out of the Ledger — [[ledger-issue-fatigues]], [[reclamation-worksheds-toolkit]], [[guard-baton-and-fob]] — is on the balance; what Tally fronts — [[weighhouse-line-of-credit]], a [[weighhouse-pawned-piece]] — is the first credit against the Slate.

**Into downtime.** The back room is the crew's first downtime (Core p. 124): Heal & Recover at the Row's surgeon, on the Slate; Prepare for Action with papers that will pass at a lift, by tomorrow; Research & Inquire through the runners. Restoring burnt tags costs 1 Power per scene here, as anywhere.

**Into Job 1** — [[investigation]], *A Full Set of Names*, hired through Tally or her network (Brief §7.2). What Job 1 inherits from this file: `owed` at 1–3; `noticed` at 0–2; any *arrears*; Rook's exit (van or vanished); the fob and the name on it; the truck, if anyone kept it; every story tag a Flashback made; and the chit Tally read without changing expression, which is Job 1's to open.

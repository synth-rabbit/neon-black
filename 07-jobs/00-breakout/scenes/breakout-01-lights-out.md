# Lights Out on Block Three

**Job:** [[breakout]] · **Order:** 1 · **District:** [[coldwater-outfall]] · **Story tag:** *nobody's supposed to be here* · **Core moment:** no

## Set piece

The night count. [[vera-solano|Solano]]'s voice on the yard speakers, unhurried, the way it has been twice a shift for as long as the crew have been here: *Block One, forty-one, forty-one. Block Two, thirty-eight, thirty-eight. Block Three —*

And the speakers die mid-number. The floodlights over the yard go with them, all at once, and the tower lights, and the hum in the walls that nobody had noticed until it stopped. The overlay in the corner of every eye that still has an overlay goes flat grey. In the dark of Block Three — forty-odd people in a long room of bunks, coveralls with numbers on the back, the crew's five somewhere in it and [[tomas-adair|Rook]] on the end bunk nearest the door where he always is — there is one second of nothing.

Then the block door, which has been RFID-locked every night for the length of their sentence, swings a hand's width open on its own weight. It failed open. The remediation contract's one legal requirement was a fire code for the site, and the fire code says a door on a dead circuit does not lock. Nobody in the block knows that. What they know is that the door is open and the guard who sits outside it is shouting into a radio that has nothing on the other end.

Through the open door: torches on the wire, far off. Then the wire itself, a section of it, going down flat with a sound like a sheet of tin dropped from a height, and people in matte grey soft-shell coming through the gap *at a walk*. No livery. A serial number where a name would be. They do not shout.

Rook is on his feet before anyone else. He says the sentence he has said all week: *Stay behind me.*

What is out there, in order: the block's night guard (a Security Guard, Core p. 305, Scale 0, hardship posting, a shotgun and a torch, and a radio with nothing on it); the yard, black, with the shed row on the far side and the wire beyond it; a hundred people from four blocks about to be in the yard at once; [[continuity-crisis-response-cell|the cell]], moving against all of it toward Block Three; and, three kilometers away and on its own generator, the one light still on in the Outfall — the causeway gate, which is down.

**The line security fails in a specific way.** Everything on the mains dies together: speakers, floodlights, readers, cameras, the block locks. What does not die: the causeway gate and its floodlight, on the gate house generator, and the diesel in the generator shed beyond the sheds, which somebody is already trying to start. So the routine ([[ledger-line-security]]) is reduced to torches and a sergeant, the system ([[ledger-security-system]]) is off, the lockdown clock is frozen at nothing — and the only way off the fill-rise that is not the lagoons is the one thing in the camp still working, and it is shut. **[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-142).

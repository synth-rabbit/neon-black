# The Counter Door

![[assets/jobs/breakout/breakout-07-the-counter-door.png]]

**Job:** [[breakout]] · **Order:** 7 · **District:** [[gullet-market]] · **Story tag:** *everything falls to the Gullet* · **Core moment:** no · **Denouement**

## Set piece

The Gullet at the second hour of the morning: the locks, the wharves, the customs sheds, and the market's roof of corrugated plastic and pirate cabling glowing from beneath, under the girders of the Stack's bottom bays a hundred meters overhead. Frying oil, coolant, the sea. The stalls are opening. Nobody looks at five people in wet grey because everybody in the Gullet is wet and half of them are in somebody's greys; the difference is the number on the back, and the Gullet has seen numbers before.

On the quay, a stone box: the port's old customs weighhouse. Two doors. The **water door** on the quay side, a loading door, with a very large man in it who does not move out of the way and then does ([[bohdan-adeyemi]]). The **counter door** on the market side — the one Rook named. Inside, under the roof, a great brass balance that still works; by the counter door, a slate in chalk, names and figures, and at the bottom of the column, in a hand that has not changed in twenty years, five names the crew recognize with a figure beside them that is not zero. Rook's referral reached her before they did ([[marisol-okonkwo]]).

Behind the counter, [[marisol-okonkwo|Tally]]: sixty-odd, broad, canvas apron with brass-dust on it, one cheap prosthetic eye. She wipes her hands, looks at the five of them, looks at the Slate, and says something to the effect of *you'll be the ones*. Then she feeds them.

**The offer** is [[the-weighhouse]]'s and it is everything the crew need on the day they need it: food on the back-room table before anyone asks; the back room itself — a bed, a table, a hot plate, the cheapest lodging in Palisade and the most expensive; papers that will pass at a lift, by tomorrow; a surgeon on [[suture-row]] who will not file, for whoever needs one (a collar seam, a camp-serviced replacement part, a tracker that may still answer); and a first job. All of it at a percentage that does not end, chalked on the Slate where the Gullet can read it. She does not hide any of this. The correct way to lose to Tally is to get exactly what you asked for.

**What she does not ask.** Why Rook had her name. What they saw. What any of them is. She asks after their mothers and weighs the shed toolkit while she does it, and one mild question — *where'd this come from?* — goes into the Book without anyone noticing they answered.

**What she does not say.** What the figure on the Slate is for. Whether she knew the strike was coming (she is never asked in a way this scene must answer; OQ-11). Where Rook is.

**Which door they came in by** is the characterization. A crew that came up the quay and through the water door past Bohdan came in as goods. A crew that came round to the counter door, as Rook told them, came in as customers. Tally notices, and prices accordingly.

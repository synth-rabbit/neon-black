# Breakout — Vectors

The forces in [[breakout]], each with a **want**, a **push**, and a **face** (Core p. 290). The crew is never a vector. Three of the four are people; the fourth is time running out, which the book allows as a vector without a thinking agent behind it — "the flood is about to arrive in eight hours" (Core p. 290). A fifth entry, the fence, is not a vector of the breakout — she is where it ends — and is listed only so the last scene has its face.

## 1. The strike lead — Hanne Oyelaran, "Amber"

**Face:** [[hanne-oyelaran]] ([[upstart|Continuity Risk & Response]], crisis-response lead, Cell Eleven). Challenge: [[hanne-oyelaran-challenge]]; her cell: [[continuity-crisis-response-cell]].

**Want:** complete the contract exactly as written — the named person, off site, undamaged, inside the window — and get Cell Eleven out with no incident to bill.

**Push:** the cell and the emergency. She has already cut the Ledger's mains and its Nearspace, opened the block doors, and let a hundred people run for the causeway, because a general breakout is the cheapest cover in Palisade and it costs the client nothing. She will blow the causeway gate to get her contract through it. She will not spend a round on anyone who is not in her way (*The Contract Is The Ceiling*). She was told a name and a window and nothing else (*She Was Not Told Why*), which is the structural guarantee that no scene in this job can leak why Rook matters (OQ-10).

**Where she is, scene by scene:** through the wire in [[breakout-01-lights-out]]; walking against the crowd in [[breakout-02-hand-under-the-elbow]]; holding the wire gap and blowing the gate house in [[breakout-03-the-wire]]; at the gate, then ahead of the crowd on the causeway, in [[breakout-04-the-causeway]]; at the van in [[breakout-05-a-name-and-a-place]], where she looks at the crew and does not raise her weapon. Gone by [[breakout-06-the-long-walk]].

## 2. The camp's authority — Věra Solano, "Nought"

**Face:** [[vera-solano]] ([[corp-c|AP&I]], Facility Reconciliation Officer, Reconciliation Facility 4). Challenge: [[vera-solano-challenge]]; her routine: [[ledger-line-security]].

**Want:** the count to come out level. Every body accounted for, every account serviced or written off, the facility's number matching the Top Deck's expectation of it — tonight of all nights, when it cannot.

**Push:** the wire, the floodlights, the guards, the causeway gate, and the arrears column. Tonight most of it is dark. What she still has: the administration block, locked from the inside; nineteen years of debt records; a hard line to the Stack the strike did not cut; a shift sergeant with a torch line; the causeway gate on its own generator; and the generator shed, which her people are trying to start. When the mains come back she has all of it again, and the count is read.

**Where she is:** inside the administration block for the whole of the job. The crew hear her once — over the yard speakers, the instant the generators come up, reading the count into a yard that is empty ([[breakout-04-the-causeway]]). They never see her. The next time they hear her name it is a signature on their file ([[escapee-recovery-desk]]).

## 3. The crew leader — Tomas Adair, "Rook"

**Face:** [[tomas-adair]] (the crew's leader on the work line; the person the strike went in for). Challenge: [[tomas-adair-challenge]].

**Want:** **[OPEN]** — not stated by the Bible, and not supplied here (OQ-10, OQ-39, OQ-40). His file's rule stands: Canon and Texture only.

**Push:** the two things the Bible says he does, and nothing else. He **protects the crew** — *stay behind me*, the sentence he has said all week, and the Special *He Protected Them* — for as long as he is beside them; and he **gives them a name and a place** — Tally, the Weighhouse, the Gullet — and expects it to be used (Bible §5). Between the yard and the van he is in the cell's hands and does what they do. He does not resist, does not explain, and looks back once.

**Where he is:** with the crew in Block Three and the yard ([[breakout-01-lights-out]], [[breakout-02-hand-under-the-elbow]]); with the cell from the elbow on; at the van in [[breakout-05-a-name-and-a-place]]. After that, the Bible ends, and the scene offers the MC two endings.

**[BUILD CHOICE]** (BC-143) Treating a vector whose want is canon-blank as a vector at all: the job needs him on the list because the two core moments are his, and the book allows a vector to be defined by what it does when its want is not visible — "someone pushed for this to happen" (Core p. 290). The push is the Bible's; the want stays blank.

## 4. Time running out — the Ledger's security system

**Face:** [[ledger-security-system]] (WP5; the camp's electronic layer — readers, cameras, floodlight automation, gate control), with [[ledger-line-security]] (WP4; the routine — count, quota, arrears, wire, guards) running beside it, and [[the-strike-hour]] (this folder) as the countdown that governs when each of them is awake.

**Want:** lockdown. Every gate sealed, every tower manned, the causeway lit end to end, and the count read into a yard where the number matches.

**Push:** the generators. While the mains are cut the system is dead — no readers, no cameras, no speakers, no floodlights, no lockdown clock; only the causeway gate, on its own generator, is alive, and it is *down* (**[BUILD CHOICE]** BC-142). The strike hour's progress Limit `generators-up` (tier 4) is the time the crew have. When it maxes, the floodlights come back over the yard, the speakers click on, the readers wake, and [[ledger-security-system]] enters play at *alert-2* with `lockdown` at 2; from there every count out of sequence, every reader chime on a chipped collar seam, and every gate line going hot adds *lockdown-1*, and at 6 the Ledger is sealed — Gun Turret (Core p. 319) on the gate, guards at Scale +1, the causeway lit end to end — and the only way out is the flats ([[the-sludge-flats]]).

**Pacing across the scenes:** frozen in [[breakout-01-lights-out]] and [[breakout-02-hand-under-the-elbow]]; the diesel turning over is a Threat in [[breakout-03-the-wire]] (`generators-up` climbs); the generators come up during, or at the end of, [[breakout-04-the-causeway]] — the crew are either past the dark end of the causeway when the floods return behind them, or on it. Past the far end of the causeway neither Challenge pursues (*No Bounty Beyond The Wire*); the watch passes to [[escapee-list]].

## Not a vector: the fence

**Face:** [[marisol-okonkwo]] ([[fence-network|Tally's Weighhouse]]). Challenges: [[the-weighhouse]], [[marisol-okonkwo-challenge]].

Tally does not push on the breakout; she is where it ends. Her want and push are her own file's — the crew working, owing, and clearing through her counter; the back room, a meal, papers, a surgeon, a first job, at a percentage that does not end — and they are live in [[breakout-07-the-counter-door]] as the temptation the crew should accept. **[OPEN]** (OQ-11) Whether she is connected to Continuity is not decided by anything in this job; Rook's referral reached her before the crew did ([[marisol-okonkwo]]), and how is never asked.
